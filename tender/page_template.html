Tender Tracker

Cash tender offers and exchange offers for corporate bonds, read from public SEC filings.

Read the filing before you rely on it.On filings the rules had never seen, offer type and dates were right about nine times in ten, and securities and caps about three times in four. Every row links to its filing. The Reliability tab shows how this was measured.

    Each figure is the share of rows a reviewer marked correct against the text of the filing. The large number comes from two blind tests on filings the rules had never seen, 103 offer releases in all. The small number comes from the last 90 days, the period the rules were built on, so it is the best case. The reviewers were AI models reading the filings. A person has not checked these rows.

    • Recall is unmeasured. In the last 90 day check the extractor found 32 of 33 offer releases it was shown, but that says nothing about filings the search never returned.
    • Caps in euros and caps split by pool or series are known weak spots.
    • Status is worked out from the releases seen. A finished offer shows as past expiration if its result filing was missed.
    • A model reading each filing scored far higher on one 105 row test, but it is not running here because it costs money per call.
    Source: SEC EDGAR full text search, exhibits to Form 8-K. Built by a script that runs once a day. Public filings only. This is a research aid, not advice and not a record of what any holder was paid.
