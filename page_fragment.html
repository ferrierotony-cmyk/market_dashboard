    • Recall is unmeasured. A real offer that the search never found is not on this page, and nothing here says how many.
    • Caps in euros and caps split by pool or series are known weak spots.
    • Status is worked out from the releases seen. A finished offer shows as past expiration if its result filing was missed.
    • A model reading each filing scored far higher on one 105 row test, but it is not running here because it costs money per call.
    Source: SEC EDGAR full text search, exhibits to Form 8-K. Built by a script that runs once a day. Public filings only. This is a research aid, not advice and not a record of what any holder was paid.
