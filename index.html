    Known limits

    • Recall is unmeasured. The extractor found 32 of 33 offer releases it was shown, but that says nothing about filings the search never returned.
    • Caps in euros and caps split by pool or series are known weak spots.
    • Status is worked out from the releases seen. A finished offer shows as past expiration if its result filing was missed.
    • A model reading each filing scored far higher on one 105 row test. It is not running here because it costs money per call.
    • Rows tagged HAND were added by hand from press releases the SEC search did not return, with the source linked. Their fields were typed from the release, not read by the rules, and are not in the accuracy numbers.
    Source: SEC EDGAR full text search, exhibits to Forms 8-K and 6-K, plus press releases added by hand where marked. Built by a script that runs once a day. Public filings only. A research aid, not advice and not a record of what any holder was paid.
