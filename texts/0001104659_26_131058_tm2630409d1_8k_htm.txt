Also on November 23, 2026, the Company, the subsidiary guarantors party thereto and Regions Bank, as trustee, entered into supplemental indentures (collectively, the “Supplemental Indentures”) to the indentures governing the Notes, consisting of (i) a supplemental indenture to the Indenture, dated March 15, 2024 (the “2031 Notes Indenture”), governing the 2031 Notes, and (ii) a supplemental indenture to the Indenture, dated June 23, 2026 (the “2032 Notes Indenture” and, together with the 2031 Notes Indenture, the “Indentures”), governing the 2032 Notes. The Supplemental Indentures were entered into following receipt, pursuant to the Consent Solicitation, of the consents of holders of at least a majority in aggregate principal amount of each series of Notes, with each series voting as a separate class. The Exchange Offer and Consent Solicitation expired at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 20, 2026. The Supplemental Indentures, which are effective and operative immediately, give effect to the Amendments, which eliminate substantially all of the restrictive covenants and events of default under the Indentures. The Amendments bind all holders of Notes that remain outstanding, including holders that did not consent.
