Capitalized terms used but not otherwise defined in this Notice have the meanings given to them in the Indenture. The Trustee also serves as Paying Agent and Conversion Agent under the Indenture. This Notice is being delivered to the Holders, the Trustee and the Conversion Agent for the Notes in accordance with Sections 13.01(b)(iii), 13.03, 13.07 and 14.05 of the Indenture in connection with the Merger (as defined below), the execution of the First Supplemental Indenture (as defined below), the resulting Common Stock Change Event, Make-Whole Fundamental Change and Fundamental Change, and the Company's reliance on Section 14.05 of the Indenture. This Notice does not constitute a Fundamental Change Company Notice under Section 14.01(c) of the Indenture, and the Company is not offering to repurchase, and will not repurchase, any Notes pursuant to Article 14 of the Indenture in connection with the Merger. On October 8, 2026, the Company entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (the “ Merger Agreement ”), by and among the Company, Viatris Inc., a Delaware corporation (“ Parent ”), and Peach Purchaser Sub Inc., a Delaware corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of Parent (“ Purchaser ”). Pursuant and subject to the Merger Agreement, Parent and Purchaser commenced a tender offer (the “ Offer ”) on October 30, 2026 to acquire all of the outstanding shares of Common Stock, at a purchase price of $36.50 in cash, without interest and subject to applicable withholding taxes (the “ Offer Price ”). The Offer was consummated on November 17, 2026 (the " Effective Date "), and following the consummation of the Offer, Purchaser was merged with and into the Company, with the Company continuing as the surviving corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent (the “ Merger ”, and together with the Offer, the “ Transaction ”). At the effective time of the Merger (the " Effective Time "), each share of Common Stock then outstanding (other than the excluded shares and dissenting shares as described in the Merger Agreement) was converted into the right to receive the Offer Price (the “ Merger Consideration ”). The consummation of the Transaction constitutes a Common Stock Change Event, a Make-Whole Fundamental Change, a Fundamental Change and an Exempted Fundamental Change under the Indenture, each as of the Effective Date. As described below, the Indenture also provides that, as a result of the Common Stock Change Event, the Company and the Trustee were required to execute a supplemental indenture to change each Holder’s right to convert each $1,000 principal amount of Notes into the right to convert each $1,000 principal amount of Notes into the kind and amount of shares of stock, other securities or other property or assets (which in the case of the Merger will be cash, based on the Merger Consideration) that a Holder of a number of shares of Common Stock equal to the Conversion Rate immediately prior to the Effective Time of the Merger would have owned or been entitled to receive upon the Effective Time of the Merger (the “ Reference Property ”). The Company and the Trustee did enter into such a supplemental indenture (the " First Supplemental Indenture "), a copy of which is attached to this Notice as Exhibit A. You should review this Notice carefully and consult with your financial and tax advisors. You must make your own decision as to whether or not to exercise your conversion rights (if at all) and, if so, the timing of the conversion and the amount of Notes to surrender or convert. None of the Company, Parent, Purchaser, Trustee, Paying Agent, Conversion Agent or any of their respective affiliates are making any representation or recommendation to any Holder as to whether Holders should elect to convert their Notes or take or decline taking any other action. The First Supplemental Indenture Pursuant to the First Supplemental Indenture, from and after the Effective Time, the right to convert each $1,000 principal amount of Notes into shares of Common Stock has been changed into a right to convert such principal amount of Notes solely into cash in an amount equal to $36.50 (which, for all purposes, shall be considered the last reported sale price) multiplied by the Conversion Rate in effect on the applicable Conversion Date, which for a limited period of time, will include an increase reflecting Additional Shares pursuant to Section 13.03 of the Indenture. For the avoidance of doubt, from and after the Effective Time, Holders do not have the right to convert Notes into shares of Common Stock or other securities of the Company. The Company is relying on Section 14.05 of the Indenture in connection with the Merger. Accordingly, the Company is not required to send a Fundamental Change Notice pursuant to Section 14.01, or offer to repurchase or repurchase any Notes pursuant to Article 14. As a result of the Make-Whole Fundamental Change, each Holder of the Notes will have the right to surrender the Holder’s Notes for conversion. Additional Shares will be added to the Conversion Rate for Notes surrendered in connection with the Make-Whole Fundamental Change, pursuant to Section 13.03 of the Indenture. Conversion Rights, Conversion Rate and Make-Whole Adjustment The effective date of the Make-Whole Fundamental Change resulting from the consummation of the Offer and the Merger is the Effective Date. As a result of the Make-Whole Fundamental Change, a Holder may surrender its Notes for conversion at any time from or after the Effective Date, which is November 17, 2026, until 35 Trading Days after the Effective Date, which is January 8, 2027, pursuant to Section 13.01(b)(iii) of the Indenture. In accordance with Sections 2.01 of the First Supplemental Indenture and 13.02 of the Indenture, Holders who surrender their Notes for conversion from and after the Effective Time at any time when the Notes are convertible will receive cash in an amount equal to the Conversion Rate in effect immediately prior to the Effective Date of the Merger, multiplied by the Merger Consideration per share of $36.50, and will not receive any shares of the Common Stock. Because the consummation of the Transaction constitutes a Make-Whole Fundamental Change, in accordance with Section 13.03 of the Indenture, the Conversion Rate for Notes surrendered for conversion any time beginning on November 17, 2026, the Effective Date of the Make-Whole Fundamental Change, until 5:00 p.m. (New York City time) on January 8, 2027, which is the thirty-fifth (35 th ) Trading Day immediately following the Effective Date), will be increased to the Make-Whole Conversion Rate as described below. During that period, Holders that validly surrender Notes for conversion in accordance with the Indenture will be entitled to receive $1,103.8914 in cash per $1,000 principal amount of Notes converted, based on the base Conversion Rate of 25.2752 shares of Common Stock per $1,000 principal amount of Notes plus 4.9684 Additional Shares per $1,000 principal amount of Notes. Following the Make-Whole Fundamental Change conversion period described above, Holders that validly surrender Notes for conversion at any other time when the Notes are convertible under the Indenture will be entitled to receive $922.5448 in cash per $1,000 principal amount of Notes converted, based on the base Conversion Rate of 25.2752 shares of Common Stock per $1,000 principal amount of Notes. In either case, the Company will satisfy the Conversion Obligation by paying cash to converting Holders on the tenth (10 th ) Business Day immediately following the relevant Conversion Date. The Conversion Rate after such period does not reflect any Additional Shares and, consequently, any Holder that surrenders their Notes for conversion after the Make-Whole Fundamental Change conversion period will receive less than they would have had they instead validly surrendered their Notes for conversion prior to the end of such period. 2 Conversion Procedures A Holder may convert a portion of a Note, but only if that portion is an integral multiple of $1,000 in principal amount.
