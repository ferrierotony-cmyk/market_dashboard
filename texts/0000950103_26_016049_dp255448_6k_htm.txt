Press Release dated October 22, 2026 titled “Geopark announces tender results of cash tender offer for any and all of its outstanding 8.750% Senior Notes due 2030.” FOR IMMEDIATE DISTRIBUTION GEOPARK ANNOUNCES TENDER RESULTS OF CASH TENDER OFFER FOR ANY AND ALL OF ITS OUTSTANDING 8.750% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2030 (CUSIP Nos. 37255B AC3; G38327 AD7 / ISIN US37255BAC37; USG38327AD78) Bogotá, Colombia, October 22, 2026 – GeoPark Limited (“GeoPark” or the “Company”) (NYSE: GPRK), an exempted company incorporated under the laws of Bermuda, today announced the tender results in connection with its offer to purchase for cash any and all of its outstanding 8.750% Senior Notes due 2030 (the “Notes”) (the “Tender Offer”). The Tender Offer was made upon the terms and subject to the conditions (including the Financing Condition (as defined in the Offer to Purchase)) set forth in the offer to purchase dated October 16, 2026 (the “Offer to Purchase”). As previously announced, the expiration time for the Tender Offer was 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on October 22, 2026 (such date and time, the “Expiration Time”). As of the Expiration Time, U.S.$ 266,832,000 in aggregate principal amount of the Notes outstanding had been validly tendered and not withdrawn pursuant to the Tender Offer. Settlement of the Tender Offer is expected to occur on or about October 28, 2026 (the “Settlement Date”).
