(IBC) by OPI IT Holdings Corporation, a Subsidiary of ORIX Corporation TOKYO, Japan – November 11, 2026 – ORIX Corporation (“ORIX”) hereby announces that its subsidiary, OPI IT Holdings Corporation (the “Offeror”), will commence a tender offer (the “Tender Offer”) for the common shares of Internetworking & Broadband Consulting Co., Ltd. (IBC, the “Target Company”). The purpose of the Tender Offer is to acquire all of the common shares of the Target Company (the “Target Company Shares”) (excluding treasury shares held by the Target Company) and to make the Target Company a wholly owned subsidiary. The Offeror has agreed with Mr. Hiroyuki Kato, the largest shareholder and the Representative Director, Chairman and CEO of the Target Company (“Mr. Kato”), that Mr. Kato will tender all of the Target Company Shares held by him in the Tender Offer (Number of shares held: 2,064,200 shares; Ownership ratio* 1 : 37.14%). In addition, the Offeror has agreed with Plus Fuji Co., Ltd. (“Plus Fuji”), the second-largest shareholder and Mr. Kato’s asset management company, that Plus Fuji will not tender any of the Target Company Shares held by it in the Tender Offer (Total number of shares held: 500,000 shares; Ownership ratio: 9.00%). Furthermore, the Offeror has agreed with Hikari Tsushin, Inc.’s group entities (collectively, the “Hikari Tsushin Group”) that the Hikari Tsushin Group will not tender a portion of the Target Company Shares held by it in the Tender Offer (Total number of shares: 696,800 shares; Ownership ratio: 12.54%). Moreover, the Offeror has agreed with Plus Fuji and the Hikari Tsushin Group that, upon completion of a series of procedures to be conducted on or after the commencement date of settlement of the Tender Offer to make the Offeror, Plus Fuji and some of the entities of the Hikari Tsushin Group the only shareholders of the Target Company, Plus Fuji and the Hikari Tsushin Group will sell all of the Target Company Shares held by them to the Target Company as part of the Target Company’s acquisition of treasury shares. At the meeting of its board of directors held today, the Target Company resolved to express its opinion in support of the Tender Offer and to recommend that its shareholders tender their shares in the Tender Offer. Since its establishment in 2002, the Target Company has focused its business on monitoring the performance of corporate networks and IT systems, and is currently listed on the Standard Market of Tokyo Stock Exchange, Inc. (the “Tokyo Stock Exchange”). With its proprietary “System Answer* 2 ” series of IT system performance monitoring software as its core product, the Target Company supports the stable operation of corporate IT infrastructure, leveraging the technological capabilities and the monitoring and analysis know-how it has cultivated over many years. In its domestic Investment and Operation business, ORIX has positioned the IT and information services field as one of its focus areas, and is working to expand IT infrastructure services that support corporate activities. In particular, building on its investment in HC Networks, Ltd. (“HC Networks”), a network integrator, ORIX has been expanding its service offerings across IT infrastructure, from design and construction to maintenance and operation. Following the Tender Offer, ORIX aims to build a life-cycle business model that provides end-to-end support for IT infrastructure, from implementation through monitoring, operation and improvement, by combining the product strength and monitoring and analysis know-how of the Target Company’s “System Answer” with the design and construction skills and the maintenance and operation functions of HC Networks. ORIX will also support the enhancement of the Target Company’s corporate value through the expansion of business opportunities by utilizing the customer bases and customer touchpoints of both companies, the strengthening of technological capabilities and service delivery capabilities through personnel exchanges, and the utilization of ORIX Group’s corporate customer base and sales network. (Note 2) According to the Target Company, “System Answer” means a monitoring tool that allows users to easily start performance monitoring of network and server infrastructure and enables early detection and prevention of troubles through automatic analysis functions such as future forecasting and detection of warning signs. (Note 3) According to the Target Company, “SAMS” is an abbreviation of Speedy Action Management Services, and means a service that, through a 24/7 manned monitoring system, performs immediate failure detection and recovery support and further provides analysis and improvement proposals based on failure status and performance information, thereby not only preventing troubles but also achieving cost reduction. (Note 4) According to the Target Company, the “IBC Care Service” means a maintenance service that provides vulnerability response and fault isolation support for Fortinet’s FortiGate series and Palo Alto Networks’ PA-Series as target equipment. (Note 5) According to the Target Company, “ITOGUCHI” means an infrastructure configuration monitoring tool that realizes a stable IT environment by visualizing the overall picture of complex IT systems and immediately identifying the scope of impact upon the occurrence of a failure. According to the Target Company, with the promotion of digital transformation (DX) at companies and the spread of multi-cloud environments, IT infrastructure is becoming increasingly complex and sophisticated, and the need for stable system operations and robust security continues to grow. According to the Target Company, in this business environment, in order to enhance its corporate value over the medium to long term, the Target Company aims to transform itself from a company whose business model is centered on standalone license sales of “System Answer” into a “comprehensive solution vendor” that provides end-to-end support to customers, from infrastructure construction through operation and improvement. - 7 - Under such circumstances, against the background that HC Networks and the Target Company had been engaged in sales collaboration and information exchange, such as HC Networks handling the Target Company’s IT system performance monitoring tool “System Answer” as a sales partner, in early May 2026, the Target Company approached HC Networks to the effect that it wished to discuss the possibility of a capital participation in the Target Company. In early June of the same year, based on the results of these meetings, ORIX came to believe that, rather than merely making a partial investment, making the Target Company a wholly owned subsidiary and sharing a medium- to long-term business policy with HC Networks under a common parent company would be effective in promoting the collaboration between the two companies on a continuous and agile basis, and commenced consideration of the Transaction on the premise of making the Target Company a wholly owned subsidiary. Through discussions with HC Networks and the Target Company, ORIX came to recognize that there is a high degree of complementarity between the product strength and monitoring and analysis know-how of “System Answer,” the IT system performance monitoring tool developed in-house by the Target Company, and the design, construction, maintenance and operation functions of HC Networks. ORIX believes that collaboration between the two companies will make it possible to provide consistent support from the introduction of IT infrastructure to its monitoring, operation and improvement. ORIX also believes that there is a high degree of business affinity among the Target Company, which develops, sells and supports IT system performance monitoring tools and has advanced technological capabilities and know-how and an extensive track record backed by them, HC Networks, which is promoting the expansion of the IT infrastructure field, and ORIX Group, which has diverse business resources. While a certain degree of collaboration would be possible through a capital and business alliance or the like with the Target Company, if the investment in the Target Company were limited to a partial investment, the Target Company and HC Networks would each make management decisions under different shareholder compositions, and their policies on the allocation of the management resources of the two companies and the prioritization of measures would not necessarily be aligned. On the other hand, ORIX believes that, by making the Target Company a wholly owned subsidiary of the Offeror and placing it under a common wholly owning parent company together with HC Networks, it will be possible to share a medium- to long-term business policy and to promote collaborative measures on a continuous and agile basis while ensuring consistency in the division of roles, investment policies, staffing and the like of the two companies. These initiatives are not premised on the two companies becoming a direct parent and subsidiary, but will be implemented by them as sister companies having the Offeror as their common wholly owning parent company, while each maintains its own strengths. In sharing information and mutually utilizing management resources, it is envisaged that the two companies will comply with relevant laws and regulations, contracts with customers and confidentiality obligations, and will proceed through the necessary procedures. ORIX believes that, by making the Target Company a wholly owned subsidiary of the Offeror through the Transaction, it will be possible to share a medium- to long-term business policy and to continuously and flexibly coordinate the mutual utilization of management resources, investment, staffing and the like with HC Networks and ORIX Group companies, thereby realizing the various synergies described in I. through IV. above.
