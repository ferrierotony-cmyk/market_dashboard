Each share of Company Common Stock that is outstanding immediately prior to the Company Merger Effective Time shall be converted into and become one validly issued, fully paid and nonassessable share of the Company Holdco Common Stock with the same rights, powers and privileges as the shares so converted and shall constitute the only outstanding shares of capital stock of Company Holdco.
