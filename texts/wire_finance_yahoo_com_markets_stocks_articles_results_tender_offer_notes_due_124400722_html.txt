Notes due 2027 (ISIN: XS1646530565) (the "Notes") to tender any and all of their Notes for purchase by the Company for cash, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the New Financing Condition (such invitation, the "Tender Offer"), launched on 19 October 2026 ( link ). As of the Tender Offer expiration date on 27 October 2026, Company had received valid tenders of EUR 172,975,000 in aggregate principal amount of the Notes pursuant to the Tender Offer. Following the settlement, EUR 127,025,000 in aggregate principal amount of the Notes will remain outstanding.
