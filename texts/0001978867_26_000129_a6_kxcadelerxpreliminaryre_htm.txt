Cadeler announces preliminary results of the successful share exchange offer by Cadeler plc to the shareholders of Cadeler A/S in connection with Cadeler’s redomiciliation from Denmark to the United Kingdom, reflecting a 92.02% acceptance rate Copenhagen, 29 October 2026 – With reference to the stock exchange announcement made by Cadeler A/S (” Cadeler ”, and together with its consolidated subsidiaries, the “ Cadeler Group ”) on 21 September 2026, regarding the launch of the voluntary offer by Cadeler plc to all holders of shares in Cadeler, each with a nominal value of DKK 1.00 (the “ Cadeler Shares ”), including Cadeler Shares represented by American Depositary Shares (each representing four (4) Cadeler Shares) (“ Cadeler ADSs ”), to exchange each Cadeler Share for one (1) share in Cadeler plc (the “ Offer ”) in connection with the contemplated redomiciliation of the Cadeler Group’s parent company from Denmark to the United Kingdom (the ” Redomiciliation ”), and stock exchange announcement made on 21 October 2026, regarding the extension of the offer period (“ Offer Period ”) by one (1) week, Cadeler and Cadeler plc today announce the preliminary results of the Offer. The Offer Period expired on 28 October 2026 at 5 PM Eastern Time / 10:00 PM CET. Subject to customary verification procedures, 355,254,085 Cadeler Shares (which include 11,156,931 Cadeler Shares represented by Cadeler ADSs) have been validly tendered and not validly withdrawn in the Offer, corresponding to an acceptance rate of approximately 92.02% of the total number of outstanding shares and voting rights of Cadeler Shares, excluding any Cadeler Shares held in treasury. The acceptance rate satisfies the minimum acceptance rate as set out in the combined EU/EEA prospectus and offer document dated 21 September 2026 (the “ Prospectus ”), section 17.2.3 “Conditions to the Offer”, and the Registration Statement on Form F-4 under the U.S. Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “ U.S. Securities Act ”), which includes an offering prospectus/offer to exchange (the “ U.S. Prospectus/Offer to Exchange ”). Listing and trading of the Cadeler plc shares on the New York Stock Exchange (“ NYSE ”) and on the Oslo Stock Exchange (“ OSE ”) is expected on or around 5 November 2026. The number of shares tendered in the Offer is preliminary and remains subject to potential adjustments through a verification process undertaken by the receiving agent and the exchange agent. Cadeler and Cadeler plc expect to announce the final results of the Offer on or prior to 2 November 2026. Following completion of the Offer, each Cadeler shareholder will hold the same number of Cadeler plc shares as the number of Cadeler Shares (including Cadeler Shares represented by Cadeler ADSs) validly tendered by such shareholder in the Offer. “We highly appreciate the strong support shown by our shareholders for the Redomiciliation. The high acceptance rate reflects the confidence our shareholders place in the strategic rationale for moving our parent company to the United Kingdom. This is a significant step towards a corporate structure that better reflects the scale and global reach of our operations, and towards attracting a broader and more diverse international investor base. We look forward to completing the Redomiciliation” , said Mikkel Gleerup, CEO of Cadeler. Timetable of the Offer The key dates relating to the Offer are set out below. • Announcement by Cadeler plc with final results on whether or not the conditions to the Offer have been satisfied or, to the extent legally permitted, waived: On or prior to 2 November 2026 • Settlement will be made as promptly as possible, and is expected to take place on or around 4 November 2026 (1) • Commencement of trading of Cadeler plc shares on the NYSE: On or around 5 November 2026 (2) • Commencement of trading of Cadeler plc shares on the OSE: On or around 5 November 2026 (1) In the event that the Offer conditions have been satisfied or, if applicable, waived, Cadeler plc will accept for exchange, and will exchange, all Cadeler Shares and Cadeler ADSs that have been validly tendered into the Offer as of the expiration date and Cadeler plc will deliver the Cadeler plc shares as soon as practicable after the expiration date in accordance with applicable U.S. law and Norwegian law. (2) It is currently expected that trading of Cadeler plc shares on the NYSE and the OSE will commence shortly after the expiration of the Offer. Squeeze-out As Cadeler plc at completion stands to hold more than 90% of the total number of, and the aggregate voting power of, the outstanding Cadeler Shares (excluding any treasury shares held by Cadeler), Cadeler plc intends, as promptly as practicable thereafter, to initiate and complete a compulsory acquisition of the Cadeler Shares (including Cadeler Shares represented by Cadeler ADSs) not acquired in the Offer, for cash, in accordance with the Danish Companies Act. To initiate the squeeze-out, Cadeler plc will publish a notice requesting that the remaining Cadeler shareholders transfer their Cadeler Shares, including Cadeler Shares represented by Cadeler ADSs (the “ Squeeze-out Cadeler Shares ”), to Cadeler plc, against payment of cash consideration, within a four-week period, with both the cash consideration and the four-week period set out in such notice. After the expiry of such four-week period, any Squeeze-out Cadeler Shares not committed to be transferred will be acquired compulsorily by Cadeler plc against payment of the cash consideration. As noted in the Prospectus and U.S. Prospectus/Offer to Exchange, in connection with the Offer, Cadeler plc has entered into a financing arrangement with DNB Bank ASA for the purpose of financing cash payments due in connection with the Squeeze-out (the “ Squeeze-out Facility ”). Cadeler plc intends to structure the repayment of this Squeeze-out Facility following the Redomiciliation either through upstream intercompany loans, a private placement, or a distribution of dividends from entities within the Cadeler Group to Cadeler plc. As a result, dividend distributions from the Cadeler Group, including Cadeler, to Cadeler plc may occur within 12 months following completion of the Offer. Suspension of trading and delisting Trading of the Cadeler ADSs on NYSE is expected to be suspended before market open on or around 5 November 2026, with delisting taking effect 10 days thereafter, and trading of the Cadeler Shares on the OSE is expected to be suspended, with effect from 5 November 2026. A separate stock exchange announcement relating to the suspension of the Cadeler Shares on the OSE will be published. Further, Cadeler intends to convene an extraordinary general meeting after the squeeze-out process to approve the delisting of the Cadeler Shares from OSE. In addition, Cadeler’s American Depositary Receipt programme will be terminated. About Cadeler Cadeler A/S (Cadeler) is a pure-play offshore wind installation partner and a global leader in offshore wind turbine transport and installation. The company owns and operates the industry’s largest fleet of jack-up offshore wind installation vessels and is expanding its capabilities into full-scope foundation transport and installation, as well as operations & maintenance. With its modern fleet and depth of expertise across onshore and offshore operations, Cadeler supports the safe, efficient and reliable delivery of offshore wind projects worldwide. Cadeler is listed on the NYSE (ticker: CDLR) and the OSE (ticker: CADLR). For more information, please visit www.cadeler.com.
