Mon, November 23, 2026 at 7:59 AM CST 6 min read ALPEKA.MX MONTERREY, NUEVO LEÓN, MEXICO, Nov. 23, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Alpek, S.A.B. de C.V. ("Alpek" or the "Company") announced today the expiration of its offers to purchase for cash up to U.S.$500,000,000 in aggregate principal amount (the "Aggregate Tender Cap") of its outstanding (i) 4.250% Senior Notes due 2029 (the "2029 Notes") in a principal amount of up to U.S.$300,000,000 (the "Tender Sub-Cap") for a purchase price (the "2029 Purchase Price") equal to U.S.$957.50 per U.S.$1,000 principal amount of Notes and (ii) 3.250% Senior Notes due 2031 (the "2031 Notes" and, together with the 2029 Notes, the "Notes") for a purchase price (the "2031 Purchase Price" and, together with the 2029 Purchase Price, in each case as applicable to each series of Notes, the "Purchase Price") equal to U.S.$887.50 per U.S.$1,000 principal amount of Notes (each a "Tender Offer" and together, the "Tender Offers"). The Tender Offers expired at 5:00 p.m. New York City time on November 20, 2026 (the "Expiration Time"). In accordance with the terms of the Tender Offers, withdrawal rights with respect to the tendered Notes expired at 5:00 p.m., New York City time on November 20, 2026. Accordingly, holders may not withdraw Notes previously tendered, except as required by law. As of the Expiration Time, the Company had received tenders (not validly withdrawn or revoked) from the holders of approximately $318,666,000, or 64.38%, of the total outstanding principal amount of the 2029 Notes and approximately $275,734,000, or 49.84%, of the total outstanding principal amount of the 2031 Notes. After giving effect to the Aggregate Tender Cap, the Tender Sub-Cap and the Acceptance Priority Levels (as defined below), the Company has accepted for purchase U.S.$300,000,000 in aggregate principal amount of its 2029 Notes validly tendered at a proration factor of approximately 90.97% and U.S.$200,000,000 in aggregate principal amount of its 2031 Notes validly tendered at a proration factor of approximately 68.69%. The principal amount of each valid tender of its 2029 Notes and 2031 Notes accepted for purchase was determined by multiplying such principal amount by the applicable proration factor and rounding the resulting amount down to the nearest integral multiple of $1,000. Alpek intends to purchase such Notes on November 24, 2026 (the "Settlement Date"), pursuant to the offer to purchase dated November 16, 2026 (the "Offer to Purchase"), previously distributed to holders of the Notes. On the Settlement Date, holders of the Notes who validly tendered (and did not validly withdraw) their Notes at or prior to the Expiration Time, and whose Notes were accepted for purchase, will receive the applicable Purchase Price, in addition to accrued and unpaid interest up to (but excluding) the Settlement Date.
