Additional Commentary Performance Update Private credit loan 1 quality for the Fund remains strong based on several key metrics including a weighted-average mark 2 of 98.1, PIK income of 2.0% 3 , and zero non-accruals. Notably, while broader non-traded BDCs have faced elevated redemption pressures, the Fund has remained resilient, recording its fifteenth consecutive quarter of net inflows with tender offer requests in the third quarter declining to 2.5%. For the third quarter, the Fund generated net investment income of $0.58 per share, which fully covered the distribution ($0.57 per share). In addition, the Fund realized losses of ($0.18) per share primarily driven by the closed restructurings on two broadly syndicated borrowers, and generated unrealized losses of ($0.07) 4 per share. The Fund employs an independent third-party valuation process 5 to incorporate changes in asset performance and market conditions in the Fund’s NAV on a monthly basis. The Fund’s year-to-date total net return was 4.52% (Class I) 6 outperforming leveraged loans by 123 bps 7 and high yield bonds by 448 bps 8 , and supported by the Fund’s modest software exposure, disciplined underwriting, and monthly cash dividend.
