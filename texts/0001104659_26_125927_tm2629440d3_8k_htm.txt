(the “Company”) and an ad hoc group of creditors who hold in the aggregate approximately 54% of the aggregate principal amount of the Company’s outstanding non-asset-based indebtedness reached a non-binding agreement in principle regarding the principal economic terms of a proposed comprehensive recapitalization transaction (the “Proposed Transaction”). The Proposed Transaction, which has the support of CD&R, the Company’s existing equity sponsor, is intended to enhance liquidity, reduce debt and strengthen the Company’s capital structure to support its long-term growth. Separately, on November 12, 2026, the Company announced the completion of a $500.0 million term loan financing (the “Term Loan Facility”) led by Monarch Alternative Capital LP. The Term Loan Facility is intended to support the Company’s operations and provide a path to completing the Proposed Transaction, with CD&R bearing the majority of the cost of the prepayment premium in the event that the Term Loan Facility is prepaid in connection with the Proposed Transaction. The Proposed Transaction is non-binding and remains subject to, among other things, the negotiation and execution of mutually acceptable definitive documentation, the receipt of applicable approvals and the satisfaction of applicable closing conditions. There can be no assurance that definitive agreements with respect to the Proposed Transaction will be entered into or that any such transaction will be consummated, or as to the timing or ultimate terms thereof. The Company’s management continues to evaluate its capital structure and may pursue additional financing arrangements to support its long-term business objectives. Any such arrangements would be subject to the negotiation and execution of definitive documentation and the satisfaction of applicable closing conditions. The Term Loan Facility is described in further detail below. Item 1.01. Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement. On November 10, 2026 (the “Closing Date”), Fortify Building Solutions, LLC (the “Borrower”), an indirect subsidiary of the Company, entered into a Cash Flow Credit Agreement, by and among the Borrower, Fortify Building Solutions Parent, LLC (“FBS Parent”) as a guarantor, the lenders party thereto, and Monarch Finance LLC, as administrative agent and collateral agent (the “Cash Flow Credit Agreement”). The obligations under the Cash Flow Credit Agreement are secured by perfected security interests in (i) substantially all tangible and intangible assets of the Borrower and certain subsidiaries of the Borrower (the “Guarantors”), including the capital stock of subsidiaries owned by the Borrower and each Guarantor, subject to certain exceptions, and the capital stock of the Borrower (such assets, other than the ABL Priority Collateral (as defined below), the “Cash Flow Priority Collateral”), and (ii) certain accounts receivable, inventory, deposit accounts and related assets of the Borrower and each Guarantor (the “ABL Priority Collateral”). The security interests securing the obligations under the Cash Flow Credit Agreement would rank senior to the security interests securing any asset-based revolving credit facility (the “ABL Facility”) with respect to the Cash Flow Priority Collateral and junior to the security interests securing the ABL Facility with respect to the ABL Priority Collateral. The obligations under the Cash Flow Credit Agreement are also guaranteed on a secured basis by certain intellectual property-holding subsidiaries of the Company in an amount up to $375.0 million. The Cash Flow Credit Agreement provides for, among other things, initial term loan commitments in an aggregate principal amount of $500.0 million. The loans made pursuant to the initial term loan commitments will: (i) mature on November 10, 2030; (ii) bear interest at a floating rate measured by reference to, at the Borrower’s option, either (A) a Term SOFR rate (subject to a floor of 3.00%) plus an applicable margin of 7.75% per annum or (B) an alternate base rate (subject to a floor of 3.00%) plus an applicable margin of 7.75% per annum; and (iii) not be subject to scheduled amortization. The net proceeds of the borrowings under the Cash Flow Credit Agreement may be used, at the option of the Borrower: (i) for general corporate purposes; (ii) to make any direct or indirect distribution to the Company or certain of its subsidiaries, including to fund the repayment of indebtedness outstanding under the Company’s existing asset-based revolving credit facility; (iii) to pay fees and expenses incurred in connection with the Cash Flow Credit Agreement; and (iv) for any other purpose not prohibited by the Cash Flow Credit Agreement. The Term Loan Facility may be prepaid, at the option of the Borrower, in whole or in part, subject to minimum principal amount requirements and the payment of the following prepayment premiums: (i) from the Closing Date through the date that is six months after the Closing Date, 2.00% of the aggregate principal amount so prepaid or repaid; (ii) after the date that is six months after the Closing Date through the date that is nine months after the Closing Date, 5.00% of the aggregate principal amount so prepaid or repaid; (iii) after the date that is nine months after the Closing Date through the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date, the greater of a make-whole premium and 3.00% of the aggregate principal amount so prepaid or repaid; (iv) after the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date through the date that is 30 months after the Closing Date, 3.00% of the aggregate principal amount so prepaid or repaid; (v) after the date that is 30 months after the Closing Date through the date that is 36 months after the Closing Date, 2.00% of the aggregate principal amount so prepaid or repaid; and (vi) thereafter, 0.00% of the aggregate principal amount so prepaid or repaid. Subject to certain exceptions, the Term Loan Facility is subject to mandatory prepayments in an amount equal to the net cash proceeds from: (i) certain asset sales; (ii) certain debt incurrences; and (iii) certain insurance recoveries and condemnation events. The Cash Flow Credit Agreement contains customary representations and warranties and customary affirmative and negative covenants. The negative covenants, among other things, limit or restrict the ability of the Borrower and its subsidiaries to (i) incur additional indebtedness; (ii) declare or pay dividends or make other restricted payments; (iii) incur additional liens; (iv) consolidate, merge, sell or otherwise dispose of all or substantially all of their assets; (v) make investments; (vi) transfer or sell assets; (vii) enter into restrictive agreements; and (viii) enter into certain transactions with affiliates. Subject to certain conditions and the receipt of commitments from participating lenders, the Borrower may establish one or more incremental term loan facilities without the consent of the then-existing lenders. Such incremental facilities may be incurred in an aggregate principal amount equal to the sum of: (i) up to $150.0 million of pari passu secured indebtedness, provided that the consolidated secured leverage ratio does not exceed 6.50 to 1.00 on a pro forma basis; plus (ii) unsecured or junior secured indebtedness (x) in an unlimited amount if it does not require cash payments of interest, amortization or any other fees or expenses in cash or (y) otherwise up to $300.0 million. The foregoing description of the Cash Flow Credit Agreement does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Cash Flow Credit Agreement, a copy of which will be filed as an exhibit to the Company’s Annual Report on Form 10-K for the period ending December 31, 2026.
