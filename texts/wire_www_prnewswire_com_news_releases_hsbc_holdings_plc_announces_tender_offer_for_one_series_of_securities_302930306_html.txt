LONDON , Nov. 5, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- HSBC Holdings plc (the ' Company ', ' we ' or ' us ') has announced the anticipated launch of an offer to purchase (the ' Offer ') for cash up to $1,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount (such amount, as may be increased or decreased by the Company in its sole discretion, including following the pricing of the New Securities, as defined below, the ' Maximum Tender Amount ') of the outstanding $3,000,000,000 6.000% Perpetual Subordinated Contingent Convertible Securities (Callable May 22, 2027 and Every Five Years Thereafter) (the ' Securities '), subject to the terms and conditions set out in the Offer to Purchase (as defined below). The launch of the Offer is expected to be at or prior to 10:00 a.m. (New York City time) on November 5, 2026 (the ' Launch Date '). The Offer to Purchase will be available from 10:00 a.m. (New York City time) on the Launch Date at the following link: https://www.gbsc-usa.com/hsbc/ . The Offer is made upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase dated November 5, 2026, relating to the Securities (the ' Offer to Purchase '). As of the date of the Offer to Purchase, the aggregate outstanding principal amount of Securities subject to the Offer is $3,000,000,000. References to '$' are to U.S. dollars. Title of Securities CUSIP / ISIN First Optional Redemption Date Principal Amount Outstanding Purchase Price (1) Consideration (2) Maximum Tender Amount (3) 6.000% Perpetual Subordinated Contingent Convertible Securities (Callable May 22, 2027 and Every Five Years Thereafter) CUSIP: 404280 BL2 ISIN: US404280BL25 May 22, 2027 $3,000,000,000 100.50 % $1,005.00 per $1,000.00 of principal amount tendered and accepted for purchase $1,000,000,000 in aggregate principal amount (1) The Purchase Price shall be 100.50% of the principal amount of the Securities accepted for purchase pursuant to the Offer. (2) The Securities may be tendered only in an aggregate principal amount of $200,000 and in integral multiples of $1,000 in excess thereof. Holders who tender less than all of their Securities must continue to hold at least $200,000 in principal amount of Securities. (3) Subject to the right of the Company to increase or decrease the Maximum Tender Amount in its sole discretion, including following the pricing of the New Securities (as defined below). The Offer is being undertaken to proactively manage the Company's Tier 1 capital base. The Offer will expire at 5:00 p.m. (New York City time) on November 19, 2026, unless extended or earlier terminated by us in our sole discretion (such date and time, as the same may be extended, the ' Expiration Time '). Securities tendered for purchase may be validly withdrawn at any time at or prior to the Expiration Time (such date and time, as the same may be extended, the ' Withdrawal Date '), but not thereafter, unless extended or earlier terminated by us in our sole discretion. Subject to the Maximum Tender Amount and provided that all conditions to the Offer have been satisfied or waived by us at or prior to the Expiration Time, we expect the settlement date to occur on November 24, 2026, unless extended or earlier terminated by us in our sole discretion (such date and time, as the same may be extended, the ' Settlement Date '). Upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase, including the Maximum Tender Amount and proration, if any, holders who validly tender Securities at or prior to the Expiration Time, and whose Securities have not been validly withdrawn at or prior to the Withdrawal Date and are accepted for purchase by us, will receive consideration equal to $1,005.00 per $1,000.00 of principal amount of Securities accepted, which will be payable in cash on the Settlement Date as described below (the ' Consideration '). In addition to the Consideration, holders whose Securities are accepted for purchase will also be paid a cash amount equal to accrued and unpaid interest on such Securities (excluding any interest cancelled or deemed to have been cancelled pursuant to the terms of the Securities) from, and including, the last interest payment date for such Securities to, but not including, the Settlement Date, rounded to the nearest cent (such amount, the ' Accrued Interest '). Accrued Interest will be payable on the Settlement Date. For the avoidance of doubt, interest will cease to accrue on the Settlement Date for all Securities accepted in the Offer. Under no circumstances will any interest be payable to holders because of any delay on the part of Global Bondholder Services Corporation, as depositary, The Depository Trust Company (' DTC ') or any other party in the transmission of funds to holders. It is expected that the Offer will be financed with cash on hand. On the date of the Offer to Purchase, the Company expects to launch a proposed new issuance (the ' Proposed Issuance ') of one series of perpetual subordinated contingent convertible securities (the ' New Securities ') which are not subject to the Offer. No assurance can be given that the Proposed Issuance will be completed. The Offer is subject to the terms and conditions described in the Offer to Purchase. In particular, the Company is offering to purchase the Securities for cash up to the Maximum Tender Amount. The Company reserves the right, but is under no obligation, to increase or decrease the Maximum Tender Amount at any time, including following the pricing of the New Securities. There can be no assurance that the Company will exercise its right to increase or decrease the Maximum Tender Amount. Our obligation to complete the Offer is conditioned on the successful closing, on terms and conditions reasonably satisfactory to us, of the Proposed Issuance at or prior to the Expiration Time. The Securities may be subject to proration, on the basis of a proration factor calculated as described in the Offer to Purchase, if the aggregate principal amount of Securities validly tendered and not validly withdrawn would, if accepted in full, exceed the Maximum Tender Amount. To avoid purchases of tendered Securities in principal amounts other than integral multiples of $1,000, the Company will make appropriate adjustments downward to the nearest $1,000 principal amount with respect to each holder's validly tendered Securities accepted for purchase. Depending on the principal amount of Securities validly tendered and the proration factor applied, if the principal amount of Securities that are not accepted and are returned to a holder as a result of proration would result in less than an aggregate principal amount of $200,000 being returned to such holder, the Company, at its sole discretion, will either accept all of such holder's validly tendered Securities without proration or reject all of the Securities tendered by such holder. The Offer is not conditioned upon any minimum level of participation. The Company will not be able to definitively determine whether the Maximum Tender Amount is reached, or what the effects of proration may be with respect to the Securities, until after the Expiration Time has passed. The Company reserves the right to amend or waive any of the conditions of the Offer, in whole or in part, at any time or from time to time, in our sole discretion, subject to applicable law. If any of the conditions are not satisfied at the Expiration Time, we may, in our sole discretion and subject to applicable law, (a) terminate the Offer, (b) extend the Offer, on the same or amended terms, and thereby delay acceptance of any validly tendered Securities, or (c) continue to accept tenders. If the Company exercises any such right to amend, modify or waive the terms or conditions of the Offer, the Company will give written notice thereof to the Information Agent and will make a public announcement thereof as promptly as practicable and as required by applicable law. The Company will also extend the Offer if required by applicable law. All Securities accepted in the Offer will be cancelled and retired, and will no longer remain outstanding obligations of the Company. Holders of Securities are advised to read carefully the Offer to Purchase in full, including the 'Risk Factors' section, for full details of and information on the procedures for participating in the Offer. The Company has retained HSBC Bank plc as dealer manager for the Offer (the ' Dealer Manager '). Questions and requests for assistance related to the Offer may be directed to the Dealer Manager at UK: +44 (0)20 7992 6237, US: +1 (212) 525-5552 (Collect) or +1 (888) HSBC-4LM (Toll Free), or by email at [email protected] . You may also contact your broker, dealer, custodian bank, trust company or other nominee for assistance concerning the Offer. If the Offer is terminated, Securities tendered pursuant to the Offer will be returned promptly to the tendering holders. Holders of Securities are advised to check with any bank, securities broker or other intermediary through which they hold Securities as to when such intermediary would need to receive instructions from a beneficial owner in order for that beneficial owner to be able to participate in, or withdraw their instruction to participate in, the Offer before the deadlines specified herein and in the Offer to Purchase. The deadlines set by any such intermediary and DTC for the submission and withdrawal of tender instructions will also be earlier than the relevant deadlines specified herein and in the Offer to Purchase. Holders of the Securities are urged to carefully read the Offer to Purchase before making any decision with respect to the Offer.
