Announces Pricing Terms, Expiration and Results of Cash Tender Offers and Consent Solicitations for Its 4.125% Senior Notes due 2028 and 4.125% Senior Notes due 2029 November 11, 2026 LYNCHBURG, Va.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Nov. 11, 2026-- BWX Technologies, Inc. (NYSE: BWXT) (“BWXT”) announced today the pricing terms, expiration and results of its previously announced (i) offers to purchase for cash (the “Offers”) from each holder and beneficial owner (each, a “Holder”) any and all of its outstanding 4.125% Senior Notes due 2028 (CUSIP Nos. 05605H AB6 / U12213 AB7) (the “2028 Notes”) and 4.125% Senior Notes due 2029 (CUSIP Nos. 05605H AC4 / U12213 AC5) (the “2029 Notes” and, together with the 2028 Notes, the “Notes” and each, a “Series”) and (ii) solicitations (the “Solicitations”) of consents (“Consents”) to certain proposed amendments to each of the indentures governing the Notes (as amended or supplemented, the “Indentures”). The Offers and the Solicitations are being made on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase and Consent Solicitation Statement, dated November 4, 2026 (the “Statement”). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this press release have the meanings given to them in the Statement. The Offers and the Solicitations expired at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 10, 2026 (the “Expiration Time”). Withdrawal rights for the Offers and revocation rights for the Solicitations expired at the Expiration Time, and accordingly, Notes validly tendered in the Offers and Consents validly delivered in the Solicitations may no longer be withdrawn or revoked except where additional withdrawal or revocation rights are required by law. Subject to the satisfaction or waiver of certain conditions described in the Statement, and outlined further below, BWXT has accepted for purchase any and all of the Notes validly tendered and not withdrawn as set forth in the table below under “Principal Amount Tendered” (the “Accepted Notes”). The “Consideration” per $1,000 principal amount of Accepted Notes has been calculated as described in the Statement. The Consideration does not include accrued and unpaid interest on such Accepted Notes from the last interest payment date to, but not including, the Settlement Date, which will be paid in addition to the Consideration. The Consideration was determined at 3:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 10, 2026. Holders of Accepted Notes will receive the Consideration, plus accrued and unpaid interest on such Accepted Notes from the last interest payment date to, but not including, November 13, 2026, the second business day following the Expiration Time (the “Settlement Date”). Interest will cease to accrue on the Settlement Date for all Accepted Notes. Accepted Notes purchased in the Offers will be cancelled. BWXT has also received the Requisite Consents to adopt the Proposed Amendments with respect to each Series of Notes. BWXT has executed and delivered to the trustee a New Supplemental Indenture to each Indenture. The New Supplemental Indentures became effective upon execution and the Proposed Amendments will become operative when the Notes with respect to which Consents have been delivered are paid for on the Settlement Date. BWXT’s obligation to accept for purchase, and to pay for, Notes and Consents validly tendered or delivered and not validly withdrawn or revoked pursuant to the Offers and the Solicitations is subject to the satisfaction or waiver of certain conditions described in the Statement, including the successful completion of the New Notes Offering and certain general conditions, each as described in the Statement. BWXT may waive any condition, in whole or in part, with respect to either or both Series. No assurance can be given that the New Notes Offering will be completed.
