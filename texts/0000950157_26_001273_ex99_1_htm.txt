Occidental is offering to purchase for cash, in two separate pools (each, a “ Pool ” and, together, the “ Pools ”), subject to the order of priority set forth in the tables above, (i) up to $500.0 million aggregate principal amount (as such amount may be increased by Occidental, the “ Pool 1 Cap ”) of its 7.200% Debentures due 2029 (the “ 7.200% 2029 Debentures ”), 6.625% Senior Notes due 2030 (the “ 6.625% 2030 Notes ”), 7.950% Debentures due 2029 (the “ 7.950% 2029 Debentures ”), 8.450% Senior Notes due 2029 (the “ 8.450% 2029 Notes ”) and 8.875% Senior Notes due 2030 (the “ 8.875% 2030 Notes ”) and (ii) up to $500.0 million aggregate principal amount (as such amount may be increased by Occidental, the “ Pool 2 Cap ”) of its 6.050% Senior Notes due 2054 (the “ 6.050% 2054 Notes ”), 5.550% Senior Notes due 2034 (the “ 5.550% 2034 Notes ”), 4.10% Senior Notes due 2047 (the “ 4.10% 2047 Notes ”), 4.20% Senior Notes due 2048 (the “ 4.20% 2048 Notes ”), 4.400% Senior Notes due 2049 (the “ 4.400% 2049 Notes ”), 4.400% Senior Notes due 2046 (the “ 4.400% 2046 Notes ”), 4.500% Senior Notes due 2044 (the “ 4.500% 2044 Notes ”), 4.625% Senior Notes due 2045 (the “ 4.625% 2045 Notes ”), 4.300% Senior Notes due 2039 (the “ 4.300% 2039 Notes ”), 6.200% Senior Notes due 2040 (the “ 6.200% 2040 Notes ”) and 6.600% Senior Notes due 2046 (the “ 6.600% 2046 Notes ” and, together with the 7.200% 2029 Debentures, the 6.625% 2030 Notes, the 7.950% 2029 Debentures, the 8.450% 2029 Notes, the 8.875% 2030 Notes, the 6.050% 2054 Notes, the 5.550% 2034 Notes, the 4.10% 2047 Notes, the 4.20% 2048 Notes, the 4.400% 2049 Notes, the 4.400% 2046 Notes, the 4.500% 2044 Notes, the 4.625% 2045 Notes, the 4.300% 2039 Notes and the 6.200% 2040 Notes, the “ Notes ”). Subject to the Pool 1 Cap and the Pool 2 Cap, as applicable, and any proration, the amount of a series of Notes of a Pool that is purchased in the related Tender Offers on the Early Settlement Date or the Settlement Date (each defined below), as applicable, will be based on the order of priority (the “ Acceptance Priority Level ”) for such series of Notes of such Pool as set forth in the tables above. Each Tender Offer and Consent Solicitation will expire at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on December 17, 2026, unless extended or earlier terminated by Occidental (the “ Expiration Date ”). No tenders submitted after the Expiration Date will be valid. Subject to the terms and conditions of the Tender Offers and Consent Solicitations, the consideration (the “ Total Consideration ”) for each $1,000 principal amount of Notes validly tendered at or prior to the Early Tender Time (as defined below) and accepted for purchase pursuant to the Tender Offers will be determined in the manner described in the Offer to Purchase by reference to the applicable fixed spread specified in the tables above plus the yield of the applicable U.S. Treasury security specified in the tables above (the “ Reference Treasury Security ”), based on the bid-side price of such Reference Treasury Security as quoted on the Bloomberg Reference Page specified in the tables above at 10:00 a.m., New York City time, on December 3, 2026, unless extended or earlier terminated by Occidental with respect to any Tender Offer and Consent Solicitation (such date and time, as it may be extended, the “ Price Determination Time ”). Holders of Notes that are validly tendered at or prior to 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on December 2, 2026 (subject to Occidental’s extension or early termination with respect to any Tender Offer and Consent Solicitation, the “ Early Tender Time ”) and accepted for purchase pursuant to the applicable Tender Offer will receive the Total Consideration for such series of Notes, which includes the applicable early tender premium for such series of Notes as set forth in the tables above (the “ Early Tender Premium ”). The Total Consideration for a series of Notes minus the Early Tender Premium for such series of Notes is referred to as the “ Tender Offer Consideration ” for such series of Notes. Holders of Notes validly tendered after the Early Tender Time, but at or prior to the Expiration Date, and accepted for purchase pursuant to the applicable Tender Offer will receive the applicable Tender Offer Consideration but will not be eligible to receive the Early Tender Premium. All holders of Notes validly tendered and accepted for purchase pursuant to the Tender Offers will also receive accrued and unpaid interest, if any, on such Notes from the last interest payment date with respect to those Notes to, but not including, the Early Settlement Date or Settlement Date, as applicable. Notes that have been tendered may be withdrawn from the applicable Tender Offer at or prior to 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on December 2, 2026 (subject to Occidental’s extension or early termination with respect to the applicable Tender Offer, the “ Withdrawal Deadline ”). After the Withdrawal Deadline, holders of Notes cannot withdraw their tendered Notes or revoke their consents under a Consent Solicitation unless Occidental is required to extend withdrawal rights under applicable law. Occidental reserves the right, but is under no obligation, to increase the Pool 1 Cap and/or the Pool 2 Cap at any time, subject to applicable law. If Occidental increases the Pool 1 Cap and/or the Pool 2 Cap, it does not expect to extend the applicable Withdrawal Deadline, subject to applicable law. Subject to the Pool 1 Cap and the Pool 2 Cap, as applicable, and any proration, Occidental will purchase any Notes that have been validly tendered at or prior to the Early Tender Time and accepted in the applicable Tender Offer promptly following the Early Tender Time (such date, the “ Early Settlement Date ”). The Early Settlement Date is expected to occur on or about the second business day following the Early Tender Time. Settlement for Notes validly tendered after the Early Tender Time, but at or prior to the Expiration Date, and accepted for purchase in the applicable Tender Offer, will occur promptly following the Expiration Date (such date, the “ Settlement Date ”). The Settlement Date is expected to occur no later than the second business day following the Expiration Date. If an aggregate principal amount of Notes of a particular Pool validly tendered at or prior to the Early Tender Time exceeds the Pool 1 Cap or the Pool 2 Cap, as applicable, Occidental will not accept for purchase any Notes of such Pool tendered after the applicable Early Tender Time and will, subject to the Pool 1 Cap or the Pool 2 Cap, as applicable, accept for purchase only the Notes of such Pool validly tendered at or prior to the Early Tender Time pursuant to the Acceptance Priority Level. Notes of a Pool validly tendered at or prior to the Early Tender Time will have priority over Notes of such Pool tendered after the Early Tender Time, regardless of the Acceptance Priority Level. Acceptance of tenders of any series of Notes of a Pool may be subject to proration if the aggregate principal amount of all Notes of such Pool validly tendered is greater than the Pool 1 Cap or Pool 2 Cap, as applicable. As part of the Tender Offers, Occidental is also soliciting consents (the “ Consent Solicitations ”) from the holders of the Notes for certain proposed amendments (the “ Proposed Amendments ”) described in the Offer to Purchase that would, among other things, eliminate certain of the covenants contained in the indentures governing the Notes with respect to the applicable series of Notes and change the minimum notice period for a notice of redemption to holders in respect of such applicable series of Notes to 5 business days prior to the applicable redemption date. Adoption of the Proposed Amendments with respect to each series of Notes requires the requisite consent applicable to such series of Notes as described in the Offer to Purchase (the “ Requisite Consent ”). Each holder tendering Notes pursuant to the Tender Offers must also deliver consents to the Proposed Amendments pursuant to the related Consent Solicitation and will be deemed to have delivered their consents by virtue of such tender. Holders may not deliver consents without also tendering their Notes. The Proposed Amendments relating to a series of Notes will become operative with respect to a series of Notes upon Occidental’s acceptance for purchase, pursuant to the applicable Tender Offer, of a principal amount of the applicable series of Notes representing the Requisite Consent for such series of Notes and payment therefor on the Early Settlement Date or Settlement Date, as applicable. If the Proposed Amendments become operative with respect to a series of Notes, holders of that series of Notes that do not tender their Notes of such series at or prior to the Expiration Date, or at all, will be bound by the Proposed Amendments, meaning that such holders will no longer have the benefit of certain covenants contained in the indenture governing such series of Notes, and Notes of such series held by such holders will be subject to redemption based upon the shorter notice period. In the event of any proration of a series of Notes or if a series is not accepted for purchase, the related consents will be null and void and the Proposed Amendments will not become operative for that series, except that any previously executed supplemental indenture for that series will remain valid and binding. The Tender Offers are not conditioned on the tender of any minimum principal amount of Notes, the consummation of any other Tender Offer or obtaining any Requisite Consent. However, the Tender Offers and Consent Solicitations are subject to, and conditioned upon, the satisfaction or waiver of certain conditions described in the Offer to Purchase. J.P. About Occidental Occidental is an international energy company that produces , markets and transports oil and natural gas to maximize value and provide resources fundamental to life. The company leverages its global leadership in carbon management to advance lower-carbon technologies and products. Headquartered in Houston, Occidental primarily operates in the United States, the Middle East and North Africa. To learn more, visit oxy.com .
