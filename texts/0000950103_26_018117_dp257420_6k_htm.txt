Item 1 Millicom (Tigo) announces tender results of cash tender offer for any and all of its outstanding 6.25% senior notes due 2029 Luxembourg, November 24, 2026 – Millicom International Cellular S.A. (“Millicom” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: TIGO), a public limited liability company (société anonyme) organized and established under the laws of the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg, today announced the tender results in connection with its offer to purchase for cash any and all of its outstanding 6.25% Senior Notes due 2029 (the “Notes”) (the “Tender Offer”). The Tender Offer was made upon the terms and subject to the conditions (including the Financing Condition (as defined in the Offer to Purchase)) set forth in the offer to purchase dated November 17, 2026 (the “Offer to Purchase”). As previously announced, the expiration time for the Tender Offer was 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 23, 2026 (such date and time, the “Expiration Time”). As of the Expiration Time, U.S.$431,262,000 aggregate principal amount of the Notes, representing approximately 70% of the outstanding Notes, had been validly tendered and not validly withdrawn pursuant to the Tender Offer. Settlement of the Tender Offer is expected to occur on or about November 25, 2026 (the “Settlement Date”). Item 2 Millicom (Tigo) completes issuance of 7.500% $900 million Senior Notes due 2033 Luxembourg, November 24, 2026 – Millicom International Cellular S.A. (“Millicom”) today completed the issuance of its 7.500% $900,000,000 Senior Notes due 2033 (the “Notes”) in a private offering that is exempt from the registration requirements of the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities Act”). Millicom expects to use the proceeds from the issuance of the Notes (i) to fund the purchase of any and all of Millicom’s outstanding 6.250% senior notes due 2029 (the “6.250% Notes”) validly tendered and accepted for purchase in a concurrent tender offer (the “Tender Offer”), (ii) to redeem any 6.250% Notes that are not purchased in the Tender Offer, which, depending on the final outcome of the Tender Offer, we expect will occur on or about March 25, 2027, (iii) to redeem in full Millicom’s outstanding SEK 2.25 billion floating rate senior unsecured sustainability bonds due 2027, in each case, together with accrued and unpaid interest thereon, (iv) for working capital and general corporate purposes and (v) to pay fees and expenses in connection therewith and with the offering. The Notes have been offered only to persons reasonably believed to be qualified institutional buyers in reliance on Rule 144A under the Securities Act, and outside the United States, only to non-U.S. investors pursuant to Regulation S. The Notes will not be registered under the Securities Act or the securities laws of any other jurisdiction and may not be offered or sold in the United States absent registration or an applicable exemption from the registration requirements of the Securities Act and applicable state laws.
