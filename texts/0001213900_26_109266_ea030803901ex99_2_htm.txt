(a) Unless the context of this Agreement otherwise requires or unless otherwise specified, (i) words of any gender shall be construed as masculine, feminine, neuter or any other gender, as applicable; (ii) words using the singular or plural number also include the plural or singular number, respectively; (iii) the terms “hereof,” “herein,” “hereby,” “herewith,” “hereto” and derivative or similar words refer to this entire Agreement; (iv) the terms “Article” or “Section” refer to the specified Article or Section of this Agreement; (v) the terms “Schedule” or “Exhibit” refer to the specified Schedule or Exhibit of this Agreement; (vi) the words “including,” “included,” or “includes” shall mean “including, without limitation;” (vii) the word “extent” in the phrase “to the extent” means the degree to which a subject or thing extends and such phrase shall not simply mean “if;” and (viii) the word “or” shall be disjunctive but not exclusive. (b) Unless the context of this Agreement otherwise requires, references to statutes shall include all regulations promulgated thereunder and references to statutes or regulations shall be construed as including all statutory and regulatory provisions consolidating, amending or replacing the statute or regulation. (d) Whenever this Agreement refers to a number of days, such number shall refer to calendar days unless Business Days are specified. Page 23 Article II TRANSACTIONS; CLOSING Section 2.1 Pre-Closing Actions . (a) Prior to the Company Merger Effective Time, the Company shall (i) obtain any necessary consents, waivers or releases from third parties; (ii) adopt applicable resolutions of the Company Board; and (iii) take all other appropriate actions necessary or desirable to effectuate the provisions of this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby. (b) No later than three (3) Business Days prior to the Closing Date, the Company shall deliver to SPAC a written report setting forth in reasonable detail an estimate of all Company Transaction Expenses (together with written invoices and wire transfer instructions for the payment thereof), solely to the extent such fees and expenses are incurred and expected to remain unpaid as of the close of business on the Business Day immediately preceding the Closing Date (the “ Company Transaction Expenses Certificate ”). From the date of this Agreement until the delivery of the Company Transaction Expenses Certificate, the Company shall inform SPAC regularly of the anticipated amount of the Company Transaction Expenses. (c) As soon as reasonably practicable, but in any event no later than three (3) Business Days prior to the Closing Date, SPAC shall deliver to the Company a written notice setting forth (i) the aggregate amount of cash proceeds required to satisfy the exercise of the SPAC Share Redemption; (ii) a written report setting forth a good faith estimate of all SPAC Transaction Expenses (together with written invoices (actual or pro forma) and wire transfer instructions for the payment thereof), which amounts (excluding any taxes payable by SPAC in connection with the SPAC Share Redemption and other Transfer Taxes) shall not exceed the SPAC Expense Cap or Extended SPAC Expense Cap, as applicable; (iii) the estimated amount of Trust Cash available after giving effect to the SPAC Share Redemptions and payment of SPAC Transaction Expenses; (iv) the aggregate amount of PIPE Investment proceeds committed pursuant to executed PIPE Subscription Agreements and expected to be funded to PubCo; (v) the aggregate amount of all Working Capital Loans to be repaid on the Closing Date; and (vi) the resulting estimated SPAC Contribution Amount to PubCo (the “ SPAC Financing Certificate ”). For the avoidance of doubt, nothing contained herein shall affect SPAC’s ability to be reimbursed (and any invoices to the SPAC to be paid) for any SPAC Transaction Expenses incurred after the delivery of the SPAC Financing Certificate, subject to the SPAC Expense Cap or Extended SPAC Expense Cap, as applicable, and the provisions set forth in Section 6.2 . The Company shall have one (1) Business Day to review and raise any objections to the SPAC Financing Certificate, and the Company and SPAC shall cooperate in good faith to resolve any objection. SPAC may adjust the SPAC Financing Certificate accordingly, which shall thereafter become the SPAC Financing Certificate for all purposes of this Agreement. From the date of this Agreement until the delivery of the SPAC Financing Certificate, the SPAC shall inform the Company regularly of the anticipated amount of the SPAC Transaction Expenses.
