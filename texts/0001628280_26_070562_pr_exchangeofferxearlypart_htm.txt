EX-99.1 2 pr-exchangeofferxearlypart.htm EX-99.1 Document Exhibit 99.1 Dream Finders Announces Early Participation Results of Private Exchange Offer and Consent Solicitation JACKSONVILLE, Fla., — November 5, 2026 — Dream Finders Homes, Inc. (NYSE: DFH) (“Dream Finders”) today announced the early participation results of its previously announced private exchange offers (the “Exchange Offer”). Following Dream Finders’ recently completed acquisition of Beazer Homes USA, Inc. (“Beazer”), Dream Finders announced the offers to Eligible Holders (as defined herein) to exchange any and all outstanding notes issued by Beazer and set forth in the table below (the “Beazer Notes”) for new notes to be issued by Dream Finders (the “Dream Finders Notes”), and the related solicitation of consents to adopt certain proposed amendments to the indentures governing the Beazer Notes (the “Proposed Amendments” and such solicitation, the “Consent Solicitation”). As of 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 4, 2026 (the “Early Participation Date”), the following principal amounts of each series of Beazer Notes had been validly tendered and not validly withdrawn (and consents thereby validly delivered and not validly revoked): Series of Beazer Notes Principal Amount Outstanding Beazer Notes Tendered as of the Early Participation Date Principal Amount Approximate Percentage 7.500% Senior Notes due 2031 (CUSIP Nos. 07556QBU8 / U0758TAT4) $250,000,000 $248,062,000 99.22% 8.000% Senior Notes due 2032 (CUSIP Nos. 07556QBV6 / U0758TAU1) $400,000,000 $393,125,000 98.28% As of the Early Participation Date, Dream Finders received consents from holders of at least a majority in aggregate principal amount of each series of Beazer Notes (the “Requisite Consents”) to adopt the Proposed Amendments with respect to each series of Beazer Notes. Accordingly, Beazer and the trustee for each series of Beazer Notes are expected to execute and deliver supplemental indentures to the indentures governing the Beazer Notes to effect the Proposed Amendments, which will eliminate substantially all of the restrictive covenants and events of default under such indentures. Dream Finders has elected not to have an early settlement date. As a result, the settlement date is expected to be November 23, 2026, the first business day after the Expiration Date (as defined below), at which time Dream Finders Notes will be issued in exchange for all validly tendered and not validly withdrawn Beazer Notes tendered on or prior to the Expiration Date, and the Proposed Amendments will become operative on such date. Once operative, the Proposed Amendments will be binding on all holders of the applicable series of Beazer Notes, including holders who did not tender their Beazer Notes. Withdrawal rights terminated at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 4, 2026, and tendered Beazer Notes may no longer be withdrawn, except in limited circumstances where additional withdrawal rights are required by law. As previously announced, Eligible Holders who validly tendered (and did not validly withdraw) their Beazer Notes on or prior to the Early Participation Date will receive, for each $1,000 principal amount of Beazer Notes accepted for exchange, $1,000 principal amount of the corresponding series of Dream Finders Notes and $1.00 in cash, which includes an early participation premium of $50 principal amount of Dream Finders Notes (such $50 principal amount and $1.00 in cash per $1,000 of Beazer Notes exchanged, the “Early Participation Premium”). Eligible Holders who validly tender their Beazer Notes after the Early Participation Date but on or prior to 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 20, 2026, unless extended (such time and date, as the same may be extended, the “Expiration Date”), will receive only $950 principal amount of the corresponding series of Dream Finders Notes per $1,000 principal amount of Beazer Notes accepted for exchange. In each case, Eligible Holders will also receive accrued and unpaid interest on their accepted Beazer Notes, payable in cash, to, but not including, the settlement date. The Exchange Offer and Consent Solicitation are being made pursuant to the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the exchange and consent solicitation statement dated October 22, 2026 (as it may be amended or supplemented, the “Offering Memorandum”). Dream Finders, in its sole discretion, may terminate, withdraw, amend or extend the Exchange Offer and Consent Solicitation, subject to the terms and conditions set forth in the Offering Memorandum. About Dream Finders Homes Dream Finders Homes (NYSE: DFH), headquartered in Jacksonville, Florida, was recognized as the 2025 National Builder of the Year by Builder magazine. Dream Finders builds single-family homes throughout the Southeast, Mid-Atlantic, Midwest, Southwest and West, including Florida, Texas, Tennessee, North Carolina, South Carolina, Georgia, Colorado, Arizona, California, Nevada, Indiana, Delaware and the Washington, D.C. metropolitan area, which comprises Washington, D.C., Northern Virginia and Maryland. As the Official Home Builder of the PGA TOUR, the Jacksonville Jaguars and the Tampa Bay Rays, Dream Finders is deeply committed to excellence beyond homebuilding and into the communities it serves. Through its wholly owned subsidiaries, Dream Finders also provides mortgage financing as well as title agency and underwriting services to homebuyers. Dream Finders achieves its growth and returns by maintaining an asset-light homebuilding model.
