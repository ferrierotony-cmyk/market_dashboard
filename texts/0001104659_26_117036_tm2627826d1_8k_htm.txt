On October 19, 2026, Kimberly-Clark Corporation, a Delaware corporation (“Kimberly-Clark”), announced that it has priced a private offering (the “Offering”) of senior unsecured notes in a combined aggregate principal amount of $4.9 billion (collectively, the “Notes”). The Notes will be issued by Kimberly-Clark in five tranches: (i) $750,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.100% Notes due 2028, (ii) $1,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.300% Notes due 2029, (iii) $1,250,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.450% Notes due 2031, (iv) $750,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.650% Notes due 2034, and (v) $1,150,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.800% Notes due 2036. Kimberly-Clark expects that the Offering will be completed on or about October 29, 2026, subject to customary closing conditions. The Offering is being conducted in connection with the previously announced proposed acquisition (“Acquisition”) of Kenvue Inc. (“Kenvue”), which is expected to be consummated in the fourth quarter of 2026, subject to the satisfaction of certain customary closing conditions. The Notes are being offered only to persons who are either reasonably believed to be “qualified institutional buyers” under Rule 144A or who are non-“U.S. persons” under Regulation S as defined under applicable securities laws. Kimberly-Clark intends to use the net proceeds from the Offering, together with cash on hand and/or borrowings under its commercial paper program, to fund the cash consideration payable in connection with the Acquisition, and to pay related fees and expenses. Kimberly-Clark expects to use any remaining net proceeds from the Offering for general corporate purposes. The Offering is not conditioned on the consummation of the Acquisition. However, if (i) the Acquisition has not been consummated prior to the date that is five business days after the later of (x) May 31, 2027 or (y) any later date as the parties to the Acquisition agreement may agree as the “Outside Date” thereunder or (ii) Kimberly-Clark notifies the trustee for the Notes that Kimberly-Clark will not pursue the consummation of the Acquisition, then Kimberly-Clark will be required to redeem all outstanding Notes at a special mandatory redemption price equal to 101% of the aggregate principal amount of the Notes, plus accrued and unpaid interest, if any, to, but not including, the applicable special mandatory redemption date. In connection with the Acquisition, on September 28, 2026, Kimberly-Clark commenced an offer to exchange all of Kenvue’s existing senior notes in exchange for new senior notes to be issued by Kimberly-Clark (the “Exchange Offers”) and solicitation of consents with respect to certain amendments to the indentures governing Kenvue’s existing senior notes (the “Consent Solicitations”) pursuant to a separate confidential offering memorandum and consent solicitation statement. If (i) the Exchange Offers are withdrawn (but the Acquisition has been consummated and the Notes are issued and outstanding) or (ii) if the Acquisition is consummated, the Notes are issued and outstanding and the Exchange Offers are not completed within four (4) business days after the expiration date of the Exchange Offers (as may be extended pursuant to the terms described in the offering memorandum and consent solicitation statement), the Notes will be fully and unconditionally guaranteed (the “Kenvue Guarantees”), on an unsubordinated unsecured basis, by Kenvue (or its successor, as applicable). The consummation of the Offering is not contingent on the completion of the Exchange Offers and Consent Solicitations and the completion of the Exchange Offers or Consent Solicitations is not guaranteed to occur on the terms described in the offering memorandum and consent solicitation statement or at all. This Current Report on Form 8-K (including Exhibit 99.1) does not constitute an offer to exchange or a solicitation of consent with respect to the Exchange Offers and Consent Solicitations and shall not constitute an offer, solicitation or sale in any jurisdiction in which such offer, solicitation or sale would be unlawful. The Notes (including the Kenvue Guarantees, if any) have not been registered under the U.S. Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities Act”), or securities laws of any state or any other jurisdiction. Accordingly, the Notes will be subject to restrictions on transferability and resale and may not be transferred or resold except as permitted under the Securities Act and other applicable securities laws, pursuant to registration or exemption therefrom. The Notes are being offered and sold only (a) to persons reasonably believed to be “qualified institutional buyers” (as defined in Rule 144A under the Securities Act) in compliance with Rule 144A under the Securities Act and (b) outside the United States to non-U.S. persons in compliance with Regulation S under the Securities Act.
