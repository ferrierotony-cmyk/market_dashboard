ANNOUNCES RESULTS OF THE THE OFFER TO PURCHASE RELATING TO ITS WARRANTS REDWOOD CITY, Calif., November 9, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – Jasper Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: JSPR) (“Jasper” or the “Company”), a clinical stage biotechnology company focused on the design and development of innovative therapies to treat immune-mediated diseases, today announced the results of its offer to purchase (the “Offer”) all of its outstanding warrants to purchase shares of its common stock, par value $0.0001, which were issued and sold as part of an underwritten public offering on September 18, 2025 (the “Warrants”) at a purchase price of $0.324 in cash per Warrant, without interest, which expired at 12:00 a.m., Eastern Time on November 7, 2026. The Company has been advised that a total of 8,510,658 Warrants were properly tendered and not properly withdrawn, which amount represents approximately 68.94% of the outstanding Warrants. Pursuant to the terms of the Offer, the Company expects to pay an aggregate of $2,757,453.19 in cash in exchange for such Warrants. Such payment will be made promptly. The Offer was made pursuant to an Offer to Purchase and Letter of Transmittal, and certain other materials contained in the Company’s tender offer statement on Schedule TO originally filed with the SEC on October 7, 2026, as amended from time to time (the “Offer to Purchase”). All questions concerning the Offer should be directed to Jasper at (650) 549-1400. This announcement is for informational purposes only and shall not constitute an offer to purchase or a solicitation of an offer to sell the Warrants. The Offer and Consent Solicitation were made only through the Schedule TO and Offer to Purchase. About Jasper Jasper is a clinical stage biotechnology company focused on the design and development of innovative therapies to treat immune-mediated diseases. The company is advancing a pipeline of medicines including vensobafusp alfa, briquilimab, and KP701. Vensobafusp alfa is a potential best-in-class dual-complement inhibitor that has demonstrated positive outcomes in paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria (PNH) and is under evaluation in other high unmet need nephrology disorders. Briquilimab is an anti-KIT antibody which has demonstrated positive clinical results both as a conditioning agent for stem cell transplant in severe combined immunodeficiencies (SCID), Fanconi anemia, as well as mast cell mediated diseases such as the chronic urticarias. KP701, a novel, dual-acting anti-CD79BxCD32B monoclonal antibody (mAb) for autoantibody-mediated disorders currently advancing to the clinic.
