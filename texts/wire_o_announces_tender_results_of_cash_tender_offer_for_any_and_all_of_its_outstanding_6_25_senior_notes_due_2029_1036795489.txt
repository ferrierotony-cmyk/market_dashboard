(“Millicom” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: TIGO), a public limited liability company (société anonyme) organized and established under the laws of the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg, today announced the tender results in connection with its offer to purchase for cash any and all of its outstanding 6.25% Senior Notes due 2029 (the “Notes”) (the “Tender Offer”). The Tender Offer was made upon the terms and subject to the conditions (including the Financing Condition (as defined in the Offer to Purchase)) set forth in the offer to purchase dated November 17, 2026 (the “Offer to Purchase”). As previously announced, the expiration time for the Tender Offer was 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 23, 2026 (such date and time, the “Expiration Time”). As of the Expiration Time, U.S.$431,262,000 aggregate principal amount of the Notes, representing approximately 70% of the outstanding Notes, had been validly tendered and not validly withdrawn pursuant to the Tender Offer. Settlement of the Tender Offer is expected to occur on or about November 25, 2026 (the “Settlement Date”). Upon the terms and subject to the conditions of the Tender Offer set forth in the Offer to Purchase, all Notes validly tendered at or prior to the Expiration Time and not validly withdrawn at or prior to the Withdrawal Deadline (as defined in the Offer to Purchase), if accepted by Millicom for purchase, will be paid in full by Millicom on the Settlement Date, and Holders of such Notes will receive the Consideration plus accrued and unpaid interest from the last interest payment date for the Notes to, but not including, the Settlement Date. The aggregate principal amount of the Notes reflected in DTC’s records is U.S.$684,562,000 (the “Pre-Factor Amount”). After giving effect to the pool factor applied by DTC, the aggregate principal amount actually outstanding is U.S.$616,105,800 (the “Post-Factor Amount”). The Consideration will be multiplied by a scaling factor of 0.90, so Holders will receive U.S.$900 for each U.S.$1,000 Pre-Factor Amount of Notes validly tendered and accepted for purchase, which equals U.S.$1,000 for each U.S.$1,000 Post-Factor Amount. References to minimum denominations and integral multiples are to Pre-Factor Amounts. The obligation of Millicom to purchase Notes in the Tender Offer is conditioned on the satisfaction or waiver of certain conditions, including, without limitation, the Financing Condition, described in the Offer to Purchase. On November 18, 2026, Millicom priced an offering of $900,000,000 aggregate principal amount of its 7.500% Senior Notes due 2033 (the “New Notes”). Although Millicom expects the offering of the New Notes to close on November 24, 2026, no assurance can be given that the offering will be completed on the expected terms or at all, or that the Financing Condition will be satisfied.
