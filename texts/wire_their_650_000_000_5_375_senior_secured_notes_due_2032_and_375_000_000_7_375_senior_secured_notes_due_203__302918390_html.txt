Announce Expiration and Results of the Consent Solicitation for their €650,000,000 5.375% Senior Secured Notes due 2032 and £375,000,000 7.375% Senior Secured Notes due 2032 News provided by Boots Group Finco L.P. and The Boots Group Luxco S.à r.l. Oct 23, 2026, 14:28 ET Share this article Share to X Share this article Share to X LONDON , Oct. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Boots Group Finco L.P. and The Boots Group Luxco S.à r.l. (together, the " Co-Issuers ") today announced the expiration and results of the previously announced consent solicitation (the " Consent Solicitation ") from holders (the " Holders ") of their outstanding €650,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.375% Senior Secured Notes due 2032 (the " EUR Notes ") and £375,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 7.375% Senior Secured Notes due 2032 (the " GBP Notes " and, together with the EUR Notes, the " Notes "). The Consent Solicitation expired at 4:00 p.m., London time, on October 23, 2026 (the " Expiration Date "). The table below sets forth the aggregate principal amount of Notes outstanding and the aggregate principal amount of Notes for which consents were validly delivered on or prior to the Expiration Date and not validly revoked prior to the earlier of the Consent Time (as defined below) and the Expiration Date. In accordance with the terms of the Indenture, for purposes of determining whether the Requisite Consents were received, (i) the EUR Notes and the GBP Notes were treated as a single class, (ii) the principal amount of the EUR Notes and the GBP Notes was determined as the U.S. dollar equivalent thereof, converted at the spot rate at 11:00 a.m. (New York City time) on October 21, 2026, being the Business Day immediately preceding the date on which the Supplemental Indenture was executed, and (iii) Notes required to be disregarded under the Indenture were disregarded. On or prior to the Expiration Date, the Co-Issuers received valid Consents, which were not validly revoked prior to the earlier of the Consent Time and the Expiration Date, from Holders of at least a majority in aggregate principal amount of the outstanding Notes (with the EUR Notes and the GBP Notes treated as a single class) (the " Requisite Consents ") to approve the Proposed Acquisition Amendments (as defined below) to the indenture dated as of August 1, 2025 governing the Notes (as amended or supplemented from time to time, the " Indenture "), as described in the consent solicitation statement dated as of October 19, 2026 (the " Consent Solicitation Statement "). All capitalized terms not defined herein are defined in the Consent Solicitation Statement. On October 22, 2026, the Co-Issuers and U.S. Bank Trust Company, National Association, as trustee (the " Trustee "), executed a supplemental indenture (the " Supplemental Indenture ") to effect the Proposed Acquisition Amendments in accordance with the Consent Solicitation Statement (such time of execution, the " Consent Time "). The Supplemental Indenture became effective and binding upon the Co-Issuers, the Trustee and every Holder (including each Holder that did not deliver a consent) and their transferees immediately upon execution. Consents may no longer be revoked. The Proposed Acquisition Amendments will not become operative until immediately prior to the time at which the Acquisition (as defined below) is completed and will cease to be operative if the Acquisition is not consummated or the Co-Issuers do not pay, or cause to be paid, the Consent Fee (as defined below) for the benefit of the relevant Holders, in each case as described in the Consent Solicitation Statement. The Acquisition is currently expected to be consummated in the first half of 2027, but this date may be extended pursuant to the terms of the Share Purchase Agreement (as defined below). Subject to the terms and conditions of the Consent Solicitation Statement, the Co-Issuers will pay, or cause to be paid, for the benefit of each Holder of the Notes who validly delivered its consent to the Proposed Acquisition Amendments on or prior to the Expiration Date, and did not validly revoke such consent prior to the earlier of the Consent Time and the Expiration Date, a cash payment equal to €2.50 per €1,000 principal amount of EUR Notes and £2.50 per £1,000 principal amount of GBP Notes, in each case for which such consents were validly delivered and not validly revoked (the " Consent Fee "). The Consent Fee will be paid on the date on which the Effective Time (as defined below) occurs or promptly thereafter. No accrued interest will be paid as part of the Consent Fee, and the right to receive the Consent Fee is not transferable with the Notes. The payment of the Consent Fee is conditioned upon the satisfaction or waiver (subject to the prior consent of the Buyer (as defined below)) of the conditions set forth in the Consent Solicitation Statement, including the closing of the Acquisition (which condition may not be waived). As the Consent Time and the Expiration Date have both occurred, Notes in respect of which consents were delivered will be, or have been, unblocked in the relevant Clearing System in accordance with its procedures. A Holder that validly delivered a consent on or prior to the Expiration Date and did not validly revoke such consent prior to the earlier of the Consent Time and the Expiration Date will remain entitled to receive the Consent Fee, subject to the satisfaction of the Payment Conditions, notwithstanding any sale or other transfer of such Notes after they cease to be blocked. Such Holders should maintain the necessary arrangements with the relevant Clearing Participant and any custodian or other intermediary in order to receive the Consent Fee when paid. The Acquisition and the Proposed Acquisition Amendments On October 7, 2026, Oak UK Bidco Limited (the " Buyer "), which is directly or indirectly owned by funds managed, formed, controlled or advised by Wittington Investments, Limited (" Wittington "), together with funds managed, formed, controlled or advised by Fairfax Financial Holdings Limited (" Fairfax "), entered into a share purchase agreement (the " Share Purchase Agreement ") by and among, inter alios , The Boots Group Limited (the " Seller "), Blazing Star Boots Superco (Jersey) Limited (the " Company ") and the Buyer. Pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement, the Buyer will, subject to the terms and conditions thereof acquire all of the issued and outstanding ordinary shares of the Company (the " Acquisition " and the time at which the Acquisition is completed, the " Effective Time "). Subject to the conditions described in the Consent Solicitation Statement, the Co-Issuers sought consents from Holders to: (i) amend the defined terms "Sponsor" and "Investors" in the Indenture in order to refer to Wittington, Fairfax and their respective related persons to reflect the Acquisition, and (ii) add to, amend, supplement or change certain other defined terms and provisions contained in the Indenture and to make any other changes to the Indenture of a technical or conforming nature, in each case related to the foregoing (collectively, the " Proposed Acquisition Amendments ").
