Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement Agreement and Plan of Merger On October 27, 2026, Ultralife Corporation, a Delaware corporation (“Ultralife” or “Company”), entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (the “Merger Agreement”) with EnerSys Mergersub Inc., a Delaware corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent (“Merger Sub”), and EnerSys, a Delaware corporation (“EnerSys” or “Parent”). The Merger Agreement provides for the acquisition of Ultralife by Parent in a two-step transaction, consisting of a tender offer followed by a subsequent merger of Merger Sub with and into Ultralife (the “Merger”). Pursuant to the Merger Agreement, Merger Sub will, as promptly as practicable, and in any event within 10 business days after the date of the Merger Agreement, commence a tender offer (the “Offer”) to purchase all of the outstanding shares of Ultralife common stock, par value $0.10 per share (the “Shares”), for $16.00 per Share (the “Offer Price”), net to the seller in cash, without interest and subject to any required withholding of taxes. Following consummation of the Offer, the Merger will be effected under Section 251(h) of the Delaware General Corporation Law, as amended (the “DGCL”), without a meeting or vote of Ultralife’s stockholders, with Ultralife surviving the Merger as a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent. The Offer will initially be scheduled to expire at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on the date that is 25 business days after the commencement of the Offer, subject to extension in accordance with the terms of the Merger Agreement. If, on any then-scheduled expiration date, any Offer Condition (as defined below) has not been satisfied or waived, Merger Sub may extend the Offer in consecutive increments of up to 10 business days each, and Merger Sub may extend the Offer for any minimum period required by applicable law or the SEC (as defined below) or Nasdaq or their respective staffs, but in no event will Merger Sub be required to extend the Offer beyond the Outside Date (as defined below). The date on which the Offer expires, as extended in accordance with the Merger Agreement, is referred to as the “Expiration Date,” and the date and time Merger Sub accepts Shares validly tendered and not validly withdrawn pursuant to the Offer is referred to as the “Acceptance Time.” The obligation of Merger Sub to consummate the Offer will be subject to the satisfaction or waiver of customary conditions (collectively, the “Offer Conditions”), including (i) the valid tender and “receipt” (as defined in Section 251(h) of the DGCL), and non-withdrawal, of a number of Shares that, together with Shares then beneficially owned by Merger Sub and its affiliates, represents at least one Share more than 50% of the Shares issued and outstanding at the Acceptance Time (the “Minimum Condition”), (ii) the expiration or termination of the waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, as amended (the “HSR Act”), (iii) clearance under the United Kingdom National Security and Investment Act 2021 (the “NSIA”) and the receipt of any other required clearance, approval or consent, or the expiration or termination of any required waiting period, under the laws of any jurisdiction other than the United States, (iv) the absence of any law, order or other legal restraint prohibiting the making or consummation of the Offer or the Merger, (v) the accuracy of Ultralife’s representations and warranties, subject to specified materiality qualifications, (vi) Ultralife’s compliance with its covenants under the Merger Agreement, (vii) the absence of a continuing Material Adverse Effect (as defined in the Merger Agreement) on Ultralife since the date of the Merger Agreement, (viii) receipt of a certificate of an executive officer of Ultralife confirming the satisfaction of the conditions in clauses (v) through (vii) and (ix) the Merger Agreement not having been validly terminated in accordance with its terms. Consummation of the Offer will not be subject to a financing condition. At the effective time of the Merger (the “Effective Time”), each Share other than (i) Shares held by Ultralife (including Shares held in its treasury), (ii) Shares owned, directly or indirectly, by Parent or Merger Sub (or any other subsidiary of Parent), (iii) Shares irrevocably accepted for payment pursuant to the Offer and (iv) Shares held by stockholders who are entitled to and have properly demanded appraisal of such Shares under Section 262 of the DGCL and have not failed to perfect, withdrawn or lost those rights (the “Dissenting Shares”) will be converted into the right to receive the Offer Price in cash, without interest and subject to any required withholding of taxes (the “Merger Consideration”). Shares accepted for payment pursuant to the Offer will not receive any additional consideration in the Merger. 2 As of immediately prior to the Effective Time, each option to purchase Shares (each, a “Company Stock Option”) that is outstanding and has a per-Share exercise price less than the Offer Price (an “In the Money Option”) will automatically accelerate and become fully vested and will be cancelled in exchange for a cash payment, without interest and subject to applicable withholding, equal to (x) the Offer Price minus the applicable per-Share exercise price multiplied by (y) the number of Shares subject to such Company Stock Option. Each Company Stock Option that has a per-Share exercise price equal to or greater than the Offer Price (an “Out of the Money Option”), whether vested or unvested, will be cancelled for no consideration. Each outstanding share of Ultralife restricted stock (collectively, “Company Restricted Stock”), whether vested or unvested, will automatically accelerate and become fully vested and will be cancelled and converted into the right to receive cash equal to the Offer Price, without interest and subject to applicable withholding. Payments in respect of In the Money Options and Company Restricted Stock will be made reasonably promptly (and in no event later than 30 business days) after the Effective Time. Company Stock Options and Company Restricted Stock are collectively referred to as the “Company Equity Awards.” No Company Equity Award will be assumed or substituted with an award relating to Parent stock. The Merger Agreement contains representations and warranties and covenants customary for a transaction of this nature. The parties have agreed to use reasonable best efforts to consummate the transactions contemplated by the Merger Agreement and to obtain required clearances under the HSR Act, the NSIA and other applicable antitrust laws; however, Parent is not required to agree to any divestiture, hold-separate arrangement, behavioral remedy or other condition or restriction in order to obtain any such clearance. During the period from the date of the Merger Agreement until the earlier of the Effective Time and termination of the Merger Agreement, Ultralife has agreed, subject to specified exceptions, to conduct its business in the ordinary course and to use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve substantially intact its business organization and relationships, and not to take certain specified actions without Parent’s prior written consent. Ultralife has also agreed to use reasonable best efforts to obtain, at Parent’s election, either a waiver and consent from the other shareholders of its Indian joint venture or the sale of Ultralife’s interest in such joint venture, which is not a condition to the Offer. At the closing of the Merger, Parent will repay, on behalf of Ultralife, all amounts outstanding under Ultralife’s existing credit agreement. Ultralife is subject to customary restrictions on soliciting alternative acquisition proposals and engaging in discussions or negotiations with third parties regarding alternative acquisition proposals. Notwithstanding those restrictions, prior to the Acceptance Time, Ultralife may, under specified circumstances, provide information to and engage in discussions or negotiations with a third party regarding an unsolicited bona fide written Acquisition Proposal (as defined in the Merger Agreement) that the board of directors of Ultralife (the “Board”) determines in good faith, after consultation with its financial advisors and outside legal counsel, constitutes or would reasonably be expected to lead to a Superior Proposal (as defined in the Merger Agreement), if the Board determines, after consultation with its outside legal counsel, that failure to do so would reasonably be expected to be inconsistent with the Board’s fiduciary duties under applicable law. Subject to the terms of the Merger Agreement, prior to the Acceptance Time, the Board may change its recommendation or terminate the Merger Agreement to enter into a definitive agreement for a Superior Proposal, and may change its recommendation in response to an Intervening Event (as defined in the Merger Agreement), in each case provided that Ultralife gives Parent at least three business days’ prior written notice (or two business days in the case of certain subsequent changes or developments) and negotiates in good faith with Parent during that period regarding any revisions to the Merger Agreement or the Offer proposed by Parent. The Merger Agreement contains customary termination rights, including termination by mutual written consent before the Acceptance Time and by either Parent or Ultralife (i) if the Acceptance Time has not occurred on or before April 27, 2027 (as it may be extended, the “Outside Date”), subject to an extension of 60 days, and a further extension of 60 days, if all Offer Conditions other than those relating to the HSR Act, the NSIA or other antitrust laws have been satisfied or waived, (ii) if the Offer expires or is terminated without acceptance of Shares or (iii) if a final and non-appealable legal restraint permanently prohibits the Offer or the Merger. Parent may terminate the Merger Agreement before the Acceptance Time for an uncured breach by Ultralife of its representations, warranties, covenants or agreements that would result in the failure of an Offer Condition or upon the occurrence of a Triggering Event (as defined in the Merger Agreement), including an adverse change in the Board’s recommendation. Ultralife may terminate the Merger Agreement before the Acceptance Time (i) for an uncured breach by Parent or Merger Sub, (ii) to enter into a definitive agreement for a Superior Proposal, subject to payment of the Company Termination Fee, (iii) if the Offer Conditions have been satisfied or waived and Merger Sub fails to consummate the Offer within two business days following the Expiration Date or (iv) if Merger Sub fails to commence the Offer, terminates the Offer prior to the Expiration Date or makes a material change to the Offer, in each case in breach of the Merger Agreement. 3 Under specified circumstances, including certain transactions following an Acquisition Proposal, a Triggering Event or Ultralife’s termination to enter into a Superior Proposal, Ultralife will be required to pay Parent a termination fee of $10,300,000.00 (the “Company Termination Fee”). Tender and Support Agreement Concurrently with the execution and delivery of the Merger Agreement, certain stockholders, solely in their respective capacities as stockholders of Ultralife, entered into a tender and support agreement (the “Support Agreement”) with EnerSys and Merger Sub. As of October 27, 2026, such stockholders collectively beneficially owned approximately 40.3% of the outstanding Shares. Pursuant to the Support Agreement, each such stockholder agreed, among other things, (i) to tender all Shares beneficially owned by such stockholder pursuant to the Offer no later than 10 business days after the commencement of the Offer and not to withdraw such Shares, (ii) to vote against competing acquisition proposals and in favor of the Merger and the other transactions contemplated by the Merger Agreement and (iii) to certain other restrictions on its ability to take actions with respect to Ultralife and its Shares. The Shares subject to the Support Agreement do not, without the tender of additional Shares by other holders, satisfy the Minimum Condition. The Support Agreement will terminate upon the first to occur of (a) the valid termination of the Merger Agreement in accordance with its terms, (b) the Effective Time, (c) any amendment to the Merger Agreement, without such stockholder’s consent, that reduces the amount or changes the form of the Offer Price or Merger Consideration, imposes additional material conditions on its payment or extends the Outside Date beyond the date contemplated by the Merger Agreement (after giving effect to permitted extensions), (d) the expiration of the Offer without the acceptance of Shares for payment, and (e) the mutual written consent of the applicable stockholder and Parent.
