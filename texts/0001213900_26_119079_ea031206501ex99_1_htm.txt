The board of directors of each of Strathcona and Greenfire has determined that it would be in the best interests of its corporation to complete an arrangement involving the acquisition by Strathcona of all of the issued and outstanding Greenfire Shares, and the subsequent vertical short form amalgamation of Greenfire into Strathcona, in accordance with the terms set out herein and the Plan of Arrangement. B. Strathcona and Greenfire have entered into a lock-up agreement with the Significant Shareholder pursuant to which, among other things, the Significant Shareholder has agreed to support the transactions contemplated by this Agreement, subject to the conditions set forth in such agreement. C. Strathcona has entered into a lock-up agreement with all of the directors of Greenfire and the Greenfire Executives, pursuant to which, among other things, such directors and Greenfire Executives have agreed to vote all of the Greenfire Shares held by them in favour of the Greenfire Resolution, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in such agreement. D. Greenfire has entered into a lock-up agreement with all of the directors of Strathcona and the Strathcona Executives, pursuant to which, among other things, such directors and Strathcona Executives have agreed to vote all of the Strathcona Shares held by them in favour of the Strathcona Resolution, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in such agreement. E. The Parties have entered into this Agreement to provide for the matters referred to in the foregoing recitals and for other matters relating to such arrangements.
