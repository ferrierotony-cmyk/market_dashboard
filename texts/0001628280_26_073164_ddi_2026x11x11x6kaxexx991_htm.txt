(NASDAQ: DDI) (“DoubleDown” or the “Company”), a leading developer and publisher of digital games on mobile and web-based platforms, today announced unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. Third Quarter 2026 vs. Third Quarter 2025 Summary: • Revenue decreased 1.6% to $94.3 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to $95.8 million in the third quarter of 2025. ◦ Revenue from the Company’s social casino/free-to-play games was $76.8 million in the third quarter of 2026, a 3.5% decrease from the third quarter of 2025. The decrease was primarily due to lower revenue from the Company’s DoubleDown titles (excluding WHOW Games). ◦ Direct-to-Consumer (“DTC”) 1 revenue rose to $44.6 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $16.6 million in the third quarter of 2025. DTC revenue as a percentage of total social casino revenue increased to 58.0% in the third quarter of 2026 from 20.9% in the third quarter of 2025 and 52.4% in the second quarter of 2026. ◦ Revenue from SuprNation, the Company’s iGaming subsidiary, increased 7.4% year-over-year to $17.4 million, primarily as a result of the continued success of the Company’s newest brand, Los Vegas . • Operating expenses decreased to $56.8 million in the third quarter of 2026 from $60.9 million in the third quarter of 2025, primarily reflecting lower platform fees as a greater share of social casino revenue was generated through DTC channels, partially offset by higher gaming duties in the iGaming segment. • Profit for the interim period (excluding non-controlling interest) decreased 43.9% to $18.4 million, or earnings per fully diluted common share of $7.41 ($0.37 per American Depositary Share (“ADS”)), in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $32.7 million, or earnings per fully diluted common share of $13.21 ($0.66 per ADS), in the third quarter of 2025. ◦ The decline primarily reflects net foreign currency losses recognized in the third quarter of 2026, mainly unrealized and realized losses on U.S. dollar-denominated financial assets as well as higher income tax expense. These factors were partially offset by higher operating profit, which increased to $37.5 million from $35.0 million, driven by lower cost of revenue attributable to a higher proportion of DTC revenue. ◦ Each ADS represents 0.05 share of a common share. ◦ Adjusted EBITDA rose 7.2% to $40.2 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $37.5 million in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted EBITDA margin increased to 42.7% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 39.1% in the third quarter of 2025. • Beginning in the fourth quarter of 2025, social casino KPIs are inclusive of those from WHOW Games. 1 Direct-to-Consumer revenue represents revenue from purchases made through Company-owned channels, including web storefront transactions and other direct payment flows. ◦ Payer Conversion ratio for the Company’s social casino/free-to-play games increased to 9.5% in the third quarter of 2026 from 7.8% in the third quarter of 2025, primarily as a result of the inclusion of WHOW Games, which has a higher Payer Conversion ratio. ◦ Average Revenue Per Daily Active User (“ARPDAU”) for the Company’s social casino/free-to-play games increased to $1.44 in the third quarter of 2026 from $1.39 in the third quarter of 2025, primarily as a result of the inclusion of WHOW Games, which has a higher Payer Conversion ratio that more than offset its lower average revenue per payer. ◦ Average monthly revenue per payer for the social casino/free-to-play games decreased to $216 in the third quarter of 2026 from $272 in the third quarter of 2025, primarily as a result of the inclusion of WHOW Games, which has a lower average revenue per payer. • Net cash flows from operating activities decreased to $23.6 million in the third quarter of 2026 from $33.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. The decrease is primarily due to higher income taxes paid. “Following our strong results in the first half of 2026, DoubleDown’s third quarter results reflect another period of solid performance, and exceeded consensus expectations for revenue and Adjusted EBITDA. Operational excellence drove our operating profit to $37.5 million and a 7.2% year-over-year growth in adjusted EBITDA and expanded our third quarter adjusted EBITDA margin to 42.7%. Direct-to-Consumer expansion continues, with revenue of $44.6 million in the third quarter representing 58% of social casino revenue, up from 52% last quarter and 21% a year ago. “Our ability to consistently drive a high conversion of revenue to cash flow remains a top operating priority, and enabled DoubleDown to generate $23.6 million of net cash flow from operations in the third quarter and a total of $94.7 million in the first nine months of 2026. “Looking ahead, we remain focused on execution across our social casino and iGaming portfolio while continuing to evaluate acquisitions in casual gaming, online gambling and mobile entertainment. Use and Reconciliation of Non-IFRS Financial Measures In addition to our results determined in accordance with IFRS, we believe the following non-IFRS financial measure is useful in evaluating our operating performance. We present “ adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization ” (“Adjusted EBITDA”) because we believe it assists investors and analysts by facilitating comparison of period-to-period operational performance on a consistent basis by excluding items that we do not believe are indicative of our core operating performance. The items excluded from the Adjusted EBITDA may have a material impact on our financial results. Certain of those items are non-recurring, while others are non-cash in nature. Accordingly, the Adjusted EBITDA is presented as supplemental disclosure and should not be considered in isolation from, as a substitute for, or superior to, the financial information prepared in accordance with IFRS, and should be read in conjunction with the condensed consolidated interim financial statements furnished in our report on Form 6-K filed with the SEC. In our reconciliation from our reported IFRS “profit before income tax” to our Adjusted EBITDA, we eliminate the impact of the following four line items: (i) depreciation and amortization; (ii) finance income; (iii) finance cost; and (iv) other (income) expense.
