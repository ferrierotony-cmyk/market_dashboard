(the "Company") issued a press release announcing that it and its wholly-owned subsidiary, Pyxus Holdings, Inc. (the "Issuer"), have commenced a private offer to exchange (the "Exchange Offer") any and all of the Issuer’s outstanding 8.50% Senior Secured Notes due 2027 (the "Existing Notes") for the following consideration (the "Early Tender Exchange Consideration"): • $344.37 in value of shares of the Company’s common stock (no par value) (such shares, the "Offered Shares") at a price of $3.25 per share (such consideration, the "Early Tender Equity Consideration"); and • $675.90 in aggregate principal amount of new floating rate senior secured notes (such consideration, the "Early Tender Exchange Notes Consideration," and such notes, the "Exchange Notes") to be issued by the Issuer and guaranteed by specified parent and subsidiary guarantors, in each case, per $1,000 aggregate principal amount of Existing Notes validly tendered at or prior to 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 24, 2026 ("Early Tender Time") and accepted for exchange; or for the following consideration (the "Exchange Consideration"): • $327.15 in value of the Offered Shares at a price of $3.25 per share (such consideration, together with the Early Tender Equity Consideration, "Equity Consideration"); and • $642.11 in aggregate principal amount of Exchange Notes (such consideration, together with the Early Tender Exchange Notes Consideration, "Exchange Notes Consideration") to be issued by the Issuer and guaranteed by specified parent and subsidiary guarantors, in each case, per $1,000 aggregate principal amount of Existing Notes validly tendered after the Early Tender Time but at or prior to the Expiration Date (as defined below) and accepted for exchange. In conjunction with the Exchange Offer, the Company is soliciting consents (the "Consent Solicitation") from the holders of the Existing Notes to amend the indenture governing the Existing Notes (the "Existing Notes Indenture"), the Existing Notes and the related intercreditor and security documents as necessary to, among other things, (i) eliminate most of the restrictive covenants and certain of the affirmative covenants applicable to the Existing Notes, (ii) eliminate the change of control repurchase obligation in the Existing Notes Indenture and the Existing Notes, (iii) subordinate in right of payment the Existing Notes and guarantees thereof to the Issuer’s existing and future senior indebtedness (including the Exchange Notes and its ABL credit agreement), (iv) eliminate certain events of default and (v) release all of the collateral securing the Existing Notes. The Exchange Offer and the Consent Solicitation will expire at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on December 10, 2026, unless extended or earlier terminated by the Company (such date and time, as the same may be extended or earlier terminated, the "Expiration Date").
