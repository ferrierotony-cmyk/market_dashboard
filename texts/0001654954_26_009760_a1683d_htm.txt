Form 20-F X Form 40-F 13 November 2026 HSBC HOLDINGS PLC ANNOUNCES THE SATISFACTION OF THE NEW ISSUE CONDITION IN RESPECT OF ITS TENDER OFFER FOR ONE SERIES OF SECURITIES HSBC Holdings plc (the ‘ Company ’, ‘ we ’ or ‘ us ’) today announces the successful closing, on terms and conditions reasonably satisfactory to the Company, of the offering of $1,500,000,000 7.625% Perpetual Subordinated Contingent Convertible Securities (Callable During Any Optional Redemption Period) and as a result, the satisfaction of the New Issue Condition (as defined in the Offer to Purchase) pursuant to its offer to purchase (the ‘ Offer ’) for cash up to $1,500,000,000 aggregate principal amount (the ‘ Maximum Tender Amount ’) of the outstanding $3,000,000,000 6.000% Perpetual Subordinated Contingent Convertible Securities (Callable 22 May 2027 and Every Five Years Thereafter) (the ‘ Securities ’), previously announced on 5 November 2026.
