EX-99.1 2 tm2627373d1_ex99-1.htm EXHIBIT 99.1 Exhibit 99.1 Kimberly-Clark Announces Successful Consent Solicitations for Kenvue Notes and Amendment to Terms of Exchange Offers DALLAS, October 13, 2026 – Kimberly-Clark Corporation (NASDAQ: KMB) (“ Kimberly-Clark ”) announced today that the requisite number of consents have been received to adopt certain proposed amendments (the “ Amendments ”) with respect to all outstanding notes of Kenvue Inc. (NYSE: KVUE) (“ Kenvue ”). The results are based on (i) early tenders in the offers to exchange (each an “ Exchange Offer ” and, collectively, the “ Exchange Offers ”) any and all outstanding notes (the “ Kenvue Notes ”) issued by Kenvue for up to $7,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount of new notes to be issued by Kimberly-Clark (the “ Kimberly-Clark Notes ”) and cash and (ii) related consent solicitations (each, a “ Consent Solicitation ” and, collectively, the “ Consent Solicitations ”) being made by Kimberly-Clark on behalf of Kenvue to adopt the Amendments to the indenture (the “ Kenvue Indenture ”) governing the Kenvue Notes. Kenvue has executed a supplemental indenture to the Kenvue Indenture that contains the Amendments. The Amendments will become operative only upon settlement of the Exchange Offers. The settlement date for the Exchange Offers (the “ Settlement Date ”) is expected to occur promptly after the Expiration Date (as defined below), which is expected to be extended to occur on or about the closing date of Kimberly-Clark’s previously announced pending acquisition of Kenvue (the “ Acquisition ”). Kimberly-Clark does not anticipate the Settlement Date to occur before November 2, 2026. As of 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on October 9, 2026 (the “ Early Participation Date ”), the principal amounts of Kenvue Notes set forth in the table below were validly tendered and not validly withdrawn (and consents thereby validly given and not validly revoked). For each $1,000 principal amount of Kenvue Notes validly tendered and not validly withdrawn at or prior to the Early Participation Date and accepted by Kimberly-Clark, Eligible Holders (as defined below) will be eligible to receive $970 principal amount of Kimberly-Clark Notes of the applicable series (the “ Exchange Consideration ”), an early participation premium, payable in principal amount of Kimberly-Clark Notes of $30 (the “ Early Participation Premium ”) and $1.00 in cash (the “ Cash Payment ”), subject to rounding as described herein. The total consideration consists of (a) the Exchange Consideration plus (b) the Early Participation Premium plus (c) the Cash Payment. To be eligible to receive the Early Participation Premium and the Cash Payment, Eligible Holders must also beneficially own such Kenvue Notes at the Expiration Date. The Early Participation Premium and the Cash Payment will be paid on the Settlement Date to the noteholder of record on the Expiration Date. Kimberly-Clark also announced an amendment to the consideration available to Eligible Holders of Kenvue Notes who validly tender and do not validly withdraw their Kenvue Notes after the Early Participation Date (as defined below) and at or prior to the Expiration Date. For each $1,000 principal amount of Kenvue Notes validly tendered and not validly withdrawn after the Early Participation Date but at or prior to the Expiration Date and accepted by Kimberly-Clark, Eligible Holders will be now eligible to receive the Exchange Consideration and the Early Participation Premium (but not the Cash Payment), subject to rounding as described herein. Tendered Kenvue Notes may not be withdrawn, except in the limited circumstances where additional withdrawal rights are required by law. The aggregate principal amount of each series of Kimberly-Clark Notes issued to each participating holder for each series of Kenvue Notes validly tendered (and not validly withdrawn) and accepted by Kimberly-Clark will be rounded down, if necessary, to the nearest whole multiple of $1,000 in excess of $2,000.
