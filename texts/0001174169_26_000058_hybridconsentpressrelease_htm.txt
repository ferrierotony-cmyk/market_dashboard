Announces Successful Completion of Consent Solicitations for Three Series of Subordinated Notes OAKVILLE, Ontario, November 24, 2026 – Algonquin Power & Utilities Corp. (TSX/NYSE: AQN) (“AQN”) today announced the results of its previously announced (i) solicitations of consents (the “U.S. Solicitations”) from holders of its US$350,000,000 outstanding 6.20% Fixed-to-Floating Subordinated Notes – Series 2019‑A due July 1, 2079 (NYSE: AQNB) (CUSIP No.: 015857808/ISIN: US0158578080) (the “2019‑A Notes”) and its US$750,000,000 outstanding 4.750% Fixed-to-Fixed Reset Rate Junior Subordinated Notes Series 2022‑B due January 18, 2082 (CUSIP No.: 015857AH8/ISIN: US015857AH86) (the “2022‑B Notes” and, together with the 2019‑A Notes, the “U.S. Notes”) to certain proposed amendments to the indentures governing the U.S. Notes, and (ii) solicitation of written consents and proxies (the “Canadian Solicitation” and, together with the U.S. Solicitations, the “Consent Solicitations”) from holders of its C$400,000,000 outstanding 5.250% Fixed-to-Fixed Reset Rate Junior Subordinated Notes, Series 2022‑A, due January 18, 2082 (CUSIP No.: 015857AG0/ISIN: CA015857AG06) (the “2022‑A Notes” and, together with the U.S. Notes, the “Notes”) to approve an extraordinary resolution (the “Extraordinary Resolution”) authorizing certain proposed amendments to the indenture governing the 2022‑A Notes. The U.S. Solicitations expired at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 23, 2026 (the “Expiration Time”), and the deadline for delivery of written consents in the Canadian Solicitation was 5:00 p.m., Toronto time, on November 23, 2026 (the “Written Consent Deadline”). The required approvals were obtained for each series of Notes. The Proposed Amendments As previously announced, for each series of Notes, the proposed amendments (the “Proposed Amendments”) delete in its entirety the “Automatic Conversion” article of the applicable supplemental indenture, pursuant to which the Notes of such series would automatically convert into a newly issued series of preferred shares of AQN upon the occurrence of certain bankruptcy and insolvency-related events in respect of AQN, together with the related definitions, the related covenant restricting AQN from issuing preferred shares ranking in priority to the preferred shares issuable upon such conversion, and other related conforming changes. The Proposed Amendments do not change the interest rate, interest payment dates, maturity date, redemption provisions, interest deferral provisions or subordination provisions of any series of Notes. U.S. Solicitations The U.S. Solicitations were made solely on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in AQN’s consent solicitation statement dated November 12, 2026 (the “U.S. Statement”). As of the Expiration Time, AQN had received valid consents that were not validly revoked from holders of not less than a majority of the outstanding principal amount of each series of U.S. Notes, voting separately (the “Requisite Consents”). Accordingly, the Requisite Consents required to adopt the Proposed Amendments have been received with respect to each of the 2019‑A Notes and the 2022‑B Notes. The U.S. Solicitations have expired, and consents delivered in the U.S. Solicitations may no longer be revoked. AQN has delivered to the trustees under the indentures governing the U.S. Notes the documents required under such indentures and has instructed the trustees to execute a supplemental indenture with respect to each series of U.S. Notes giving effect to the Proposed Amendments (each, a “U.S. Supplemental Indenture”). AQN expects each U.S. Supplemental Indenture to be executed promptly. The Proposed Amendments with respect to each series of U.S. Notes will become effective upon execution of the applicable U.S. Supplemental Indenture but will not become operative until the Consent Payment (as defined below) for such series has been paid. Once the Proposed Amendments become operative with respect to a series of U.S. Notes, they will bind all holders of such series, whether or not they delivered a consent. Subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the remaining conditions described in the U.S. Statement, AQN expects to pay, on or about November 25, 2026, to each holder of U.S. Notes as of 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 10, 2026 who validly delivered (and did not validly revoke) a consent prior to the Expiration Time a consent payment of US$0.025 per US$25.00 principal amount of 2019‑A Notes and US$1.00 per US$1,000 principal amount of 2022‑B Notes (the “Consent Payment”). No Consent Payment will be made with respect to a series of U.S. Notes if the applicable U.S. Supplemental Indenture is not executed or does not become effective, or if any of the other conditions described in the U.S. Statement are not satisfied or waived. Holders of U.S. Notes who did not validly deliver a consent prior to the Expiration Time will not receive the Consent Payment. RBC Capital Markets, LLC acted as Solicitation Agent and D.F. King & Co., Inc. acted as Information Agent and Tabulation Agent for the U.S. Solicitations. Questions regarding the U.S. Solicitations or the Consent Payment may be directed to D.F. King & Co., Inc. at the address and telephone number set forth on the back cover of the U.S. Statement. Canadian Solicitation The Canadian Solicitation was made solely on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in AQN’s notice of meeting and consent and proxy solicitation statement dated November 12, 2026 (the “Canadian Statement”). As of the Written Consent Deadline, holders of not less than 66⅔% of the aggregate principal amount of the outstanding 2022‑A Notes had delivered valid consents to the Extraordinary Resolution (and had not validly revoked them). Accordingly, the Extraordinary Resolution has been passed by written consent in accordance with the indenture governing the 2022‑A Notes, and the special meeting of holders of 2022‑A Notes that was scheduled to be held at 12:00 p.m., Toronto time, on December 11, 2026 (the “Meeting”) has been cancelled. No further action is required from holders of 2022‑A Notes in respect of the Meeting. As a result of approval of the Extraordinary Resolution, AQN has requested that TSX Trust Company, as trustee under the indenture governing the 2022‑A Notes, enter into promptly, a supplemental indenture with AQN giving effect to the Proposed Amendments with respect to the 2022‑A Notes (the “Canadian Supplemental Indenture”). Upon entry into the Canadian Supplemental Indenture, the Proposed Amendments will be binding on all holders of 2022‑A Notes, whether or not they responded to the Canadian Solicitation. Following entry into the Canadian Supplemental Indenture, AQN expects to pay, on or about November 25, 2026, a fee of C$1.00 per C$1,000 principal amount of 2022‑A Notes (the “Fee”) to each holder of 2022‑A Notes as of the close of business on November 10, 2026 who responded to the Canadian Solicitation by the Written Consent Deadline, whether by consenting to or withholding consent from, or by voting for or against, the Extraordinary Resolution, and who did not validly revoke such instructions. Holders of 2022-A Notes who did not respond by the Written Consent Deadline, or who validly revoked such response, will not receive the Fee. No Fee will be paid if the Canadian Supplemental Indenture is not entered into. RBC Dominion Securities Inc. acted as Solicitation Agent and TSX Trust Company acted as Tabulation Agent for the Canadian Solicitation. Questions regarding the Canadian Solicitation or the Fee may be directed to the Solicitation Agent at the address and telephone number set forth on the back cover of the Canadian Statement. AQN is committed to providing safe, secure, reliable, cost-effective, and sustainable energy and water solutions through its portfolio of generation, transmission, and distribution utility investments to over one million customer connections, largely in the United States and Canada. AQN’s common shares, preferred shares, Series A, and preferred shares, Series D are listed on the Toronto Stock Exchange under the symbols AQN, AQN.PR.A, and AQN.PR.D, respectively.
