The Offer to Purchase and any related documents are referred to herein collectively as the " Tender Offer Documents ." Capitalized terms used but not defined in this press release have the meanings given to them in the Offer to Purchase. The Notes offered to be purchased for cash in the offers (each, an " Offer ," and, collectively, the " Offers ") from each registered holder (each, a " Holder ," and, collectively, the " Holders ") are subject to a cap on the aggregate Total Consideration (as defined below) that we will be obligated to pay for Notes purchased in the Offers, consisting of: (i) a $1,500,000,000 cap on the aggregate Total Consideration (the " Pool 1 Maximum Amount ") payable for the 2.450% Senior Notes due 2050, 2.375% Senior Notes due 2040, 4.200% Senior Notes due 2059, 3.950% Senior Notes due 2049, 4.125% Senior Notes due 2054, 3.950% Senior Notes due 2044, 1.625% Senior Notes due 2032, 3.875% Senior Notes due 2039, 3.600% Senior Notes due 2034, 5.700% Senior Notes due 2055, 5.800% Senior Notes due 2065, 3.200% Senior Notes due 2030 and 5.650% Senior Notes due 2045, each issued by Mars, and the 4.500% Senior Debentures due 2046, 2.100% Senior Notes due 2030 and 5.750% Senior Notes due 2054, each issued by Kellanova, a Delaware corporation (" Kellanova ") (collectively, the " Pool 1 Notes "), and subject to the Acceptance Priority Levels set forth in the table below (" Pool 1 "); and (ii) a $500,000,000 cap on the aggregate Total Consideration (the " Pool 2 Maximum Amount " and, together with the Pool 1 Maximum Amount, the " Maximum Amounts ," and each, a " Maximum Amount ") payable for the 4.550% Senior Notes due 2028, 4.600% Senior Notes due 2028 and 4.800% Senior Notes due 2030, each issued by Mars, and the 3.400% Senior Notes due 2027 and 4.300% Senior Notes due 2028, each issued by Kellanova (collectively, the " Pool 2 Notes " and, together with the Pool 1 Notes, the " Notes ," and each series of Notes, a " series " of Notes), and subject to the Acceptance Priority Levels set forth in the table below (" Pool 2 ," and, together with Pool 1, the " Pools ," and each, a " Pool "). The Offers are subject to the terms and conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase, including proration, the application of the Acceptance Priority Levels set forth in the table below and a $250,000,000 cap on the aggregate Total Consideration payable for the 3.400% Senior Notes due 2027 issued by Kellanova (the " 2027 Notes ") pursuant to the Offer for such 2027 Notes (the " 2027 Notes Cap "). Subject to applicable law, we may, but are under no obligation to, increase, decrease or eliminate any Maximum Amount and/or the 2027 Notes Cap at any time, including on or after the Price Determination Date (as defined below). Additionally, we may increase the amount of Notes accepted for payment in any Offer by no more than 2% of the outstanding Notes of the applicable series, as further described in the Acceptance Priority Procedures set forth in the Offer to Purchase, without amending or extending the applicable Offer. We may also increase or decrease the percentage of the Notes accepted for payment in any Offer (including by more than 2% of the outstanding Notes of the applicable series) by a press release or other public announcement that is widely disseminated no later than 9:00 a.m., New York City time, on the third business day before the scheduled Expiration Date (as defined below). If proration is required with respect to a series of Notes, we will multiply the principal amount of each valid tender of such series of Notes by the applicable proration rate and round the resulting amount down to the nearest integral multiple of the minimum denomination in order to determine the principal amount of such tender that will be accepted pursuant to the applicable Offer. The " Total Consideration " for each series of Notes payable per $1,000 principal amount of such series of Notes validly tendered for purchase will be based on the applicable Fixed Spread for such series of Notes plus the Reference Yield based on the bid-side price of the applicable Reference Security as quoted on the applicable Bloomberg Reference Page as of 2:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 20, 2026, unless extended by the Company with respect to the applicable Offer (such date and time with respect to an Offer, as the same may be extended by the Company with respect to such Offer, the " Price Determination Date "). Unless extended with respect to any Offer, promptly after the Price Determination Date, the Company will announce in a press release, among other things, the Total Consideration applicable to each series of Notes. Holders must validly tender (and not validly withdraw) their Notes at or prior to the Expiration Date to receive the Total Consideration. The formula for determining the Total Consideration is set forth on Annex A to the Offer to Purchase. In addition to the Total Consideration, all Holders of Notes accepted for purchase pursuant to the Offers will, on the Settlement Date (as defined below), also receive accrued and unpaid interest on those Notes from the last interest payment date with respect to those Notes to, but excluding, the Settlement Date. The Offers are scheduled to expire at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 20, 2026 (such time and date, as it may be extended, the " Expiration Date "), unless extended or earlier terminated by the Company with respect to any Offer. Tenders of Notes of a series may be validly withdrawn at any time at or prior to 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 20, 2026, unless extended by the Company with respect to any Offer, by following the procedures described in the Offer to Purchase. The " Settlement Date " will be the second business day after the Expiration Date and is expected to be November 24, 2026. The Company's obligation to accept for purchase and to pay for Notes of each series validly tendered and not validly withdrawn pursuant to the Offers is subject to the satisfaction or waiver, in the Company's discretion, of certain conditions, which are more fully described in the Offer to Purchase. If any condition is not satisfied, the Company is not obligated to accept for payment, purchase or pay for, and may delay the acceptance for payment of, any tendered Notes, in each case subject to applicable law, and may terminate or alter any or all of the Offers. Subject to applicable law, the Company reserves the right to (i) waive any and all conditions to any or all of the Offers as permitted by law, (ii) extend or terminate any or all of the Offers, including the Expiration Date, or (iii) otherwise amend any of the Offers, and may eliminate, increase or decrease any Maximum Amount and/or the 2027 Notes Cap at any time, including on or after the Price Determination Date. The Offers are not contingent upon the tender of any aggregate minimum principal amount of Notes of any series (subject to minimum denomination requirements as set forth in the Offer to Purchase), and none of the Offers is conditioned on the consummation of any of the other Offers. The complete terms and conditions of the Offers are set forth in the Tender Offer Documents. Holders of Notes are urged to read the Tender Offer Documents carefully. About Mars, Incorporated Mars, Incorporated is driven by the belief that the world we want tomorrow starts with how we do business today. Based on combined Mars and Kellanova fiscal year 2025 net sales, we are now a $65bn+ family-owned business, with a diverse portfolio of quality snacking and food products that delight millions of people every day and leading pet care products and veterinary services that support pets all around the world. We produce some of the world's best-loved brands including ROYAL CANIN®, PEDIGREE®, WHISKAS®, CESAR®, M&M'S®, SNICKERS®, EXTRA®, Pringles®, Cheez-It® and BEN'S ORIGINAL™. Our international networks of pet hospitals, including BANFIELD™, BLUEPEARL™, VCA™ and ANICURA™, span preventive, general, specialty and emergency veterinary care, and our global veterinary diagnostics business ANTECH® offers breakthrough capabilities in pet diagnostics. The Mars Five Principles — Quality, Responsibility, Mutuality, Efficiency and Freedom — inspire our approximately 170,000 associates to act every day to help create a better world for people, pets and the planet.
