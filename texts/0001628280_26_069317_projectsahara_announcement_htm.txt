(NASDAQ: OGI) (TSX: OGI) (“ Organigram ”) and Aurora Cannabis Inc. (NASDAQ: ACB) (TSX: ACB) (“ Aurora ”) today announced that they have entered into an arrangement agreement (the “ Arrangement Agreement ”) pursuant to which Organigram will acquire all of the issued and outstanding common shares (the “ Aurora Shares ”) of Aurora at a price of C$7.80 per Aurora Common Share (the “ Transaction ”). The Transaction values Aurora at approximately C$561 million on a fully diluted equity value basis. The consideration represents a 93% premium to the unaffected closing share price of Aurora Shares on the TSX as of August 10, 2026, the last full trading day of Aurora prior to the announcement by Curaleaf Holdings, Inc. (“ Curaleaf ”) of its intention to launch a hostile takeover bid for Aurora (the “ Unaffected Price ”), and a 21% premium relative to the current bid value of the Curaleaf hostile offer 3 (the “ Hostile Bid ”) as most recently amended on October 6, 2026, which was unanimously rejected by Aurora’s Board of Directors. The Transaction provides Aurora shareholders with compelling immediate value, meaningful choice in how they receive their consideration, and the opportunity for continued participation in the future growth and value creation of the combined company. Together, Organigram and Aurora will create a global cannabis champion with a leadership position in Canada, Europe and Australia and a growing presence across other key regions. The two Canadian-headquartered cannabis companies will boast a global platform with greater scale, broader capabilities, financial strength and international reach. Organigram’s recreational cannabis leadership, production network, R&D platform and European medical commercial operations complement Aurora’s global medical cannabis leadership, established international presence, and EU-GMP certified manufacturing capabilities. Transaction Details Under the terms of the Arrangement Agreement, Aurora shareholders will have the option to elect to receive: (i) C$7.80 per Aurora Share in cash (the “ Cash Consideration ”); (ii) 5.8626 common shares in the capital of Organigram (the “ Organigram Shares ”) per Aurora Share (the “ Share Consideration ”); or (iii) a combined consideration of C$3.75 in cash plus 3.0431 Organigram Shares (the “ Combined Consideration ”), in each case per Aurora Common Share (collectively, the “ Consideration ”). (iv) Aurora shareholders electing to receive the Cash Consideration or the Share Consideration will be subject to proration as set out in the Arrangement Agreement so that the aggregate consideration will include no more than 205,826,172 Organigram Shares and no more than C$253,638,772 in cash. Based on the 20-day volume-weighted average trading price of Organigram Shares on the TSX of approximately C$1.33 on November 2, 2026, the Consideration represents C$7.80 per Aurora Common Share. The Transaction will be carried out by way of a court-approved plan of arrangement under the Business Corporations Act (British Columbia) and is subject to the following approvals: (i) At least two-thirds of the votes cast by Aurora shareholders present in person or represented by proxy at a special meeting of Aurora shareholders (the “Aurora Meeting”); and (ii) A simple majority of the votes cast by Aurora shareholders present in person or represented by proxy at the Aurora Meeting, excluding votes from certain shareholders, as required under Multilateral Instrument 61-101 - Protection of Minority Security Holders in Special Transactions (“ MI 61-101 ”). The issuance of Organigram Shares pursuant to the Transaction and Private Placement (defined below) is subject to the approval by a simple majority of votes cast by Organigram shareholders at a special meeting of Organigram shareholders, excluding votes held by BAT, as required under MI 61-101 and the rules of the TSX. Details of the Transaction and the required shareholder votes will be included in a joint information circular that Organigram and Aurora expect to mail to their respective shareholders in due course. The special meetings for Organigram shareholders and Aurora shareholders are expected to be held in December 2026. BAT, which is entitled to cast 30% of the votes attaching to Organigram Shares, has entered into an irrevocable voting agreement agreeing to vote its Organigram Shares in favour of the Transaction and against any competing acquisition proposals. The voting agreement with BAT will not be terminable until six months following termination of the Arrangement Agreement in certain circumstances. In addition, each of the other directors and officers of each of Aurora and Organigram have entered into customary voting agreements agreeing to vote their Aurora Shares or Organigram Shares in favour of the Transaction. The voting and support agreements with directors and officers will terminate automatically upon termination of the Arrangement Agreement. In addition to the shareholder approvals, the Transaction will be subject to regulatory approvals, as well as the approval of the Supreme Court of British Columbia. Subject to the satisfaction of such conditions, the Transaction is expected to be completed in January 2027. 3 The Transaction would be structured to provide a tax-deferred rollover to eligible Canadian-resident Aurora shareholders who receive Organigram Shares, subject to applicable law and advice from their own tax advisors. BAT Concurrent Private Placement / Financing In connection with financing the Transaction and concurrently with the closing of the Transaction, BAT will acquire, on a private placement basis (the “ Private Placement ”), 141.0 million Organigram Shares at a price of C$1.33 per share for gross proceeds of £0.70926 (or C$1.33 based on the GBP/CAD exchange rate on November 2, 2026) million for total gross proceeds of £100,000,000 (or C$187.5 million based on the GBP/CAD exchange rate on November 2, 2026) (the “Private Placement Proceeds”) pursuant to the terms of a subscription agreement dated November 2, 2026 (the “ Subscription Agreement ”). To the extent BAT’s ownership of Organigram Shares in Organigram exceeds 30.0% of the total issued and outstanding Organigram Shares on a post-issuance basis (the “30% Threshold”), BAT would, in lieu of Organigram Shares, be issued non-voting Class A convertible preferred shares of Organigram (the “ Preferred Shares ”). The Private Placement Proceeds will be used to finance the cash portion of the Consideration and certain related transaction expenses of Organigram. The Preferred Shares are eligible for conversion into Organigram Shares in accordance with their terms, provided that such conversion would not result in ownership by BAT or its affiliates, associates, related parties and any joint actors exceeding 30.0% of the total issued and outstanding Organigram Shares on a post-conversion basis. ATB Financial Senior Secured Credit Facilities ATB Financial ("ATB") has provided a fully committed and underwritten commitment for senior secured credit facilities of up to $210 million (the "Loan Facilities"). The Loan Facilities will be established by amending and restating Organigram's existing $70 million senior secured credit agreement with ATB. ATB alone is providing the full commitment, as lead arranger and sole bookrunner. ATB's commitment is not conditional on syndication. ATB may syndicate a portion of the Loan Facilities to other lenders, who may become party to the credit agreement from time to time. ATB will also act as administrative agent for the Loan Facilities. The Loan Facilities include: • A $10 million senior secured revolving operating credit facility, provided by ATB; • A $65 million senior secured revolving credit facility (the "Revolving Facility"), provided initially by ATB and, after any syndication, by such other lenders as may become party to the credit agreement from time to time. Up to $35 million of the Revolving Facility may, subject to certain conditions, be used to partially finance the acquisition of Aurora; and • A $135 million senior secured non-revolving term credit facility (the "Term Facility"), provided initially by ATB and, after any syndication, by such other lenders as may become party to the credit agreement from time to time. The Term Facility has two tranches: (i) "Term Tranche 1", in the amount of $20 million, which was drawn in full to finance the acquisition of Sanity Group and remains outstanding; and (ii) "Term Tranche 2", in the amount of $115 million, which will be available on the Closing Date to partially finance the acquisition of Aurora. The amended and restated Loan Facilities will be available on the Closing Date. Availability is subject only to a limited set of conditions precedent, customary for acquisition financings on a "certain funds" basis. These include completion of the acquisition substantially at the same time as funding, receipt of the final order approving the arrangement, receipt of the committed equity proceeds, and the accuracy of certain specified representations. The Loan Facilities will mature three years after closing and bear interest at 4 ATB's applicable benchmark rates plus an applicable margin based on the ratio of net funded debt to EBITDA. The Loan Facilities will be subject to customary financial covenants, including a maximum net funded debt to EBITDA ratio and a fixed charge coverage ratio. Superior Alternative for Aurora Shareholders to the Hostile Bid The Transaction emerged from a robust strategic review process conducted by Aurora, under the oversight of a committee of independent directors (the “Aurora Special Committee”), advised by independent legal and financial advisors. The review process involved an extensive market check, including outreach to multiple parties, in connection with the unanimous rejection of the Hostile Bid, as detailed in the Notice of Change to Aurora’s Directors’ Circular dated October 14, 2026. The Aurora Special Committee and the Board of Directors of Aurora (the “Aurora Board”) believe that the Transaction is superior to the Hostile Bid and the best actionable path forward to maximize value for Aurora shareholders. The Aurora Special Committee and the Aurora Board continue to reiterate that the Hostile Bid is not in the best interests of Aurora or its shareholders and unanimously recommend that shareholders REJECT the Hostile Bid by taking no action and DO NOT TENDER their Aurora Shares to the Hostile Bid. Aurora intends to file a second Notice of Change to its Directors’ Circular confirming the Aurora Board’s continued recommendation to reject the Hostile Bid and vote in favour of the Transaction (the “Second Notice of Change”), which will be made available on Aurora’s SEDAR+ profile at www.sedarplus.ca and on EDGAR at www.sec.gov. About Organigram Organigram Global Inc. is listed on the NASDAQ Global Select Market and TSX. The Company's wholly owned subsidiary, Organigram Inc., is a licensed cultivator and processor. Through its acquisition of Sanity Group GmbH ("Sanity Group"), Organigram participates in the German medical cannabis market and other emerging markets within Europe. Organigram is focused on producing high-quality cannabis for adult consumers, as well as extending the Company's global footprint. Organigram has also developed and acquired a portfolio of cannabis brands, including Edison, Big Bag O' Buds, SHRED, Monjour, Tremblant, Collective Project, Trailblazer, BOXHOT and DEBUNK. Through its acquisition of Sanity Group, Organigram's European brands include Vayamed, avaay, ZOIKS, Endosane Pharmaceuticals, VAAY, and Grashaus Projects. Organigram operates facilities in Moncton, New Brunswick and Lac Supérieur, Quebec, with a dedicated edibles manufacturing facility in Winnipeg, Manitoba. The Company also operates two additional cannabis processing facilities in Southwestern Ontario; one in Aylmer and the other in London. The facility in Aylmer houses best-in-class extraction capabilities, and is optimized for formulation refinement, post-processing of minor cannabinoids, and infused pre-roll production. The facility in London will be optimized for labelling, packaging, and national fulfillment. The Company is regulated by Health Canada under the Cannabis Act and the Cannabis Regulations.
