EX-99.1 6 pr-exchangeofferclose.htm EX-99.1 Document Exhibit 99.1 Dream Finders Announces Expiration and Final Results of Private Exchange Offer and Consent Solicitation JACKSONVILLE, Fla., — November 23, 2026 — Dream Finders Homes, Inc. (NYSE: DFH) (“Dream Finders”) today announced the expiration and final results of its previously announced private exchange offers (the “Exchange Offer”). Following Dream Finders’ recently completed acquisition of Beazer Homes USA, Inc. (“Beazer”), Dream Finders made the offers to Eligible Holders (as defined herein) to exchange any and all outstanding notes issued by Beazer and set forth in the table below (the “Beazer Notes”) for new notes to be issued by Dream Finders (the “Dream Finders Notes”), and solicited consents to adopt certain proposed amendments to the indentures governing the Beazer Notes (the “Proposed Amendments”, and such solicitation, the “Consent Solicitation”). The Exchange Offer and Consent Solicitation expired at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 20, 2026 (the “Expiration Date”). As of the Expiration Date, the following principal amounts of each series of Beazer Notes had been validly tendered and not validly withdrawn (and consents thereby validly delivered and not validly revoked): Series of Beazer Notes Principal Amount Outstanding Beazer Notes Tendered after the Early Participation Date Total Amount of Beazer Notes Tendered as of the Expiration Date (1) Principal Amount Principal Amount Approximate Percentage 7.500% Senior Notes due 2031 (CUSIP Nos. 07556QBU8 / U0758TAT4) $250,000,000 $— $248,062,000 99.22% 8.000% Senior Notes due 2032 (CUSIP Nos. 07556QBV6 / U0758TAU1) $400,000,000 $1,235,000 $394,360,000 98.59% (1) Dream Finders accepted for exchange all Beazer Notes validly tendered and not validly withdrawn on or before the Expiration Date. The Exchange Offer and Consent Solicitation were made pursuant to the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the exchange and consent solicitation statement dated October 22, 2026 (as amended or supplemented, the “Offering Memorandum”). For each $1,000 principal amount of Beazer Notes validly tendered (and not validly withdrawn) on or prior to 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 4, 2026 (the “Early Participation Date”) and accepted for exchange, Eligible Holders will receive $1,000 principal amount of the corresponding series of Dream Finders Notes and $1.00 in cash, which includes an early participation premium of $50 principal amount of the corresponding series of Dream Finders Notes and $1.00 in cash per $1,000 principal amount of Beazer Notes exchanged (the “Early Participation Premium”). Eligible Holders who validly tendered their Beazer Notes after the Early Participation Date but on or prior to the Expiration Date will receive only $950 principal amount of the corresponding series of Dream Finders Notes per $1,000 principal amount of Beazer Notes accepted for exchange and will not receive the Early Participation Premium. As a result, Dream Finders expects to issue $248,062,000 aggregate principal amount of its 7.500% Senior Notes due 2031 and $394,298,000 aggregate principal amount of its 8.000% Senior Notes due 2032 (collectively, the “Dream Finders Notes”) in connection with the consummation of the Exchange Offer and Consent Solicitation. Eligible Holders will also receive accrued and unpaid interest on their accepted Beazer Notes, payable in cash, to, but not including, the Settlement Date (as defined herein). As previously announced on November 5, 2026, Dream Finders received consents from holders of at least a majority in aggregate principal amount of each series of Beazer Notes (the “Requisite Consents”) to adopt the Proposed Amendments with respect to each series of Beazer Notes, which will eliminate substantially all of the restrictive covenants and events of default under the indentures governing the Beazer Notes. As of the Expiration Date, the conditions to the consummation of the Exchange Offer, as described in the Offering Memorandum, had been satisfied or waived. Settlement of the Exchange Offer is expected to occur today, November 23, 2026 (the “Settlement Date”), at which time Dream Finders will issue Dream Finders Notes in exchange for all Beazer Notes validly tendered (and not validly withdrawn) and accepted for exchange, and the Proposed Amendments will become operative and binding on all holders of the applicable series of Beazer Notes, including holders who did not tender their Beazer Notes. About Dream Finders Homes Dream Finders Homes (NYSE: DFH), headquartered in Jacksonville, Florida, was recognized as the 2025 National Builder of the Year by Builder magazine. Dream Finders builds single-family homes throughout the Southeast, Mid-Atlantic, Midwest, Southwest and West, including Florida, Texas, Tennessee, North Carolina, South Carolina, Georgia, Colorado, Arizona, California, Nevada, Indiana, Delaware and the Washington, D.C. metropolitan area, which comprises Washington, D.C., Northern Virginia and Maryland. As the Official Home Builder of the PGA TOUR, the Jacksonville Jaguars and the Tampa Bay Rays, Dream Finders is deeply committed to excellence beyond homebuilding and into the communities it serves. Through its wholly owned subsidiaries, Dream Finders also provides mortgage financing as well as title agency and underwriting services to homebuyers. Dream Finders achieves its growth and returns by maintaining an asset-light homebuilding model.
