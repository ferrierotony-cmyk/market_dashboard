Settlement for Notes Tendered On or Prior to the Early Tender Date Subject to the satisfaction and/or waiver of the Conditions (as defined in the Offer to Purchase), the Offeror has accepted for purchase all Notes validly tendered (and not validly withdrawn) in the Offers on or prior to the Early Tender Date. Holders of the Notes that have validly tendered and not validly withdrawn their Notes on or prior to the Early Tender Date shall receive the Total Consideration and Accrued Interest on the Early Settlement Date, which is expected to occur on October 7, 2025. Extension of the Total Consideration for all Notes Tendered On or Prior to the Expiration Date The Offeror has also amended the Offers by offering the Total Consideration, including the Early Tender Payment, in respect of all Notes that are validly tendered by the Expiration Date, regardless of whether such Notes were tendered before or after the Early Tender Date.
