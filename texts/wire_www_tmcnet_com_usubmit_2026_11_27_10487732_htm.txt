its 5.541% Series 1 Senior Unsecured Notes [November 27, 2026] CAE announces successful completion of consent solicitation targeting its 5.541% Series 1 Senior Unsecured Notes Tweet MONTREAL , Nov. 27, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- (Nasdaq: CAE) (TSX: CAE) – CAE Inc. (" CAE " or the " Company ") announced today that, in connection with its previously announced consent and proxy solicitation with respect to its 5.541% Series 1 Senior Unsecured Notes due June 12, 2028 (the " Notes "), it has received the requisite written consents to amend the indenture governing the Notes. As previously announced, CAE solicited written consents and proxies (the " Consent and Proxy Solicitation ") from the holders (the " Noteholders ") of CAE's outstanding Notes issued under the master trust indenture, dated as of June 12, 2023 (the " Master Indenture "), as supplemented by a first supplemental indenture dated as of June 12, 2023 (together with the Master Indenture, the " Indenture "), between CAE, as issuer, and Computershare Advantage Trust of Canada (as successor to BNY Trust Company of Canada), as trustee (the " Trustee "), to approve an amendment to the definitions relating to the accounting principles referenced in the Master Indenture (the " Proposed Amendment ") by way of Extraordinary Resolution (as defined in the Indenture) (the " Amendment Resolution "), the full text of which is set forth in the joint consent and proxy solicitation statement (as it may be amended or supplemented, the " Consent and Proxy Solicitation Statement "), and to authorize CAE and the Trustee to enter into a second supplemental indenture to the Indenture (the " 2nd Supplemental Indenture ") pursuant to which the Proposed Amendment will come into force, all subject to the terms and conditions set forth in the Consent and Proxy Solicitation Statement. The deadline for submitting consents in respect of the Notes expired at 5:00 p.m. (Eastern time) on November 26, 2026 (the " Consent Deadline "). The Consent and Proxy Solicitation required the written consents of Noteholders representing not less than 66 2/3% of the outstanding aggregate principal amount of the Notes. As at the Consent Deadline, Noteholders representing in excess of 66 2/3% of the outstanding aggregate principal amount of the Notes delivered consents. CAE, the Trustee and the subsidiary guarantors party thereto have executed the 2nd Supplemental Indenture pursuant to which the Proposed Amendment has come into force and is effective. Because the Amendment Resolution was passed by written consent of the Noteholders by the Consent Deadline, the meeting of Noteholders that was previously scheduled for 9:00 a.m. (Eastern time) on December 16, 2026 is cancelled. Noteholders as of 5:00 p.m. (Eastern time) on November 26, 2026 who authorized and directed their CDS Participant (as defined in the Consent and Proxy Solicitation Statement) to elect to consent prior to the Consent Deadline, and whose CDS Participant made such election through CDSX will receive the consent fee in the amount of $1.00 per $1,000 principal amount of Notes (the " Consent Fee "). Noteholders who failed to deliver a valid consent prior to the Consent Deadline will not be entitled to receive the Consent Fee. This press release is for informational purposes only and does not amend the Consent and Proxy Solicitation, which has expired on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Consent and Proxy Solicitation Statement. CAE has retained Scotia Capital Inc. to act as solicitation agent for the Consent and Proxy Solicitation (the " Solicitation Agent ").
