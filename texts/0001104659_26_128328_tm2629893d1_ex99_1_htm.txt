(Nasdaq: RPID) (the “Company”), an innovative life sciences technology company providing mission-critical automation solutions to facilitate the efficient manufacturing and fast, safe release of healthcare products, today announced it has signed a definitive agreement pursuant to which Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany, has agreed to acquire, subject to the terms and conditions set forth in the agreement, all outstanding shares of Rapid Micro Biosystems at a price of $0.75 per share in an all-cash transaction. "Over the past 18 months, we have built a strong partnership with Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany, centered on a shared commitment to innovation and helping customers modernize and automate their quality-control workflows,” said Robert Spignesi, President and CEO of Rapid Micro Biosystems. “Rapid Micro has established the Growth Direct platform as the leading solution for automating microbial quality control, with most of the world's top pharmaceutical manufacturers as customers. We are confident that joining the Life Science business of Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany will significantly expand the Growth Direct platform’s reach and more fully serve our combined global customer base." “Quality control is essential to the reliable manufacture of medicines,” said Paolo Carli, Head of Advanced Solutions for the Life Science business of Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany. “Our customers need reliable results faster, more efficient quality-control workflows and robust data integrity to support regulatory compliance. Rapid Micro Biosystems will strengthen our ability to meet those needs, building on our existing offering and bringing greater automation to a critical step in pharmaceutical manufacturing.” Transaction Details The transaction is expected to close in December 2026, subject to the successful completion of the tender offer, including satisfaction of the minimum tender condition, and other customary closing conditions. Following completion of the transaction, Rapid Micro Biosystems will become part of the Life Science business of Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany. Advisors Perella Weinberg Partners is serving as financial advisor to the Company and Goodwin Procter LLP is serving as legal counsel. About Rapid Micro Biosystems Rapid Micro Biosystems is an innovative life sciences technology company providing mission-critical automation solutions to facilitate the efficient manufacturing and fast, safe release of healthcare products such as biologics, vaccines, cell and gene therapies, and sterile injectables. The Company’s flagship Growth Direct system automates and modernizes the antiquated, manual microbial quality control (“MQC”) testing workflows used in the largest and most complex pharmaceutical manufacturing operations across the globe. The Growth Direct system brings the quality control lab to the manufacturing floor, unlocking the power of MQC automation to deliver the faster results, greater accuracy, increased operational efficiency, better compliance with data integrity regulations, and quicker decision making that customers rely on to ensure safe and consistent supply of important healthcare products. The Company is headquartered in Lexington, Massachusetts and has U.S. manufacturing in Lowell, Massachusetts, with global locations in Switzerland, Germany, and the Netherlands.
