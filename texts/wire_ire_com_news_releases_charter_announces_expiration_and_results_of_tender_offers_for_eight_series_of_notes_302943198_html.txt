Nov 18, 2026, 08:00 ET Share this article Share this article STAMFORD, Conn. , Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Charter Communications, Inc. (NASDAQ: CHTR , CHTRP) (along with its subsidiaries, " Charter ") announced the expiration and results, as of 5:00 p.m. (New York City time) on November 17, 2026 (the " Expiration Date "), of the previously announced eight separate offers (each, an " Offer " and, collectively, the " Offers ") by its wholly-owned subsidiaries, Charter Communications Operating, LLC (" CCO ") and Charter Communications Operating Capital Corp. (" CCO Capital " and, together with CCO, collectively, the " Issuers " or the " Company "), to purchase for cash the outstanding series of their notes listed in the table below (collectively, the " Notes " and each a " Series "). In addition, the Company today announced that it had increased the aggregate Consideration for the Offers to $1.501 billion (the " Offer Cap "), excluding Accrued Interest and fees and expenses related to the Offers, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase dated November 10, 2026 (as supplemented, the " Offer to Purchase "). The Company has exercised its right to increase the aggregate principal amount of 3.700% Senior Secured Notes due 2051 accepted for purchase by less than 2% of the outstanding aggregate principal amount of such Series of Notes, as further described in the Acceptance Priority Procedures set forth in the Offer to Purchase, without amending or extending the Offer. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this press release have the same meanings given to them in the Offer to Purchase. Because the aggregate Consideration for all Notes validly tendered and not validly withdrawn at or prior to the Expiration Date exceeded the Offer Cap, the Company has accepted for purchase all validly tendered Notes with Acceptance Priority Levels 1 through 3. The Company will not accept for purchase any Notes with an Acceptance Priority Level of 4 or lower. The total aggregate principal amount of the Notes accepted by the Company in connection with the Offers is $2.7 billion. Withdrawal rights for the Offers expired at 5:00 p.m. (New York City time) on November 17, 2026. As of the Expiration Date, all conditions to the Offers were deemed satisfied or waived by the Company. The Consideration does not include the applicable Accrued Interest, which will be paid in cash in addition to the applicable Consideration on the Settlement Date. The settlement date for the Offers is expected to be on November 19, 2026, the second business day after the Expiration Date (the " Settlement Date "). On the Settlement Date, Holders whose Notes have been accepted for purchase will receive the applicable Consideration, which is based on the previously announced pricing terms for the Offers, in cash. Such Holders will also receive an additional cash payment equal to accrued and unpaid interest on such Notes to, but excluding, the Settlement Date (the "Accrued Interest"). Notes validly tendered but not accepted for purchase will be promptly returned or credited to the tendering Holders' accounts at The Depository Trust Company (" DTC ").
