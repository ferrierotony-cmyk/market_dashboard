Notes NEW YORK – November 19, 2026 – CRH plc (NYSE: CRH) announced today the early results of the previously announced (i) cash tender offers (the “Tender Offers” and each a “Tender Offer”) by CRH America Finance, Inc. (“CRH AF”), to purchase any and all of the outstanding 4.375% Senior Notes due 2029 (the “2029 Arcosa Notes”) and 6.875% Senior Notes due 2032 (the “2032 Arcosa Notes” and, together with the 2029 Arcosa Notes, the “Arcosa Notes”), issued by Arcosa, Inc. (“Arcosa”), an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of CRH, (ii) offers to eligible holders to exchange (the “Exchange Offers” and each an “Exchange Offer”, and together with the Tender Offers, the “Offers” and each an “Offer”) any and all of the Arcosa Notes for up to $1,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount of new notes issued by CRH AF and fully and unconditionally guaranteed by CRH plc (the “New CRH AF Notes”) and (iii) related solicitations of consent (the “Consent Solicitations” and each a “Consent Solicitation”) by Arcosa from holders of the Arcosa Notes with respect to certain proposed amendments (the “Proposed Amendments”) to the indentures governing the Arcosa Notes (the “Existing Arcosa Indentures”). The terms and conditions of the Offers and related Consent Solicitations are described in the Offer to Purchase, dated November 4, 2026 (the “Offer to Purchase”), and the Offering Memorandum, dated November 4, 2026 (the “Offering Memorandum”), as applicable, and remain unchanged. The table below sets forth the principal amount of each series of Arcosa Notes that were validly tendered, and not validly withdrawn (and related consents thereby delivered), as of 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 18, 2026 (the “Early Tender Deadline” with respect to the Tender Offers, and the “Early Tender Date” with respect to the Exchange Offers). Each holder of Arcosa Notes who validly tendered Arcosa Notes in an Offer is deemed to have delivered its consent, with respect to the principal amount of such tendered Arcosa Notes, to the Proposed Amendments to the corresponding Existing Arcosa Indenture. Withdrawal and revocation rights for the Offers and related Consent Solicitations expired at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 18, 2026. As a result, tenders of Arcosa Notes may not be withdrawn, and delivered consents may not be revoked, except in certain limited circumstances where additional withdrawal or revocation rights are required by law. There is no early settlement date with respect to the Exchange Offers and the exchange of Arcosa Notes validly tendered, and not validly withdrawn, in the applicable Exchange Offer by the Early Tender Date and accepted for exchange is expected to be made on December 8, 2026 (the “Final Settlement Date”). CRH AF’s obligation to accept for purchase or exchange, and to pay for or exchange, Arcosa Notes validly tendered in the Offers is subject to the satisfaction or waiver of a number of conditions described in the Offer to Purchase and the Offering Memorandum, as applicable. CRH also announced that, as of November 18, 2026, Arcosa has received valid consents sufficient to effect the applicable Proposed Amendments for both series of Arcosa Notes. Accordingly, Arcosa and the trustee under the Existing Arcosa Indentures have executed and delivered supplemental indentures relating to the applicable Proposed Amendments that will become operative upon the purchase by CRH AF of all Arcosa Notes validly tendered and not validly withdrawn into the applicable Tender Offer on the Early Settlement Date. Each Offer will expire at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on December 4, 2026, unless extended (such date and time, as the same may be extended, the “Expiration Date”) or earlier terminated. Holders who validly tender their Arcosa Notes into the applicable Offer after the Early Tender Deadline or Early Tender Date, as applicable, but on or prior to the Expiration Date, will not be eligible to receive the applicable early tender premium of $50 per $1,000 principal amount of Arcosa Notes accepted for purchase, with respect to the applicable Tender Offer, or $1,000 principal amount of New CRH AF Notes per $1,000 principal amount of Arcosa Notes accepted for exchange, with respect to the applicable Exchange Offer, and will instead receive the applicable tender consideration or exchange consideration as described in the Offer to Purchase or the Offering Memorandum, as applicable. CRH AF reserves the right, in its sole discretion, subject to applicable law, with respect to each Offer, (a) to accept for purchase and pay for all Arcosa Notes validly tendered on or before the Final Settlement Date and to keep the applicable Offer open or extend the Expiration Date to a later date and time as announced by CRH AF, (b) to waive any or all conditions to an Offer for Arcosa Notes tendered on or before the Expiration Date, (c) to extend, withdraw, terminate or amend the terms and conditions of such Offer, at any time and for any reason. Holders should not tender Arcosa Notes that they do not wish to be purchased or exchanged in the Offers. The New CRH AF Notes have not been registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) under the U.S. Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities Act”), or with any securities regulatory authority of any State or other jurisdiction. The New CRH AF Notes may not be offered or sold in the United States or to, or for the account or benefit of, any U.S. persons except pursuant to an exemption from, or in a transaction not subject to, the registration requirements of the Securities Act. The Exchange Offers will only be made, and the New CRH AF Notes are only being offered and will only be issued, to holders of Arcosa Notes that are Eligible Holders (as defined in the Offering Memorandum).
