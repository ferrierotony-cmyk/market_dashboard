Entry Into a Material Definitive Agreement. On October 29, 2026, Xerox Corporation (“Xerox”), Xerox Holdings Corporation (“Holdings” and together with Xerox, the “Companies”), the subsidiary guarantors named therein (the “Guarantors”) and certain holders of 5.500% Senior Notes due 2028 of Holdings (the “2028 Notes”), the Existing First Lien Notes (as defined below) and/or term loans under the Term Loan Credit Agreement (as defined below), or their investment advisors, sub-advisors or managers (each, a “Consenting Holder” and collectively, the “Consenting Holders” and, together with the Companies and the Guarantors, the “ESA Parties”) entered into an exchange support agreement (the “Exchange Support Agreement”) pursuant to which, the ESA Parties have agreed to the terms of and to support the transactions described below. The transactions (collectively, the “Transactions”) include (A) the commencement of an exchange offer (the “Exchange Offer”) to exchange approximately $174.0 million aggregate principal amount of 2028 Notes held by the Consenting Holders (representing approximately 31.1% of the outstanding aggregate principal amount of the 2028 Notes) for, per $1,000 principal amount of 2028 Notes exchanged in the Exchange Offer: (i) $690.00 aggregate principal amount of 10.250% Senior Secured First Lien Notes (2026 Issue) due 2030 newly issued by Xerox (the “New 1L Exchange Notes”), with terms, priority, and collateral substantially identical to those of the Existing First Lien Notes under the Existing First Lien Indenture (as defined below), as modified by the Supplemental Indenture (as defined below), and (ii) 3,872,062 shares of common stock, par value $1.00 per share, of Holdings (the “Common Stock”) or, in limited circumstances, an additional $60.00 principal amount of New 1L Exchange Notes, and (B) (i) an amendment (the “Term Loan Credit Agreement Amendment”) to the First Lien Term Loan Credit Agreement, dated as of November 17, 2023, among Xerox, as borrower, Holdings, as guarantor, the other guarantors party thereto, the lenders party thereto from time to time and Jefferies Finance LLC, as administrative agent and collateral agent (as amended, restated, amended and restated, supplemented or otherwise modified prior to the date hereof, the “Term Loan Credit Agreement”) and (ii) a supplemental indenture (the “Supplemental Indenture”) to the Indenture, dated as of April 11, 2025, among Xerox, as issuer, Holdings, as guarantor, the other guarantors party thereto and U.S. Bank Trust Company, National Association, as trustee and collateral agent, relating to the 10.250% Senior Secured First Lien Notes due 2030 (the “Existing First Lien Notes”) of Xerox (as amended, restated, amended and restated, supplemented or otherwise modified prior to the date hereof, the “Existing First Lien Indenture”), in each case, to add a negative covenant limiting the Companies’ ability to permit certain entities to incur additional debt or issue additional equity. Pursuant to the Exchange Support Agreement, the Consenting Holders have agreed to, among other things: (i) participate in the Exchange Offer, including by delivering an exchange agreement (the form of which is attached to the Exchange Support Agreement, the “Exchange Agreement”) to formally exchange such Consenting Holders’ 2028 Notes in the Exchange Offer by November 12, 2026, the early exchange deadline for the Exchange Offer (the “Early Exchange Deadline”), subject to the conditions outlined in the Exchange Support Agreement, and (ii), to the extent they hold term loans under the Term Loan Credit Agreement, provide consent to implement the Term Loan Credit Agreement Amendment. Further, effective upon consummation of the Exchange Offer, each of the Consenting Holders has agreed (and each beneficial owner of New 1L Exchange Notes, by its acceptance thereof, will agree) not to institute, pursue or support any action or proceeding against the Companies, their subsidiaries or their respective affiliates and representatives and the agents, trustees and collateral agents under the Companies’ and their subsidiaries’ existing funded indebtedness, in each case, arising out of or relating to the joint venture previously announced in February 2026 and related transactions, subject to the conditions outlined in the Exchange Support Agreement. Concurrently, the Companies have agreed to, among other things, use commercially reasonable efforts to (i) take or cause to be taken all actions reasonably necessary to consummate the Transactions, including by entering into the Exchange Agreement, the Term Loan Credit Agreement Amendment and the Supplemental Indenture, and (ii) cause Holdings to file a registration statement for the resale of the Common Stock to be issued in the Exchange Offer on or prior to the settlement date of the Exchange Offer (the “Settlement Date”), in each case, as outlined in, and subject to the conditions of, the Exchange Support Agreement. The Exchange Support Agreement may be terminated by the Consenting Holders that hold, in the aggregate, at least 66.67% of the outstanding principal amount of 2028 Notes held by all Consenting Holders, or the Companies, in certain circumstances including if, among others, the early settlement of the Exchange Offer is not consummated on or before November 20, 2026 (in the case of termination by the Consenting Holders), or the counterparty is in breach in any material respects of their obligations, representations, warranties or covenants contained in the Exchange Support Agreement (after an applicable cure period). The Settlement Date is expected to occur on or about November 13, 2026, the business day following the Early Exchange Deadline. Concurrently with entrance into the Exchange Support Agreement, the Companies entered into the Term Loan Credit Agreement Amendment as described above. Although the Term Loan Credit Agreement Amendment has been entered into, its operative provisions will become effective only upon the Settlement Date.
