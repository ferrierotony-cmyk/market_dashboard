Announces Increased Consent Fee in Consent Solicitation Relating to Surgery Center Holdings, Inc.’s 7.250% Senior Notes due 2032 BRENTWOOD, Tenn., November 18, 2026 – Surgery Partners, Inc. (“Surgery Partners”) (Nasdaq: SGRY) announced today that its indirect wholly owned subsidiary, Surgery Center Holdings, Inc. (the “Issuer”), has amended its previously announced solicitation of consents (the “Consent Solicitation”) from holders (“Holders”) of its outstanding $1,225,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 7.250% Senior Notes due 2032 (CUSIP Nos. 86881W AF9 / U86786 AD9 / U86786 AE7; ISINs US86881WAF95 / USU86786AD98 / USU86786AE71) (the “Notes”) to certain proposed amendments (the “Proposed Amendments”) to the indenture governing the Notes, dated as of April 10, 2024 (as supplemented, the “Indenture”), among the Issuer, the guarantors party thereto and Wilmington Trust, National Association, as trustee (the “Trustee”), to increase the consent fee payable to Holders who validly deliver (and do not validly revoke) consents prior to the Expiration Time (as defined below) from $2.50 to $5.00 per $1,000 in principal amount of Notes (the “Increased Consent Fee”). The Consent Solicitation is being made in accordance with the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Issuer’s Consent Solicitation Statement, dated November 16, 2026, as amended by Amendment No. 1 thereto, dated November 16, 2026, and Amendment No. 2 thereto, dated November 18, 2026 (as so amended and as it may be further amended or supplemented from time to time, the “Consent Solicitation Statement”). The Consent Solicitation is scheduled to expire at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 20, 2026, unless extended or earlier terminated (such time and date, as it may be extended, the “Expiration Time”). Holders who have previously delivered (and not validly revoked) consents do not need to redeliver such consents or take any other action in order to be eligible to receive the Increased Consent Fee. Payment of the Increased Consent Fee is conditioned upon the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions set forth in the Consent Solicitation Statement, including the receipt of consents from Holders of a majority of the aggregate principal amount of the Notes outstanding, calculated in accordance with the Indenture. If such conditions are satisfied or waived, the Increased Consent Fee will be paid promptly following the later of the Expiration Time and the Effective Time (as defined in the Consent Solicitation Statement), whether or not the Merger (as defined in the Consent Solicitation Statement) is consummated. Holders who do not deliver consents, or who validly revoke their consents, will not receive the Increased Consent Fee. Except as described in this press release, the terms of the Consent Solicitation remain unchanged. Holders are referred to the Consent Solicitation Statement for the detailed terms and conditions of the Consent Solicitation. Jefferies LLC is serving as solicitation agent (the “Solicitation Agent”) in connection with the Consent Solicitation. Questions regarding the terms of the Consent Solicitation may be directed to Jefferies LLC at (212) 284-2368 (collect). About Surgery Partners Surgery Partners is a leading healthcare services company with a differentiated outpatient delivery model focused on providing high quality, cost effective solutions for surgical and related ancillary care in support of both patients and physicians. Founded in 2004, Surgery Partners is one of the largest and fastest growing surgical services businesses in the country, with more than 200 locations in 30 states, including ambulatory surgery centers, surgical hospitals, multi-specialty physician practices and urgent care facilities.
