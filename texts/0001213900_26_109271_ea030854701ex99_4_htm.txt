AND QUANTISIMO CORP Exhibit 99.4 SPONSOR SUPPORT AGREEMENT This SPONSOR SUPPORT AGREEMENT (this “ Agreement ”) is made and entered into as of October 8, 2026, by and among GigAcquisitions8 Corp., a Cayman Islands exempted company (“ Sponsor ”), GigCapital8 Corp., a Cayman Islands exempted company (“ SPAC ”), Quantisimo Holdings Corp., a BVI business company limited by shares (“ PubCo ”) and Quantisimo Corp, a BVI business company (the “Company”). Capitalized terms used but not defined herein have the meanings assigned to them in the Agreement and Plan of Merger by and among SPAC, PubCo, the Company and the other parties thereto, dated as of October 8, 2026 (as may be amended from time to time, the “ Merger Agreement ”). WHEREAS, as of the date hereof, Sponsor owns 6,058,167 SPAC Class B Shares (the “ Founder Shares ”) and 10,000 private placement units (the “ Private Placement Units ” and, together with the Founder Shares, the SPAC Class A Shares and SPAC Public Rights comprising or issuable in respect of the Private Placement Units, and any New Securities (as defined below) of which ownership of record or the power to vote is hereafter acquired by Sponsor prior to the termination of this Agreement, the “ Sponsor Shares ”); WHEREAS, in connection with SPAC’s initial public offering (the “ IPO ”), SPAC, Sponsor and the then current officers and directors of SPAC entered into a letter agreement, dated as of October 3, 2025 (the “ Insider Letter ”), pursuant to which Sponsor agreed to certain voting requirements, transfer restrictions and waiver of redemption rights with respect to the SPAC Common Stock owned by it; WHEREAS, Section 5 of SPAC’s Amended and Restated Memorandum and Articles of Association (the “ SPAC Charter ”) provides, among other matters, that the SPAC Class B Shares will automatically convert into SPAC Class A Shares upon the consummation of an initial business combination, subject to adjustment if additional SPAC Class A Shares or Equity-Linked Securities (as defined in the SPAC Charter) are issued or deemed issued in excess of the amounts sold in the IPO (the “ Anti-Dilution Right ”), excluding certain exempted issuances; WHEREAS, concurrently with the execution and delivery of this Agreement, SPAC, PubCo, the Company and the other parties thereto are entering into the Merger Agreement, pursuant to which, upon the consummation of the transactions contemplated thereby (the “ Closing ”), among other matters, (a) SPAC Merger Sub will merge with and into SPAC, with SPAC surviving such merger as a direct wholly owned subsidiary of PubCo in accordance with the Cayman Companies Act (the “ SPAC Merger ”), and (b) immediately following confirmation of the effective filing of the SPAC Merger, Target Merger Sub will merge with and into the Company, with the Company surviving such merger as a direct wholly owned subsidiary of PubCo in accordance with the BVI Act (the “ Company Merger ” and, together with the SPAC Merger and the other transactions contemplated by the Merger Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements, including the Contribution, the “ Transactions ”); and WHEREAS, as a condition and inducement to PubCo’s and the Company’s willingness to enter into the Merger Agreement, PubCo and the Company require that Sponsor enter into this Agreement. Sponsor Voting Requirements . At any meeting of the SPAC Shareholders, however called, or at any adjournment or postponement thereof, or in any other circumstance in which the vote, consent or other approval of the SPAC Shareholders is sought, Sponsor shall (i) if a meeting is held, appear at each such meeting (in person or by proxy) or otherwise cause all of the Sponsor Shares to be counted as present thereat for purposes of calculating a quorum and (ii) vote (or cause to be voted), or execute and deliver a written consent (or cause a written consent to be executed and delivered) covering, all of the Sponsor Shares: (a) in favor of the approval and adoption of the Merger Agreement, the Mergers and the other Transactions (together, the “ SPAC Shareholder Approval Matters ”); (b) against any SPAC Alternative Transaction; (c) against any merger, consolidation, combination, sale of substantial assets, reorganization, recapitalization, dissolution, liquidation or winding up of or by SPAC (other than the Transactions); (d) against any change in the business of SPAC; and (e) against any proposal, action or agreement involving SPAC that would or would reasonably be expected to (i) impede, frustrate, prevent or nullify any provision of this Agreement, the Merger Agreement or any Ancillary Agreement, (ii) result in a breach in any material respect of any covenant, representation, warranty or any other obligation or agreement of SPAC under the Merger Agreement or any Ancillary Agreement, (iii) result in any of the conditions in respect of obligations of SPAC or the Parties set forth in Article IX of the Merger Agreement not being fulfilled, or (iv) change in any manner the capitalization of, including the voting rights of any class of share capital of, SPAC (other than in connection with the SPAC Shareholder Approval Matters). 2. During the Interim Period, for the benefit of PubCo and the Company, (a) Sponsor agrees that it shall fully comply with, and perform all of its obligations, covenants and agreements set forth in, the Insider Letter, including not redeeming its Sponsor Shares in connection with the Transactions and complying with the transfer restrictions with respect to the Sponsor Shares, (b) without limiting clause (a), Sponsor shall not redeem, tender or submit for redemption, or otherwise exercise any right to redeem, any Sponsor Shares (including any SPAC Class A Shares underlying the Private Placement Units, any securities issued upon conversion of any Working Capital Loan and any New Securities), whether in connection with the SPAC Share Redemption, any extension of SPAC’s deadline to consummate a business combination, any tender offer or otherwise, (c) Sponsor shall not Transfer (as defined in the Insider Letter) any Sponsor Shares, including any Transfer otherwise permitted under paragraph 10 of the Insider Letter, unless, prior to such Transfer, the transferee executes and delivers to SPAC, PubCo and the Company a joinder agreeing to be bound by this Agreement in accordance with Section 10(h) , and SPAC shall not register any Transfer not in compliance with this clause (c), which shall be null and void ab initio , (d) SPAC agrees to enforce the Insider Letter in accordance with its terms, including, at the Company’s request, against each holder of Founder Shares that is a party thereto, and (e) each of Sponsor and SPAC agrees not to amend, modify, terminate or waive any provision of the Insider Letter without the prior written consent of the Company (which may be withheld in its sole discretion); and Sponsor and SPAC shall give the Company a reasonable opportunity to review and comment on any proposed amendment to the Insider Letter. 3.
