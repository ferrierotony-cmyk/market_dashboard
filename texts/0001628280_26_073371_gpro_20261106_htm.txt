Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement. Amendment No. 1 to Agreement and Plan of Merger On November 6, 2026, GoPro, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “Company”), entered into Amendment No. 1 (the “Amendment”) to the Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated as of September 1, 2026 (as amended, the “Merger Agreement”), by and among Action Acquisition LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“Parent”), Starman Optical, Inc., a Delaware corporation and a wholly-owned subsidiary of Parent (“Merger Sub”), and the Company. The Merger Agreement was previously reported on a Current Report on Form 8-K filed by the Company with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) on September 2, 2026. Capitalized terms used but not defined herein have the meanings ascribed to them in the Merger Agreement. The Amendment effects the following changes to the Merger Agreement: (a) The Amendment extends the “Outside Date” (the date by which the merger must be consummated, after which either party may terminate the Merger Agreement) from December 31, 2026 to March 31, 2027. (b) The Amendment amends Section 2.4(a) of the Merger Agreement so that at the Effective Time, the existing certificate of incorporation of the Company (rather than a new certificate in the form previously attached as Exhibit A to the Merger Agreement) will become the certificate of incorporation of the surviving corporation, subject to Section 5.11 of the Merger Agreement. (c) The Amendment amends Section 2.9 of the Merger Agreement to provide that if, from the date of the Amendment to the earlier of the Effective Time or termination of the Merger Agreement, the issued and outstanding shares of Common Stock or securities convertible or exchangeable into or exercisable for shares of Common Stock have been changed into a different number of shares or securities or a different class by reason of any reclassification, stock split (including a reverse stock split), stock dividend, recapitalization, merger, issuer tender offer or exchange offer, or other similar transaction, or a stock dividend with a record date within such period is declared, then the Merger Consideration and the number of shares of surviving corporation common stock into which each share of Merger Sub’s common stock is converted will be equitably adjusted to provide Parent and the holders of shares of Common Stock and Company Equity Awards the same economic effect (including the same relative percentage ownership of the surviving corporation) as contemplated by the Merger Agreement prior to such event. (d) The Amendment removes Exhibit A (Form of Certificate of Incorporation of Surviving Corporation) from the Merger Agreement in its entirety. (e) The Amendment acknowledges and confirms that the Merger Agreement incorrectly identified Parent as “Action Acquisitions LLC” instead of “Action Acquisition LLC” as a result of a scrivener’s error, and that Action Acquisition LLC has at all times been the party to and bound by the Merger Agreement as “Parent.” The Amendment also acknowledges that, effective as of the date of the Merger Agreement, all references in the Merger Agreement (including the preamble and signature pages thereto) and in any exhibit, schedule, certificate or other document delivered pursuant thereto by “Action Acquisitions LLC” shall be deemed to refer to Action Acquisition LLC. (f) The Amendment requires Parent, as promptly as practicable after execution of the Amendment, to use reasonable best efforts to obtain an amendment to the Commitment Letter to extend the termination date referenced therein to 11:59 p.m. (New York City time) on March 31, 2027. Except as expressly amended by the Amendment, all terms of the Merger Agreement remain in full force and effect. The foregoing description of the Amendment does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Amendment, which is filed as Exhibit 2.1 hereto and incorporated herein by reference.
