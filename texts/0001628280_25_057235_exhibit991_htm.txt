Additionally, the Company is amending the Tender Offers to increase (i) the Maximum Tender Amount so as to accept for purchase up to $1,200,106,000 aggregate principal amount of the Notes validly tendered and not validly withdrawn prior to the Early Tender Time, and (ii) the Series Cap so as to accept for purchase up to $254,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the 2028 Notes validly tendered and not validly withdrawn prior to the Early Tender Time (such amount, the “Series Cap”). The Tender Offers have been made upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase dated December 2, 2025, as amended and supplemented by this press release (as so amended and supplemented and as it may be further amended or supplemented from time to time, the “Offer to Purchase”). Capitalized terms not defined in this announcement have the meanings given to them in the Offer to Purchase. According to information provided by D.F. The Tender Offers are subject to the satisfaction of certain conditions, as set forth in the Offer to Purchase. The Financing Condition for the Tender Offers as described in the Offer to Purchase has been satisfied.
