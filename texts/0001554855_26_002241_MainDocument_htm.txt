Adjusted EBITDA reached US$2,503 million (-11% q/q; +84% y/y), YPF’s second-highest quarterly result after the record set in 2Q26. The Adjusted EBITDA margin remained strong at 38%. The sequential decline primarily reflected inventory valuation effects and lower refined product realizations prices as international prices declined in 3Q26. These impacts were partly offset by seasonally higher natural gas sales and lower price-linked costs, mainly third-party crude purchases, royalties and other taxes. Year-on-year growth was primarily driven by higher pricing environment, lower lifting costs as exposure to conventional assets declined, combined with growing shale output and increased refinery throughput. 3 3Q26 YPF YPF 3Q26 CAPEX totaled US$1,287 million (-4% q/q; +26% y/y), with 70% allocated to unconventional operations. The sequential decline mainly reflected the signing bonus accrued in 2Q26 for unconventional concessions covering five blocks dedicated to the Argentina LNG Project, partly offset by increased drilling and workover activity in 3Q26. Shale oil production averaged 224 kbbl/d (+5% q/q; +32% y/y), accounting for 86% of total oil production, up from 80% in 2Q26 and 71% in 3Q25. We remain firmly on track to meet our 2026 average production target of 215 kbbl/d and comfortably achieve a December 2026 exit rate of 250 kbbl/d. YPF acquired the remaining 25% stake in YPF Luz for US$430 million through acquisition financing, bringing ownership to 100% and fully consolidating the company. The transaction positions YPF to capture the value of a leading power generation platform, with long-term growth opportunities in efficient thermal generation and renewables. Refinery throughput remained strong at 344 kbbl/d (-2% q/q; +5% y/y). Despite scheduled maintenance at La Plata in September 2026, YPF again met domestic demand without fuel imports, increased fuel exports and achieved record premium gasoline production in 3Q26. Free cash flow reached US$901 million (+US$77 million q/q; +US$1.7 billion y/y), YPF’s second-highest quarterly result. Strong Adjusted EBITDA and initial proceeds from the sales of Metrogas and the Mendoza Non-Operated and Chachahuen conventional clusters comfortably covered CAPEX and regular interest and income tax payments. Consequently, net leverage declined to 0.8x, its lowest level in a decade. In September 2026, YPF returned to international capital markets with a US$1.2 billion 9-year bond at a 7.85% yield and a record-low spread for YPF of ~300 basis points over U.S. Treasuries. A portion of the proceeds pre-funded 2027 and 2029 maturities. Key project milestones:  VMOS: The two monobuoys arrived at the port in Río Negro Province in early October 2026. ABOUT YPF YPF is the largest energy company in Argentina, fully integrated in the oil and gas value chain. Our main businesses are: (i) in the upstream, we produce ~30% of the country’s oil and gas, and we are the largest shale producer in Vaca Muerta, in process of divestment of conventional mature fields; (ii) in the downstream, we operate 3 refineries (+50% of Argentina’s refining capacity) and lead the local diesel and gasoline sales (market share >55%); and (iii) in new energies, YPF Luz, our subsidiary, is the third largest power generation company in Argentina.
