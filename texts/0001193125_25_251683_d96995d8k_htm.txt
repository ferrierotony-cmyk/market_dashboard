As GigCapital7 Corp., a Cayman Islands exempted company (“ GigCapital7 ”), stated in its Current Report on Form 8-K that it filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on October 15, 2025, GigCapital7 and Hadron Energy, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “ Company ”) will conduct meetings with members of the investment community in connection with certain financing activities in furtherance of their previously announced business combination.
