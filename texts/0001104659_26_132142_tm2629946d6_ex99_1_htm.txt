The Arrangement Aurora and Organigram entered into an arrangement agreement dated November 2, 2026 (as supplemented, modified or amended, the “ Arrangement Agreement ”), which was unanimously approved by the respective boards of directors of Aurora and Organigram. Pursuant to the Arrangement Agreement and the accompanying plan of arrangement, Organigram will acquire all of the issued and outstanding Aurora Common Shares. Aurora Shareholders will be entitled to receive, at the election of each Aurora Shareholder, for each Aurora Common Share: (a) $7.80 in cash (the “ Cash Alternative ”); (b) 5.8626 common shares of Organigram (“ Organigram Common Shares ”) (the “ Share Alternative ”); or (c) a combination of $3.75 in cash and 3.0431 Organigram Common Shares (the “ Combination Alternative ”), subject, in the case of the Cash Alternative and the Share Alternative, to proration such that the aggregate cash payable and the aggregate number of Organigram Common Shares issuable under the Arrangement will not exceed $3.75 and 3.0431 Organigram Common Shares, respectively, multiplied by the number of Aurora Common Shares outstanding immediately prior to completion of the Arrangement (collectively, the “ Consideration ”). Current Aurora Shareholders are expected to hold approximately 37% of the economic interest and 41% of the voting rights in Organigram, on a fully diluted basis, immediately after the completion of the Arrangement and the BAT Private Placement (as defined herein), with the balance held by current holders (the “ Organigram Shareholders ”) of Organigram Common Shares (including BT DE Investments Inc. (“ BAT ”)). The Arrangement is currently anticipated to be completed in January 2027, and in any event not before January 6, 2027, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of all conditions precedent in the Arrangement Agreement, including receipt of all court, shareholder and regulatory approvals. Reasons for and Benefits of the Arrangement Organigram and Aurora expect that the Arrangement will create a global cannabis leader with leading positions in Canada, Europe and Australia, supported by Organigram’s strategic shareholder, BAT. The combined company is i expected to have a global platform with greater scale, broader capabilities, financial strength and international reach, bringing together Organigram’s recreational cannabis leadership, production network, research and development platform and European medical commercial operations with Aurora’s global medical cannabis leadership, established international presence and EU-GMP certified manufacturing capabilities. The anticipated benefits of the Arrangement include: · Creates a Global Cannabis Leader with a scaled, Canadian-headquartered global cannabis platform with leading positions in five nationally legal cannabis markets – Canada, Germany, Poland, Australia and New Zealand – and meaningful operations in other key markets. · Significant Premium and Choice of Consideration . Aurora Shareholders may elect the Cash Alternative, the Share Alternative or the Combination Alternative, subject, in the case of the Cash Alternative and the Share Alternative, to proration, with an implied value of $7.80 per Aurora Common Share, representing a premium of approximately 93% to the Unaffected Price (as defined in the Joint Information Circular) and approximately 21% to the implied value of the Hostile Bid (as defined in the Joint Information Circular) as of November 2, 2026. · Superior to the Hostile Bid , which the board of directors of Aurora (the “ Aurora Board ”) and the independent special committee of the Aurora Board (the “ Aurora Special Committee ”) have twice unanimously determined to be financially inadequate, with the Arrangement offering a larger cash component at a fixed price. · Attractive Valuation. Approximately 9.4x LTM Adjusted EBITDA and 1.4x LTM Net Revenue for the twelve months ended June 30, 2026. · Complementary Capabilities across Organigram’s Canadian adult-use leadership, diversified production and cultivation capabilities, research and development platform and European medical market presence, and Aurora’s global medical cannabis leadership, genetics expertise and EU-GMP certified manufacturing capacity. · Meaningful Synergies of more than $50 million annually, with Aurora Shareholders who retain Organigram Common Shares participating in the value of such synergies to the extent realized. · Accelerates Product Innovation and Commercial Growth , through the combined company’s scale and cross-border infrastructure, which are expected to create a platform for shared innovation and brand building, supported by BAT’s demonstrated expertise in research and development, innovative product development and global supply chain management. · Attractive Organigram Common Shares , listed on the Toronto Stock Exchange and Nasdaq, with a single class of voting shares, no controlling shareholder and a supportive strategic shareholder in BAT. · Preferred Outcome of a Robust Competitive Strategic Process following the unsolicited offer by Curaleaf, as amended, which the Aurora Special Committee and the Aurora Board have determined remains inadequate and significantly undervalues Aurora. · Role of the Special Committee. The Arrangement is the result of a strategic process undertaken with the supervision and involvement of the Aurora Special Committee, advised by experienced and qualified external legal and financial advisors. The Arrangement was unanimously recommended to the Aurora Board by the Aurora Special Committee on the basis described herein and on the basis of the legal and financial advice that was received by the Aurora Special Committee. · High Likelihood of Completion. The Arrangement is subject to only a limited number of customary conditions, and is not subject to any financing or due diligence condition. The only Required Regulatory Approvals are approval under the Competition Act (Canada) and clearance under German foreign direct investment laws, and Aurora and Organigram believe that meaningful regulatory challenges are unlikely ii given the complementary nature of their respective operations. Completion of the Arrangement is expected to occur in January 2027. A: The Aurora Shareholders’ Meeting is being held for the purposes of obtaining the Aurora Shareholder approval contemplated by the Arrangement Agreement and the Interim Order. Pursuant to the Interim Order, the Arrangement Resolution must be approved by not less than 66⅔% of the votes cast by Aurora Shareholders present virtually or represented by proxy and entitled to vote at the Aurora Shareholders’ Meeting, voting together as a single class, and by a simple majority of such votes cast, excluding the votes attached to Aurora Common Shares held by persons described in items (a) through (d) of section 8.1(2) of MI 61-101, being only Mr. Miguel Martin. The full text of the Arrangement Resolution is set forth in Appendix “A” to this Joint Information Circular. Q: When and where is the Aurora Shareholders’ Meeting being held?
