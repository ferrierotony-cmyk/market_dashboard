On November 16, 2026, Aytu BioPharma Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “Company”), entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (the “Merger Agreement”) with Rosemont Pharmaceuticals Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Parent”), and RoseApollo Merger Sub, Inc., a Delaware corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent (“Purchaser”). The Merger Agreement provides for the acquisition of the Company by Parent in a two-step transaction, consisting of a tender offer and, subject to the terms of the Merger Agreement and conditioned upon the consummation of the Offer, a subsequent merger of Purchaser with and into the Company (the “Merger”), with the Company continuing as the surviving corporation in the Merger and a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent. Pursuant to the Merger Agreement, Purchaser will commence a tender offer (the “Offer”) as promptly as practicable after the date of the Merger Agreement, and in any event no later than ten business days after the date of the Merger Agreement, to acquire (subject to the terms and conditions of the Merger Agreement) all of the Company’s issued and outstanding shares of common stock, par value $0.0001 per share (the “Shares”), for $2.70 per Share, net to the seller in cash, without interest (the “Offer Price”), subject to applicable tax withholding. Unless extended in accordance with the Merger Agreement, the Offer will expire one minute after 11:59 p.m., New York City time, on the twentieth day (calculated as set forth in Rule 14d-1(g)(3) under the Exchange Act (as defined below)) following and including the commencement of the Offer (subject to extension as set forth in the Merger Agreement). The Company’s board of directors (the “Company Board”) and a special committee of the Company Board has unanimously recommended that the Company’s stockholders accept the Offer and tender their Shares to Purchaser pursuant to the Offer. The obligation of Parent and Purchaser to consummate the Offer is subject to the satisfaction or waiver, to the extent permitted, of certain conditions set forth in the Merger Agreement, including that there have been validly tendered and not validly withdrawn a number of Shares that, together with Shares beneficially owned by Parent, Purchaser and their wholly owned subsidiaries, represent a majority of the Shares outstanding as of the consummation of the Offer. In addition, Purchaser’s obligation to consummate the Offer is conditioned upon, among other things, the accuracy of the Company’s representations and warranties, subject to specified materiality standards; the Company’s material compliance with its covenants; the absence of a Company material adverse effect; all required third-party consents having been obtained; Parent’s receipt of customary payoff letters for outstanding Payoff Debt (as defined in the Merger Agreement); and no greater than 15% of the Company Warrants (as defined in the Merger Agreement), measured by the number of underlying Shares, remain outstanding without having been exercised prior to the Expiration Date (as defined in the Merger Agreement) with all Shares issued upon such exercise validly tendered and not withdrawn pursuant to the Offer pursuant to a Company Warrant Exercise Agreement (as defined in the Merger Agreement), or terminated pursuant to a Support Agreement (as defined below) or Company Warrant Termination Agreement (as defined in the Merger Agreement). Consummation of the Offer is not subject to a financing condition. As soon as practicable following Purchaser’s acceptance for payment of the Shares validly tendered and not validly withdrawn pursuant to the Offer (the “Acceptance Time”), subject to the satisfaction or waiver of limited customary conditions set forth in the Merger Agreement, the Merger will be effected under Section 251(h) of the Delaware General Corporation Law (the “DGCL”), without a meeting or vote of the Company’s stockholders. At the effective time of the Merger (the “Effective Time”), each issued and outstanding Share, other than Shares held by the Company as treasury stock, Shares held directly by Parent or Purchaser or any direct or indirect wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, Parent or Purchaser, and Shares whose holders have properly exercised and perfected appraisal rights under Section 262 of the DGCL, will not be converted into the right to receive the Offer Price in cash (the “Merger Consideration”). Effective immediately prior to the Effective Time, each outstanding option to purchase Shares (each, a “Company Option”) shall, to the extent not vested, automatically become fully vested in connection with the consummation of the transactions contemplated in the Merger Agreement pursuant to the terms of the underlying award agreements governing each such Company Option and will be automatically cancelled and converted into the right to receive, without interest and less applicable tax withholding, a lump-sum cash payment equal to the number of Shares underlying the Company Option multiplied by the excess, if any, of the Merger Consideration over the applicable per-Share exercise price. Each Company Option with a per-Share exercise price equal to or greater than the Merger Consideration will be cancelled for no consideration; provided that, before cancellation, the Company will provide holders at least ten days to exercise their Company Options (and shall accelerate the vesting of any such Company Options, to the extent unvested, that are so exercised, prior to such exercise). Effective immediately prior to the Effective Time, the vesting restrictions and requirements of forfeiture with respect to each outstanding Share that is subject to vesting, repurchase or other lapse restrictions, whether granted under a Company Equity Plans (as defined in the Merger Agreement) or otherwise (each, a “Company Restricted Share”), shall have terminated and such Company Restricted Shares shall be treated as Shares in accordance with the Merger Agreement; provided, that any Company Restricted Shares held by an employee of the Company or its applicable Subsidiary that is not the subject of a timely and validly filed election under Section 83(b) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, shall be paid the applicable Merger Consideration (less any applicable withholding taxes) within the later of fifteen business days following the closing date and the Surviving Corporation’s (as defined in the Merger Agreement) next regular payroll date (but in no event earlier than the date of receipt of file-stamped evidence of the Merger filing from the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware). Effective as of, and conditional upon the occurrence of, the Effective Time, the Company Equity Plans (as defined in the Merger Agreement) and Company ESPP (as defined in the Merger Agreement) shall be terminated. All rights under the Company Equity Plans outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time shall cease to be outstanding, except the right to receive the consideration set forth in the Merger Agreement. The Company is required to use reasonable best efforts to cause each holder of a Company Warrant that is not a party to a Support Agreement to exercise its Company Warrants before the applicable deadline, pursuant to a Company Warrant Exercise Agreement or enter into a Company Warrant Termination Agreement. Each Company Warrant subject to a Company Warrant Termination Agreement or Support Agreement will be cancelled immediately prior to the Effective Time in exchange for the cash consideration specified in the applicable agreement. Each Company Warrant (as defined in the Merger Agreement) that is outstanding and unexercised immediately prior to the Effective Time and for which a Support Agreement, Company Warrant Termination Agreement or Company Warrant Exercise Agreement has not been delivered shall be treated in accordance with its terms. The Merger Agreement contains representations, warranties and covenants of the parties customary for a transaction of this nature. Subject to the terms and conditions of the Merger Agreement, the parties have agreed to use reasonable best efforts to consummate the Offer, the Merger and the other transactions contemplated by the Merger Agreement, including obtaining required governmental authorizations, making required governmental filings and obtaining required third-party consents. The Company has agreed that, subject to specified exceptions, during the period from the date of the Merger Agreement until the earlier of the Effective Time and valid termination of the Merger Agreement, among other things, the Company will conduct its business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and will not take certain specified actions without Parent’s prior written consent. The Company has also agreed to customary non-solicitation restrictions, including not to solicit, initiate or knowingly induce, encourage or facilitate a Takeover Proposal (as defined in the Merger Agreement) from a third part or effect a Company Adverse Recommendation Change (as defined in the Merger Agreement). The Merger Agreement also includes customary termination provisions for both the Company and Parent, including, among others, the right of both parties to terminate for failure to consummate the Offer on or before February 14, 2027, being the 90 th day after the date of the Merger Agreement. The Company is required to pay Parent a termination fee of $2.4 million upon termination of the Merger Agreement in specified circumstances, including if the Company terminates the Merger Agreement to enter into a definitive agreement for a Superior Proposal (as defined in the Merger Agreement) or if Parent terminates the Merger Agreement following a Company Adverse Recommendation Change. The Termination Fee will also be payable if the Merger Agreement is terminated in certain other specified circumstances, a qualifying Takeover Proposal was publicly disclosed and not timely withdrawn before termination, and, within twelve months after termination, the Company enters into a definitive agreement fora qualifying Takeover Proposal that is later consummated, or consummates a qualifying Takeover Proposal. The representations and warranties of the Company contained in the Merger Agreement were made solely for the benefit of Parent and Purchaser and only for purposes of the Merger Agreement.
