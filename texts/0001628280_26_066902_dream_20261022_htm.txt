As previously disclosed in a Current Report on Form 8-K, on October 20, 2026, Dream Finders Homes, Inc., a Texas corporation (the “Company”), completed its previously announced acquisition (the “Transaction”) of Beazer Homes USA, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Beazer”), pursuant to the Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated as of August 6, 2026. Following the consummation of the Transaction, Beazer became a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company. On October 22, 2026, the Company announced that it is commencing private exchange offers (the “Exchange Offer”) and related consent solicitations (the “Consent Solicitation”) with respect to Beazer’s outstanding 7.500% Senior Notes due 2031 (the “2031 Beazer Notes”) and 8.000% Senior Notes due 2032 (the “2032 Beazer Notes” and, together with the 2031 Beazer Notes, the “Beazer Notes”). Pursuant to the terms and conditions set forth in an exchange and consent solicitation statement dated October 22, 2026 (the “Offering Memorandum”), the Company is offering to issue new notes of the Company (collectively, the “Dream Finders Notes”) in exchange for any and all of the $250 million aggregate principal amount of outstanding 2031 Beazer Notes and the $400 million aggregate principal amount of outstanding 2032 Beazer Notes held by holders eligible to participate in the Exchange Offer (the “Eligible Holders”). In connection with the Exchange Offer, the Company is soliciting consents from the Eligible Holders to certain proposed amendments to the Beazer Notes and the related indentures under which they were issued to eliminate substantially all of the restrictive covenants and events of default thereunder. The Exchange Offer and the Consent Solicitation are being made pursuant to the Offering Memorandum, copies of which will be made available to Eligible Holders. The Company’s obligation to accept and exchange the Beazer Notes validly tendered pursuant to the Exchange Offer is subject to certain conditions as set forth in the Offering Memorandum. A copy of the press release issued by the Company is attached as Exhibit 99.1 hereto and is incorporated by reference herein. The Dream Finders Notes have not been and will not be registered under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities Act”), or the securities laws of any other jurisdiction, and are being offered for exchange only to persons reasonably believed to be “qualified institutional buyers” (as defined in Rule 144A under the Securities Act) in reliance on the exemption from registration provided by Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act and outside the United States to persons other than U.S. persons (as defined in Rule 902 under the Securities Act) in reliance upon Regulation S under the Securities Act. This Current Report on Form 8-K does not constitute an offer to sell or a solicitation of an offer to buy any securities, and there will be no sale of any securities in any jurisdiction in which such offer, solicitation or sale would be unlawful.
