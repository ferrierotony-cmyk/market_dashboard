Learn more SPRING, Texas, November 16, 2026 --( BUSINESS WIRE )--ExxonMobil Holdings Corporation ("ExxonMobil") (NYSE: XOM ) today announces that its wholly owned subsidiaries Pioneer Natural Resources Company (" Pioneer ") and Exxon Mobil Corporation (" Exxon " and, together with Pioneer, the " Offerors " and each, an " Offeror "), have commenced cash tender offers (each, a " Tender Offer " and collectively, the " Tender Offers ") to purchase up to $3,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount (the " Maximum Waterfall Tender Amount ") of the following series of notes (collectively, the " Notes "): (i) Pioneer's 1.900% Senior Notes due 2030 (the " 2030 Pioneer Notes ") and 2.150% Senior Notes due 2031 (the " 2031 Pioneer Notes " and, together with the 2030 Pioneer Notes, the " Pioneer Notes "); and (ii) Exxon's 3.482% Notes due 2030 (the " 2030 Exxon Notes ") and 4.227% Notes due 2040 (the " 2040 Exxon Notes " and, together with the 2030 Exxon Notes, the " Exxon Notes "). Pioneer is offering to purchase only the Pioneer Notes, and Exxon is offering to purchase only the Exxon Notes. Holders will receive a price equal to the Total Consideration based on the applicable Reference Yield and the applicable Fixed Spread plus Accrued Interest (each as defined below). Each Tender Offer is being made upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase dated November 16, 2026 (the " Offer to Purchase "). Terms not defined in this announcement have the meanings given to them in the Offer to Purchase. The Offerors are offering to purchase the Notes for cash up to the Maximum Waterfall Tender Amount in aggregate principal amount. Subject to the Maximum Waterfall Tender Amount, the amount of a series of Notes that is purchased in the Tender Offers will be based on the order of priority (the " Acceptance Priority Level ") for such series, as set forth in the table below. Notes of a series may be subject to proration if the aggregate principal amount of the Notes of such series validly tendered would cause the Maximum Waterfall Tender Amount to be exceeded. The Maximum Waterfall Tender Amount applies to the Pioneer Notes and the Exxon Notes in the aggregate. The Offerors, acting jointly, reserve the right, but are not obligated, to increase or decrease the Maximum Waterfall Tender Amount, in their sole and absolute discretion, subject to applicable law. Notes purchased in the Tender Offers will be cancelled. No Tender Offer is conditioned on any minimum principal amount of Notes being tendered. The consummation of each Tender Offer is subject to, and conditioned upon, the satisfaction or waiver, where permitted, of the conditions discussed in the Offer to Purchase. Holders who desire to participate in any Tender Offer must validly tender their Notes at or prior to the applicable Expiration Date. Tenders of Notes may be validly withdrawn at any time on or prior to the applicable Expiration Date but tenders will thereafter be irrevocable, except in certain limited circumstances where additional withdrawal rights are required by law. The Pioneer Notes may be tendered only in principal amounts equal to the minimum denomination of $1,000 and integral multiples of $1,000 in excess thereof, and the Exxon Notes may be tendered only in principal amounts equal to the minimum denomination of $2,000 and integral multiples of $1,000 in excess thereof (as applicable, the " Authorized Denomination "). In the event proration is required, the applicable Offeror will multiply the principal amount of each valid tender of such series of Notes by the applicable proration factor and make appropriate downward adjustments to avoid purchases of Notes in principal amounts other than an Authorized Denomination, in order to determine the principal amount of such tender that will be accepted pursuant to the applicable Tender Offer. Upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase, holders of the Notes (" Holders ") who validly tender and who do not validly withdraw Notes at or prior to the applicable Expiration Date and whose Notes are accepted for purchase by the applicable Offeror (subject to the applicable Acceptance Priority Levels and to proration, if any) will receive the Total Consideration, plus Accrued Interest. The Total Consideration payable for the Notes will be a price per $1,000 principal amount of Notes, calculated with reference to the Settlement Date, that would reflect a yield to the applicable maturity date or par call date, as applicable, of such Notes equal to the sum of (i) the applicable Reference Yield determined at the Price Determination Time, plus (ii) the applicable Fixed Spread. The Total Consideration payable for each of the 2030 Pioneer Notes, the 2031 Pioneer Notes, the 2030 Exxon Notes and the 2040 Exxon Notes will be determined as set out in the calculation in Schedule A to the Offer to Purchase. The " Reference Yield " means the bid side yield to maturity, determined in accordance with market convention, of the applicable reference security for such series of Notes listed in the table above (the " Reference Security "), based on the bid price for the applicable Reference Security as reported on the applicable Bloomberg Reference Page listed in the table above at the Price Determination Time. The " Price Determination Time " is expected to be 2:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 20, 2026. In addition to the Total Consideration, Holders whose Notes are accepted for purchase will be paid the Accrued Interest on the Settlement Date. Interest will cease to accrue on the Settlement Date for all Notes accepted in any Tender Offer. The Settlement Date will promptly follow the applicable Expiration Date and is expected to be November 24, 2026, which is the second business day after the applicable Expiration Date, unless extended. The deadlines set by any such intermediary and the Depository Trust Company for the submission and withdrawal of tender instructions may be earlier than the relevant deadlines specified herein and in the Offer to Purchase. About ExxonMobil ExxonMobil, one of the largest publicly traded international energy and petrochemical companies, creates solutions that improve quality of life and meet society's evolving needs. The corporation's primary businesses - Upstream, Product Solutions and Low Carbon Solutions – provide products that enable modern life, including energy, chemicals, lubricants, and lower emissions technologies. ExxonMobil holds an industry-leading portfolio of resources, and is one of the largest integrated fuels, lubricants, and chemical companies in the world. ExxonMobil also owns and operates the largest CO2 pipeline network in the United States. In 2021, ExxonMobil announced Scope 1 and 2 greenhouse gas emission-reduction plans for 2030 for operated assets, compared to 2016 levels. The plans are to achieve a 20-30% reduction in corporate-wide greenhouse gas intensity; a 40-50% reduction in greenhouse gas intensity of upstream operations; a 70-80% reduction in corporate-wide methane intensity; and a 60-70% reduction in corporate-wide flaring intensity.
