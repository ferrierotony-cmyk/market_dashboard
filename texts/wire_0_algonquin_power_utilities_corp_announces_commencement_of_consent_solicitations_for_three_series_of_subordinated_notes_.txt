Announces Commencement of Consent Solicitations for Three Series of Subordinated Notes Algonquin Power & Utilities Corp. - Business Wire - Thu Nov 12, 5:30AM CST Algonquin Power & Utilities Corp. (TSX/NYSE: AQN) (“AQN”) today announced that it has commenced (i) solicitations of consents (the “U.S. Solicitations”) from holders of its outstanding 6.20% Fixed-to-Floating Subordinated Notes – Series 2019‑A due July 1, 2079 (NYSE: AQNB) (the “2019‑A Notes”) and its outstanding 4.750% Fixed-to-Fixed Reset Rate Junior Subordinated Notes Series 2022-B due January 18, 2082 (the “2022‑B Notes” and, together with the 2019‑A Notes, the “U.S. Notes”) to certain proposed amendments to the indentures governing the U.S. Notes, and (ii) a solicitation of written consents and proxies (the “Canadian Solicitation” and, together with the U.S. Solicitations, the “Consent Solicitations”) from holders of its outstanding 5.250% Fixed-to-Fixed Reset Rate Junior Subordinated Notes, Series 2022‑A, due January 18, 2082 (the “2022‑A Notes” and, together with the U.S. Notes, the “Notes”) to approve an extraordinary resolution authorizing certain proposed amendments to the indenture governing the 2022‑A Notes. The Proposed Amendments For each series of Notes, the proposed amendments (the “Proposed Amendments”) would delete in its entirety the “Automatic Conversion” article of the applicable supplemental indenture, pursuant to which the Notes of such series would automatically convert into a newly issued series of preferred shares of AQN upon the occurrence of certain bankruptcy and insolvency-related events in respect of AQN, together with the related definitions, the related covenant restricting AQN from issuing preferred shares ranking in priority to the preferred shares issuable upon such conversion, and other related conforming changes. If the Proposed Amendments become operative, holders would retain their Notes as subordinated indebtedness of AQN rather than receive preferred shares. The Proposed Amendments would not change the interest rate, interest payment dates, maturity date, redemption provisions, interest deferral provisions or subordination provisions of any series of Notes. The Automatic Conversion was included in the terms of the Notes at issuance in light of the methodology then applied by credit rating agencies in assigning equity credit to hybrid securities; following the rating agencies’ revised methodology for hybrid instruments, the Automatic Conversion no longer contributes to the credit ratings of, or the equity credit assigned to, the Notes, and AQN accordingly does not expect its removal to affect the instrument ratings of the Notes. In addition, to the extent AQN issues hybrid securities in the future, AQN intends to issue such securities without an automatic conversion feature and wishes for all of its hybrid securities, including the Notes, to rank pari passu with one another. U.S. Solicitations The U.S. Solicitations are being made solely on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in AQN’s consent solicitation statement dated November 12, 2026 (the “U.S. Statement”). Each U.S. Solicitation will expire at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 23, 2026, unless extended or earlier terminated by AQN in its sole discretion (such time and date, as it may be extended, the “Expiration Time”). Only holders of record of the U.S. Notes as of 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 10, 2026 (the “U.S. Record Date”) are eligible to deliver consents. Consents may be revoked at any time prior to the earlier of the time at which the supplemental indenture giving effect to the Proposed Amendments with respect to the applicable series is executed (the “Effective Time”) and the Expiration Time, but not thereafter. Beneficial owners of U.S. Notes held through a DTC participant must instruct their broker or other custodian to deliver a consent on their behalf, and such custodians may set deadlines earlier than the Expiration Time. Consents must be delivered through The Depository Trust Company’s Automated Tender Offer Program. The delivery of a consent will not affect a holder’s right to sell or transfer the Notes. Only holders as of the U.S. Record Date, or their duly designated proxies, including, for the purposes of the Consent Solicitations, DTC participants, may deliver a consent. A consent duly delivered pursuant to the terms of the U.S. Statement will bind the holder and any subsequent registered holder or transferee of the U.S. Notes to which such consent relates. Adoption of the Proposed Amendments with respect to a series of U.S. Notes requires the consent of holders of not less than a majority of the outstanding principal amount of such series, voting separately (the “Requisite Consents”). The U.S. Solicitation for each series of U.S. Notes is independent of the U.S. Solicitation for the other series and of the Canadian Solicitation, and receipt of the Requisite Consents with respect to one series is not a condition to the completion of the U.S. Solicitation with respect to the other series. Subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions described in the U.S. Statement, including receipt of the Requisite Consents for the applicable series, AQN expects to pay, promptly following the Expiration Time, to each holder of U.S. Notes as of the U.S. Record Date who has validly delivered (and not validly revoked) a consent prior to the earlier of the Effective Time and the Expiration Time a consent payment as described in the table above (the “Consent Payment”). The Proposed Amendments with respect to a series of U.S. Notes will become effective upon execution of the applicable supplemental indenture but will not become operative until the Consent Payment for such series has been paid. No Consent Payment will be made with respect to a series of U.S. Notes if the Requisite Consents for such series are not received prior to the Expiration Time, if the applicable supplemental indenture is not executed or does not become effective, or if any of the other conditions described in the U.S. Statement are not satisfied or waived. If the Proposed Amendments become operative with respect to a series of U.S. Notes, they will bind all holders of such series, whether or not they consented; non-consenting holders will not receive the Consent Payment. AQN may terminate, extend or amend any U.S. Solicitation at any time prior to the Effective Time as described in the U.S. Statement. AQN has retained RBC Capital Markets, LLC as Solicitation Agent and D.F. King & Co., Inc. as Information Agent and Tabulation Agent for the U.S. Solicitations. Under the indenture governing the 2022-A Notes, the Proposed Amendments may be approved by an extraordinary resolution of holders (the “Extraordinary Resolution”) passed either by an instrument in writing signed by holders of not less than 66⅔% of the aggregate principal amount of the outstanding 2022-A Notes or at a meeting of holders. AQN is soliciting written consents to the Extraordinary Resolution and, concurrently, proxies for a special meeting of holders of 2022-A Notes to be held at 12:00 p.m., Toronto time, on December 11, 2026, at AQN’s offices at 354 Davis Road, Suite 100, Oakville, Ontario (the “Meeting”). The Meeting will be held only if the Extraordinary Resolution is not passed by written consent. Only holders of 2022-A Notes as of the close of business on November 10, 2026 (the “Canadian Record Date”) are entitled to deliver consents and proxies and to vote at the Meeting, if held. The deadline for delivery of consents for purposes of the written consent solicitation is 5:00 p.m., Toronto time, on November 23, 2026 (the “Written Consent Deadline”) unless such deadline is extended. If holders of not less than 66⅔% of the aggregate principal amount of the outstanding 2022-A Notes have delivered valid consents in favour of the Extraordinary Resolution by the Written Consent Deadline (and have not validly revoked such responses), the Extraordinary Resolution will be passed by written consent and the Meeting will be canceled, and AQN will announce the results by news release. If the written consent threshold is not met, the Meeting will proceed and the deadline for delivery of proxies will be 12:00 p.m., Toronto time, on December 9, 2026 (the “Proxy Cut-Off Time”) unless such deadline is extended or the Meeting is adjourned or postponed. At the Meeting, the Extraordinary Resolution must be passed by holders of not less than 66⅔% of the principal amount of 2022‑A Notes represented and voted at the Meeting (at which holders of more than 50% of the outstanding principal amount will constitute a quorum), subject to adjournment as described in the Canadian Statement. If the Extraordinary Resolution is approved, whether by written consent or at the Meeting, and AQN, at its option, and the trustee execute the supplemental indenture giving effect to the Proposed Amendments, AQN will pay a fee as described in the table above (the “Fee”) to each holder of 2022-A Notes as of the Canadian Record Date who responded to the Canadian Solicitation by the Written Consent Deadline, whether by consenting to or withholding consent from, or by voting for or against, the Extraordinary Resolution, and who did not validly revoke such instructions. Holders who respond after the Written Consent Deadline, or revoke their consent or voting instructions to the Proposed Amendments by the Written Consent Deadline, will not be eligible to receive the Fee. No Fee will be paid if the Extraordinary Resolution is not approved or if the supplemental indenture does not become effective. If approved and implemented, the Proposed Amendments will be binding on all holders of 2022-A Notes, whether or not they responded. The 2022-A Notes are held in book-entry form through CDS, and beneficial holders must instruct their CDS participant to make an election through CDSX in sufficient time. Brokers, dealers or other intermediaries may set deadlines for the return of written consents and proxies from beneficial holders of Notes that are in advance of the Written Consent Deadline and the Proxy Cut-Off Time. The Canadian Solicitation and the U.S. Solicitations are not conditioned on one another. AQN reserves the right to terminate, extend or modify the Canadian Solicitation, to cancel, postpone or adjourn the Meeting and, notwithstanding approval of the Extraordinary Resolution, to elect not to enter into the supplemental indenture, in each case as described in the Canadian Statement.
