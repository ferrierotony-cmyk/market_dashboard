DoubleU Games beneficially owns 1,661,191 common shares, par value KRW 10,000 per share, of the Company (the “Common Shares”), representing approximately 67.05% of the outstanding Common Shares. All of the remaining Common Shares are held in the form of American Depositary Shares (the “ADSs”), with each ADS representing one-twentieth (1/20) of one Common Share, which are listed on the Nasdaq Global Select Market (“Nasdaq”) under the symbol “DDI.” The board of directors of the Company (the “Board”), following the unanimous recommendation of the Special Committee (as defined below), has unanimously approved the Transaction Agreement and the transactions contemplated thereby (the “Transactions”). Pursuant to the Transaction Agreement, and upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth therein, DoubleU Games has agreed to commence, as promptly as practicable and in any event no later than seven (7) business days after the date of the Transaction Agreement, a cash tender offer (the “Offer”) to purchase all of the outstanding ADSs not owned by DoubleU Games or its affiliates at a price of US$16.25 per ADS (the “Final Offer Price”), net to the seller in cash, without interest and less any applicable withholding taxes. Common Shares held directly are not subject to the Offer. The Offer will remain open for at least twenty (20) business days following its commencement and may be extended in accordance with the Transaction Agreement, but not beyond the earlier of the termination of the Transaction Agreement and March 10, 2027 (the “End Date”). Under the Transaction Agreement, the obligation of DoubleU Games to accept for payment and pay for ADSs tendered in the Offer is subject to customary conditions, including, among others, (i) the condition that, immediately following the acceptance for payment of the ADSs validly tendered and not validly withdrawn, DoubleU Games would own at least 95% of the total issued and outstanding Common Shares (the “Minimum Tender Condition”), which DoubleU Games, after reasonably determining that the Minimum Tender Condition is not reasonably expected to be satisfied, consulting in good faith with the Special Committee and obtaining the prior written consent of the Company (given by the Board following the recommendation of the Special Committee), which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed, may reduce, but not waive, to a percentage not lower than 90%; (ii) the condition that more than 50% of the ADSs held by holders of ADSs or Common Shares other than DoubleU Games, its affiliates and the directors and officers of DoubleU Games and the Company (the “Unaffiliated Holders”) be validly tendered and not validly withdrawn prior to the expiration of the Offer (the “Majority-of-the-Minority Condition”), which may not be waived by any party; (iii) the absence of any legal restraint enjoining the Offer or the Post-Offer Transaction (as defined below); (iv) the accuracy of the Company’s representations and warranties (subject to specified materiality standards) and the absence of an uncured material breach of the Transaction Agreement; (v) the absence of a Material Adverse Effect (as defined in the Transaction Agreement) on the Company; (vi) the Company Recommendation (as defined below) having been made and not withdrawn or adversely modified (which condition DoubleU Games may waive in its sole discretion); and (vii) the Transaction Agreement not having been terminated. The Offer is not subject to any financing condition, and DoubleU Games has represented that it has, or prior to the acceptance of ADSs for payment will have, funds sufficient, from cash on hand and committed debt financing, to pay for all ADSs subject to the Offer and to fund the Post-Offer Transaction in full as and when such obligations are due and payable. Page 2 Following the completion of the Offer, DoubleU Games has agreed to acquire, as promptly as practicable, all Common Shares (including Common Shares represented by ADSs) not acquired in the Offer through a second-step transaction under the Korean Commercial Act (the “Post-Offer Transaction”), which, depending on DoubleU Games’ resulting ownership, may be effected as a comprehensive share exchange (including a small-scale, simplified or general comprehensive share exchange) or a squeeze-out by a controlling-shareholder holding 95% or more together with its subsidiaries, with the form selected by DoubleU Games after consulting in good faith with the Special Committee. The consideration in the Post-Offer Transaction will be equivalent to the Final Offer Price (US$325.00 per Common Share, based on the Final Offer Price and the ratio of twenty (20) ADSs to one (1) Common Share), and the amount receivable per ADS will not, after deduction of ADS cancellation fees and other depositary charges, be less than the Final Offer Price. Following the completion of the Transactions, the Company will become a wholly owned subsidiary of DoubleU Games, and DoubleU Games intends to cause the ADSs to be delisted from Nasdaq and deregistered under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), and the Company’s reporting obligations under the Exchange Act to be terminated. The Transaction Agreement contains representations, warranties and covenants of the Company and DoubleU Games that are customary for a transaction of this nature, including covenants regarding the conduct of the Company’s business between the date of the Transaction Agreement and the acceptance of ADSs for payment in the Offer. DoubleU Games has also agreed, among other things, that (i) none of a failure of the Board to make or maintain the Company Recommendation, a failure of the Special Committee to recommend that the Board make or maintain the Company Recommendation or any request by the Special Committee or the Board for an increase in the Final Offer Price or other negotiation by the Board or the Special Committee regarding the Final Offer Price or other terms of the Offer or the Transactions will result in any retributive action by DoubleU Games against the Company, the Special Committee, the Board or any member, employee or representative thereof, (ii) while the Transaction Agreement remains in effect and subject to limited exceptions, DoubleU Games will not sell, transfer or otherwise dispose of its Common Shares to any person or support, or vote in favor of, any alternative transaction involving the Company proposed by any third party unless such transaction has been recommended by the Special Committee and the Board, and (iii) prior to completion of the Post-Offer Transaction and subject to limited exceptions, DoubleU Games will not cause, request, propose or vote in favor of the delisting of the ADSs from Nasdaq, the termination of the deposit agreement for the ADSs or the deregistration of the ADSs or Common Shares under the Exchange Act without the prior written consent of the Company acting through the Board (following the recommendation of or consultation with the Special Committee, as applicable). The Transaction Agreement may be terminated prior to the acceptance of ADSs for payment under certain circumstances, including by mutual consent, by either party if the acceptance of ADSs for payment has not occurred by the End Date, or if, as of the final expiration of the Offer, DoubleU Games and its affiliates would own less than 90% of the Common Shares or the Majority-of-the-Minority Condition has not been satisfied, upon a legal restraint on the Transactions becoming final and non-appealable or upon certain material breaches by the other party that are not cured within the applicable cure period, and by DoubleU Games upon a change in the Company Recommendation. The Board established a special committee composed solely of independent and disinterested directors (the “Special Committee”), which, advised by its own independent legal counsel and financial advisor, negotiated the price and other terms of the Transaction Agreement with DoubleU Games. The Special Committee unanimously (i) determined that the Transaction Agreement and the Transactions, including the Offer and the Post-Offer Transaction, are advisable, fair to, and in the best interests of, the Company and its shareholders, including the Unaffiliated Holders, and (ii) recommended that the Board approve the Transaction Agreement and the Transactions. Following such recommendation of the Special Committee and after consultation with its advisors and consideration of the fairness opinion of Kroll (as defined below), the Board unanimously (i) determined that the Transaction Agreement and the Transactions, including the Offer, are advisable, fair to, and in the best interests of, the Company and its shareholders, including the Unaffiliated Holders; (ii) approved the Transaction Agreement and the Transactions, including the Offer; and (iii) recommended that the Unaffiliated Holders accept the Offer and tender their ADSs pursuant to the Offer (such recommendation of the Board, the “Company Recommendation”). Page 3 The Special Committee received, and provided to the Board, the written opinion of Kroll, LLC, operating through its Duff & Phelps Opinions Practice (“Kroll”), its independent financial advisor, to the effect that, as of the date of such opinion and based upon and subject to the assumptions, qualifications and limitations set forth therein, the Final Offer Price to be received by the Unaffiliated Holders is fair, from a financial point of view, to such holders (without giving effect to any impact of the Transactions on any particular holder of Common Shares or ADSs other than in its capacity as a holder of ADSs). No termination fee, break-up fee or similar payment is payable by either party upon or in connection with the termination of the Transaction Agreement, and nothing in the Transaction Agreement restricts the Special Committee or the Board from receiving, considering or responding to any unsolicited proposal. Additionally, the Board, following the recommendation of the Special Committee, may make a change to the Company Recommendation if each of the Special Committee and the Board determines in good faith, after consultation with outside legal counsel, that the failure to do so would reasonably be expected to be inconsistent with its fiduciary duties under applicable law, subject to at least five (5) business days’ prior written notice to DoubleU Games (during which DoubleU Games may propose amendments to the terms of the Transaction Agreement) and the other terms of the Transaction Agreement.
