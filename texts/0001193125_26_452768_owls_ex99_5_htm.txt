The Maker shall deliver seventy-five percent (75%), but not less than seventy-five percent (75%), of the COC Repayment Price to the Holder immediately prior to or concurrently with the consummation of the Change of Control. Upon the Holder’s receipt of the seventy-five percent (75%) of the COC Repayment Price in full, the Maker and the Holder agree that (i) the Holder shall have ten (10) days following its receipt of seventy-five percent (75%) of the COC Repayment Price in full (the “ COC Election Period ”) to convert twenty-five percent (25%) of the COC Repayment Price worth of the Outstanding Principal Amount of this Note, in whole or in part, at the lesser of the Repayment Share Price or the Conversion Price, in accordance with the provisions of Article 3, and (ii) immediately at the end of the COC Election Period, the Maker shall deliver the balance of the COC Repayment Price that shall not have been previously converted by the Holder (if any) in full, and upon the Holder’s receipt thereof, this Note shall be deemed cancelled and all Outstanding Principal Amount and other amounts owing hereunder shall be deemed fully satisfied, and the Holder shall have no further rights to convert any such remaining portion of the Outstanding Principal Amount or otherwise exercise any conversion rights under this Note; provided , however , that the Holder shall have the same COC Election Period to exercise the outstanding Warrant, in whole or in part.
