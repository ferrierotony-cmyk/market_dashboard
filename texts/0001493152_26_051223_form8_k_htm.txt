Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement. On November 5, 2026, Agassi Sports Entertainment Corp. (the “ Company ”, “ we ” and “ us ”), entered into separate Media Value Letter Agreements (the “ Media Value Agreements ”) with each of Sinclair Television Group, Inc. (“ Sinclair ”) and The Tennis Channel, LLC (the “ Tennis Channel ”, and together with Sinclair, the “ Media Value Providers ”), discussed in greater detail under Item 8.01 , below. In consideration for Sinclair and Tennis Channel providing an aggregate of $1.5 million of Media Value (as defined in Item 8.01 ) to the Company, we agreed to issue the Media Value Providers an aggregate of 300,000 shares of restricted common stock (60,000 shares to Sinclair and 240,000 shares to the Tennis Channel) (the “ Media Value Shares ”), with an agreed upon value of $5.00 per share, pursuant to separate Media Value Share Issuance Agreements entered into between each of the Media Value Providers and the Company on November 5, 2026 (the “ Equity Agreement ”). The Equity Agreements confirmed the $5.00 per share value of the Media Value Shares; provide that the Media Value Shares shall not be subject to forfeiture, cancellation, repurchase, redemption, set-off or clawback by the Company for any reason; include customary representations and warranties of the Company and the Media Value Providers; and provide for mutual indemnification rights for breaches of the Equity Agreements by either party. The Media Value Shares are subject to lock-up restrictions prohibiting each Media Value Provider from transferring any Media Value Shares during the six months following the date of the Equity Agreements. Thereafter, half of the Media Value Shares issued to each Media Value Provider, subject to equitable adjustment for stock dividends, stock splits and similar transactions, remain subject to the lock-up until the first anniversary of the date of the agreements, while the remaining Media Value Shares are released from the lock-up. These restrictions also apply to certain securities issued in respect of the Media Value Shares and prohibit pledges, short sales, hedging and other transactions that transfer the economic consequences of ownership. The lock-up permits customary transfers without consideration to affiliates and direct equity holders, subject to prior notice, the transferee’s written agreement to the applicable restrictions and compliance with applicable securities laws. Exceptions also apply to transfers in board-approved tender offers, exchange offers, mergers or change of control transactions, registered offerings in which the Media Value Provider includes its securities pursuant to the applicable Equity Agreement, and transfers required by law or court order. The lock-up terminates automatically if the Company materially breaches the applicable agreement and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice from the Media Value Provider. The Equity Agreements grant the Media Value Providers piggyback registration rights to include the resale of the Media Value Shares in certain registration statements filed by the Company for its own account or for other securityholders, subject to timely election, customary information requirements, specified exceptions and limitations, and customary underwriter, placement agent and Securities and Exchange Commission cutbacks. These piggyback rights expire on the earlier of five years after the date of the Equity Agreements and the date all Media Value Shares may be sold without restriction or limitation, and without a restrictive legend, under Rule 144. In addition, if, at any time after six months following the date of the Equity Agreements, the Media Value Shares cannot be sold without restriction or limitation under Rule 144, the Media Value Provider may request that the Company register their resale. Upon such request, the Company must file a resale registration statement within 45 days, or 30 days if the Company is eligible to use Form S-3 or another short-form registration statement, use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain effectiveness thereof within 90 days after the request, and maintain effectiveness thereof until all covered securities have been sold or may be sold without restriction or limitation under Rule 144. The Company will bear the registration expenses of such filing, subject to specified exclusions, and the parties have agreed to customary mutual indemnification provisions. If the Company fails to include Media Value Shares required to be included in a registration statement or fails to meet the applicable filing or effectiveness deadlines, subject to the Equity Agreement’s exceptions and limitations, a Media Value Provider may, upon written notice, suspend its obligations under the Media Value Agreement, including providing Media Value and procuring, placing or fulfilling Ad Buys, without breaching either agreement. The suspension may continue until the affected shares are covered by an effective resale registration statement or the Company, at its expense, obtains an appropriate Rule 144 resale opinion. The Media Value Provider must immediately resume performance upon satisfaction of either condition, and any suspension will not extend the term of the Media Value Agreement. We also agreed that, without the approval of the Media Value Providers, we would not grant any registration rights that conflict with, or rank senior to, the registration rights of the Media Value Providers, until such time as the Media Value Shares have been registered under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “ Securities Act ”), unless we provide the same rights to the Media Value Providers.
