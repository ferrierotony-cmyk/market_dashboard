Fain (315) 210-6110 pfain@ulbi.com Investor Relations Contact: Alliance Advisors IR Jody Burfening (212) 838-3777 jburfening@allianceadvisors.com EnerSys to Acquire Ultralife Corporation in a $320 Million All-Cash Transaction Newark, N.Y. October 27, 2026 -- Ultralife Corporation (NASDAQ: ULBI) today announced that it has entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (“merger agreement”) with EnerSys (NYSE: ENS) under which upon closing EnerSys will acquire Ultralife Corporation in an all-cash transaction valued at approximately $320 million, including the assumption of net debt. Under the terms of the merger agreement, which has been unanimously approved by the Boards of Directors of both companies, Ultralife Corporation shareholders will receive $16.00 in cash for each share of Ultralife Corporation common stock they own. The purchase price represents a premium of 166% to Ultralife Corporation’s closing stock price on October 26, 2026. Strategic Benefits of the Transaction: Ultralife is highly complementary to EnerSys and a combination creates a compelling platform for continued innovation, customer support and growth. Together, we believe EnerSys and Ultralife would be well positioned to: ● Establish a unique portfolio of integrated, mission-critical power solutions by combining complementary products, engineering capabilities and application-specific design expertise; ● Increase scale and broaden exposure to the large and rapidly growing defense market with capabilities across mission-critical applications in munitions, soldier power, drones, counter drones, and autonomous systems accelerating focused growth; ● Expand EnerSys’ advanced defense power technology portfolio with the addition of Ultralife’s differentiated multi-chemistry capabilities, purpose-built advanced manufacturing capacity with existing security and environmental permits, and operational expertise to scale production of EnerSys’ proprietary liquid reserve battery technology; and ● Deepen customer relationships by creating a broader, Foreign Entity of Concern (FEOC)-compliant product portfolio for U.S. Department of War and allied country customers. Management Commentary: "Combining with EnerSys is an exciting next step that will allow us to unlock value and growth opportunities for both companies, while delivering substantial and certain cash value to Ultralife shareholders,” said Michael E. Manna, President and Chief Executive Officer of Ultralife Corporation. “This transaction is a testament to the hard work and dedication of our entire organization, and now offers even greater opportunities for our valued employees. We look forward to partnering with the EnerSys team with the combined scale, resources, and shared vision to accelerate innovation and better serve our global customer base." “This acquisition is a compelling strategic fit for EnerSys and directly advances our strategy to build a broader, more differentiated defense power platform,” said Shawn O'Connell, President and Chief Executive Officer of EnerSys. “Building on the success of our recent acquisitions, Ultralife expands our portfolio of advanced, reliable, high-performance power solutions. Together, we will be better positioned to support the U.S. Department of War (DoW) and allied forces with leading-edge, cost-effective technologies for the increasingly electrified battlefield of the future. We know the Ultralife business and its team well, and are confident that our shared commitment to engineering, problem solving, and customer focus will create a strong cultural fit. We look forward to combining our strengths to accelerate growth, enhance performance, and deliver greater value to our customers and shareholders.” Transaction Details and Timing: EnerSys intends to finance the transaction through cash on hand and, accordingly, is not subject to a financing contingency. The transaction has been unanimously approved by the Boards of Directors of both companies. Under the terms of the merger agreement, EnerSys, through a subsidiary, will commence a tender offer to acquire all of the outstanding shares of Ultralife common stock at a price of $16.00 per share in cash at closing, representing a total enterprise value of approximately $320 million. The transaction is subject to customary closing conditions, including the tender of at least a majority of the outstanding shares of Ultralife’s common stock and receipt of applicable regulatory approvals, and is expected to close in the first quarter of calendar year 2027. Upon completion of the transaction, Ultralife Corporation’s common stock will no longer be listed on any public market. About Ultralife Corporation Ultralife is a global leader in mission-critical battery, power, communications and compute solutions for the most demanding applications in government/defense, medical, oil & gas and industrial markets across the globe. Headquartered in Newark, New York, the Company's business segments include Battery & Energy Products and Communications Systems. Ultralife has operations in North America, Europe and Asia. For more information, visit www.ultralifecorporation.com. About EnerSys EnerSys is a global leader in stored energy solutions helping industrial, infrastructure and defense customers address critical power and operational needs with batteries, chargers and other power equipment. The company delivers integrated solutions that combine energy storage technologies, power electronics, software-enabled intelligence, technical expertise and comprehensive global customer support. EnerSys supports customers across communications networks, data centers, energy infrastructure, material handling, transportation, aerospace and defense — including applications where power continuity is essential. Serving customers in more than 100 countries, EnerSys helps organizations manage energy more reliably, efficiently and intelligently in complex operating environments where uptime, safety and resilience matter.
