Effective Time . This Supplemental Indenture shall become effective and binding upon the Issuer and the Trustees at the time at which it has been executed and delivered by each of the Issuer and the Trustees (the “ Effective Time ”). Notwithstanding Section 9.4 of the Original Indenture, from the Effective Time until the Operative Date, the Proposed Amendments shall not be operative and the Second Supplemental Indenture and the Notes shall not be modified hereby. SECTION 3.2. Operative Date . (a) The Proposed Amendments shall become operative on the date (the “ Operative Date ”) on which the Issuer pays, or causes to be paid, directly or indirectly, to DTC the aggregate Consent Payment due to each Holder of Notes who validly delivered (and did not validly revoke) a Consent prior to the earlier of the Effective Time and the Expiration Time, in accordance with the Consent Solicitation Statement. (b) Promptly following the Operative Date, the Issuer shall deliver to the Trustees written notice confirming that the Consent Payment has been paid in accordance with the Consent Solicitation Statement and specifying the Operative Date. The Trustees shall be entitled to rely conclusively on such notice, without further inquiry or investigation, as to the occurrence and the date of the Operative Date. Unless and until the Trustees have received such notice, the Trustees shall be entitled to assume that the Operative Date has not occurred. (c) Prior to the Operative Date, the Second Supplemental Indenture and the Notes (including Article 4 and Article 8 of the Second Supplemental Indenture) shall continue in full force and effect without giving effect to the Proposed Amendments. SECTION 3.3. Failure to Become Operative . If, at any time prior to the Operative Date, the Issuer notifies the Trustees in writing that the Consent Solicitation has been terminated or that the Consent Payment will not be paid then this Supplemental Indenture shall terminate automatically and be of no further force or effect (other than this Section 3.3 and Article 4, which shall survive), the Proposed Amendments shall not become operative, and the Second Supplemental Indenture and the Notes shall continue in full force and effect as if this Supplemental Indenture had not been executed. SECTION 3.4. Effect on Holders . From and after the Operative Date, in accordance with Section 9.4 of the Original Indenture, the Second Supplemental Indenture shall be modified in accordance herewith, this Supplemental Indenture shall form a part of the Indenture for all purposes and every Holder of Notes theretofore or thereafter authenticated and delivered under the Indenture shall be bound hereby, whether or not such Holder delivered a Consent. SECTION 3.5. Notation on Notes .
