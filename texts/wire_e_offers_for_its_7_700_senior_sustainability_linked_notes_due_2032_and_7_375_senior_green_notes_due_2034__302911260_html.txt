Properties"), today announced (i) the pricing terms of its four separate private offers to exchange (collectively, the "Exchange Offers") any and all of its outstanding series of notes listed in the tables below (collectively, the "Old Notes") for newly issued 6.793% Senior Sustainability-Linked Notes due 2036 (the "New 2036 Notes") or 7.731% Senior Sustainability-Linked Notes due 2056 (the "New 2056 Notes" and, together with the New 2036 Notes, the "New Notes"), as applicable, (ii) the results of the Exchange Offers as of 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on October 15, 2026, (iii) the amendment of the Exchange Offers relating to the Pool 1 Notes (as defined in the Exchange Offer Memorandum (as defined below)) to increase the Total Consideration (as defined in the Exchange Offer Memorandum) payable in respect of each series of Pool 1 Notes, and the extension of the Expiration Date with respect to each such Exchange Offers relating to the Pool 1 Notes, and (iv) the expiration of, and the Trust's acceptance of all Old Notes validly tendered in, the Exchange Offers relating to the Pool 2 Notes (as defined in the Exchange Offer Memorandum). The Exchange Offers were initially made upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Exchange Offer Memorandum dated October 8, 2026 (as supplemented by the press release dated October 13, 2026, and as further supplemented by this press release, the "Exchange Offer Memorandum") and the eligibility certification (the "Eligibility Letter"). Capitalized terms used but not defined herein have the meanings assigned to them in the Exchange Offer Memorandum. The Trust also announced today that it has amended the Exchange Offers with respect to the Pool 1 Notes to (i) increase the Total Consideration payable in respect of each series of Pool 1 Notes, as set forth in the table above (the "Revised Total Consideration"), and (ii) extend the Expiration Date with respect to each Exchange Offer relating to the Pool 1 Notes from 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on October 15, 2026, to 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on October 20, 2026, unless further extended or earlier terminated by the Trust with respect to any such Exchange Offer (such date and time with respect to an Exchange Offer relating to the Pool 1 Notes, as the same may be further extended with respect to such Exchange Offer, the "Pool 1 Expiration Date"), in order to provide Eligible Holders with additional time to consider and participate in the Exchange Offers relating to the Pool 1 Notes at the increased Total Consideration. Eligible Holders whose Pool 1 Notes are validly tendered (and not validly withdrawn) at or prior to the Pool 1 Expiration Date and accepted for exchange by the Trust will receive the applicable Revised Total Consideration set forth in the table above, including Eligible Holders that tendered Pool 1 Notes prior to the date of this press release. The New Notes Coupon for the New 2036 Notes will remain 6.793% notwithstanding the extension of the Exchange Offers relating to the Pool 1 Notes and the increase in the Total Consideration payable in respect of the Pool 1 Notes. The Revised Total Consideration is a fixed amount and will not be recalculated notwithstanding the extension of the Pool 1 Expiration Date. The New 2036 Notes will mature on October 22, 2036 and will bear interest from and including the Pool 1 Settlement Date (as defined below). Consequently, the Withdrawal Deadline with respect to the Pool 1 Notes has been extended to 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on October 20, 2026, unless further extended by the Trust with respect to any such Exchange Offer (such date and time with respect to an Exchange Offer relating to the Pool 1 Notes, as the same may be further extended with respect to such Exchange Offer, the "Pool 1 Withdrawal Deadline"). As of the date hereof, no Pool 1 Notes have been accepted for exchange by the Trust. In connection with the new settlement date for the New 2036 Notes expected to be October 22, 2026 (as further described under "Settlement" below) and the new maturity date on October 22, 2036, the interest payment dates for the New 2036 Notes are also adjusted to April 22 and October 22 of each year, commencing on April 22, 2027, and the Interest Rate Step-Up Date of the New 2036 Notes is adjusted to October 22, 2032. Except as described in this press release, all other terms and conditions of the Exchange Offers relating to the Pool 1 Notes remain unchanged. Consummation of each Exchange Offer relating to the Pool 1 Notes remains subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions described in the Exchange Offer Memorandum (see the "Conditions to the Exchange Offers" section in the Exchange Offer Memorandum), including the applicable Minimum Issuance Condition. The Trust may, in its sole discretion, (i) further extend, amend, terminate or withdraw any Exchange Offer relating to the Pool 1 Notes and (ii) further increase or otherwise modify the Total Consideration payable in respect of any series of Pool 1 Notes. In addition, the Trust reserves the right to terminate or withdraw any Exchange Offer relating to the Pool 1 Notes at any time and for any reason before any Pool 1 Notes are accepted for exchange, including if any of the conditions described under "Conditions to the Exchange Offers" in the Exchange Offer Memorandum are not satisfied. There can be no assurance that the Trust will further increase the Total Consideration payable in respect of any series of Pool 1 Notes or further extend the Pool 1 Expiration Date or Pool 1 Withdrawal Deadline. Expiration and Acceptance of the Pool 2 Notes All the terms and conditions of the Exchange Offers relating to the Pool 2 Notes remain unchanged. The Exchange Offers with respect to the Pool 2 Notes expired at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on October 15, 2026, and have not been extended. The Trust has accepted for exchange all Pool 2 Notes validly tendered (and not validly withdrawn) at or prior to the Pool 2 Expiration Date, consisting of US$241,620,000 aggregate principal amount of the 2044 Notes and US$311,085,000 aggregate principal amount of the 2050 Notes, representing US$552,705,000 principal amount of Pool 2 Notes in the aggregate, and will issue US$498,600,469 aggregate principal amount of New 2056 Notes in exchange therefor. All conditions to the Exchange Offers relating to the Pool 2 Notes, including the applicable Minimum Issuance Condition, have been satisfied or waived. The Withdrawal Deadline with respect to the Pool 2 Notes expired at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on October 15, 2026, and Pool 2 Notes validly tendered pursuant to the Exchange Offers may no longer be withdrawn, unless otherwise required by applicable law. The Trust further intends to commence, on or about Monday, October 19, 2026, a new offer to exchange any and all of the Pool 2 Notes that were not tendered for exchange in the Exchange Offers and that will remain outstanding following the consummation of the Exchange Offers for the Pool 2 Notes for New 2056 Notes on the same terms as the Exchange Offers relating to the Pool 2 Notes that expired on October 15, 2026, subject to market and other conditions. Any such offering would be made only to holders eligible to participate under the eligibility and transfer restrictions applicable to the Exchange Offers. It is expected that such new offer for such outstanding Pool 2 Notes will be settled on or around October 27, 2026, unless extended. There can be no assurance as to whether or when any such offering will be announced, commenced or completed, or as to the terms of any such offering. This press release does not constitute an offer to exchange, sell, or the solicitation of an offer to buy, any securities in any such offering, and nothing herein shall constitute an amendment or supplement to the terms of any such offering. Settlement In addition to the Total Consideration or Revised Total Consideration, as applicable, Eligible Holders whose Old Notes are validly tendered (and not validly withdrawn) and accepted by the Trust for exchange pursuant to the Exchange Offers will also receive the applicable accrued and unpaid interest (rounded to the nearest cent US$0.01) from and including the last interest payment date of the applicable series of Old Notes to, but not including, the applicable Settlement Date (such payment, the "Accrued Interest Payment"). The New Notes will be issued, and the Accrued Interest Payment will be paid in cash, on the applicable Settlement Date. With respect to the Pool 2 Notes, the Settlement Date is expected to be October 20, 2026, the third business day after the Pool 2 Expiration Date, and the Accrued Interest Payment will be US$15.44 per US$1,000 principal amount of 2044 Notes and US$16.86 per US$1,000 principal amount of 2050 Notes. With respect to the Pool 1 Notes, the Settlement Date is expected to occur promptly following the Pool 1 Expiration Date and is expected to be October 22, 2026, the second business day after the Pool 1 Expiration Date, unless the Pool 1 Expiration Date is further extended with respect to any Exchange Offer relating to the Pool 1 Notes (the "Pool 1 Settlement Date"), and the Accrued Interest Payment is expected to be US$19.04 per US$1,000 principal amount of 2032 Notes and US$14.14 per US$1,000 principal amount of 2034 Notes.
