ARRANGEMENT AGREEMENT THIS ARRANGEMENT AGREEMENT dated November 2, 2026, BETWEEN: ORGANIGRAM GLOBAL INC., a corporation existing under the laws of Canada (“Purchaser”) - and - AURORA CANNABIS INC., a corporation existing under the laws of the Province of British Columbia (“Aurora”) WHEREAS: A. Purchaser and Aurora wish to propose an arrangement involving the acquisition by Purchaser of all of the issued and outstanding Aurora Shares in exchange for the Consideration, consisting of a combination of cash and Purchaser Shares; B. The Parties intend to carry out the transactions contemplated herein by way of an arrangement under the provisions of the BCBCA; C. Upon the effectiveness of the Arrangement, Aurora Shareholders will be entitled to receive, for each Aurora Share held, $3.75 in cash and 3.0431 Purchaser Shares, subject to the elections, maximum amounts and pro rating as set forth in the Plan of Arrangement; D. Purchaser has entered into a voting and support agreement with each of the directors of Aurora and each of the Aurora Executives, pursuant to which, among other things, such directors and Aurora Executives have agreed to vote all of the Aurora Shares held by them in favour of the Arrangement Resolution, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in such agreement; E. Aurora has entered into a voting and support agreement with the Purchaser Significant Shareholders, each of the directors of Purchaser and each of the Purchaser Executives, pursuant to which, among other things, the Purchaser Significant Shareholders, such directors and Purchaser Executives have each agreed to vote all of the Purchaser Shares held by them in favour of the Purchaser Resolution, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in such agreement; and F. The Parties have entered into this Agreement to provide for the matters referred to in the foregoing recitals and for other matters relating to such arrangements.
