Cloud Software Group Announces Amendment of Offer to Purchase for Cash up to $2.0 Billion Aggregate Principal Amount of its 9.00% Senior Secured Second Lien Notes due 2029 PR Newswire November 3, 2026 5 min read FORT LAUDERDALE, Fla., Nov. 3, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Cloud Software Group Holdings, Inc. ("Cloud Software Group" or the "Company") today announced that it is amending its previously announced cash tender offer (the "Offer") to purchase its 9.00% Senior Secured Second Lien Notes due 2029 (the "Notes") to increase the consideration for each $1,000 principal amount of Notes validly tendered and accepted for purchase by the Company (the "Consideration") from $1,017.50 to $1,022.50. In addition to the Consideration, holders of Notes whose Notes are accepted for purchase will receive a cash payment equal to the accrued and unpaid interest on such accepted Notes from and including the immediately preceding interest payment date for such Notes to, but excluding, the Settlement Date (as defined in the Offer to Purchase (as defined below)). The Offer is subject to a $2,000,000,000 cap on the aggregate principal amount of Notes purchased in the Offer, subject to proration and the terms and conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase. The Offer is being made upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase dated November 2, 2026 (as it may be amended or supplemented from time to time, the "Offer to Purchase"). The Expiration Date (as defined in the Offer to Purchase) is unchanged and remains 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 9, 2026. Except for the change described above, all of the terms and conditions set forth in the original Offer to Purchase remain unchanged. The Company has retained Citigroup Global Markets Inc. to act as dealer manager (the "Dealer Manager") for the Offer. Global Bondholder Services Corporation will act as the tender and information agent (the "Tender and Information Agent") for the Offer. For additional information, please contact: Citigroup Global Markets Inc. at +1 (800) 558-3745 (toll-free) or +1 (212) 723-6106 (collect) and ask for Steve Cheeseman, Kevin Mills, or Joyce Lam. Requests for documents and questions regarding the tendering of Notes may be directed to Global Bondholder Services Corporation by telephone at (212) 430-3774 (for banks and brokers only) and (855) 654-2015 (for all others toll-free) or to the Dealer Manager at its telephone number. Copies of the Offer to Purchase are available at: https://www.gbsc-usa.com/cloud . You may also contact your custodian bank, broker, dealer, commercial bank, trust company or other nominee for assistance concerning the Offer. Holders of Notes are advised to check with each bank, securities broker or other intermediary through which they hold Notes as to when such intermediary would need to receive instructions from a beneficial owner in order for that Holder to be able to participate in, or withdraw their instruction to participate in the Offer before the deadlines specified herein and in the Offer to Purchase. The deadlines set by any such intermediary and DTC for the submission and withdrawal of tender instructions may be earlier than the relevant deadlines specified herein and in the Offer to Purchase.
