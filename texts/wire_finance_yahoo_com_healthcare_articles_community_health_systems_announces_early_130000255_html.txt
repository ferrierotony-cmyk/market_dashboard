Learn more FRANKLIN, Tenn., November 24, 2026 --( BUSINESS WIRE )--Community Health Systems, Inc. (the "Company") (NYSE:CYH) today announced the results, as of 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 23, 2026 (the "Early Tender Deadline"), of a private offer by its wholly owned subsidiary, CHS/Community Health Systems, Inc. (the "Issuer"), to exchange (the "Exchange Offer") any and all of its $1,244,476,000 aggregate principal amount of outstanding 6.875% Junior-Priority Secured Notes due 2029 (the "Old Notes") for new 10.250% Senior Secured Notes due 2035 (the "New Notes"), and of the related solicitation of consents ("Consents" and such solicitation, the "Consent Solicitation") from eligible holders of the Old Notes to adopt certain proposed amendments (the "Proposed Amendments") to the indenture governing the Old Notes. The Issuer was advised by the exchange agent for the Exchange Offer that, as of the Early Tender Deadline, a total of $1,214,977,000 aggregate principal amount of outstanding Old Notes, representing approximately 97.63% of the outstanding Old Notes, were validly tendered (and not validly withdrawn) in the Exchange Offer, and the related Consents were validly delivered (and not validly revoked) in the Consent Solicitation. As of the Early Tender Deadline, the condition that certain institutional investors that are holders of the Old Notes (the "Supporting Holders") validly tender (and not validly withdraw) $643,667,000 aggregate principal amount of the Old Notes held by them (representing approximately 52% of the aggregate principal amount of the Old Notes) had been satisfied. In addition, as of the Early Tender Deadline, the Issuer had received the Requisite Consents (as defined in the Offering Memorandum (as defined below)) to adopt the Proposed Amendments. Holders whose Old Notes were validly tendered (and not validly withdrawn) at or prior to the Early Tender Deadline will receive on the Settlement Date (as defined below), in exchange for each $1,000 principal amount of Old Notes so tendered and accepted for exchange, $1,000 principal amount of New Notes, which includes an early tender premium of $30 principal amount of New Notes (the "Early Tender Premium"). Holders whose Old Notes are validly tendered after the Early Tender Deadline and at or prior to 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on Thursday, December 10, 2026 (the "Expiration Date") will receive on the Settlement Date, in exchange for each $1,000 principal amount of Old Notes so tendered and accepted for exchange, $970 principal amount of New Notes (the "Exchange Consideration"), which does not include the Early Tender Premium. Story Continues The tender withdrawal deadline has passed. Accordingly, tenders of Old Notes may no longer be withdrawn. The Exchange Offer and the Consent Solicitation remain subject to the other conditions set forth in the offering memorandum and consent solicitation statement, dated November 9, 2026 (as amended or supplemented from time to time, the "Offering Memorandum"). The Issuer reserves the right to terminate, withdraw or amend the Exchange Offer and the Consent Solicitation at any time and for any reason, as described in the Offering Memorandum. Assuming that the other conditions to the Exchange Offer and the Consent Solicitation have been or concurrently are or will be satisfied or waived, the settlement date for the Old Notes validly tendered (and not validly withdrawn) in the Exchange Offer at or prior to the Expiration Date and accepted by the Issuer is expected to be December 14, 2026 (the "Settlement Date"). On the Settlement Date, the Issuer expects to issue, after adjusting for rounding, $1,214,977,000 of New Notes in respect of the Old Notes tendered at or prior to the Early Tender Deadline. In addition, the Issuer has agreed to issue to the Supporting Holders, and the Supporting Holders have agreed to accept from the Issuer, an aggregate principal amount of New Notes equal to (x) $1,244,476,000 minus (y) the aggregate principal amount of Old Notes validly tendered (and not validly withdrawn) and accepted for exchange in the Exchange Offer (the "Non-Participating Amount"), and the Supporting Holders have agreed to deliver to the Issuer in exchange for such New Notes an aggregate principal amount of 6.125% Junior-Priority Secured Notes due 2030 (the "2030 Junior-Priority Secured Notes") held by them equal to the quotient of (x) the Non-Participating Amount divided by (y) 0.87, rounded down to the nearest incremental multiple of $1,000. Such New Notes will be issued in a private exchange offer with the Supporting Holders and will form a single series with, and have terms identical to, the New Notes issued in the Exchange Offer. The New Notes will be guaranteed on a senior secured basis by the Company and certain of its existing and future domestic subsidiaries that guarantee the Issuer's outstanding ABL facility and senior notes. In addition, the New Notes and related guarantees will be secured by (i) shared first-priority liens on the collateral that secures the Issuer's existing secured notes and (ii) shared second-priority liens on the collateral that secures the Issuer's outstanding ABL facility, in each case subject to permitted liens and certain exceptions described in the Offering Memorandum. The New Notes and related guarantees have not been registered under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the "Securities Act") or any state securities laws. The New Notes may not be offered or sold in the United States or to any U.S. persons except pursuant to an exemption from, or in a transaction not subject to, the registration requirements of the Securities Act. The Exchange Offer is being made, and the New Notes are being offered and issued only (i) in the United States to holders of Old Notes who the Issuer reasonably believes are "qualified institutional buyers" (as defined in Rule 144A under the Securities Act) and (ii) outside the United States to holders of Old Notes who are (A) persons other than U.S. persons, within the meaning of Regulation S under the Securities Act, and (B) "non-U.S. qualified offerees" (as defined in the eligibility letter). The complete terms and conditions of the Exchange Offer and the Consent Solicitation are set forth in the Offering Memorandum. Copies of the Offering Memorandum may be obtained from Global Bondholder Services Corporation, the exchange agent and information agent for the Exchange Offer and the Consent Solicitation, at (833) 789‑0200 (toll free) or (212) 222‑0810 (collect).
