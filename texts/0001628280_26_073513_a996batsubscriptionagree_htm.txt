Representations and Warranties and Acknowledgements of the Purchaser SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT This SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT dated November 2, 2026 (this “Agreement”) is made by and between BT DE Investments Inc., a corporation existing under the Laws of the State of Delaware (the “Purchaser”), and Organigram Global Inc., a corporation existing under the Act (the “Company”). RECITALS: A. Concurrently with the execution of this Agreement, the Company will enter into the Arrangement Agreement (as defined herein) pursuant to which the Company will agree to acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of Aurora Cannabis Inc. (“Aurora”), subject to the terms and conditions set forth therein (the “Aurora Transaction”). B. In connection with the Aurora Transaction, the Purchaser wishes to subscribe for and purchase from the Company, and the Company wishes to issue and sell to the Purchaser, on a private placement basis (the “Private Placement”), Subscription Shares (as defined herein) at the Private Placement Share Price (as defined herein) for an aggregate subscription price equal to the Subscription Proceeds. C. The net proceeds of the Private Placement will be used by the Company to fund a portion of the aggregate Cash Consideration and other amounts payable by the Company under the Arrangement Agreement. D. The Purchaser and the Company wish to enter into this Agreement to record their agreement in respect of the Private Placement.
