(“ BP ”) (NYSE: BP) (LSE: BP) today announced the tender results for the previously announced sixteen concurrent and separate offers (each, a “ Tender Offer ” and collectively, the “ Tender Offers ”), made by its wholly-owned subsidiaries BP Capital Markets America Inc. (“ BPCMA ”) and BP Capital Markets p.l.c. (“ BPCM ” and, together with BPCMA, the “ Offerors ” and each an “ Offeror ”), to purchase for cash any and all of the outstanding notes of the series listed in the table below (collectively, the “ Notes ”). The Tender Offers were made upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the offer to purchase dated November 17, 2026 (the “ Offer to Purchase ”). Terms not defined in this announcement have the meanings given to them in the Offer to Purchase. The Tender Offers expired at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on Monday, November 23, 2026 (the “ Expiration Time ”). The Offerors expect to settle all Notes validly tendered and not validly withdrawn at or prior to the Expiration Time and accepted for purchase on November 25, 2026 (the “ Settlement Date ”). The Offerors have been advised by the Tender and Information Agent, D.F. King & Co., Inc., that an aggregate principal amount of $8,599,897,000 of Notes were validly tendered at or prior to the Expiration Time and not validly withdrawn. The Maximum Purchase Condition has been satisfied with respect to the Tender Offers with respect to the series of Notes with Acceptance Priority Levels 1 through 12. All Notes of those series validly tendered and not validly withdrawn at or prior to the Expiration Time have been accepted for purchase. The Offerors have not accepted any Notes with Acceptance Priority Levels 13 through 16 and will promptly return all validly tendered Notes of such series to the respective tendering Holders. Upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase, Holders whose Notes have been accepted for purchase will receive the applicable Total Consideration for each $1,000 principal amount of such Notes in cash on the Settlement Date. Holders of Notes accepted for purchase will also receive accrued and unpaid interest on such Notes from the last interest payment date for such Notes preceding the Settlement Date up to, but not including, the Settlement Date (the “ Accrued Interest ”). Notes purchased in the Tender Offers will be retired and cancelled.
