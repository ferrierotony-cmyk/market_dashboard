Copenhagen, 21 October 2026 – Reference is made to the stock exchange announcement dated 21 September 2026 made by Cadeler A/S (" Cadeler ", and together with its consolidated subsidiaries, the " Cadeler Group ") regarding the launch of the voluntary offer (the " Offer ") by Cadeler plc to all holders of shares in Cadeler, each with a nominal value of DKK 1.00 (the " Cadeler Shares "), including Cadeler Shares represented by American Depositary Shares (each representing four (4) Cadeler Shares), to exchange each Cadeler Share for one (1) share in Cadeler plc, each with a nominal value of USD 1.00, in connection with the contemplated redomiciliation of the Cadeler Group’s parent company from Denmark to the United Kingdom (the " Redomiciliation "). Cadeler plc hereby announces that the period during which shareholders of Cadeler may accept the Offer (the " Offer Period "), which was previously scheduled to expire today, 21 October 2026 at 5:00 PM Eastern Time / 11:00 PM CEST, has been extended to 28 October 2026 at 5:00 PM Eastern Time / 10:00 PM CET. DNB Carnegie, as the Receiving Agent, and Computershare Inc., as the exchange agent for the Offer, has advised Cadeler that, as of 3:53 PM Eastern Time / 9:53 PM CEST on 21 October 2026, approximately 345,537,852 Cadeler Shares had been validly tendered and not validly withdrawn in the Offer, representing approximately 89.5% of the total number of Cadeler Shares outstanding at such date and time (excluding treasury shares). The Offer Period is being extended to permit additional shareholders to tender their Cadeler Shares and the achievement of the conditions to the Offer, including the condition that a minimum of 90% of the total number and of the aggregate voting power of the outstanding Cadeler Shares be tendered in the Offer. Acceptances of the Offer already received will remain binding and there is no need for shareholders of Cadeler that have already accepted the Offer to take any further action to confirm their acceptances. The full terms and conditions of the Offer, including procedures for how to accept the Offer, are set out in the combined EU/EEA prospectus and offer document published by Cadeler plc on 21 September 2026 (the " Prospectus ") and the offering prospectus/offer to exchange (the " U.S. Prospectus/Offer to Exchange ") included in a Registration Statement on Form F-4 under the U.S. Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the " U.S. Securities Act ") filed by Cadeler plc with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the " SEC "). Cadeler plc may in its sole discretion further extend the Offer Period up to a total of 10 weeks (from and including 22 September 2026). The latest possible date of the Offer Period is 1 December 2026, if the Offer Period is extended up to a total of 10 weeks. Acceptance of the Offer Cadeler shareholders wishing to accept the Offer must, prior to expiry of the Offer Period on 28 October 2026 at 5:00 PM Eastern Time / 10:00 PM CET and in accordance with the procedures set out in the Prospectus, duly complete and return the acceptance form which is included as appendix B to the Prospectus. The acceptance form may be submitted by e-mail to retail@dnb.no, or by post to DNB Carnegie, Registrars department, Dronning Eufemias gate 30, P.O. Box 1600 Sentrum, N-0021 Oslo, Norway. Norwegian private individuals with a BankID may also accept the Offer electronically through a link provided in the acceptance form. Cadeler shareholders who hold their Cadeler Shares through a broker, bank or other nominee must contact such nominee to accept the Offer on their behalf. The full terms and conditions of the Offer, including procedures for how to accept the Offer, are set out in the Prospectus and the U.S. Prospectus/Offer to Exchange. Holders of Cadeler ADSs wishing to tender in the Offer must do so prior to 5:00 PM Eastern Time / 10:00 PM CET on 28 October 2026 in accordance with the procedures set out in the U.S. Prospectus/Offer to Exchange and related letter of transmittal, and any amendments or supplements thereto. About Cadeler Cadeler A/S (Cadeler) is a pure-play offshore wind installation partner and a global leader in offshore wind turbine transport and installation. The company owns and operates the industry’s largest fleet of jack-up offshore wind installation vessels and is expanding its capabilities into full-scope foundation transport and installation, as well as operations & maintenance. With its modern fleet and depth of expertise across onshore and offshore operations, Cadeler supports the safe, efficient and reliable delivery of offshore wind projects worldwide. Cadeler is listed on the New York Stock Exchange (ticker: CDLR) and the Oslo Stock Exchange (ticker: CADLR). For more information, please visit www.cadeler.com.
