Item 8.01. Other Events. On November 3, 2026 (the “Effective Date”), the Company announced that its Board of Directors (the “Board”) authorized an increase to the Company’s previously-authorized share repurchase program (the “Repurchase Program”) such that the amount of the Company’s common stock, $0.01 par value per share (the “Common Stock”), available for repurchase as of the Effective Date is $20 million (exclusive of fees and commissions). Under the Repurchase Program, the Company may repurchase its Common Stock through a variety of methods, including open market purchases, which may or may not be pursuant to Rule 10b5-1 trading plans, privately negotiated transactions, accelerated share repurchases, block trades, tender offers, or any combination of such methods. The timing and amount of shares of Common Stock that may be repurchased under the Repurchase Program will depend on a variety of factors including, but not limited to, the share price, market conditions, corporate considerations, business opportunities, debt agreements, and regulatory requirements. The Repurchase Program does not have an expiration date and may be amended or terminated by the Board at any time without prior notice. Prior to the Effective Date, there was approximately $11.1 million available for repurchase under the Repurchase Program. As of October 31, 2026, there were 22,984,460 shares of Common Stock outstanding. Item 9.01. Financial Statements and Exhibits.
