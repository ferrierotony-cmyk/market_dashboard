(3) The Total Consideration per $1,000 principal amount of each series of Notes validly tendered and accepted for purchase will be determined by reference to the applicable Fixed Spread plus the yield of the applicable Reference Security as quoted on the applicable Bloomberg Reference Page as of 2:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 23, 2026, unless extended with respect to the applicable Tender Offer (the “Price Determination Time”), calculated to the maturity date or, where applicable, the par call date of such Notes, as described in the Offer to Purchase. The Total Consideration does not include Accrued Interest (as defined below). The Tender Offers will each expire at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on Monday, November 23, 2026 (the “Expiration Time”), or any other date and time to which the applicable Offeror extends the applicable Tender Offer. Notes tendered may be withdrawn at or prior to, but not after, 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 23, 2026 (such date and time, as it may be extended with respect to the Notes, the “Withdrawal Deadline”). Each Tender Offer is independent of the other Tender Offers, and the applicable Offeror may terminate or modify any of its Tender Offers without terminating or modifying any other Tender Offer. The Offerors expect to settle all Notes validly tendered and not validly withdrawn at or prior to the Expiration Time and accepted for purchase on the second business day after the Expiration Time, which is expected to be November 25, 2026 (the “Settlement Date”). Holders of Notes accepted for purchase will also receive accrued and unpaid interest on such Notes from the last interest payment date for such Notes preceding the Settlement Date up to, but not including, the Settlement Date (the “Accrued Interest”). The Tender Offers are subject to the satisfaction or waiver of certain conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase. Each Tender Offer is conditioned on (i) the aggregate Total Consideration, excluding Accrued Interest, payable for Notes purchased in the Tender Offers not exceeding $6,000,000,000 (the “Maximum Purchase Consideration”), and (ii) the Maximum Purchase Consideration being sufficient to pay the Total Consideration, excluding Accrued Interest, for all validly tendered and not validly withdrawn Notes of such series (after accounting for all validly tendered and not validly withdrawn Notes of all series that have a higher Acceptance Priority Level) (the “Maximum Purchase Condition”). The Tender Offers are not conditioned upon the tender of any minimum principal amount of the Notes and are not subject to a financing condition. The Offerors will accept Notes for purchase in accordance with the Acceptance Priority Levels, provided that the Offerors may (x) waive the Maximum Purchase Condition with respect to one or more Tender Offers or (y) skip any Tender Offer for Notes that would cause the Maximum Purchase Consideration to be exceeded and accept Notes of a series with a lower Acceptance Priority Level, as more fully described in the Offer to Purchase. If a given series of Notes is accepted for purchase pursuant to the Tender Offers, all Notes of that series that are validly tendered and not validly withdrawn will be accepted for purchase. No series of Notes will be subject to proration pursuant to the Tender Offers. Subject to applicable law, the Offerors reserve the right, but are under no obligation, to increase the Maximum Purchase Consideration or waive the Maximum Purchase Condition at any time, with or without extending the Withdrawal Deadline. No assurance can be given that the Offerors will increase the Maximum Purchase Consideration or waive the Maximum Purchase Condition. If Holders tender more Notes in the Tender Offers than they expect to be accepted for purchase based on the Maximum Purchase Consideration and the Offerors subsequently accept more of such Notes tendered than such Holders expected as a result of an increase in the Maximum Purchase Consideration, such Holders may not be able to withdraw any of their previously tendered Notes.
