ANNOUNCES SUCCESSFUL RESULTS OF CONSENT SOLICITATION RELATED TO ITS 8.125% SERIES 2012-1 SENIOR TPE SECURED NOTES DUE 2037 PR Newswire Fri, November 13, 2026 at 4:28 PM CST 3 min read PIURA, Peru, November 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Terminales Portuarios Euroandinos Paita S.A. (the "Company") announced today the successful results of its previously announced consent solicitation (the "Consent Solicitation"), where it sought the consents (the "Consents") from the holders of its 8.125% Series 2012-1 Senior TPE Secured Notes due 2037 (the "Notes"), upon the terms and conditions described in the Company's Consent Solicitation Statement, dated November 9, 2026 (the "Consent Solicitation Statement"), with respect to (i) the adoption of certain proposed amendments (the "Proposed Amendments") to the Collateral Trust Indenture, dated as of April 11, 2012 (as amended, supplemented or otherwise modified prior to the date hereof, the "Indenture"), by and between the Company and Citibank N.A., as collateral indenture trustee (the "Indenture Trustee"), Securities Intermediary, Paying Agent and Transfer Agent and (ii) directing the Indenture Trustee to consent to Citibank del Perú S.A. (the "Peruvian Trustee") amending that certain payment and guarantee trust agreement ( Contrato de Fideicomiso no Discrecional en Administración y Garantía ), dated as of April 13, 2012 (the "Trust Agreement"), by and between the Company and the Peruvian Trustee, as trustee. Unless otherwise defined herein, capitalized terms used herein have the meanings given to them in the Consent Solicitation Statement. As of 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 13, 2026 (the "Expiration Time"), Holders representing a majority in aggregate principal amount of the outstanding Notes not held by the Company or any of its affiliates have validly delivered (and not withdrawn) the Requisite Consents to the Proposed Amendments described in the Consent Solicitation Statement. The Revocation Deadline for the Consent Solicitation has passed and, therefore, Consents that have been validly delivered cannot be revoked. Subject to the conditions set forth in the Consent Solicitation Statement, upon payment by the Company to Holders, which is expected to occur on November 20, 2026, of the Consent Fee equal to U.S.$5.00 per U.S.$1,000 principal amount of the Notes (subject to the Scaling Factor) for which such Holder has validly delivered (and not validly revoked) Consents to the Proposed Amendments prior to the Expiration Time, the Proposed Amendments, including the Supplemental Indenture and the Amendment to the Trust Agreement, will be effective and operative. The Company has retained Santander US Capital Markets LLC as solicitation agent (the "Solicitation Agent"). Any questions or requests for assistance or for copies of the Consent Solicitation Statement or related documents may be directed to the Information and Tabulation Agent at its telephone numbers or e-mail address set forth below. A Holder of Notes as of the Record Time also may contact the Solicitation Agent, at Santander US Capital Markets LLC, +1 (855) 404-3636 (Toll-Free), +1 (212) 350-0660 (Collect), AmericasLM@santander.us, or such Holder's broker, dealer, commercial bank, trust company or other nominee for assistance concerning the Consent Solicitation. The Information and Tabulation Agent for the Consent Solicitation is S&P Global Inc., +1 (212) 849-3880 (Banks and Brokers call), +1 (888) 593-9546 (All others call toll-free), consentoffer@spglobal.com.
