("I-Systems") into TIM S.A. (“Company”) (“Protocol”) is included as Annex II to this Management Proposal. 2. Description of the transaction, including: (a) Terms and conditions As approved by the Company’s Board of Directors and disclosed in a Material Fact on this date, the incorporation of I-Systems into TIM S.A. is proposed pursuant to the Protocol. As a result of the Incorporation, all of I-Systems’ shareholders’ equity will be transferred to the Company, based on the book value determined as of July 31, 2026 (“Base Date”), corresponding to R$1,562,205,580.51 (one billion, five hundred sixty-two million, two hundred five thousand, five hundred eighty Brazilian reais and fifty-one centavos), pursuant to the appraisal report prepared for purposes of Article 227 of Law No. 6,404/76, as amended (“Brazilian Corporations Law”), and attached to the Protocol (“Appraisal Report”). Considering that the Company currently holds all shares issued by I-Systems, the Incorporation will not result in an increase in the Company’s capital stock, the issuance of new shares or any change in its shareholding structure, and Article 264 of the Brazilian Corporations Law will not apply. The Incorporation will not result in any amendment to the Company’s ByLaws or give rise to withdrawal rights for the Company’s shareholders. I-Systems holds authorizations from the Brazilian Telecommunications Agency (“ANATEL”) to provide Multimedia Communication Services (“SCM”) and Private Limited Services (“SLP”). ANATEL approved the transaction and the resulting transfer and consolidation, in TIM’s name, of the Authorizations and other regulatory registrations relating to the services currently provided by I-Systems, pursuant to Decision No. 277 of October 16, 2026 and Act No. 13,546, dated October 27, 2026 (Proceeding No. 53500.069420/2026-83), in accordance with applicable regulations. Consummation of the Incorporation will be subject to completion of the operational and technological procedures required to integrate I-Systems’ systems and processes into the Company’s operational structure and to the full implementation of the system routines required to reflect the effects of the Incorporation (“Condition Precedent”). Management expects the Condition Precedent to be satisfied by the date of the Extraordinary Shareholders’ Meeting called to consider and approve the Incorporation. In that event, the Shareholders’ Meeting will approve the transaction and the Incorporation will become effective on December 1, 2026 (“Effective Date”). If the Condition Precedent has not been satisfied by the date of the Shareholders’ Meeting, the resolutions approving the Incorporation will be subject, for their effectiveness, to the Condition Precedent and will authorize TIM’s Management to certify satisfaction of the Condition Precedent and establish a new Effective Date for the transaction. Revenues, expenses and changes in shareholders’ equity occurring between the Base Date and the Effective Date will be recorded in I-Systems and subsequently absorbed by the Incorporating Company, without any change to the amounts adopted for purposes of the Incorporation. As of the Effective Date, the Incorporation will be deemed consummated for all legal purposes, with the resulting dissolution of I-Systems and its universal succession by the Company in all its rights and obligations, pursuant to Article 227 of Law No. 6,404/76. As a result of its dissolution, all shares issued by I-Systems will be canceled pursuant to Article 226, Paragraph 1, of the Brazilian Corporations Law, without replacement by shares issued by the Company. As this is the incorporation of a company wholly owned by the Company, there will be no share exchange ratio or allocation of Company shares as a result of the Incorporation. The Incorporation will be submitted for approval at the Extraordinary Shareholders’ Meetings of I-Systems and the Company called to be held on November 30, 2026. (b) Indemnification obligations: (i) to the officers of any of the companies involved; and (ii) if the transaction is not consummated There is no obligation to indemnify the officers of either company involved, nor any obligation to indemnify as a result of any failure to consummate the Incorporation, considering that I-Systems is a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company. (c) Comparative table of the rights, benefits and restrictions of the shares of the companies involved or resulting companies, before and after the transaction Not applicable, considering that I-Systems has no shareholder other than the Company for whom a comparative table of the rights, benefits and restrictions of the shares of the companies involved or resulting companies, before and after the Incorporation, could be relevant. There will be no capital increase or issuance of new Company shares as a result of the Incorporation, and all Company shares will retain the same rights and benefits before and after the Incorporation. All shares issued by I-Systems will be canceled and extinguished upon the Incorporation. (d) Potential need for approval by debenture holders or other creditors Not applicable. On August 14, 2026, I-Systems completed the full settlement of its financial indebtedness through the early repayment of the loan entered into with Banco Itaú and the optional early redemption of all debentures issued by it. Consequently, I Systems has no financial indebtedness to third parties. (e) Assets and liabilities comprising each portion of the shareholders’ equity, in the event of a spin-off Not applicable. Analysis of the following aspects of the transaction: (a) Description of the main expected benefits, including: (i) synergies; (ii) tax benefits; and (iii) strategic advantages The main expected benefits of the Incorporation are: (i) simplification of the TIM Group’s corporate structure following the acquisition of all of I-Systems’ capital stock; (ii) realization of operating efficiencies resulting from full control of the operation and integration of I-Systems’ assets, teams and processes into the Company’s structure; (iii) consolidation, in a single company, of the assets, resources and operations related to fiber optic infrastructure; (iv) strengthening of the Company’s strategy in the broadband and FTTH segment, expanding its ability to improve the quality of connectivity services and accelerate fixed-mobile convergence initiatives; and (v) positioning the Company to capture future growth and value-creation opportunities in the fiber optic market. In addition, the Incorporation may streamline the Company’s tax and administrative structures by eliminating taxes levied on services provided between the companies involved. (b) Costs No significant costs that could materially impact the Company are expected. (c) Risk factors Considering that I-Systems is a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company, no material additional risks have been identified beyond those already inherent to the activities currently carried out by the Company and I-Systems. (d) If the transaction is a related-party transaction, potential alternatives that could have been used to achieve the same objectives, indicating the reasons why such alternatives were not pursued The Incorporation is a related-party transaction because I-Systems is a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company. Management believes that maintaining the current corporate structure or adopting other forms of corporate reorganization would not achieve, to the same extent, the simplification of the corporate structure, the consolidation of assets and operations in a single company, and the potential operating and investment efficiencies expected from the Incorporation. (e) Share exchange ratio Not applicable, since I-Systems has no shareholders other than the Company and no new shares will be issued in connection with the Incorporation.
