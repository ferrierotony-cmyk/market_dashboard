THE DEBTORS STRONGLY RECOMMEND THAT YOU VOTE TO ACCEPT THE PREPACKAGED PLAN. II. PRELIMINARY STATEMENT The Company is a commercial-stage pharmaceutical company pioneering novel cancer therapies. Its lead product, XPOVIO ® (selinexor) (“ XPOVIO ”), is a first-in-class, oral Selective Inhibitor of Nuclear Export compound approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (the “ FDA ”) in two multiple myeloma indications and approved in more than 50 countries and territories, where it is commercialized through the Company’s licensing partners. The Company’s primary focus is on marketing XPOVIO in its currently approved indications and on developing and seeking regulatory approval of selinexor for the treatment of myelofibrosis, for which the Company submitted a supplemental New Drug Application to the FDA on August 31, 2026 seeking accelerated approval. As described in greater detail in Article VI herein, the Company has incurred significant operating losses since its inception and has depended on periodic access to the capital markets, which in turn has depended on the progress of its clinical programs. When the Company’s two Phase 3 readouts in 2026 fell short of expectations and the Company faced increasing liquidity constraints, the Company’s secured creditors worked constructively with the Company—first to give the Company time to reach the trial readouts, and then to give it time to evaluate its alternatives in light of the results—through a series of payment deferrals, forbearances, and out-of-court transactions. Beginning in July 2025, the Company, with the assistance of Centerview Partners (“ Centerview ”), evaluated a range of strategic alternatives and, in July 2026, launched a formal marketing and sale process for all or a portion of the Company’s business (the “ Marketing and Sale Process ”). Although the Marketing and Sale Process generated multiple proposals, none were actionable before the Company exhausted its liquidity. The Marketing and Sale Process instead yielded the proposal of Ayrmid Ltd. (“ Ayrmid ”), the parent company of Gamida Cell Inc., a commercial-stage cell therapy company, to combine the two businesses, with the Company’s funded-debt creditors receiving a majority equity stake in the combined, better-capitalized commercial-stage oncology company together with a contingent interest in the myelofibrosis opportunity. Following negotiations among the Company, Ayrmid, the Ad Hoc Group, and the Royalty Investors, the Debtors entered into a restructuring support agreement, dated as of November 2, 2026 (as may be amended, supplemented, or otherwise modified from time to time, the “ Restructuring Support Agreement ”), a copy of which is attached hereto as Exhibit B , with Ayrmid and the Consenting Stakeholders. As of the date of this Disclosure Statement, Holders of 100% of the 1L Term Loan Claims, 100% of the 1.5L 2028 Notes Claims, 100% of the 2L 2029 Notes Claims, 100% of the RIFA Claims, and 100% of the Existing Preferred Interests have executed the Restructuring Support Agreement. 2 In the event of a Reorganization Transaction, pursuant to the Prepackaged Plan, the Debtors will pursue a comprehensive restructuring of their balance sheet through the Reorganization Transaction, pursuant to which the Company’s business will be combined with Ayrmid’s business under an Entity serving as a holding company (“ NewCo ”). In such event, the Prepackaged Plan provides that: • all claims under the 1L Term Loan Facility, the 1.5L 2028 Notes, and the 2L 2029 Notes (collectively, the “ Funded Debt Claims ”)—approximately $276 million in the aggregate—together with any Excess DIP Claims (as defined below), will be exchanged for 100% of the common stock of Reorganized Parent (the “ Reorganized Parent Common Stock ”), which will in turn be exchanged in the Merger for 55.0% of the New Common Stock of NewCo (subject to dilution by the Omisirge Contingent Equity and the Management Incentive Plan), together with contingent value rights entitling the holders thereof to a percentage of future net sales of XPOVIO in the myelofibrosis indication (the “ MF CVRs ”); • Ayrmid’s existing equityholders will receive the remaining 45.0% of the New Common Stock (which will not be diluted by the Omisirge Contingent Equity, but will be subject to dilution by the Management Incentive Plan), together with the Omisirge Contingent Equity, which is tied to the future revenues of Ayrmid’s Omisirge ® product; • the claims under the $55 million new-money DIP Facility in respect of new-money loans will be repaid in Cash from (a) up to $50 million of the proceeds of the NewCo Facility, a new senior secured exit term loan facility of up to $135 million to be provided to NewCo by one or more third-party lenders, and (b) the Company’s unrestricted Cash on hand at closing in excess of the reserves provided for in the Prepackaged Plan, and the holders of any remaining new-money claims under the DIP Facility and of the loans under the 1L Term Loan Facility rolled up into the DIP Facility (collectively, the “ Excess DIP Claims ”) will receive Reorganized Parent Common Stock, which will be exchanged in the Merger as described above; • the claims under the RIFA will be exchanged for the rights and interests under the New Royalty Agreement, pursuant to which NewCo will pay the Royalty Investors an unsecured royalty equal to 3.0% of the net revenues received by NewCo from all products containing selinexor for the five (5) year period following the Effective Date, and the RIFA (including any change of control payment thereunder) will be canceled; III. QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS REGARDING THIS DISCLOSURE STATEMENT AND PREPACKAGED PLAN A. What is chapter 11? Chapter 11 is the principal business reorganization chapter of the Bankruptcy Code. In addition to permitting debtor rehabilitation, chapter 11 promotes equality of treatment for creditors and similarly situated equity interest holders, subject to the priority of distributions prescribed by the Bankruptcy Code. 4 The commencement of a chapter 11 case creates an estate that comprises all of the legal and equitable interests of the debtor as of the date the chapter 11 case is commenced. The Bankruptcy Code provides that the debtor may continue to operate its business and remain in possession of its property as a “debtor in possession.” Consummating a chapter 11 plan is the principal objective of a chapter 11 case. A bankruptcy court’s confirmation of a plan binds the debtor, any person acquiring property under the plan, any creditor or equity interest holder of the debtor, and any other entity as may be ordered by the bankruptcy court. Subject to certain limited exceptions, the order issued by a bankruptcy court confirming a plan provides for the treatment of the debtor’s liabilities in accordance with the terms of the confirmed plan. Antitrust and Foreign Investment Approvals . Depending on the value of the transaction and the identity of the parties, the Merger may constitute a reportable transaction under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, as amended (the “ HSR Act ”), in which case the Merger could not be consummated until the applicable waiting period under the HSR Act has expired or been terminated. The applicable waiting period is generally 30 days, except that it is 15 days for acquisitions of assets or voting securities of a debtor effected pursuant to section 363(b) of the Bankruptcy Code. The Debtors and Ayrmid are evaluating whether the Merger will require a filing under the HSR Act or any foreign merger control regime, including in Ireland and the European Union. In addition, because Ayrmid is a non-U.S. entity and the equityholders of Ayrmid include non-U.S. persons, the Debtors and Ayrmid are evaluating whether the Merger will require a filing with, or clearance from, the Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States (“ CFIUS ”). Any Sale Transaction may also be subject to premerger notification under the HSR Act and, to the extent the Purchaser is a foreign person, review by CFIUS, as well as other applicable federal, state, local, or foreign antitrust, competition, foreign investment, or sector-specific approvals. The applicability of any 16 such requirement will depend on, among other things, whether the Debtors pursue the Sale Transaction or the Reorganization Transaction, the size and structure of the applicable transaction, and the nature and ownership of the ultimate acquirer or investor. In the event that it becomes necessary to confirm the Prepackaged Plan over the rejection of certain Classes, the Debtors may seek confirmation of the Prepackaged Plan notwithstanding the dissent of such rejecting Classes. The Bankruptcy Court may confirm the Prepackaged Plan pursuant to the “cramdown” provisions of the Bankruptcy Code, which allow the Bankruptcy Court to confirm a plan that has been rejected by an impaired Class if it determines that the Prepackaged Plan satisfies section 1129(b) of the Bankruptcy Code. The Prepackaged Plan provides that section 1129(a)(10) of the Bankruptcy Code will be satisfied for purposes of Confirmation by acceptance of the Prepackaged Plan by one or more of the Classes entitled to vote. How will the preservation of the Causes of Action impact my recovery under the Prepackaged Plan? The Prepackaged Plan provides for the retention of all Causes of Action other than those that are expressly waived, relinquished, exculpated, released, compromised, or settled. In accordance with section 1123(b) of the Bankruptcy Code, but subject to Article VIII of the Prepackaged Plan, each Reorganized Debtor or Post-Effective Date Debtor, as applicable, shall retain and may enforce all rights to commence and pursue, as appropriate, any and all Causes of Action of the Debtors, whether arising before or after the Petition Date, including any actions specifically enumerated in the Schedule of Retained Causes of Action, and the Reorganized Debtors’ and Post-Effective Date Debtors’ rights to commence, prosecute, or settle such Causes of Action shall be preserved notwithstanding the occurrence of the Effective Date, other than the Causes of Action released by the Debtors pursuant to the releases and exculpations contained in the Prepackaged Plan, including in Article VIII of the Prepackaged Plan, which shall be deemed released and waived by the Debtors and the Reorganized Debtors (or Post-Effective Date Debtors, as applicable) as of the Effective Date. The Reorganized Debtors or Post-Effective Date Debtors, as applicable, may pursue such retained Causes of Action, as appropriate, in accordance with the best interests of the Reorganized Debtors or Post-Effective Date Debtors. No Entity (other than the Released Parties) may rely on the absence of a specific reference in the Prepackaged Plan, the Plan Supplement, or this Disclosure Statement to any Cause of Action against it as any indication that the Debtors or the Reorganized Debtors (or Post-Effective Date Debtors), as applicable, will not pursue any and all available Causes of Action of the Debtors against it. The Debtors and Reorganized Debtors (or Post-Effective Date Debtors, as applicable) expressly reserve all rights to prosecute any and all Causes of Action against any Entity, except as otherwise expressly provided in the Prepackaged Plan, including Article VIII of the Prepackaged Plan. Unless any Causes of Action of the Debtors against an Entity are expressly waived, relinquished, exculpated, released, compromised, or settled in the Prepackaged Plan or a Final Order, the Reorganized Debtors and Post-Effective Date Debtors expressly reserve all Causes of Action, for later adjudication, and, therefore, no preclusion doctrine, including the doctrines of res judicata, collateral estoppel, issue preclusion, claim preclusion, estoppel (judicial, equitable, or otherwise), or laches, shall apply to such Causes of Action upon, after, or as a consequence of the Confirmation or Consummation. For the avoidance of doubt, the Debtors and the Reorganized Debtors (or Post- 19 Effective Date Debtors, as applicable) do not reserve any Causes of Action that have been expressly released or exculpated pursuant to the Prepackaged Plan (including, for the avoidance of doubt, Claims and Causes of Action against the Consenting Stakeholders released pursuant to the releases by the Debtors provided in Article VIII.C of the Prepackaged Plan and the exculpation contained in Article VIII.E of the Prepackaged Plan). The Reorganized Debtors and Post-Effective Date Debtors reserve and shall retain such Causes of Action of the Debtors notwithstanding the rejection or repudiation of any Executory Contract or Unexpired Lease during the Chapter 11 Cases or pursuant to the Prepackaged Plan. In accordance with section 1123(b)(3) of the Bankruptcy Code, any Causes of Action that a Debtor may hold against any Entity shall vest in the Reorganized Debtors or Post-Effective Date Debtors, except as otherwise expressly provided in the Prepackaged Plan, including Article VIII of the Prepackaged Plan. The applicable Reorganized Debtors or Post-Effective Date Debtors, through their authorized agents or representatives, shall retain and may exclusively enforce any and all such Causes of Action.
