Commences Offer to Purchase for Cash Any and All 4.100% Senior Notes due 2029 BOSTON, Mass., November 16, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — Santander Holdings USA, Inc. (“ SHUSA ”) today announced that it has commenced a cash tender offer (the “ Offer ”) to purchase any and all of the outstanding notes described below, upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase dated November 16, 2026 (the “ Offer to Purchase ”). The Notes offered to be purchased in the Offer are the 4.100% Senior Notes due 2029 (the “ Notes ”) for the consideration described below. If the Notes are accepted for purchase pursuant to the Offer, all Notes that are validly tendered and not validly withdrawn will be accepted for purchase. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this press release have the meanings given to them in the Offer to Purchase. The Total Consideration for the Notes payable per each $1,000 principal amount of such Notes validly tendered and accepted for purchase will be based on the Fixed Spread for the Notes, plus the Reference Yield based on the bid-side price of the Reference Security as quoted on the Bloomberg Reference Page as of 3:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 20, 2026, unless extended by SHUSA with respect to the Offer, and will be calculated assuming payments through the maturity date or the par call date of the Notes, as applicable, as described in the Offer to Purchase. Promptly after the Price Determination Time, unless extended with respect to the Offer, SHUSA will announce in a press release, among other things, the Total Consideration applicable to the Notes accepted for purchase. In addition to the Total Consideration, Holders whose Notes are accepted for purchase pursuant to the Offer will also receive accrued and unpaid interest on those Notes from the last interest payment date with respect to those Notes up to, but excluding, the Settlement Date. The Offer is expected to expire on the Expiration Date, which is 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 20, 2026, unless extended or earlier terminated. Notes tendered for purchase may be validly withdrawn at any time at or prior to 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 20, 2026, unless extended by SHUSA, but may not thereafter be validly withdrawn, except as provided in the Offer to Purchase or required by applicable law. The Settlement Date is expected to be the second business day after the Expiration Date and is expected to be November 24, 2026. The Offer is subject to certain conditions as described in the Offer to Purchase. If any condition is not satisfied or waived, SHUSA is not obligated to accept for payment, purchase or pay for, and may delay the acceptance for payment of, any tendered Notes, subject to applicable law, and may terminate or amend the Offer. The Offer is not conditioned on the tender of any aggregate minimum principal amount of Notes (subject to minimum denomination requirements as set forth in the Offer to Purchase). SHUSA has retained Santander US Capital Markets LLC to act as the sole dealer manager for the Offer. D.F. King & Co., Inc. will act as the Tender and Information Agent for the Offer.
