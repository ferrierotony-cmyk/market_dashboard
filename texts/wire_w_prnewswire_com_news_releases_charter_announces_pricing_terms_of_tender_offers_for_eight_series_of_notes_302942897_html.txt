Nov 17, 2026, 17:06 ET Share this article Share this article STAMFORD, Conn. , Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Charter Communications, Inc. (NASDAQ: CHTR , CHTRP) (along with its subsidiaries, " Charter ") announced today the pricing terms of the previously announced eight separate offers (each, an " Offer " and, collectively, the " Offers ") by its wholly-owned subsidiaries, Charter Communications Operating, LLC ("CCO") and Charter Communications Operating Capital Corp. ( "CCO Capital " and, together with CCO, collectively, the " Issuers " or the " Company "), to purchase for cash the outstanding series of their notes listed in the table below (collectively, the " Notes " and each a " Series ") for aggregate Consideration (as defined below) of up to $1.5 billion (the " Offer Cap "), excluding Accrued Interest and fees and expenses related to the Offers, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase dated November 10, 2026 (as supplemented, the " Offer to Purchase "). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this press release have the same meanings given to them in the Offer to Purchase. Certain information regarding the Notes and the pricing for the Offers is set forth in the table below. (5) The consideration for each $1,000 principal amount of each Series of Notes validly tendered, not validly withdrawn and accepted for purchase (the " Consideration ") was determined in accordance with standard market practice, as described in the Offer to Purchase, to result in a price as of the Settlement Date based on a yield to the applicable par call date or maturity date (in accordance with market practice) in accordance with the formula set forth in Schedule A to the Offer to Purchase, for the applicable Series of Notes, equal to the sum of (i) the applicable Reference Yield plus (ii) the applicable Fixed Spread specified in the table above for such Series of Notes. The Consideration does not include the applicable Accrued Interest, which will be payable in cash in addition to the applicable Consideration. The Offers will expire at 5:00 p.m. (New York City time) today, November 17, 2026 (such time and date, as it may be extended, the " Expiration Date "), unless extended or earlier terminated by the Company. Tendered Notes may be validly withdrawn at any time at or prior to the Withdrawal Deadline, which, unless the Offers are extended, is the Expiration Date, but not thereafter, unless otherwise required by applicable law. The " Settlement Date " will be the second business day after the Expiration Date and is expected to be November 19, 2026. The Offers remain subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions described in the Offer to Purchase, including the General Conditions. The complete terms and conditions of the Offers are set forth in the Offer to Purchase. Holders of Notes are urged to read the Offer to Purchase carefully. If proration of tendered Notes is required, the Company will use commercially reasonable efforts to announce the results of proration by press release prior to 10:00 a.m. (New York City time) on the business day following the Expiration Date. The Company intends to announce the results of the Offers in a separate press release following the Expiration Date. If the Company terminates any Offer with respect to one or more Series of Notes, it will give prompt notice to the Information Agent and Tender Agent, and all Notes tendered pursuant to such terminated Offer will be returned promptly to the tendering Holders thereof. With effect from such termination, any Notes blocked in The Depository Trust Company ("DTC") will be released. Holders of Notes are advised to check with any bank, securities broker or other intermediary through which they hold Notes as to when such intermediary needs to receive instructions from a Holder in order for that Holder to be able to participate in, or (in the circumstances in which withdrawal is permitted) withdraw their instruction to participate in, the Offers before the deadlines specified herein and in the Offer to Purchase. The deadlines set by any such intermediary and DTC for the submission and withdrawal of tender instructions may be earlier than the relevant deadlines specified herein and in the Offer to Purchase .
