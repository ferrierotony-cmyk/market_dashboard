Regulation FD Disclosure. On November 16, 2026, Surgery Partners, Inc. (the “Company”) announced that its indirect wholly owned subsidiary, Surgery Center Holdings, Inc. (the “Issuer”), has commenced a solicitation of consents (the “Consent Solicitation”) from holders of its outstanding $1,225,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 7.250% Senior Notes due 2032 (the “Notes”) to certain proposed amendments (the “Proposed Amendments”) to the indenture governing the Notes, dated as of April 10, 2024 (as supplemented, the “Indenture”), among the Issuer, the guarantors party thereto and Wilmington Trust, National Association, as trustee. The Consent Solicitation is being conducted in connection with the Company’s previously announced entry into an Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated October 23, 2026 (as amended by Amendment No. 1 thereto, dated as of November 8, 2026, and as it may be further amended, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time, the “Merger Agreement”), with Spyglass Topco, LLC (“Topco”), Spyglass Merger Subsidiary, Inc., a wholly owned subsidiary of Topco (“Merger Sub”), and, solely for the limited purposes set forth therein, Bon Secours Mercy Health, Inc. (“BSMH”), pursuant to which Merger Sub will be merged with and into the Company (the “Merger”), with the Company surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Topco. If the Proposed Amendments are not consented to and operative, the consummation of the Merger would constitute a “Change of Control” under the Indenture. The purpose of the Consent Solicitation is to amend the Indenture to (i) provide that, concurrently with and following the consummation of the Merger in accordance with the terms of the Merger Agreement on or prior to the date that is five business days after the End Date (as defined in the Merger Agreement), each of BSMH, HealthSpan Partners, a 501(c)(3) Ohio corporation affiliated with BSMH, and their respective affiliates, and certain groups of which any of them are members, will be “Permitted Holders” under the Indenture, in addition to the existing Permitted Holders, and (ii) add to, amend, supplement or change certain other defined terms in the Indenture related to the foregoing. The Proposed Amendments becoming operative is not a condition to the consummation of the Merger. The Consent Solicitation is being made solely pursuant to, and subject to the terms and conditions set forth in, the Issuer’s Consent Solicitation Statement, dated November 16, 2026 (as it may be amended or supplemented from time to time, the “Consent Solicitation Statement”). This Current Report on Form 8-K does not constitute a solicitation of consents with respect to the Notes, and the information herein is qualified in its entirety by reference to the Consent Solicitation Statement. In connection with the Consent Solicitation, the Company issued a press release on 16, 2026 announcing the commencement of the Consent Solicitation. A copy of such press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 attached hereto and is incorporated herein by reference. The information contained in this Item 7.01 on Form 8-K is being furnished and shall not be deemed “filed” for the purposes of Section 18 of the Exchange Act, or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that Section, nor shall it be deemed incorporated by reference in any registration statement or other document filed by the Company under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as otherwise expressly stated in such filing.
