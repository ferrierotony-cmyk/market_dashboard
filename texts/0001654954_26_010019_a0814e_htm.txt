Form 20-F X Form 40-F 20 November 2026 HSBC HOLDINGS PLC ANNOUNCES RESULTS OF ITS TENDER OFFER FOR ONE SERIES OF SECURITIES HSBC Holdings plc (the ‘ Company ’, ‘ we ’ or ‘ us ’) today announces the results of its offer to purchase (the ‘ Offer ’) for cash up to $1,500,000,000 aggregate principal amount (the ‘ Maximum Tender Amount ’) of the outstanding $3,000,000,000 6.000% Perpetual Subordinated Contingent Convertible Securities (Callable 22 May 2027 and Every Five Years Thereafter) (the ‘ Securities ’), previously announced on 5 November 2026, which was made upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase dated 5 November 2026, as amended, relating to the Securities (the ‘ Offer to Purchase ’). The Offer to Purchase is available at the following link: https://www.gbsc-usa.com/hsbc/ . Capitalised terms used herein but not otherwise defined herein shall have the meaning provided in the Offer to Purchase. As previously announced, the Maximum Tender Amount was increased from an aggregate principal amount of up to $1,000,000,000 to an aggregate principal amount of up to $1,500,000,000 of the Securities on 6 November 2026. The Offer expired at 5:00 p.m. (New York City time) on 19 November 2026 (the ‘ Expiration Time ’). References to ‘$’ are to US dollars. In addition to the Consideration, holders whose Securities are accepted for purchase will also be paid on the Settlement Date a cash amount equal to the accrued and unpaid interest on such Securities (excluding any interest cancelled or deemed to have been cancelled pursuant to the terms of the Securities) from, and including, the last interest payment date for such Securities to, but not including, the Settlement Date, rounded to the nearest cent (such amount, the ‘ Accrued Interest ’). For the avoidance of doubt, interest will cease to accrue on the Settlement Date for all Securities accepted in the Offer. Under no circumstances will any interest be payable to holders because of any delay on the part of Global Bondholder Services Corporation, as depositary, The Depository Trust Company or any other party in the transmission of funds to holders. All Securities accepted in the Offer will be cancelled and retired, and will no longer remain outstanding obligations of the Company.
