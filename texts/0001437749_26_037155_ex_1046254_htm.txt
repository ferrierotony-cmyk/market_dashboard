(the “Company” or “Aytu”) (Nasdaq:AYTU), a pharmaceutical company focused on advancing innovative medicines for complex central nervous system diseases to improve the quality of life for patients, today announced that it has entered into a definitive merger agreement with Rosemont Pharmaceuticals Holdings, Inc. (“Rosemont”), pursuant to which Rosemont would acquire Aytu, through a tender offer for $2.70 per share. Under the terms of the merger agreement, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Rosemont will commence a tender offer to acquire all outstanding shares of Aytu common stock. The transaction is structured as a tender offer to be followed by a merger, subject to customary conditions to the tender offer and merger, including the tender of shares representing at least a majority of the total number of outstanding shares of Aytu common stock and the exercise or termination of certain outstanding warrants. Following the successful completion of the tender offer, Rosemont will acquire all remaining shares not tendered in the tender offer (other than shares for which appraisal rights are properly exercised) through a second-step merger at the same price paid in the tender offer. Rosemont intends to commence the tender offer and file a Schedule TO within 10 business days, and Aytu will file a Schedule 14D-9 with respect to the tender offer substantially concurrently with the commencement of the tender offer, each of which will include additional details on the transaction and process undertaken by the Company. The transaction was unanimously approved by the Aytu board of directors and closing is anticipated to occur in late December 2026, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions to the tender offer. Aytu’s executive officers, directors and certain stockholders affiliated with Nantahala Capital Management and Stonepine Capital Management, have signed tender and support agreements under which such stockholders agreed, among other things, to tender their shares in the tender offer and to the termination or exercise of any warrants held by them prior to the Effective Time, in exchange for the consideration payable in connection with the transaction in accordance with the terms of such warrants. Josh Disbrow, Chief Executive Officer of Aytu BioPharma, said: “This agreement with Rosemont reflects the work our team has done to build our CNS and pediatric portfolio and earn the trust of the patients and healthcare providers we serve. We see a strong strategic fit between our portfolio and Rosemont’s experience developing and commercializing specialty medicines. Importantly, we believe the transaction delivers compelling value to our shareholders and that, together, we can help more patients get the treatment they need.” Alok Sonig, Chief Executive Officer of Rosemont Pharmaceuticals, said: “The proposed acquisition of Aytu marks an important step in Rosemont’s US growth strategy. Aytu has built an established CNS adult and pediatric-focused specialty pharmaceutical business with talented people, a differentiated portfolio and proven commercial capabilities. Together with Rosemont’s product development, regulatory, manufacturing and commercial expertise, we believe we can build a strong platform for future growth. We look forward to welcoming the Aytu team once the transaction is complete.” Canaccord Genuity is acting as financial advisor to Aytu BioPharma. Dorsey & Whitney LLP is serving as legal counsel to Aytu in connection with the transaction. Bourne Partners is acting as financial advisor to Rosemont. Ropes & Gray LLP is serving as legal counsel to Rosemont. About Aytu BioPharma Aytu is a pharmaceutical company focused on advancing innovative medicines for complex central nervous system diseases to improve the quality of life for patients. Aytu’s prescription products include EXXUA® (gepirone) extended-release tablets (see Full Prescribing Information, including Boxed WARNING) for the treatment of major depressive disorder (MDD), and treatments for attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD). Aytu is committed to delivering its medications through best-in-class patient access programs that help to enable optimal patient outcomes. For more information, please visit aytubio.com or follow us on LinkedIn. About Rosemont Pharmaceuticals Rosemont Pharmaceuticals is a global pharmaceutical company specialising in the development, manufacture and supply of oral liquid medicines. For nearly 60 years, Rosemont has focused on creating alternative dosage forms that help address the needs of patients who may have difficulty swallowing solid oral medicines. Through its broad portfolio across multiple therapeutic areas, Rosemont works to support patients, caregivers and healthcare professionals by improving access to medicines in formulations designed to meet diverse patient needs. Rosemont Pharmaceuticals is headquartered in Leeds in the United Kingdom and serves patients across international markets through a growing portfolio of licensed medicines.
