(“Millicom” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: TIGO), a public limited liability company (société anonyme) organized and established under the laws of the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg, today announced the commencement of an offer to purchase for cash any and all of its outstanding 6.25% Senior Notes due 2029 (the “Notes”) (the “Tender Offer”). The Tender Offer is being made upon the terms and subject to the conditions (including the Financing Condition (as defined in the Offer to Purchase)) set forth in the offer to purchase dated November 17, 2026 (as it may be amended or supplemented from time to time, the “Offer to Purchase”). The table below summarizes certain payment terms for the Notes: Description of Security CUSIP No. ISIN Outstanding Principal Amount as of the Date Hereof Consideration per U.S.$1,000 Outstanding Principal Amount* 6.25% Senior Notes due 2029 144A: 600814 AQ0 Reg S: L6388G HV5 144A: US600814AQ03 Reg S: USL6388GHV51 U.S.$616,105,800 U.S.$1,000 * The Consideration for the Notes will be paid together with accrued and unpaid interest from the last interest payment date for the Notes up to, but not including, the Settlement Date (as defined in the Offer to Purchase). At any time on or after March 25, 2027, the Company intends to redeem any Notes not validly tendered in the Tender Offer at the then applicable redemption price of 100.000% of the principal amount thereof, plus accrued and unpaid interest and additional amounts, if any, to, but not including, the applicable redemption date. The Tender Offer will expire at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 23, 2026, or any other date and time to which Millicom extends the Tender Offer (such date and time, as it may be extended, the “Expiration Time”). Holders of Notes must validly tender their Notes at or prior to the Expiration Time and not validly withdraw them at or prior to the Withdrawal Deadline (as defined in the Offer to Purchase), which is 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 23, 2026, in order to be eligible to receive the Consideration (as defined in the Offer to Purchase) plus accrued and unpaid interest for such Notes. Tendered Notes may be validly withdrawn at any time at or prior to the Withdrawal Deadline but not thereafter. Notes may be tendered and accepted for payment in principal amounts equal to minimum denominations of U.S.$200,000 and integral multiples of U.S.$1,000 in excess thereof. Holders who tender less than all of their Notes must continue to hold Notes in at least the minimum authorized denomination of U.S.$200,000 principal amount. No alternative, conditional or contingent tenders will be accepted. The obligation of Millicom to purchase Notes in the Tender Offer is conditioned on the satisfaction or waiver of certain conditions, including, without limitation, the Financing Condition, described in the Offer to Purchase. Subject to applicable law, Millicom reserves the right, in its sole discretion, to waive any and all conditions to the Tender Offer, extend the Expiration Time, or amend or terminate the Tender Offer. The Tender Offer is being made in connection with a concurrent offering of senior notes (the “New Notes”) to be issued by Millicom (the “New Notes Offering”). The New Notes Offering will be exempt from the registration requirements of the U.S. Securities Act of 1933, as amended. In addition, following the Tender Offer and subject to satisfaction of the Financing Condition, the Company intends to call any Notes not validly tendered and accepted in the Tender Offer for redemption on or about March 25, 2027, at the then applicable redemption price of 100.000% of the principal amount thereof, plus accrued and unpaid interest and additional amounts, if any, to, but not including, the redemption date, using any remaining net proceeds from the New Notes Offering and/or cash on balance sheet, as applicable. However, there can be no assurance that such redemption will be consummated at such time or at all. This press release does not constitute a notice of redemption in respect of the Notes. The Tender Offer is not an offer to sell or a solicitation of an offer to buy the New Notes. The aggregate principal amount of the Notes reflected in DTC’s records is U.S.$684,562,000 (the “Pre-Factor Amount”). After giving effect to the pool factor applied by DTC, the aggregate principal amount actually outstanding is U.S.$616,105,800 (the “Post-Factor Amount”). The Consideration will be multiplied by a scaling factor of 0.90, so Holders will receive U.S.$900 for each U.S.$1,000 Pre-Factor Amount of Notes validly tendered and accepted for purchase, which equals U.S.$1,000 for each U.S.$1,000 Post-Factor Amount. References to minimum denominations and integral multiples are to Pre-Factor Amounts. The information and tender agent for the Tender Offer is Citibank, N.A., London Branch.
