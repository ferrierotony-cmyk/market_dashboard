Leadership transition On August 10, 2026, JBS announced a leadership transition contemplating that Mr. Wesley Batista Filho will serve as Global CEO of the Company, effective January 2027. This leadership transition furthermore contemplates that JBS' current executive director and Global CEO, Mr. Gilberto Tomazoni, will, following a leadership transition period, step down from his current position and assume the role of Non-Executive Director and Vice-Chairman of the Board of Directors of JBS (the "Board"). In addition to the previously announced leadership transition, JBS proposes a number of additional changes to its Board. It proposes that the Board is further expanded with the addition of Mr. Donald Elliott Baker as Non-Executive Director. In addition, it is proposed that Mr. Wesley Mendonça Batista, who currently serves as Vice-Chairman of the Board will assume the role of Chairman of the Board. Following these changes, it is contemplated that the Board will comprise a single Executive Director and ten Non-Executive Directors. Each of these changes are proposed to take effect as per January 1, 2027. In order to effect the leadership transition and other changes to the Board as described above, it is proposed that the general meeting resolves on each of the proposals set out under items 2a through 2d below. 2a. Appointment of Mr. Wesley Batista Filho as Executive Director (voting item) It is proposed to appoint Mr. Wesley Batista Filho as Executive Director of JBS, with effect as of January 1, 2027, for a term ending immediately after the annual general meeting of JBS held in 2027. Upon his appointment, Mr. Wesley Batista Filho will assume the position of Global CEO of JBS. Wesley Batista Filho currently serves as JBS USA CEO, overseeing the Company's North American business since 2023. He began his career at JBS in 2011 as a trainee at the Greeley, Colorado, beef facility before moving to São Paulo to work in the beef export division. From 2012 to 2013, he led the company's beef operations in Uruguay and Paraguay. He then moved to Calgary, Alberta, where he served as President of JBS Canada from 2014 to 2015, before returning to Greeley to lead JBS USA Fed Beef, the company's largest global business unit, from 2016 to 2017. Wesley returned to São Paulo in 2017 to serve as CEO of JBS Brazil. In 2020, he became CEO of Seara, the company's value-added, poultry and pork business in Brazil, in addition to his responsibilities as JBS Brazil CEO. In 2022, he assumed the role of 4 / 7 Global President of Operations, overseeing the company's global operational footprint, including in North America, South America, Oceania and Europe. In 2023, Wesley returned to Greeley for a third time and was appointed CEO of JBS USA, the company's largest global platform in terms of revenue. JBS USA is a leading American food company with more than 60 beef, pork, poultry, prepared foods and other food production facilities and 70,000 team members in 31 states. The Board, following the recommendation of the Board's Nominating Committee, has decided to nominate Mr. Batista Filho for appointment as Executive Director. On November 20, 2026, Mr. Batista Filho held 716,887 Class A Common Shares (including Brazilian Depositary Receipts for Class A Common Shares ("JBS BDRs")) in the share capital of JBS. 2b. Appointment of Mr. Gilberto Tomazoni as Non-Executive Director (voting item) It is proposed that Mr. Gilberto Tomazoni be appointed as Non-Executive Director of JBS, with effect as of January 1, 2027, for a term ending immediately after the annual general meeting held in 2027. Upon his appointment, Mr. Gilberto Tomazoni will assume the position of Vice- Chairman of the Board and cease his current term as Executive Director. Mr. Gilberto Tomazoni will continue to serve as Chairman of the board of directors of Pilgrim's Pride Corporation (PPC) and will become Chairman of the J&F Institute, an institution dedicated to developing the next generation of business leaders. Mr. Gilberto Tomazoni, 68 years old, has been a member of the Board and Chief Executive Officer of JBS (including its predecessor JBS S.A.) since 2019 and chairman of the Sustainability Committee since 2025 and a member of the Compensation Committee since 2026. Mr. Tomazoni has also been JBS S.A.'s Global Chief Executive Officer since December 2018. Mr. Tomazoni has extensive experience in, and knowledge of, the food industry. He worked at Sadia for 27 years, where he started as a trainee and became Sadia's chief executive officer. He served for three years as vice president of Bunge Alimentos, managing the food and ingredients business, and was also the executive director for their South and Central American business. He joined JBS S.A. as Global President of the poultry business in 2013, and subsequently led the establishment of Seara as its chief executive officer. In 2015, he was named President of JBS' Global Operations. In 2013, he was named chairman of PPC's board of directors. He is also a member of the International Advisory Council of Fundação Dom Cabral (FDC). Mr. Tomazoni holds a degree in mechanical engineering from Universidade Federal de Santa Catarina in Santa Catarina, Brazil and a postgraduate degree in management. The Board, following the recommendation of the Board's Nominating Committee, has decided to nominate Mr. Tomazoni for appointment as Non-Executive Director. 5 / 7 On November 20, 2026, Mr. Tomazoni held 5,866,485 Class A Common Shares (including JBS BDRs) in the share capital of JBS. 2c. Appointment of Mr. Donald Elliott Baker as Non-Executive Director (voting item) It is proposed that Mr. Donald Elliott Baker be appointed as Non-Executive Director of JBS, with effect as of January 1, 2027, for a term ending immediately after the annual general meeting held in 2027. Mr. Baker, 65 years old and a United States national, is an attorney and has been a partner of the international law firm White & Case LLP since January 1999, with more than 30 years of experience advising clients on cross-border transactions. He relocated to São Paulo, Brazil in 1997 to establish the Firm's Brazilian office, where he has played an important role in building out its Latin American practice. Mr. Baker regularly advises clients on international capital markets, corporate finance, mergers and acquisitions and corporate governance matters. He is a member of the Firm's elected Partnership Committee and previously served for eight years on White & Case's four-partner global Executive Committee. Mr. Baker holds a law degree from the University of Miami School of Law and a Bachelor of Science in Economics from The Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania, and is a member of the New York and Florida Bars. The Board, following the recommendation of the Board's Nominating Committee, has decided to nominate Mr. Baker for appointment as Non-Executive Director. On November 20, 2026, Mr. Baker did not hold any shares in the share capital of JBS or JBS BDRs, nor did he hold any positions as supervisory or non-executive director with other entities. 2d. Appointment of Mr. Wesley Mendonça Batista as Chairman of the Board (voting item) Mr. Wesley Mendonça Batista, 56 years old, has been a member of the Board of JBS (including its predecessor JBS S.A.) since 2012 and currently serves as Non-Executive Director, Vice- Chairman and member of the Nominating Committee and Compensation Committee. As explained in the introduction of this agenda item 2, it is contemplated that Mr. Wesley Mendonça Batista assumes the role of Chairman of the Board. As per JBS' articles of association, assuming the role of Chairman requires a resolution by the general meeting. Accordingly, it is proposed that the general meeting resolves to appoint Mr. Wesley Mendonça Batista as Chairman of the Board effective as per January 1, 2027. As per that date the current Chairman of the Board, Mr. Jeremiah O' Callaghan, will cease to hold the title of Chairman. Mr. W.M. Batista does not qualify as independent in accordance with the Dutch Code and the NYSE rules. In this context, it is noted JBS has established the role of Lead Independent Director, which position is taken by an independent Non-Executive Director. 6 / 7 3. Amendment of the Articles of Association (voting item) It is proposed to amend JBS' articles of association (the "Articles of Association") in accordance with the draft deed of amendment of the Articles of Association, that has been made available on JBS' website (www.jbsglobal.com) and at JBS' offices in the Netherlands. The proposed amendments to the Articles of Association would enable the Board to designate its Chairman from time to time and expands the municipalities in which general meetings may be held. A triptych of the current articles of association containing an explanation on the proposed changes to the articles of association can be found at (www.jbsglobal.com). The proposed resolution to amend the Articles of Association also includes the authorization of each member of the Board to execute the notarial deed of amendment of the Articles of Association, with the right to grant a power of attorney to another person to, directly or indirectly, execute the notarial deed of amendment of the Articles of Association, and with the right to substitute another person for itself as authorized person. 4.
