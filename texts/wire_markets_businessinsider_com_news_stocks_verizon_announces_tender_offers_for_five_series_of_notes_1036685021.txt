(“Verizon”) (NYSE, Nasdaq: VZ) today announced the commencement of five separate offers to purchase for cash up to $500 million aggregate purchase price of the outstanding series of its notes listed in the table below (collectively, the “Notes” and such tender offers, the “Offers”), on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase dated October 29, 2026 (the “Offer to Purchase”). Verizon is offering to purchase validly tendered Notes using a “waterfall” methodology under which the Notes will be accepted in the order of their Acceptance Priority Levels, subject to the Waterfall Cap (as defined below) and, in the case of the 3.000% Notes due November 20, 2060, the 3.700% Notes due March 22, 2061 and the 2.650% Notes due November 20, 2040 (collectively, the “Sub Cap Notes”), the Sub Cap (as defined below). The Offers are subject to the terms and conditions described in the Offer to Purchase, including (i) the Acceptance Priority Procedures (as described below), (ii) a $155 million cap (the “Sub Cap”) on the total cash Verizon pays to purchase Sub Cap Notes validly tendered under the Offers (excluding the applicable Accrued Coupon Payment (as defined below)) and (iii) a $500 million cap (the “Waterfall Cap”) on the total cash Verizon pays to purchase all Notes validly tendered under the Offers (excluding the applicable Accrued Coupon Payment and including cash paid to purchase any Sub Cap Notes). On the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase, Verizon is offering to purchase the following outstanding notes for the consideration described below. Terms not otherwise defined are defined in the Offer to Purchase. The Total Consideration does not include the applicable Accrued Coupon Payment, which will be payable in cash in addition to the applicable Total Consideration. The Offers will each expire at 5:00 p.m. (New York City time) on November 4, 2026, unless extended or earlier terminated by Verizon (such date and time with respect to an Offer, as the same may be extended with respect to such Offer, the “Expiration Date”). All Holders (as defined in the Offer to Purchase) whose Notes are accepted in an Offer will receive a cash payment equal to accrued and unpaid interest on such Notes to, but excluding, the Settlement Date (as defined below) (the “Accrued Coupon Payment”) in addition to their Total Consideration. Notes may be validly withdrawn at any time at or prior to 5:00 p.m. (New York City time) on November 4, 2026, unless extended or earlier terminated with respect to any Offer, but not thereafter. Upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase, Holders who validly tender, and do not validly withdraw, Notes at or prior to the Expiration Date, and whose Notes are accepted for purchase by Verizon, will receive the applicable Total Consideration per $1,000 principal amount of such Notes. Subject to applicable law and limitations described in the Offer to Purchase, Verizon expressly reserves the right to amend, extend or, to the extent the conditions described in the Offer to Purchase are not satisfied or waived, terminate any of the Offers at any time at or prior to the Expiration Date. Subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions of the Offers, the “Acceptance Priority Procedures” with respect to the Offers will operate as follows: if the aggregate Total Consideration for all Notes validly tendered at or prior to the Expiration Date does not exceed the Waterfall Cap, and the aggregate Total Consideration for all Sub Cap Notes validly tendered at or prior to the Expiration Date does not exceed the Sub Cap, then Verizon will accept all such Notes; if the aggregate Total Consideration for all Sub Cap Notes validly tendered at or prior to the Expiration Date exceeds the Sub Cap, and the aggregate Total Consideration for all Notes validly tendered at or prior to the Expiration Date does not exceed the Waterfall Cap (subject to Verizon’s right to waive or increase the Sub Cap at its discretion, subject to applicable law), then Verizon will first , accept for purchase all validly tendered Sub Cap Notes of each series starting at the highest Acceptance Priority Level (level 1) and moving sequentially to Sub Cap Notes of each series having a lower Acceptance Priority Level (the lowest of which is level 3), until the aggregate Total Consideration for all validly tendered Sub Cap Notes of the applicable series, together with the aggregate Total Consideration for all Sub Cap Notes accepted for purchase from series with higher Acceptance Priority Levels, is as close as possible to, but does not exceed, the Sub Cap; second , to the extent the Sub Cap would otherwise be exceeded, accept on a prorated basis validly tendered Sub Cap Notes of the series with the next lower Acceptance Priority Level, and Verizon will not accept for purchase any Sub Cap Notes of a series with an Acceptance Priority Level below the prorated series; and third , accept for purchase all validly tendered Notes of each series starting with Acceptance Priority Levels following the Sub Cap Notes (level 4 and level 5); and if the aggregate Total Consideration for all Notes validly tendered at or prior to the Expiration Date exceeds the Waterfall Cap and the aggregate Total Consideration for all Sub Cap Notes so tendered exceeds the Sub Cap (in each case, subject to Verizon’s right to waive or increase the Waterfall Cap or the Sub Cap at its discretion, subject to applicable law), then subject to the limitations imposed by the Waterfall Cap described below, Verizon will first , accept for purchase all validly tendered Sub Cap Notes of each series starting at the highest Acceptance Priority Level (level 1) and moving sequentially to Sub Cap Notes of each series having a lower Acceptance Priority Level (the lowest of which is level 3), until the aggregate Total Consideration for all validly tendered Sub Cap Notes of the applicable series, together with the aggregate Total Consideration for all Sub Cap Notes accepted for purchase from series with higher Acceptance Priority Levels, is as close as possible to, but does not exceed, the Sub Cap; second , to the extent the Sub Cap would otherwise be exceeded, accept on a prorated basis validly tendered Sub Cap Notes of the series with the next lower Acceptance Priority Level, and Verizon will not accept for purchase any Sub Cap Notes of a series with an Acceptance Priority Level below the prorated series; third , for the Waterfall Cap, subject to the limitations imposed by the Sub Cap above, accept for purchase validly tendered Notes of each series starting at the highest Acceptance Priority Level (level 1) and moving sequentially to the series of Notes having the lowest Acceptance Priority Level (level 5), until the aggregate Total Consideration for all validly tendered Notes of the applicable series, together with the aggregate Total Consideration for all Notes (including Sub Cap Notes) accepted for purchase from series with higher Acceptance Priority Levels, is as close as possible to, but does not exceed, the Waterfall Cap; and fourth , accept on a prorated basis (following the procedures described in the Offer to Purchase) validly tendered Notes of the series with the next lower Acceptance Priority Level, and Verizon will not accept for purchase any Notes of a series with an Acceptance Priority Level below the prorated series. For the avoidance of doubt, if the aggregate Total Consideration for all Notes validly tendered at or prior to the Expiration Date exceeds the Waterfall Cap but the aggregate Total Consideration for all Sub Cap Notes so tendered does not exceed the Sub Cap (in each case, subject to Verizon’s right to waive or increase the Waterfall Cap or the Sub Cap at its discretion, subject to applicable law), then Verizon will first accept for purchase validly tendered Notes of each series starting with the Acceptance Priority Level 1 and moving sequentially to the series of Notes having the lowest Acceptance Priority Level (level 5), until the aggregate Total Consideration for all validly tendered Notes of the applicable series, together with the aggregate Total Consideration for all Notes (including Sub Cap Notes) accepted for purchase from series with higher Acceptance Priority Levels, is as close as possible to, but does not exceed, the Waterfall Cap; and second, accept on a prorated basis validly tendered Notes of the series with the next lower Acceptance Priority Level, and Verizon will not accept for purchase any Notes of a series with an Acceptance Priority Level below the prorated series. The “Settlement Date,” if any, is the date on which Verizon will settle all Notes of a series validly tendered and accepted for purchase, subject to all conditions having been satisfied or waived by Verizon. The Settlement Date is expected to be the third business day following the applicable Expiration Date (November 9, 2026), unless the Expiration Date is extended with respect to any Offer. The applicable Total Consideration payable by Verizon for each $1,000 principal amount of each series of Notes validly tendered at or prior to the Expiration Date, and not validly withdrawn, and accepted by Verizon will be paid in cash on the Settlement Date. Promptly after 2:00 p.m. (New York City time) on November 4, 2026, unless extended with respect to an Offer (such date and time with respect to an Offer, as the same may be extended with respect to such Offer, the “Price Determination Date”), Verizon will issue a press release announcing, among other things, the Offer Yield (as defined in the Offer to Purchase) and the Total Consideration for each series of Notes. If the Expiration Date with respect to an Offer is extended by more than 24 hours, the applicable Price Determination Date will be extended to 2:00 p.m. (New York City time) on the same day as the new Expiration Date. Promptly after the Expiration Date, and in any event by 10:00 a.m. (New York City time) on the next business day, Verizon will announce the aggregate principal amount of Notes accepted in each Offer and any applicable proration factor.
