TABLE OF CONTENTS Item 1 Report of Other Relevant Information dated November 12, 2026 Item 1 Santander announces a 5% increase in the exchange ratio for its intended exchange offer for the outstanding shares of Santander Brasil · The improved exchange ratio is 0.4260 Banco Santander shares per Santander Brasil unit and 0.2130 per common or preferred share, before adjustments for dividends paid since 30 July. · The consideration will continue to consist of newly issued Banco Santander shares, representing up to approximately 1.1% of the current share capital. Madrid, 12 November 2026 - PRESS RELEASE Banco Santander has decided to enhance the terms announced on 30 July 2026 for the voluntary offer it intends to launch to acquire all of Santander Brasil’s issued and outstanding common shares, preferred shares, units and American Depositary Shares (ADSs) that it does not already own, representing approximately 10% of Santander Brasil’s share capital (through concurrent exchange offers in Brazil and the US), following recent market developments and feedback from stakeholders. Banco Santander has increased the previously announced exchange ratio by 5% to make the offer more attractive for holders of Santander Brasil securities. Holders who tender their securities will now receive (i) for each unit or ADS of Santander Brasil, 0.4260 newly issued Banco Santander shares (instead of the 0.4056 announced on 30 July) and (ii) for each common share or preferred share 1 of Santander Brasil, 0.2130 newly issued Banco Santander shares (instead of the 0.2028 announced on 30 July) 2 . After adjusting for the dividends already paid by Banco Santander and Santander Brasil since 30 July 2026, the exchange ratios as at today’s date would be 0.4246 Banco Santander shares per unit or ADS, 0.2124 per common share and 0.2122 per preferred share of Santander Brasil, already reflecting the improvement announced today. In all cases, the Banco Santander shares will continue to be delivered in the form of Brazilian Depositary Receipts (BDRs) or ADSs, as applicable, which will be tradable on the São Paulo stock exchange and the New York Stock Exchange, respectively. Assuming full acceptance of the offer, Banco Santander would issue approximately 166 million new shares, equivalent to approximately 1.1% of its current share capital and the maximum consideration would be approximately €1.9 billion, based on yesterday’s closing market price. Banco Santander will seek shareholders’ approval for the corresponding capital increases to settle the offer at an extraordinary general shareholders’ meeting that is expected to be held on 15 December 2026. As previously indicated, the transaction is fully aligned with Santander’s strategy of delivering long-term shareholder value creation and meets the group’s disciplined capital allocation framework. It compares favourably with alternative deployment options, while remaining capital neutral. Considering the revised terms of the offer, the transaction is expected to increase earnings per share by approximately 0.3% from 2029 and tangible book value per share by approximately 0.6%, as well as to generate an attractive return on invested capital, based on current market expectations. It is also expected to strengthen the group’s long-term earnings growth and to enhance its capacity for organic capital generation in the years ahead. For Santander Brasil’s minority shareholders, the offer provides an attractive opportunity to realize the value of their investment at a premium to the market price, now on improved terms compared with those announced on 30 July. At the same time, it enables them to become shareholders in one of the world’s leading diversified financial groups, with a broader earnings base, resilient profitability and a proven track record of sustainable value creation. Commencement of the offer and the offer itself will be subject to customary conditions for transactions of this nature, including the obtaining of the relevant regulatory approvals, the approval by Banco Santander’s extraordinary general shareholders’ meeting of the corresponding capital increases and the absence of any material adverse change. 1 Each Santander Brasil unit is composed of one ordinary share and one preferred share. 2 Subject to certain adjustments for certain events that may occur between 30 July 2026 and the expiration of the offer, including potential dividends, interest on equity and/or share bonuses paid by Banco Santander and/or Santander Brasil and/or stock splits and/or reverse stock splits by either of them. Share buybacks conducted during such period shall not cause adjustment of the exchange ratio.
