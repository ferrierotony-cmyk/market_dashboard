(Paris:ETL) (LSE:ETL) (a joint-stock company ( société anonyme ) established under the laws of the Republic of France) (THE " COMPANY ") ANNOUNCES THE RESULTS OF THE TENDER OFFER TO THE QUALIFYING HOLDERS OF ITS OUTSTANDING €600,000,000 1.500 PER CENT. SENIOR NOTES DUE 2028 (OF WHICH €600,000,000 ARE CURRENTLY OUTSTANDING) (ISIN: FR00140005C6) (THE "EXISTING NOTES"); TO TENDER THEIR EXISTING NOTES FOR PURCHASE BY THE COMPANY FOR CASH IN AN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF UP TO THE MAXIMUM ACCEPTANCE AMOUNT (AS DEFINED HEREIN), SUBJECT TO THE RIGHT OF THE COMPANY TO INCREASE OR DECREASE SUCH AMOUNT, ON THE TERMS AND SUBJECT TO THE CONDITIONS SET OUT IN FULL IN THE TENDER OFFER MEMORANDUM This notice must be read in conjunction with the tender offer memorandum dated 16 November 2026 (the " Tender Offer Memorandum "). Capitalised terms used in this notice and not otherwise defined herein shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Tender Offer Memorandum. On 16 November 2026, the Company announced the launch of a Tender Offer to Qualifying Holders (as defined in the Tender Offer Memorandum) of the Existing Notes. Under the terms of the Tender Offer, Qualifying Holders (subject to offer restrictions) were invited to tender their Existing Notes for purchase by the Company for a cash amount, equal to the Tender Consideration and the Accrued Interest Amount upon the terms and conditions of the Tender Offer as described in the Tender Offer Memorandum. On the same day, Eutelsat Communications S.A. (the " Parent ") announced its intention to issue new euro denominated senior unsecured notes (the " New Notes "), subject to market conditions. The settlement of the Tender Offer is conditional upon the settlement of the issue of the New Notes to the satisfaction of the Company (the " Transaction Condition "). The Company is entitled to waive the Transaction Condition at its sole and absolute discretion. Story Continues Following the pricing of the New Notes on 17 November 2026, the Company announced that it would accept for purchase Existing Notes validly tendered pursuant to the Tender Offer up to a maximum aggregate amount of €600,000,000 (the " Maximum Acceptance Amount ") (which was equivalent to the current principal outstanding amount of the Existing Notes), subject to the Transaction Condition. The Tender Offer expired at 4:00 p.m. Paris time on 23 November 2026. Final results of the Tender Offer The Company is pleased to announce today the final results of the Tender Offer as follows: Aggregate principal amount of Existing Notes validly tendered and accepted for purchase (the " Final Acceptance Amount "): €496,900,000 Proration Factor applied to valid and accepted tenders of Existing Notes: N/A Aggregate principal amount of Existing Notes that will remain outstanding after the Settlement Date: €103,100,000 Settlement Subject to satisfaction or waiver of the Transaction Condition, the Settlement Date is expected to take place on 26 November 2026, on which date the Company will pay the Tender Consideration and the Accrued Interest Amount to the Qualifying Holders who have validly tendered for purchase their Existing Notes under the Tender Offer and whose tenders have been accepted.
