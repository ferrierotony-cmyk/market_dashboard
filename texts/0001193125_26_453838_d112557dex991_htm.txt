ARTICLE 2. OFFER CONDITIONS; EXTENSION; WAIVER 2.1 Offer Conditions. The Offer is subject only to the Offer Conditions set forth in Annex A. If, as of the Expiration Time, any of the Offer Conditions have not been satisfied or waived, (a) DUG may, after consultation in good faith with the Special Committee and the Board, but shall not be required, to extend the Offer for one or more additional periods, and (b) DUG shall in any event extend the Offer for such additional periods as may be required by any Applicable Law or the rules and regulations of the SEC or Nasdaq or as is necessary to resolve any comments issued by the SEC or its staff applicable to the Offer, the Schedule TO, or the Offer Documents, the Schedule 13E-3, or the Schedule 14D-9; provided that no extension of the Offer shall extend past the earlier of (x) the termination of this Agreement pursuant to Article 13 and (y) the End Date. 2.2 Determination of Minimum Tender Condition. The satisfaction of the Minimum Tender Condition shall be determined objectively on the basis specified in Annex B, by reference to the Tender Agent Certificate, and not by any subjective determination of DUG. DUG shall cause the Tender Agent to inform DDI of the Tender Agent’s determination with respect to the satisfaction of the Minimum Tender Condition, and to take into account any reasonable comment of DDI in respect thereof. 2.3 Limited-Discretion Waiver. If DUG reasonably determines that the Minimum Tender Condition is not reasonably expected to be satisfied as of the then-scheduled Expiration Time, DUG may, with the prior written consent of DDI pursuant to Section 2.4 below, at any time prior to the then-scheduled Expiration Time, reduce the Minimum Tender Condition to a percentage not lower than ninety percent (90%) of the total issued and outstanding Common Shares (the “Waiver Floor”), subject to Applicable Law and Section 2.5. DUG shall have no obligation to reduce the Minimum Tender Condition. The Majority-of-the-Minority Condition and the Termination Condition may not be waived by any Party. 2.4 DDI Consent.
