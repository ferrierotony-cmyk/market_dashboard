Nov 02, 2026, 11:01 ET Share this article Share this article FORT LAUDERDALE, Fla. , Nov. 2, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Cloud Software Group Holdings, Inc. ("Cloud Software Group" or the "Company") has commenced a cash tender offer (the "Offer") to purchase the outstanding notes described below, upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase dated November 2, 2026 (the "Offer to Purchase"). The Company is offering to purchase in the Offer its 9.00% Senior Secured Second Lien Notes due 2029 (the "Notes") for the Consideration (as defined below), subject to a $2,000,000,000 cap on the aggregate principal amount of Notes purchased in the Offer (the "Tender Cap"), subject to proration and the terms and conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase. For the avoidance of doubt, the Tender Cap for Notes purchased in the Offer shall exclude the Accrued Coupon Payment (as defined below). Subject to applicable law, the Company may, but is under no obligation to, increase, decrease or eliminate the Tender Cap at any time. In the event proration is required with respect to the Notes, the Company will multiply the principal amount of each valid tender of such Notes by the proration rate and round the resulting amount down to the nearest $1,000 principal amount in order to determine the principal amount of such tender that will be accepted pursuant to the Offer. The excess principal amount of Notes not accepted from the tendering holders of the Notes (the "Holders") will be promptly returned to such Holders, and if this excess principal amount of Notes is less than $2,000, the Company may either accept or reject all such tendering Holders' validly tendered Notes in its sole discretion. Any Notes that are accepted for purchase by the Company in the Offer will be retired and canceled. With respect to any (i) increase or decrease in the percentage of the Notes accepted for payment in the Offer or (ii) change in the Consideration, such increase, decrease or change will be communicated by a press release or other public announcement that is widely disseminated by the Company no later than 9:00 a.m., New York City time, on the third business day before the scheduled Expiration Date (as defined below) and, if necessary, the Company will extend the Offer so that such announcement is made no later than such time; provided that the Company may increase the amount of Notes accepted for payment in the Offer by no more than 2% of the outstanding Notes without amending or extending the Offer and without issuing a press release or other public announcement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this press release have the meanings given to them in the Offer to Purchase. (3) The consideration (the "Consideration") represents the amount payable per $1,000 principal amount of Notes validly tendered and accepted for purchase by the Company. The Consideration excludes Accrued Interest (as defined below), which will be paid in addition to the Consideration. Upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase, Holders of Notes that are validly tendered and not validly withdrawn at or prior to the Expiration Date and accepted for purchase pursuant to the Offer will receive the Consideration for each $1,000 principal amount of such Notes, in cash, on the Settlement Date. In addition to the Consideration, Holders whose Notes are accepted for purchase will receive a cash payment equal to the accrued and unpaid interest on such accepted Notes from and including the immediately preceding interest payment date for such Notes to, but excluding, the Settlement Date (the "Accrued Interest," and the payment thereof, the "Accrued Coupon Payment"). Accrued Interest will cease to accrue on the Settlement Date for all Notes accepted in the Offer. Under no circumstances will any interest be payable because of any delay in the transmission of funds to Holders by The Depository Trust Company ("DTC") or its participants. The Offer will expire at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 9, 2026, unless extended or earlier terminated by the Company (such time and date, as the same may be extended or terminated by the Company in its sole discretion, subject to applicable law, the "Expiration Date"). Tenders of Notes submitted after the Expiration Date will not be valid. Tendered Notes may be validly withdrawn at any time at or prior to the earlier of (i) the Expiration Date and (ii) if the Offer is extended, the 10th business day after commencement of the Offer (such time and date, as the same may be extended by the Company in its sole discretion, the "Withdrawal Deadline"), but may not thereafter be validly withdrawn, unless otherwise required by applicable law. In addition, tendered Notes may be validly withdrawn at any time after the 60th business day after commencement of the Offer if for any reason the Offer has not been consummated within 60 business days after commencement of the Offer. Promptly after the Expiration Date (and, if proration is required, using commercially reasonable efforts to do so no later than 10:00 a.m., New York City time, on the next business day after the Expiration Date, or as soon thereafter as practicable), the Company will issue a press release specifying the aggregate principal amount of Notes validly tendered and accepted for purchase in the Offer and, if proration is required, the proration factor. Provided that all conditions to the Offer have been satisfied or waived by the Company on or prior to the Expiration Date (or, in the case of the Financing Condition (as defined below), on or prior to the Settlement Date), the Company will settle all Notes validly tendered at or prior to the Expiration Date and accepted for purchase in the Offer on a date promptly following the Expiration Date (the "Settlement Date"), which is expected to be on or about November 16, 2026, the fourth business day after the Expiration Date. If the Offer is terminated, Notes tendered pursuant to the Offer will promptly be returned to the tendering Holders. The Company's obligation to accept Notes validly tendered in the Offer is subject to the Tender Cap and to the satisfaction or waiver by the Company of certain conditions described in the Offer to Purchase, including the General Conditions and the Financing Condition. The Offer is not conditioned upon the tender of any aggregate minimum principal amount of Notes (subject to minimum denomination requirements as set forth in the Offer to Purchase). Subject to applicable law, the Company expressly reserves the right, in its sole discretion, to waive any and all conditions to the Offer (including the Financing Condition), extend or terminate the Offer, increase, decrease or eliminate the Tender Cap or otherwise amend the Offer in any respect. Substantially concurrently with the commencement of the Offer, the Company launched an offering of new senior secured notes due 2034 (the "New Notes") in an aggregate principal amount of $2,000,000,000 (the "New Notes Offering"). The Company intends to use the net proceeds from the New Notes Offering, together with cash on hand, to fund the Offer and pay the Accrued Interest, as well as related premiums, fees and expenses. The Offer is conditioned upon the completion of the New Notes Offering on terms satisfactory to the Company, in its sole discretion, on or prior to the Settlement Date (the "Financing Condition"). The Company will not be required to accept for purchase any tendered Notes, or pay the Consideration, if the Financing Condition has not been satisfied or waived on or prior to the Settlement Date. The Company cannot assure you that the New Notes Offering will be successful. The New Notes Offering is being made pursuant to an exemption from the registration requirements of the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, and is being made to eligible investors pursuant to Rule 144A and Regulation S thereunder. This press release and the Offer is not an offer to sell, or a solicitation of an offer to buy, the New Notes, and the New Notes Offering is not an offer to buy, or a solicitation of an offer to sell, the Notes.
