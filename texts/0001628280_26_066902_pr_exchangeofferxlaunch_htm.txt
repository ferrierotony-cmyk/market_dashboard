EX-99.1 2 pr-exchangeofferxlaunch.htm EX-99.1 Document Exhibit 99.1 Dream Finders Homes Commences Private Exchange Offer and Consent Solicitation for Beazer Homes USA, Inc.’s Outstanding 7.500% Senior Notes due 2031 and 8.000% Senior Notes due 2032 JACKSONVILLE, Fla., — October 22, 2026 — Dream Finders Homes, Inc. (NYSE: DFH) (“Dream Finders”) today announced, in connection with its recently completed acquisition (the “Transaction”) of Beazer Homes USA, Inc. (“Beazer”), that it is commencing private exchange offers (the “Exchange Offer”) and related consent solicitations (the “Consent Solicitation”) with respect to Beazer’s outstanding notes of the series listed in the table below (collectively, the “Beazer Notes”). Pursuant to the terms and conditions set forth in an exchange and consent solicitation statement dated October 22, 2026 (the “Offering Memorandum”), Dream Finders is offering to issue new notes (collectively, the “Dream Finders Notes”) in exchange for any and all of the Beazer Notes held by holders eligible to participate in the Exchange Offer (the “Eligible Holders”). The Total Consideration includes an early participation premium (the “Early Participation Premium”), which consists of $50 principal amount of the corresponding series of Dream Finders Notes per $1,000 principal amount of Beazer Notes and the Cash Consideration. Eligible Holders validly tendering their Beazer Notes after the Early Participation Date but on or prior to 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 20, 2026, (such time and date, as the same may be extended, the “Expiration Date”) will not be eligible to receive the Early Participation Premium and will receive only the exchange consideration set out in the table above (the “Exchange Consideration”). In addition to the Exchange Consideration or Total Consideration, as applicable, Eligible Holders whose Beazer Notes are accepted for exchange will be paid accrued and unpaid interest on the applicable series of Beazer Notes from, and including, the most recent date on which interest was paid on such Eligible Holder’s Beazer Notes to, but not including, the first applicable settlement date on which such Eligible Holder’s Beazer Notes are exchanged, payable in cash on such applicable settlement date. Interest on such Beazer Notes will cease to accrue on the applicable settlement date for all Beazer Notes accepted for exchange in the Exchange Offer. Rights to withdraw tendered Beazer Notes and revoke consents terminate at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 4, 2026, unless extended (such time and date, as the same may be extended, the “Withdrawal Deadline”), except as required by applicable law. Dream Finders reserves the right to terminate, withdraw, amend or extend the Exchange Offer and Consent Solicitation in its discretion, subject to the terms and conditions set forth in the Offering Memorandum. In addition, pursuant to the Consent Solicitation, Dream Finders is soliciting consents from the Eligible Holders to certain proposed amendments (the “Proposed Amendments”) to the Beazer Notes and the related indentures under which they were issued (as supplemented, collectively, the “Beazer Indentures”) to eliminate substantially all of the restrictive covenants and events of default under the Beazer Indentures. Dream Finders is conducting the Exchange Offer to simplify its capital structure following the consummation of the Transaction. The Exchange Offer and Consent Solicitation are being made upon the terms and conditions set forth in the Offering Memorandum, copies of which will be made available to the Eligible Holders. Eligible Holders validly tendering their Beazer Notes will be deemed to have delivered consents to the Proposed Amendments with respect to such tendered Beazer Notes. Eligible Holders will not be permitted to tender their Beazer Notes without delivering consents or to deliver consents without tendering their Beazer Notes. Dream Finders’ obligation to accept and exchange the Beazer Notes validly tendered pursuant to the Exchange Offer is subject to certain conditions as set forth in the Offering Memorandum. Dream Finders may generally waive any such condition, in its sole discretion, at any time with respect to the Exchange Offer and Consent Solicitation. The Exchange Offer and Consent Solicitation are not conditioned upon the tender of any minimum aggregate principal amount of the Beazer Notes or the receipt of the requisite consents in the Consent Solicitation. This press release is neither an offer to sell nor the solicitation of an offer to buy the Dream Finders Notes or any other securities and shall not constitute an offer, solicitation or sale in any jurisdiction in which, or to any person to whom, such an offer, solicitation or sale is unlawful. About Dream Finders Homes Dream Finders Homes (NYSE: DFH), headquartered in Jacksonville, Florida, was recognized as the 2025 National Builder of the Year by Builder magazine. Dream Finders builds single-family homes throughout the Southeast, Mid-Atlantic, Midwest, Southwest and West, including Florida, Texas, Tennessee, North Carolina, South Carolina, Georgia, Colorado, Arizona, California, Nevada, Indiana, Delaware and the Washington, D.C. metropolitan area, which comprises Washington, D.C., Northern Virginia and Maryland. As the Official Home Builder of the PGA TOUR, the Jacksonville Jaguars and the Tampa Bay Rays, Dream Finders is deeply committed to excellence beyond homebuilding and into the communities it serves. Through its wholly owned subsidiaries, Dream Finders also provides mortgage financing as well as title agency and underwriting services to homebuyers. Dream Finders achieves its growth and returns by maintaining an asset-light homebuilding model.
