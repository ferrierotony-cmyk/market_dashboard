Announces Commencement of Consent Solicitation Relating to Surgery Center Holdings, Inc.’s 7.250% Senior Notes due 2032 BRENTWOOD, Tenn., November 16, 2026 – Surgery Partners, Inc. (“Surgery Partners”) (Nasdaq: SGRY) announced today that its indirect wholly owned subsidiary, Surgery Center Holdings, Inc. (the “Issuer”), has commenced a solicitation of consents (the “Consent Solicitation”) from holders (“Holders”) of its outstanding $1,225,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 7.250% Senior Notes due 2032 (CUSIP Nos. 86881W AF9 / U86786 AD9 / U86786 AE7; ISINs US86881WAF95 / USU86786AD98 / USU86786AE71) (the “Notes”) to certain proposed amendments (the “Proposed Amendments”) to the indenture governing the Notes, dated as of April 10, 2024 (as supplemented, the “Indenture”), among the Issuer, the guarantors party thereto and Wilmington Trust, National Association, as trustee (the “Trustee”). As previously announced, on October 23, 2026, Surgery Partners entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (as amended by Amendment No. 1 thereto, dated as of November 8, 2026, and as it may be further amended, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time, the “Merger Agreement”) with Spyglass Topco, LLC (“Topco”), Spyglass Merger Subsidiary, Inc., a wholly owned subsidiary of Topco (“Merger Sub”), and, solely for the limited purposes set forth therein, Bon Secours Mercy Health, Inc. (“BSMH”). Pursuant to the Merger Agreement, Merger Sub will merge with and into Surgery Partners, with Surgery Partners surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Topco (the “Merger”). If the Proposed Amendments are not consented to and operative, the consummation of the Merger would constitute a “Change of Control” under the Indenture. If the Proposed Amendments do not become operative, the Issuer will be required to make an offer to purchase all of the outstanding Notes at a price equal to 101% of the principal amount thereof, plus accrued and unpaid interest, if any, to, but excluding, the repurchase date (a “Change of Control Offer”) in accordance with the terms of the Indenture in connection with the consummation of the Merger. The purpose of the Consent Solicitation is to amend the Indenture to (i) provide that, concurrently with and following the consummation of the Merger in accordance with the terms of the Merger Agreement on or prior to the date that is five business days after the End Date (as defined in the Merger Agreement), each of BSMH, HealthSpan Partners, a 501(c)(3) Ohio corporation affiliated with BSMH, and their respective affiliates, and certain groups of which any of them are members, will be “Permitted Holders” under the Indenture, in addition to the existing Permitted Holders, and (ii) add to, amend, supplement or change certain other defined terms in the Indenture related to the foregoing. Except for the Proposed Amendments, all existing terms of the Notes and the Indenture will remain unchanged, and the Proposed Amendments will not alter the Issuer’s obligation to pay the principal of or interest on the Notes or the stated interest rate or maturity date of the Notes. The Consent Solicitation is being made in accordance with the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in a Consent Solicitation Statement, dated November 16, 2026 (as it may be amended or supplemented from time to time, the “Consent Solicitation Statement”). Only Holders of record as of 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 20, 2026 (the “Record Date”) are eligible to deliver consents. The Consent Solicitation is scheduled to expire at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 20, 2026, unless extended or earlier terminated (such time and date, as it may be extended, the “Expiration Time”). A Holder may validly revoke its consent at any time prior to the earlier of the Effective Time (as defined below) and the Expiration Time (the “Revocation Deadline”), as described in the Consent Solicitation Statement. Holders who validly deliver (and do not validly revoke) consents to the Proposed Amendments prior to the Expiration Time, and whose consents are accepted by the Issuer, will be eligible to receive a cash payment equal to $2.50 per $1,000 in principal amount of Notes for which such consents were delivered (the “Consent Fee”). Payment of the Consent Fee is conditioned upon the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions set forth in the Consent Solicitation Statement, including the receipt of the Requisite Consents (as defined below). If such conditions are satisfied or waived, the Consent Fee will be paid following the later of the Expiration Time and the Effective Time, whether or not the Merger is consummated. Holders who do not deliver consents, or who validly revoke their consents, will not receive the Consent Fee. The Proposed Amendments require the consent of Holders of a majority of the aggregate principal amount of the Notes outstanding, calculated in accordance with the Indenture (the “Requisite Consents”). The Issuer intends to execute a supplemental indenture to the Indenture setting forth the Proposed Amendments (the “Supplemental Indenture”) with the guarantors and the Trustee promptly following receipt of the Requisite Consents, which may occur prior to the Expiration Time. The time and date on which the Supplemental Indenture is executed is referred to as the “Effective Time.” The Supplemental Indenture will become effective upon its execution and delivery, but the Proposed Amendments will become operative only immediately prior to the consummation of the Merger, provided that the Consent Fee has been paid. Once operative, the Proposed Amendments will be binding on all Holders, including those that did not deliver consents. If the Consent Fee is paid but the Merger is not consummated on or prior to the date that is five business days after the End Date, the Proposed Amendments will not become operative, and Holders that received the Consent Fee will retain it. The Proposed Amendments becoming operative is not a condition to the consummation of the Merger. If the Proposed Amendments do not become operative, the Issuer will be required to make a Change of Control Offer in accordance with the terms of the Indenture in connection with the consummation of the Merger. The Merger is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, subject to the approval of Surgery Partners’ stockholders, required regulatory approvals and other customary closing conditions. The complete terms and conditions of the Consent Solicitation are set forth in the Consent Solicitation Statement that is being sent to Holders. Subject to applicable law, the Issuer may terminate, extend or amend the Consent Solicitation at any time as described in the Consent Solicitation Statement. About Surgery Partners Surgery Partners is a leading healthcare services company with a differentiated outpatient delivery model focused on providing high quality, cost effective solutions for surgical and related ancillary care in support of both patients and physicians. Founded in 2004, Surgery Partners is one of the largest and fastest growing surgical services businesses in the country, with more than 200 locations in 30 states, including ambulatory surgery centers, surgical hospitals, multi-specialty physician practices and urgent care facilities.
