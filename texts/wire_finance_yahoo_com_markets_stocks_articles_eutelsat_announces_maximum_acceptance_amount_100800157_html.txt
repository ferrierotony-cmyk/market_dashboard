Eutelsat S.A.: Announces the Maximum Acceptance Amount in Relation to the Tender Offer to the Qualifying Holders of Its Outstanding €600,000,000 1.500 Per Cent. Senior Notes Due 2028 Business Wire November 18, 2026 14 min read ETL.L LONDON, November 18, 2026 --( BUSINESS WIRE )-- Eutelsat S.A. (Paris:ETL) (LSE:ETL): NOT FOR RELEASE, PUBLICATION OR DISTRIBUTION DIRECTLY OR INDIRECTLY TO ANY U.S. PERSON (AS DEFINED IN REGULATIONS OF THE UNITED STATES SECURITIES ACT OF 1933, AS AMENDED (THE " SECURITIES ACT ")) OR ANY PERSON LOCATED OR RESIDENT IN THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA, ITS TERRITORIES AND POSSESSIONS (INCLUDING PUERTO RICO, THE US VIRGIN ISLANDS, GUAM, AMERICAN SAMOA, WAKE ISLAND AND THE NORTHERN MARIANA ISLANDS), ANY STATE OF THE UNITED STATES OR THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA OR IN ANY OTHER JURISDICTION WHERE IT IS UNLAWFUL TO RELEASE, PUBLISH OR DISTRIBUTE THIS ANNOUNCEMENT. 18 November 2026 EUTELSAT S.A. (a joint-stock company ( société anonyme ) established under the laws of the Republic of France) (THE " COMPANY ") ANNOUNCES THE MAXIMUM ACCEPTANCE AMOUNT IN RELATION TO THE TENDER OFFER TO THE QUALIFYING HOLDERS OF ITS OUTSTANDING €600,000,000 1.500 PER CENT. SENIOR NOTES DUE 2028 (OF WHICH €600,000,000 ARE CURRENTLY OUTSTANDING) (ISIN: FR00140005C6) (THE "EXISTING NOTES" ); TO TENDER THEIR EXISTING NOTES FOR PURCHASE BY THE COMPANY FOR CASH IN AN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF UP TO THE MAXIMUM ACCEPTANCE AMOUNT (AS DEFINED HEREIN), SUBJECT TO THE RIGHT OF THE COMPANY TO INCREASE OR DECREASE SUCH AMOUNT, ON THE TERMS AND SUBJECT TO THE CONDITIONS SET OUT IN FULL IN THE TENDER OFFER MEMORANDUM This notice must be read in conjunction with the tender offer memorandum dated 16 November 2026 (the " Tender Offer Memorandum "). Capitalised terms used in this notice and not otherwise defined herein shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Tender Offer Memorandum. On 16 November 2026, the Company announced the launch of a Tender Offer to Qualifying Holders (as defined in the Tender Offer Memorandum) of the Existing Notes. Under the terms of the Tender Offer, Qualifying Holders (subject to offer restrictions) were invited to tender their Existing Notes for purchase by the Company for a cash amount, equal to the Tender Consideration and the Accrued Interest Amount upon the terms and conditions of the Tender Offer as described in the Tender Offer Memorandum. On the same day, Eutelsat Communications S.A. (the " Parent ") announced its intention to issue new euro denominated senior unsecured notes (the " New Notes "), subject to market conditions. The settlement of the Tender Offer is conditional upon the settlement of the issue of the New Notes to the satisfaction of the Company (the " Transaction Condition "). As previously announced, if the Company, subject to the Transaction Condition being satisfied or waived, accepts any valid tenders of Existing Notes for purchase pursuant to the Tender Offer, it intends to accept for purchase Existing Notes up to a maximum aggregate principal amount (the " Maximum Acceptance Amount ") expected to be equal to the aggregate principal amount of the New Notes and to be announced following the pricing of the New Notes, on the terms and subject to the conditions contained in the Tender Offer Memorandum. Story Continues Tender Offer Period The Tender Offer commenced on 16 November 2026 and will expire at 4:00 p.m. Paris time on 23 November 2026 (the " Expiration Deadline "), unless extended, earlier terminated or withdrawn at the sole discretion of the Company as provided in the Tender Offer Memorandum. Maximum Acceptance Amount Following the pricing of the New Notes on 17 November 2026, the Company announces that the Maximum Acceptance Amount will be €600,000,000 (which, for the avoidance of doubt, is equivalent to the current principal outstanding amount of the Existing Notes). The Company reserves the right, in its sole and absolute discretion, to decrease the Maximum Acceptance Amount and to purchase significantly less than (or none of) the full amount of the Maximum Acceptance Amount. The Company expects the Final Acceptance Amount not to exceed the Maximum Acceptance Amount. Results of the Tender Offer If the Tender Offer is not extended, amended, terminated or withdrawn by the Company, as soon as practicable on 24 November 2026, the Company will announce (i) whether it will accept, subject to satisfaction or waiver of the Transaction Condition, for purchase any offer of Existing Notes; and if so (ii) the aggregate principal amount of Existing Notes validly tendered, (iii) the Final Acceptance Amount, (iv) the details of any Proration Factor to be applied to valid and accepted tenders of Existing Notes and (v) the aggregate principal amount of Existing Notes that will remain outstanding after the Settlement Date. Such announcement will be submitted to the Clearing Systems and published by way of announcement on a Notifying News Service and on the website of the Luxembourg Stock Exchange ( www.luxse.com ). Settlement Subject to satisfaction or waiver of the Transaction Condition, the Settlement Date is expected to take place on 26 November 2026, on which date the Company will pay the Tender Consideration and the Accrued Interest Amount to the Qualifying Holders who have validly tendered for purchase their Existing Notes under the Tender Offer and whose tenders have been accepted. Qualifying Holders are advised to check with the bank, securities broker or other intermediary through which they hold their Existing Notes whether such intermediary would require receiving instructions to participate in, or withdraw their instruction to participate in, the Tender Offer prior to the deadlines set out above. The deadlines set by each Clearing System for the submission of Electronic Instruction Notices may also be earlier than the relevant deadlines above, in which case Qualifying Holders should follow those earlier deadlines. See "Terms of the Tender Offer" of the Tender Offer Memorandum. Significant delays may be experienced where notices are delivered through the Clearing Systems and Qualifying Holders are urged to contact the Dealer Managers or the Information Agent at the contact details specified on the Contact Information page of this notice for the relevant announcements during the Tender Offer Period. All announcements will be made available upon release at the offices of the Information Agent and the Tender Agent. Further Information Qualifying Holders are advised to read carefully the Tender Offer Memorandum for full details of and information on the procedures for participating in the Tender Offer in respect of the Existing Notes. Any charges, costs and expenses charged by the Qualifying Holders intermediary shall be borne by such Qualifying Holders.
