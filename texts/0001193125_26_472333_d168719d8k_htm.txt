Financing Transactions As previously announced, on October 28, 2026, JELD-WEN, Inc. (the “ Issuer ”), a subsidiary of JELD-WEN Holding, Inc. (the “ Company ”), commenced (a) an offer to all eligible holders to exchange (the “ Exchange Offer ”) any and all of the Issuer’s outstanding 4.875% Senior Notes due 2027 (the “ Old Notes ”) for new first lien senior secured notes due 2031 (the “ First Lien Notes ”), (b) a related solicitation of consents (the “Consents”) to certain proposed amendments to the indenture governing the Old Notes (the “ Consent Solicitation ”) and (c) an offer to eligible holders participating in the Exchange Offer to elect to purchase their applicable pro rata portion of up to $410.2 million aggregate principal amount of additional First Lien Notes (the “ New Money Offer ”), in each case pursuant to, as applicable, (x) the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in that certain confidential offering memorandum and consent solicitation statement, dated as of October 28, 2026 (as amended, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time, the “ Offering Memorandum ”), and (y) the Commitment and Consent Letter, dated as of September 29, 2026 (as amended, restated, amended and restated, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time, the “ Commitment Letter ”), by and among the Company and certain holders of the Old Notes and Existing Lenders (as defined below) party thereto. Concurrently with the Exchange Offer and the Consent Solicitation, the Issuer commenced a Dutch auction (the “ Loans Dutch Auction ”) offering each lender (the “ Existing Lenders ”) holding existing term loans (the “ Existing Term Loans ”) under the Existing Credit Agreement (as defined below) the opportunity to have its Existing Term Loans purchased at a price of (a) 86.00% of the principal amount thereof in cash, (b) 100.00% of the principal amount thereof in First Lien Notes or (c) solely if the Issuer elects in its sole discretion, 100.00% of the principal amount thereof in new first lien term loans due 2031 to be incurred under a new credit agreement (the “ New First Lien Term Loan Incurrence ”). On November 20, 2026 (the “ Early Settlement Date ”), the Issuer issued $834,230,440 in aggregate principal amount of First Lien Notes in exchange for (i) the cancellation of $361,860,000 in aggregate principal amount of Old Notes as part of the Exchange Offer, (ii) approximately $381,385,693 in cash as part of the New Money Offer and (iii) the cancellation of approximately $71,022,949 in aggregate principal amount of the Existing Term Loans as part of the Loans Dutch Auction. Following the Early Settlement Date, $38,140,000 in aggregate principal amount of Old Notes remains outstanding. In addition, following the Early Exchange Time (as defined in the Offering Memorandum), an incremental $23,899,000 in aggregate principal amount of Old Notes have been tendered as part of the Exchange Offer and Consent Solicitation, with settlement expected on the Final Settlement Date (as defined in the Offering Memorandum). On the Early Settlement Date, the Issuer purchased approximately $279,913,994 in aggregate principal amount of Existing Term Loans for approximately $240,726,035 in cash as part of the Loans Dutch Auction. No Existing Lender elected to participate in the New First Lien Term Loan Incurrence on the Early Settlement Date. In connection with the foregoing, the Issuer and the Company, as applicable, entered into the agreements described below. New Notes Indenture On the Early Settlement Date, the Issuer entered into that certain indenture (the “ New Notes Indenture ”), by and among the Issuer, the Company, the subsidiary guarantors party thereto and Wilmington Savings Fund Society, FSB, as trustee and notes collateral agent, and issued $834,230,440 in aggregate principal amount of First Lien Notes thereunder as part of the Exchange Offer, the New Money Offer and the Loans Dutch Auction. The First Lien Notes will mature on November 20, 2031. Interest on the First Lien Notes will be payable semi-annually in arrears on May 15 and November 15 of each year, commencing on May 15, 2027. Interest on the First Lien Notes will be payable, at the Issuer’s election in its sole discretion, either (i) at a rate equal to 10.500% per annum, payable solely in cash, or (ii) at an aggregate rate of 11.500% per annum, of which, at the Issuer’s election at its sole discretion (x) no less than 9.000% per annum will be payable in cash and (y) up to 2.500% per annum will be payable in kind, by increasing the principal amount of the outstanding First Lien Notes or by issuing additional First Lien Notes. The First Lien Notes and the related guarantees are secured by (i) perfected first-priority security interests, subject to customary exclusions and the terms of any applicable intercreditor agreements, in substantially all of the Issuer’s and the guarantors’ existing and future assets and property (other than the ABL Priority Collateral (as defined in the New Notes Indenture)) and (ii) junior-priority liens, subject to certain exceptions and permitted liens, on certain assets and property of the Issuer and the guarantors, including the Canadian collateral, that secure, on a first-lien basis, the Issuer’s asset-based loan revolving credit facility. The New Notes Indenture contains covenants that, among other things, limit the ability of the Issuer, the Company and their subsidiaries, subject to certain exceptions, to incur additional indebtedness and guarantee indebtedness; pay dividends, redeem stock or make other distributions; make other restricted payments or investments; create or incur liens on assets; transfer, sell or otherwise dispose of assets; create restrictions on the payment of dividends or other amounts to the Issuer from its subsidiaries; engage in mergers or consolidations; and engage in certain transactions with affiliates. The Issuer may redeem some or all of the First Lien Notes, at its option, (i) at any time prior to November 20, 2028, at a price equal to 100% of the principal amount, plus a “make-whole” premium, (ii) at any time on or after November 20, 2028, but before November 20, 2029, at a redemption price equal to 104.5% of the then-outstanding principal amount of First Lien Notes redeemed, (iii) at any time on or after November 20, 2029, but before November 20, 2030, at a redemption price equal to 102.25% of the then-outstanding principal amount of First Lien Notes redeemed, and (iv) at any time on or after November 20, 2030, at a redemption price equal to 100% of the then-outstanding principal amount of First Lien Notes redeemed, in each case, plus accrued and unpaid interest, if any, to, but excluding, the redemption date. In certain circumstances involving asset sales by the Company, the Issuer or their respective subsidiaries, the Issuer may be required to repurchase the First Lien Notes at the lesser of (x) the applicable redemption price then in effect, plus a “make-whole” premium, if any, and (y) 103.000% of the then-outstanding principal amount of the First Lien Notes to be repurchased. Existing Notes Supplemental Indenture On the Early Settlement Date, following receipt of the requisite consents as part of the Consent Solicitation, the Issuer, the Company, the subsidiary guarantors party thereto and Wilmington Trust, National Association, as trustee, entered into that certain Fourth Supplemental Indenture (the “ Existing Notes Supplemental Indenture ”) to the indenture, dated as of December 14, 2017 (as amended, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time, the “ Old Notes Indenture ”), governing the Old Notes, pursuant to which, among other things, certain amendments to the Old Notes Indenture were adopted, including the elimination of substantially all of the restrictive covenants and certain of the default and events of default provisions, the modification of covenants regarding mergers and consolidations, the modification or elimination of certain other provisions contained in the Old Notes Indenture, including certain provisions relating to defeasance and future guarantors, and the inclusion of certain release provisions in connection with the Exchange Offer, the Consent Solicitation and the related transactions. Amendment No. 9 to the Existing Term Loan Credit Agreement On the Early Settlement Date, the Issuer, as borrower, the Company, the other guarantors party thereto, Bank of America, N.A., as administrative agent and collateral agent, and the lenders party thereto entered into Amendment No. 9 (the “ Existing Credit Agreement Amendment ”) to that certain Term Loan Credit Agreement, dated as of October 15, 2014 (as amended, supplemented, amended and restated or otherwise modified from time to time, the “ Existing Credit Agreement ”), that, among other things, (i) permitted the transactions contemplated by the Commitment Letter, including the issuance of the First Lien Notes and the grant of liens securing the First Lien Notes, (ii) eliminated substantially all of the restrictive covenants and certain events of default, (iii) provided for the subordination (in right of payment and lien priority) of the Existing Term Loans to the First Lien Notes and the ABL Credit Agreement pursuant to a superpriority intercreditor agreement, (iv) permitted the Issuer to elect to create or extend grace periods for payments under the Existing Credit Agreement to a date no later than September 1, 2032 and (v) permitted the exchange of remaining Existing Term Loans for additional first lien indebtedness following the Early Settlement Date (which such exchanges will be subject to the limitations set forth in the New Notes Indenture). The Existing Term Loans continue to mature on July 28, 2028. Amendment No. 9 to the ABL Credit Agreement On the Early Settlement Date, the Issuer, as borrower representative, the Company, JELD-WEN of Canada, Ltd., the other borrowers and subsidiary guarantors party thereto, Wells Fargo Bank, National Association, as administrative agent, and the other lenders party thereto entered into Amendment No. 9 (the “ ABL Amendment ”) to that certain Amended Revolving Credit Agreement, dated as of October 15, 2014 (as amended, supplemented, amended and restated or otherwise modified from time to time, the “ ABL Credit Agreement ”), that, among other things, (i) extended the Scheduled Revolving Termination Date (as defined therein) to November 20, 2031, (ii) permanently reduced the aggregate revolving commitments to $370,000,000, consisting of $350,000,000 of U.S. revolving commitments and $20,000,000 of Canadian revolving commitments (with a corresponding reduction in each lender’s commitment), (iii) added JELD-WEN European Holdings, LLC as a subsidiary guarantor, (iv) provided for the amendment and restatement of the U.S. and Canadian security agreements and the entry into certain other international guarantees and security agreements and (v) amended certain other provisions of the ABL Credit Agreement to accommodate the First Lien Notes, any New First Lien Term Loan Incurrence and the related intercreditor arrangements. In connection with the ABL Amendment, the borrowers prepaid not less than $70,000,000 of outstanding revolving loans under the ABL Credit Agreement.
