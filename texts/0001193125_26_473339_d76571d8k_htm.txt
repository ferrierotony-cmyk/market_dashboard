Other Events. On November 24, 2026, Community Health Systems, Inc. issued a press release announcing the results, as of 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 23, 2026 (the “Early Tender Deadline”), of the private offer by its wholly owned subsidiary, CHS/Community Health Systems, Inc. (the “Issuer”), to exchange (the “Exchange Offer”) any and all of its $1,244,476,000 aggregate principal amount of outstanding 6.875% Junior-Priority Secured Notes due 2029 (the “Old Notes”) for new 10.250% Senior Secured Notes due 2035 (the “New Notes”), and of the related solicitation of consents (the “Consent Solicitation”) from eligible holders of the Old Notes to adopt certain proposed amendments (the “Proposed Amendments”) to the indenture governing the Old Notes. As of the Early Tender Deadline, $1,214,977,000 aggregate principal amount of outstanding Old Notes, representing approximately 97.63% of the outstanding Old Notes, had been validly tendered (and not validly withdrawn) in the Exchange Offer, and the related consents were validly delivered (and not validly revoked) in the Consent Solicitation. In addition, as of the Early Tender Deadline, the condition that certain institutional investors that are holders of the Old Notes validly tender (and not validly withdraw) $643,667,000 aggregate principal amount of the Old Notes held by them (representing approximately 52% of the aggregate principal amount of the Old Notes) had been satisfied. In addition, as of the Early Tender Deadline, the Issuer had received the Requisite Consents (as defined in the Offering Memorandum (as defined below)) to adopt the Proposed Amendments. The Exchange Offer and the Consent Solicitation remain subject to the other conditions set forth in the offering memorandum and consent solicitation statement, dated November 9, 2026 (as amended or supplemented from time to time, the “Offering Memorandum”). Assuming that the other conditions to the Exchange Offer and the Consent Solicitation have been or concurrently are or will be satisfied or waived, the Issuer will accept for exchange all Old Notes validly tendered (and not validly withdrawn) in the Exchange Offer at or prior to 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on December 10, 2026 (the “Expiration Date”), and the settlement date is currently expected to be December 14, 2026.
