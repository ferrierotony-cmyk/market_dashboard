On September 30, LAVA Therapeutics N.V. (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing its intention to cancel the Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders (the “EGM”) scheduled to occur on September 30, 2025 to consider and approve certain matters related to the Company’s previously announced transaction with XOMA Royalty Corporation (“XOMA”), pursuant to which XOMA made an offer to purchase all of the issued and outstanding common shares in the capital of the Company (the “Transaction”). The EGM is expected to be reconvened by early November 2025. The meeting date, record date and location for the reconvened EGM will be determined and announced by the Company at a later date. Votes already cast by shareholders in respect of the EGM will not count towards the voting outcome on the matters proposed to the reconvened EGM. Shareholders as of the record date for the reconvened EGM will have to cast their votes (or cast their votes again, as the case may be) for their votes to count towards the voting outcome.
