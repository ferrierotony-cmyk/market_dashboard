(the “Company”, the “Bank”, or “First Business Bank”) (Nasdaq: FBIZ) reported quarterly net income available to common shareholders of $16.4 million, or earnings per share ("EPS") of $1.95. This compares to net income available to common shareholders of $15.4 million, or $1.84 per share, in the second quarter of 2026 and $14.2 million, or $1.70 per share, in the third quarter of 2025. “Our record third quarter results reflect strong execution across First Business Bank’s relationship-based model,” said Dave Seiler, President and Chief Executive Officer. “Annualized loan growth of approximately 10% was more than fully funded by 14% annualized core deposit growth, while a strong net interest margin and disciplined expense management drove record pre-tax, pre-provision earnings. Through the first nine months of 2026, earnings per share increased 20%, pre-tax, pre-provision earnings increased 14%, and tangible book value per share grew 13% from the prior year.” “We expect expenses to increase from the third-quarter level as we make planned investments in talent, marketing, technology, and infrastructure to support continued growth. These investments are integral to our long-term model of approximately 10% annual balance sheet growth and positive annual operating leverage. We believe consistent execution of our strategic plan, together with disciplined credit and capital management, will continue to compound earnings and tangible book value per share and support strong long-term profitability.” Quarterly Highlights • Record Earnings and Pre-Tax, Pre-Provision ("PTPP") Growth . Net income available to common shareholders reached a record $16.4 million, or $1.95 per diluted share. PTPP adjusted earnings grew to a record $21.3 million, up 7.6% and 13.1% from the linked and prior-year quarters, respectively. • Relationship-Based Deposit Growth Funded Loan Expansion. Core deposits increased by $101.3 million, or 14.1% annualized, from the linked quarter and $386.8 million, or 14.9%, from the prior-year quarter, outpacing loan growth in both periods. Core deposits improved to 76.7% of total bank funding, allowing wholesale funding to decline by $103.1 million. • Continued Loan Growth. Loans increased $88.6 million, or 9.9% annualized, from the linked quarter and $340.2 million, or 10.2%, from the third quarter of 2025. • Strong Net Interest Margin. Net interest margin was 3.80%, compared to 3.78% for the linked quarter and 3.68% for the prior-year quarter. The modest linked improvement was primarily driven by favorable loan yields and an improved funding mix. Net interest margin was 3.72% and 3.68% for the first nine months of 2026 and 2025, respectively. Elevated fees in lieu of interest continued to support the year-over-year comparison. • SBA Exit Improves Operating Efficiency. Operating expenses declined $706,000, or 2.6%, from the linked quarter, primarily due to lower SBA-related compensation costs. The Company expects annual operating leverage to remain modest but positive. • Strong Value Creation. Through the first nine months of 2026, operating revenue increased 9.8% while operating expense increased 6.8%, driving a 14.2% increase in pre-tax, pre-provision earnings and 20% EPS growth.
