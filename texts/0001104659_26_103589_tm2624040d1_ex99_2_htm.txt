LETTER TO SECURITYHOLDERS Dear unitholders and shareholders: We are pleased to announce the proposed simplification of our corporate structure, pursuant to which holders of equity interests of Brookfield Infrastructure Partners L.P. (“ BIP ”) would exchange their interests for shares in a publicly traded Canadian corporation, Brookfield Infrastructure Partners Inc. (“ BIP Inc. ”), and each of BIP and Brookfield Infrastructure Corporation (“ BIPC ” and, together with BIP, “ Brookfield Infrastructure ”) would become subsidiaries of BIP Inc. (the “ Simplification ”). Holders of limited partnership units of BIP (“ BIP Units ”, such holders, “ BIP Unitholders ”) and class A exchangeable subordinate voting shares of BIPC (“ BIPC Exchangeable Shares ”, such holders, “ BIPC Shareholders ”) will vote on whether to exchange their BIP Units and BIPC Exchangeable Shares, respectively, for class A subordinate voting shares of BIP Inc. (“ BIP Inc. Class A Shares ”). If BIP Unitholders approve the Simplification but BIPC Shareholders do not, the BIP Units will be exchanged for BIP Inc. Class A Shares and the BIPC Exchangeable Shares will remain outstanding and become exchangeable into BIP Inc. Class A Shares. The special meeting of BIP Unitholders will be held virtually on October 14, 2026 at 9:00am (Toronto time), and the special meeting of BIPC Shareholders will be held virtually on October 14, 2026 at 10:00am (Toronto time). Why you should approve the Simplification The Simplification generally is expected to be tax-deferred for Canadian and U.S. investors and completed without any meaningful cost to the business, while providing securityholders with the following benefits, among others: · improved consolidated trading liquidity through a single listed security; · increased demand from current indices and potential additional index inclusion; · stronger alignment with long-term capital allocation trends toward indexable and ETF-eligible corporate securities; · simplified investor analysis, screening, and benchmarking through a single listed reporting entity; · broader access to a larger pool of investors who prefer corporate structures; · enhanced governance framework and voting rights for Brookfield Infrastructure’s public securityholders; and · for BIP Unitholders, preferential dividend tax rates and elimination of onerous partnership tax reporting forms for many Canadian and U.S. taxable investors. Taxable Canadian BIP Unitholders will need to file a simple tax election to obtain tax-deferred treatment, which can be facilitated through www.bip.taxelection.ca . Overview of the Simplification Subject to the approval of BIP Unitholders: · BIP Inc., a corporation formed under the laws of British Columbia, will have a capital structure that includes BIP Inc. Class A Shares, class B multiple voting shares (“ BIP Inc. Class B Shares ”), class I non-voting incentive shares (“ BIP Inc. Class I Shares ”), and class A preferred shares. · Holders of BIP Units, Brookfield Infrastructure L.P. (“ Holding LP ”) redemption-exchange limited partner units, Brookfield Infrastructure Holdings Corporation class A.2 exchangeable non-voting shares and exchangeable limited partnership units of Brookfield Infrastructure Partners Exchange LP will receive, directly or indirectly, one (1) BIP Inc. Class A Share for each security held. · The shares of the BIP general partner and the Holding LP special general partner units will be exchanged, directly or indirectly, for BIP Inc. Class B Shares and BIP Inc. Class I Shares, respectively. · BIP will become a subsidiary of BIP Inc. ii · Subject to Toronto Stock Exchange (“ TSX ”) and New York Stock Exchange (“ NYSE ”) approval, the BIP Inc. Class A Shares will be listed on the TSX and NYSE and trade under the symbol “BIP”, the BIP Units will be delisted, and the preferred units of BIP will continue to be listed on the TSX and NYSE. Subject to the approval of BIPC Shareholders: · Holders of BIPC Exchangeable Shares and Class B exchangeable limited partnership units of Brookfield Infrastructure Corporation Exchange Limited Partnership will receive one (1) BIP Inc. Class A Share for each security held; and · BIPC will be delisted and cease to be a reporting issuer. The Simplification will be implemented pursuant to a court-approved plan of arrangement under the laws of the Province of British Columbia. Recommended Action Special committees of the boards of directors of each of the general partner of BIP and BIPC (collectively, the “ Boards ”) were formed to oversee and assess the terms of the Simplification. The BIP and BIPC special committees have unanimously determined that the Simplification is in the best interests of BIP and BIPC, respectively, and have recommended that the Boards approve the Simplification and recommend that all security holders vote in favor of the Simplification and the transactions contemplated thereby. The Boards 1 , on the recommendation of each of their respective special committees, have unanimously determined that the Simplification is in the best interests of each of BIP and BIPC, as applicable, and resolved to approve the Simplification and recommend that BIP Unitholders and BIPC Shareholders vote in favor of the resolutions approving the Simplification and the amendment to the BIP limited partnership agreement to provide for the Simplification, as applicable. In making their determinations, the special committees and the Boards considered, among other factors, the fairness opinion of Scotia Capital Inc. to the effect that, as of July 21, 2026 and subject to the assumptions, limitations and qualifications described therein, the consideration to be received by public holders of BIP Units and by public holders of BIPC Exchangeable Shares is fair, from a financial point of view, to such public holders. Cautionary Statement Regarding the Use of Non-IFRS Measures The financial information of BIP Inc., BIP and BIPC contained in this Circular and the documents incorporated by reference in this Circular has been prepared in accordance with IFRS Accounting Standards as issued by the International Accounting Standards Board (“ IFRS Accounting Standards ”). To measure performance, we focus on net income, a measure under IFRS Accounting Standards, as well are certain non-IFRS measures. We disclose a number of non-IFRS measures in this Circular and the documents incorporated by reference herein, including Funds from Operations (“ FFO ”), along with other measures. FFO We define FFO as net income excluding the impact of certain non-cash items including depreciation and amortization, deferred income taxes, mark-to-market gains (losses) and other income (expenses) that are not related to normal revenue earning activities or that are not normal, recurring cash operating expenses necessary for business operations. FFO is not adjusted for the income (loss) earned by data center developers which is generated through the development, commercialization, and sale of completed sites. The inclusion of this income reflects the operating performance of such investments and includes income (or losses) recognized in the current and prior periods. FFO also includes balances attributable to the partnership generated by investments in associates and joint ventures accounted for using the equity method and excludes amounts attributable to non-controlling interests based on the economic interests held by non-controlling interests in consolidated subsidiaries. FFO is a measure of operating performance that is not calculated in accordance with, and does not have any standardized meaning prescribed by IFRS Accounting Standards. FFO is therefore unlikely to be comparable to similar measures presented by other issuers. FFO has limitations as an analytical tool. (“ NAREIT ”), in part because the NAREIT definition is based on U.S. GAAP, as opposed to IFRS Accounting Standards. See “The Transaction—Exchange Procedures”. BIP Inc. and the BIP Inc. Class A Shares following the Transaction What will be the capital structure of BIP Inc. after completion of the Transaction? BIP Inc. will be authorized to issue four classes of shares: (i) an unlimited number of Class A Subordinate Voting Shares; (ii) an unlimited number of Class B Multiple Voting Shares; (iii) an unlimited number of Class I Non-Voting Incentive Shares; and (iv) an unlimited number of Class A Preferred Shares, issuable in series. No series of BIP Inc. Class A Preferred Shares will be issued initially. The BIP Inc. Class A Shares will be structured to be economically equivalent to the BIP units and BIP exchangeable securities. The BIP Inc. Class B Shares will represent Brookfield’s voting interest in our group and will be received in exchange for the BIP General Partner Shares. The BIP Inc. Class A Shares will have one vote per share and the BIP Inc. Class B Shares as a class will be entitled to cast, in the aggregate, a number of votes equal to the number of outstanding BIP Inc. Class A Shares minus 100. The aggregate number of votes attached to the BIP Inc. Class B Shares will be allocated to the outstanding BIP Inc. Class B Shares on a pro rata basis. As a result, the holders of the BIP Inc. Class A Shares will control more than 50% of the aggregate voting rights of BIP Inc. The BIP Inc. Class I Shares will represent BAM’s current entitlement to receive incentive distributions from Brookfield Infrastructure. See “The Transaction—Background to the Transaction” and “Information Concerning BIP Inc.—Share Capital”. Do you intend to pay dividends on the BIP Inc. Class A Shares? Yes. Following completion of the Transaction, BIP Inc. is expected to pay a dividend that is sustainable on a long-term basis and is consistent with the current BIP distribution policy. Following the completion of the Transaction, the BIP Inc. Board may declare dividends at its discretion. BIP Inc. will adopt a dividend policy substantially similar to BIP’s distribution policy. Dividends on the BIP Inc. Class A Shares and BIP Inc. Class B Shares will be subject to the prior rights of the holders of the BIP Inc. Class A Preferred Shares (if any), and any shares ranking senior to the BIP Inc. Class A Shares and BIP Inc. Class B Shares. See also “Information Concerning BIP Inc.—Dividend Policy”. What will happen to my participation in BIP’s Distribution Reinvestment Plan? BIP Inc. is expected to adopt a dividend reinvestment plan (DRIP) that is substantially similar to the distribution reinvestment plan currently available to Unitholders. Registered Unitholders who currently participate in BIP’s DRIP will be required to re-enroll in the BIP Inc. DRIP following completion of the Transaction. Will there be any significant shareholders of BIP Inc. after the Transaction? Yes. Brookfield, which currently holds a controlling interest in our group, will be a significant shareholder of BIP Inc. Other than Brookfield, there will be no other significant shareholders of BIP Inc. upon completion of the Transaction.
