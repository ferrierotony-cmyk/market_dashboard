Amid concerns over exchange hacks, such moves underscore evolving financial strategies. CoinDesk reports: Charlotte, North Carolina, October 28 /PRNewswire/ -- JELD-WEN Holding, Inc. (NYSE: JELD, referred to as “JELD-WEN” or the “Company”) today announced that its subsidiary, JELD-WEN, Inc. (the “Issuer”), has commenced an offer to exchange (the “Exchange Offer”) all outstanding 4.875% Senior Notes due 2027 (the “Old Notes”) for new first-priority senior secured notes due 2031 (the “New Notes”) to all eligible holders (as defined below); and to offer eligible participants in the Exchange Offer (the “Exchange Offer Participants”) the option to subscribe for their pro rata portion of up to $410.2 million in aggregate principal amount of New Notes (the “New Money Offer”; participants in the New Money Offer are referred to as “New Money Participants,” and eligible holders who do not participate in the New Money Offer are referred to as “Non-New Money Participants”). The details are set forth below. Capitalized terms used herein but not defined have the meanings assigned to them in the Confidential Offering Memorandum and Consent Solicitation Statement dated October 28, 2026 (the “Offering Memorandum”). The table below sets forth the applicable early exchange consideration (as defined below) and late exchange consideration (as defined below) offered to eligible holders of the old notes in the exchange offer: In addition, the issuer is seeking the consent (“Consent”) of holders of the old notes eligible for such consent to make certain proposed amendments (“Proposed Amendments”) to the indenture governing the old notes, dated December 14, 2017 (as amended, supplemented, or otherwise modified from time to time, the “Old Note Indenture”), to substantially remove nearly all restrictive covenants and certain default and event of default provisions, modify covenants relating to mergers and consolidations, and amend or delete other specified provisions, including certain provisions relating to the release of guarantees and future guarantors (“Consent Solicitation”), subject to the terms and conditions set forth in the offering memorandum. Several holders of the commitment letter have agreed: (i) holders representing approximately 94.5% of the aggregate principal amount of the old notes have agreed to tender their old notes prior to the early tender date of the exchange offer and to provide consent to the proposed amendments in the consent solicitation; (ii) holders representing approximately 70.0% of the aggregate principal amount of the old notes have committed (such commitment, the “backstop commitment”) to purchase, at a price of $1,000 per $1,000 principal amount, their respective pro rata portion of up to $410.2 million in new notes, and to fund such purchase on the backstop funding date; this backstop commitment shall also apply if eligible holders under the new money offer fail to subscribe for and fund the full amount, including due to failure to deliver the full purchase price to the exchange agent on the funding date. The aggregate principal amount of new notes issued pursuant to the new money offer will be reduced on a dollar-for-dollar basis depending on whether existing term loan holders elect to purchase under the Dutch auction. In conjunction with the exchange offer and solicitation of consents, and pursuant to the commitment letter, the issuer has initiated a Dutch auction (the “Loan Dutch Auction”) offering each holder of the existing term loan the following options: (A) 86.00% in cash of the principal amount of the existing term loan (“Existing Term Loan Purchase”), (B) 100.00% of the principal amount of the existing term loan in exchange for new notes, or (C), solely at the issuer’s discretion, 100.00% of the principal amount of the existing term loan in exchange for new first-priority term loans maturing in 2031. Approximately 72.2% of the aggregate principal amount of the existing term loans has been committed by lenders who have signed the commitment letter and agreed to participate in the Loan Dutch Auction, while also consenting to amendments to the existing credit agreement to, among other things: (A) permit the transactions contemplated by the commitment letter; (B) eliminate substantially all restrictive covenants and certain events of default under the existing term loan credit agreement; and (C) subordinate the existing term loan in priority of payment and lien seniority to the new notes. The exchange offer and consent solicitation will expire at 5:00 p.m., New York time, on November 27, 2026, unless extended or earlier terminated (the “Expiration Time”). Holders who validly tender their Old Notes prior to 5:00 p.m., New York time, on November 10, 2026, and do not validly withdraw them, will receive from the Issuer, for each $1,000 principal amount of Old Notes tendered: (i) the applicable Early Tender Consideration (the “Early Tender Consideration”) set forth in the table above for the portion of Old Notes validly tendered and accepted for exchange on or prior to the Early Tender Time; and (ii) the Late Tender Consideration (the “Late Tender Consideration”) set forth in the table above for the portion of Old Notes validly tendered and accepted for exchange after the Early Tender Time but on or prior to the Expiration Time. No consideration will be paid for the consent solicitation. Tenders of Old Notes may be withdrawn prior to the Withdrawal Deadline, and consents may be revoked prior to such time, but may not be withdrawn or revoked thereafter, except as otherwise required by applicable law; provided that if the Issuer elects to extend the Withdrawal Deadline, such provisions shall not apply. To obtain new notes in connection with the new money offer, each participating eligible holder (excluding backstop parties) (a “Non-Backstop New Money Participant”) must pay, by wire transfer to the Exchange Agent, the full purchase price (the “Purchase Price”) for its pro rata share, as calculated on the payment schedule (the “Payment Schedule”) attached to the New Money Subscription Form in the Offering Memorandum, prior to 5:00 p.m. (New York time) on November 10, 2026, to subscribe for up to $410.2 million in aggregate principal amount of new notes (the “New Money Note Offering”). The Payment Schedule is accessible via the website administered by the Exchange Agent at https://deals.is.kroll.com/JeldWen. “Pro rata share” means, for any exchange participant, (i)(x) the total dollar amount of old notes tendered by such participant divided by (y) $400 million, multiplied by (ii) $410.2 million (calculated on the Payment Schedule as $1.025415 per $1,000 principal amount, rounded down to the nearest whole dollar for each batch of old notes submitted). Each New Money Participant is entitled to subscribe for its pro rata share of the New Money Offer, but not less than its pro rata share. For clarity, any eligible holder that does not participate in all parts of the Exchange Offer is not eligible to participate in the New Money Offer. Each participating eligible holder must tender all of its old notes; partial tenders are not accepted. If the non-backup new fund participants do not select a total amount of existing term loans to purchase that equals the full available allocation in the loan Dutch auction, they may not receive their full pro-rata share of the new notes; in such cases, these participants will receive a refund equivalent to the amount of the shortfall. As stated in the Offering Memorandum, each Backstop Party is obligated to contribute a First Lien Note, including any shortfall amount, on the Backstop Funding Date set forth in the Backstop Funding Notice, at least four business days prior to such Backstop Funding Date, as part of the Backstop Funding Offer. Any eligible holder (excluding backup new funds participants) who (i) does not subscribe to the full amount of their pro rata share of the new funds offering, or (ii) fails to complete such contribution on the contribution date (or on the backup funds date, if a backup party), shall be entitled to receive new notes with a principal amount of $930 for every $1,000 principal amount of old notes that were validly submitted prior to or at the early exchange time and not validly withdrawn. The interest rate on the new notes shall be determined by the issuer and may be: (i) an annual interest rate of 10.500%, paid entirely in cash; or (ii) an aggregate annual interest rate of 11.500%, at the issuer’s discretion, (x) at least 9.000% per annum paid in cash, and (y) up to 2.500% per annum paid by increasing the outstanding principal amount of one or more new notes. Interest on the new notes shall be paid semi-annually in arrears. Holders may not submit old notes pursuant to the exchange offer without simultaneously submitting their consent regarding the old notes submitted; similarly, they may not submit consent pursuant to the consent solicitation without simultaneously submitting old notes pursuant to the exchange offer. The completion of the exchange offer, consent solicitation, and issuance of new notes is subject to and conditioned upon the issuer’s satisfaction or waiver of the financing conditions and general conditions. The exchange offer is made to holders of the old notes who are within the United States and reasonably believed to be “qualified institutional buyers” as defined in Rule 144A under the U.S. Securities Act of 1933, as amended, and to holders of the old notes outside the United States who are not U.S. persons and are acting in compliance with Regulation S under the Securities Act; the new notes are also issued within the above scope (collectively referred to as “Eligible Holders” for items (i) and (ii)). Only holders of the old notes who provide written certification that they are Eligible Holders are authorized to participate in the exchange offer, the new funds offer, and the solicitation of consents. The issuer urges holders of the old notes to carefully read the entire offering memorandum, including the sections titled “Risk Factors” and “Cautionary Note Regarding Forward-Looking Statements,” as well as the documents incorporated by reference into the offering memorandum, including the company’s audited consolidated financial statements (and notes) as of December 31, 2025, and the unaudited condensed consolidated financial statements (and notes) for the quarters ended March 28, 2026, and June 27, 2026, as set forth in the company’s Form 10-Q filings submitted to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), before making any decision regarding the new funds offer, exchange offer, or consent solicitation. The issuer, Kroll Issuer Services (US), as exchange agent (“Exchange Agent”) and information agent (“Information Agent”), and the trustee and note collateral agent under the old notes and new notes indenture (if applicable), or their respective affiliates, do not make any recommendation regarding whether eligible holders of the old notes should participate in the new funds offer, submit old notes pursuant to the exchange offer, or deliver consents pursuant to the consent solicitation. Each eligible holder must independently determine whether to participate in the new funds offer, whether to submit old notes and deliver consents, and, if participating, the principal amount of old notes involved. The exchange offer, new funds offer, new notes, and related guarantees have not been registered under the Securities Act or any other applicable securities laws. Unless registered, the new notes may not be offered, sold, pledged, or otherwise transferred within the United States, or to or for the account or benefit of U.S. persons (as defined in Rule 902 under the Securities Act), except pursuant to an exemption from, or transaction not subject to, the registration requirements of the Securities Act and other applicable securities laws. Kroll Issuer Services (US) has been appointed as the exchange agent and information agent. For questions regarding the exchange offer, the new funds offer, and the solicitation of consents, please contact the exchange agent and information agent using the contact information listed on the back of the offering memorandum. Regarding JELD-WEN Holding, Inc. JELD-WEN Holding, Inc. (NYSE: JELD) is a leading global designer, manufacturer, and distributor of high-performance interior and exterior doors, windows, and related building products for new construction and renovation markets. Headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, the company operates manufacturing facilities across 14 countries in North America and Europe, with approximately 13,900 employees dedicated to bringing beauty and safety to the spaces where people live. The JELD-WEN brand family includes the global brand JELD-WEN®, LaCantina® and VPI™ in North America, and Swedoor® and DANA® in Europe.
