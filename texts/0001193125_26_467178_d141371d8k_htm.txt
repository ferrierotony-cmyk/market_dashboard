Other Events. On November 6, 2026, Dana Incorporated, a Delaware corporation (the “Company”), announced that it had commenced offers to purchase for cash (collectively, the “Tender Offers”) any and all of the Company’s outstanding (i) 4.250% Senior Notes due 2030 (the “2030 Notes”) and (ii) 4.500% Senior Notes due 2032 (the “2032 Notes” and, together with the 2030 Notes, the “Notes”). The Tender Offers expired at 5:00 PM, New York City time, on November 13, 2026 (the “Expiration Time”). $181,151,000 aggregate principal amount of the 2030 Notes and $176,722,000 aggregate principal amount of the 2032 Notes were validly tendered and not validly withdrawn as of the Expiration Time. On November 17, 2026 (the “Settlement Date”), the Company accepted for purchase such 2030 Notes and 2032 Notes and paid $176,672,947.28 for such 2030 Notes and $172,542,524.70 for such 2032 Notes plus, in each case, accrued and unpaid interest on such Notes from the last interest payment date of such Notes to, but not including, the Settlement Date. The Company funded the purchase of the Notes in the Tender Offers with borrowings under its revolving credit facility. Following the settlement of the Tender Offers, $45,649,000 aggregate principal amount of the 2030 Notes and $21,730,000 aggregate principal amount of the 2032 Notes remain outstanding. On November 18, 2026, with respect to the outstanding Notes not validly tendered and accepted for purchase in the Tender Offers, the Company defeased certain covenants (the “covenant defeasance”) under that certain Indenture, dated as of January 28, 2011, by and between the Company and Computershare Trust Company, N.A., as successor in interest to Wells Fargo Bank, National Association, as trustee (the “Trustee”), as supplemented by (i) that certain Seventh Supplemental Indenture, dated as of May 13, 2021 (the “Seventh Supplemental Indenture”), by and between the Company and the Trustee, governing the 2030 Notes, and (ii) that certain Ninth Supplemental Indenture, dated as of November 24, 2021 (the “Ninth Supplemental Indenture”), by and between the Company and the Trustee, governing the 2032 Notes. To effect the covenant defeasance, the Company irrevocably deposited with the Trustee, in trust, for the benefit of the holders of the outstanding Notes, U.S. Government Obligations (as defined in each of the Seventh Supplemental Indenture and the Ninth Supplemental Indenture) in amounts sufficient to pay the principal of, premium, if any, and interest on such Notes on the dates on which any such payments are due and payable in accordance with the terms of each of the Seventh Supplemental Indenture and the Ninth Supplemental Indenture and of the applicable Notes. As a result of the covenant defeasance, the Company will not be required to comply with any covenants with respect to the Notes (except for the covenants requiring payment of principal of and interest on the Notes and the Company’s maintenance of an office or agency), and certain events of default will be deemed not to be events of default with respect to the Notes, in each case effective 90 days following the deposit described above. 1 SIGNATURES Pursuant to the requirements of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, the registrant has duly caused this report to be signed on its behalf by the undersigned hereunto duly authorized.
