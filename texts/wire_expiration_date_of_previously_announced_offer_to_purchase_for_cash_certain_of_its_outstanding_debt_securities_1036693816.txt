Dover also announced that it will extend the Expiration Date of the Tender Offers. The Tender Offers are being made upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase dated October 26, 2026 (the “Offer to Purchase”). The Expiration Date for the Tender Offers has been extended to 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 5, 2026, unless further extended or earlier terminated by Dover with respect to any Tender Offer. Tenders of Notes of a Series may be validly withdrawn at any time at or prior to 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 5, 2026, but, except as provided in the Offer to Purchase or required by applicable law, may not be validly withdrawn thereafter. The Price Determination Date (as defined below) has correspondingly been extended to 3:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 5, 2026. The “Settlement Date” will be the second business day after the Expiration Date and is expected to be November 9, 2026. The Notes offered to be purchased in the Tender Offers, in the order of acceptance priority, are the 6.650% Debentures due 2028; 6.600% Notes due 2038; 5.375% Debentures due 2035; and 5.375% Notes due 2041 (collectively, the “Notes”) for the consideration described below, up to the Tender Cap, subject to proration and the application of the Acceptance Priority Levels set forth in the table below and as further set forth in the Offer to Purchase. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this press release have the meanings given to them in the Offer to Purchase. As of 12:30 p.m., New York City time, on October 30, 2026, approximately 28.86% of the aggregate principal amount of the 2028 Debentures, approximately 30.59% of the aggregate principal amount of the 2038 Notes, approximately 39.78% of the aggregate principal amount of the 2035 Debentures and approximately 30.83% of the aggregate principal amount of the 2041 Notes have been validly tendered in the applicable Tender Offers. Subject to applicable law, Dover may, but is under no obligation to, eliminate, further increase or decrease the Tender Cap at any time prior to the Expiration Date. In the event proration is required with respect to a Series of Notes, Dover will multiply the principal amount of each valid tender of such Series of Notes by the applicable proration rate and round the resulting amount down to the nearest integral multiple of the respective minimum denomination, in order to determine the principal amount of such tender that will be accepted pursuant to the applicable Tender Offer. The excess principal amount of Notes not accepted from the tendering Holders will be promptly returned to such Holders, and if this excess principal amount of Notes is less than the respective minimum denomination of such Series of Notes, Dover may either accept or reject all such tendering Holders’ validly tendered Notes in its sole discretion. Additionally, Dover may, but is under no obligation to, accept for purchase additional Notes of any Series in an aggregate principal amount not exceeding 2% of the outstanding aggregate principal amount of such Series without amending or extending the applicable Tender Offer, subject to applicable law, as further described in the Offer to Purchase, and may also further increase or decrease the percentage of the Notes accepted for payment in the Tender Offers (including by more than 2% of the outstanding aggregate principal amount of the Notes of the applicable Series) by a press release or other public announcement that is widely disseminated by no later than 9:00 a.m. (New York City time) on the third business day before the Expiration Date. The “Total Consideration” for each Series of Notes payable per each $1,000 principal amount of such Series of Notes validly tendered for purchase will be based on the applicable Fixed Spread for such Series of Notes, plus the Reference Yield based on the bid-side price of the applicable Reference Security as quoted on the applicable Bloomberg Reference Page as of 3:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 5, 2026, unless further extended by Dover with respect to the applicable Tender Offer (such date and time with respect to a Tender Offer, as the same may be extended by Dover with respect to such Tender Offer, the “Price Determination Date”). Unless extended with respect to any Tender Offer, promptly after the Price Determination Date, Dover will announce in a press release, among other things, the Offer Yield and Total Consideration applicable to each Series of Notes accepted for purchase. In addition to the applicable Total Consideration, Holders whose Notes are accepted for purchase pursuant to a Tender Offer will receive accrued and unpaid interest on those Notes from the last interest payment date with respect to those Notes to, but excluding, the Settlement Date (the “Accrued Interest,” and the payment thereof, the “Accrued Coupon Payment”). The Tender Offers are subject to certain conditions as described in the Offer to Purchase, including the Financing Condition, which requires that Dover shall have completed an offering (the “New Notes Offering”) of a new series of senior unsecured notes on terms and conditions satisfactory to Dover that results in its receipt of net proceeds that are sufficient to repay its €600,000,000 1.25% notes due 2026 at maturity. The New Notes Offering is expected to close on November 2, 2026, subject to customary closing conditions. In no event will the information contained in this press release regarding the New Notes Offering constitute an offer to sell or a solicitation of an offer to buy any new notes. If any condition is not satisfied, Dover is not obligated to accept for payment, purchase or pay for, and may delay the acceptance for payment of, any tendered Notes, in each case subject to applicable law, and may terminate or alter any or all of the Tender Offers. The Tender Offers are not contingent upon the tender of any aggregate minimum principal amount of Notes of any Series (subject to minimum denomination requirements as set forth in the Offer to Purchase), and none of the Tender Offers is conditioned on the consummation of any of the other Tender Offers.
