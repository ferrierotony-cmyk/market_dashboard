On October 30, 2026, Sumisho Air Lease Corporation (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing that it has commenced an offer to exchange (the “Exchange Offer”) any and all of its $800,000,000 in aggregate principal amount of outstanding 4.400% Senior Notes due 2028 (the “Outstanding 2028 Notes”), $1,200,000,000 in aggregate principal amount of outstanding 4.500% Senior Notes due 2029 (the “Outstanding 2029 Notes”), $1,500,000,000 in aggregate principal amount of outstanding 4.850% Senior Notes due 2031 (the “Outstanding 2031 Notes”) and $500,000,000 in aggregate principal amount of outstanding 5.500% Senior Notes due 2036 (the “Outstanding 2036 Notes” and, together with the Outstanding 2028 Notes, the Outstanding 2029 Notes and the Outstanding 2031 Notes, the “Outstanding Notes”) for an equal principal amount of the applicable series of its registered 4.400% Senior Notes due 2028 (the “2028 Exchange Notes”), 4.500% Senior Notes due 2029 (the “2029 Exchange Notes”), 4.850% Senior Notes due 2031 (the “2031 Exchange Notes”) and 5.500% Senior Notes due 2036 (the “2036 Exchange Notes” and, together with the 2028 Exchange Notes, the 2029 Exchange Notes and the 2031 Exchange Notes, the “Exchange Notes”). The Exchange Offer will expire at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on December 1, 2026, unless extended. The terms of the Exchange Notes are substantially identical to those of the applicable series of the Outstanding Notes, except that the transfer restrictions, restrictive legends, registration rights and additional interest provisions relating to the Outstanding Notes do not apply to the Exchange Notes. The purpose of the Exchange Offer is to fulfill the Company’s obligations under the registration rights agreement entered into in connection with the issuance of the Outstanding Notes. The Company will not receive any proceeds from the Exchange Offer.
