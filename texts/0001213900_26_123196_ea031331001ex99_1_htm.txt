El Vivero - Santiago de Surco Tel: 317-6000 Lima, November 23, 2026 Sirs Superintendencia del Mercado de Valores – SMV Lima.- Reference : Results of the Offers Pursuant to Article 30 of the Consolidated Text of the Securities Market Law and the Regulations on Material Events and Reserved Information, approved by SMV Resolution No. 005-2014-SMV/01, we hereby inform you of the following: In connection with the subsequent public tender offer launched by Inversiones Aspi S.A. (the “Offeror”) for the voting common shares issued by Cementos Pacasmayo S.A.A. (the “Company”) (the “Peruvian Tender Offer”), and the simultaneous offer in the United States of America directed to holders of voting common shares issued by the Company who are residents of the United States of America and holders of American Depositary Shares (“ADSs”) representing such shares (the “U.S. Offer” and, together with the Peruvian Tender Offer, the “Offers”), we hereby report the results of the Offers: i. Peruvian Tender Offer : Today, the Lima Stock Exchange awarded the Offeror a total of 142,297,344 (One Hundred Forty-Two Million Two Hundred Ninety-Seven Thousand Three Hundred Forty-Four) voting common shares, representing 33.57% of the Company's share capital. This information is set forth in the notice published today by the Lima Stock Exchange on its website (www.bvl.com.pe). ii. U.S. Offer : Upon expiration of the U.S. Offer, a total of 42,663,590 (Forty-Two Million Six Hundred Sixty-Three Thousand Five Hundred Ninety) common shares, represented by 8,532,718 ADSs, equivalent to 10.07% of the Company's share capital, were validly tendered and not withdrawn. This information is set forth in Amendment No. 2 1 to the Tender Offer Statement and Rule 13e-3 Transaction Statement on Schedule TO , filed today by the Offeror and Holcim Ltd. with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”).
