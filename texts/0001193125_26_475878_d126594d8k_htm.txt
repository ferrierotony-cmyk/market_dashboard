(the “Company”) completed the public offer and sale of $750,000,000 aggregate principal amount of its 6.161% Fixed-to-Floating Rate Senior Notes due 2032 (the “Notes”). The Notes were issued pursuant to a Senior Debt Indenture, dated as of April 19, 2011 (the “Senior Debt Indenture”), between the Company and Deutsche Bank Trust Company Americas, as trustee (the “Trustee”), as amended by an Eighth Supplemental Indenture, dated as of March 1, 2017 (the “Eighth Supplemental Indenture”), between the Company and the Trustee and as supplemented by a Thirty-Eighth Supplemental Indenture, dated as of November 24, 2026 (the “Thirty-Eighth Supplemental Indenture”), between the Company and the Trustee. The Notes were sold pursuant to an underwriting agreement, dated as of November 16, 2026 (the “Underwriting Agreement”), between the Company and Santander US Capital Markets LLC, BofA Securities, Inc., Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC, MUFG Securities Americas Inc. and TD Securities (USA) LLC, as representatives of the several underwriters listed therein. The Underwriting Agreement, the Senior Debt Indenture, the Eighth Supplemental Indenture, the Thirty-Eighth Supplemental Indenture and the Notes are more fully described in the prospectus supplement filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “Commission”) on November 18, 2026 to the accompanying prospectus filed with the Commission on December 15, 2023 as part of the Company’s Registration Statement on Form S-3 (File No. 333-276089) (the “Registration Statement”). The Underwriting Agreement, the Senior Debt Indenture, the Eighth Supplemental Indenture, the Thirty-Eighth Supplemental Indenture and the form of the Notes are attached to this Current Report on Form 8-K as Exhibits 1.1, 4.1, 4.2, 4.3 and 4.4, respectively, and are incorporated into this Item 8.01 by reference.
