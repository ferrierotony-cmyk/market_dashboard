On October 9, 2026, Cavitation Technologies, Inc. (the “Company” or “CVAT”) and Alchemy Beverages Inc., a privately held Delaware corporation in which the Company holds a 17% equity stake, entered into definitive tender offer agreements with BrightFlixx Finance (Luxembourg) S.A. (“BrightFlixx”), a Luxembourg société anonyme. One agreement relates to the Company (the “BF-CVAT Agreement”), and the other relates to Alchemy Beverages Inc. (the “BF-ABI Agreement”). Together, the agreements are referred to as the “BrightFlixx Agreements.” The BF-CVAT Agreement replaces the Company’s tender offer agreement dated August 14, 2026, with European Guarantee Services S.à r.l. (“EGS”), and the BF-ABI Agreement replaces the tender offer agreement dated August 19, 2026, between EGS and Alchemy. The BF-CVAT Agreement Under the BF-CVAT Agreement. BrightFlixx intends to commence a cash tender offer for all outstanding shares of the Company’s common stock (the “CVAT Shares”) for an aggregate purchase price of $35 million in cash, less the Company’s outstanding liabilities, divided by the total number of CVAT Shares outstanding at the close of a Record Date. The Record Date will be fixed at 45 business days after commencement of the offer. The BF-CVAT Agreement provides that BrightFlixx’s offer to acquire all of the outstanding CVAT Shares will be subject to the satisfaction or waiver of certain conditions. The tender offer has not commenced as of the date of this report. The offer period will initially be scheduled to expire at 6:00 p.m., New York City time, on the date that is 60 business days after commencement of the offer, subject to extensions as provided in the agreement. Once the final offer price is determined following the Record Date and is publicly announced, the offer period will be extended as necessary in order to provide shareholders with at least 30 business days to review the final offer price and decide whether to tender their CVAT Shares. The tender offer for CVAT Shares will be made only pursuant to the offer to purchase, letter of transmittal, and other tender-offer documents filed with the SEC. The BF-CVAT Agreement contains customary representations, warranties, and covenants, together with specific termination provisions and conditions. The key conditions that must be satisfied or waived before BrightFlixx is obligated to purchase CVAT Shares tendered to the offer include a valid tender of at least 90% of the outstanding CVAT Shares, receipt of regulatory approval or a no further action determination by the Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States (“CFIUS”), and the concurrent consummation of the Alchemy tender offer. BrightFlixx’s obligation to complete the proposed transaction will also be subject to other conditions described in the agreement. The BF-ABI Agreement Under the BF-ABI Agreement, BrightFlixx intends to commence a cash tender offer for all outstanding shares of Alchemy common stock (the “ABI Shares”) for an aggregate purchase price of $7 million in cash, less Alchemy’s outstanding liabilities, divided by the total number of ABI Shares outstanding at the close of a Record Date. The Record Date will be fixed at 45 business days after commencement of the offer. The BF-ABI Agreement provides that BrightFlixx’s offer to acquire all outstanding ABI Shares will be subject to the satisfaction or waiver of certain conditions. The tender offer has not commenced as of the date of this report. The offer period will initially be scheduled to expire at 6:00 p.m., New York City time, on the date that is 60 business days after commencement of the offer, subject to extensions as provided in the agreement. Once the final offer price is determined following the Record Date and is publicly announced, the offer period will be extended as necessary in order to provide shareholders with at least 30 business days to review the final offer price and decide whether to tender their ABI Shares. The tender offer for ABI Shares will be made only pursuant to the offer to purchase, letter of transmittal, and other tender-offer documents filed with the SEC. The BF-ABI Agreement contains customary representations, warranties, and covenants, together with specific termination provisions and conditions. The key conditions that must be satisfied or waived before BrightFlixx is obligated to purchase ABI Shares tendered to the offer include a valid tender of at least 90% of the outstanding ABI Shares, receipt of regulatory approval or a no further action determination by CFIUS, and the concurrent consummation of the tender offer for CVAT Shares under the BF-CVAT Agreement. The description is not complete and is subject to the actual terms of the agreements. Item 1.02 Termination of a Material Definitive Agreement. On October 8, 2026, the Company terminated the tender offer agreement dated August 14, 2026, between the Company and European Guarantee Services, S.a.r.l. (“EGS”). The agreement was terminated and replaced by the BF-CVAT Agreement described under Item 1.01 of this report. Termination of the agreement with EGS was effective pursuant to written notice of termination sent by the Company to EGS. The notice of termination is not being filed as an exhibits to this report. The Company will describe any additional material termination terms to the extent required by the Form 8-K instructions. The Company did not pay a termination fee or any form of compensation to EGS in connection with the Company’s termination of the agreement with EGS. Except as described in the applicable termination documentation, the Company does not expect the termination to result in any further material obligations to EGS. On October 8, 2026, Alchemy also terminated its tender offer agreement dated August 19, 2026, with EGS. That agreement was terminated and replaced by the BF-ABI Agreement described under Item 1.01 of this report. The Company is disclosing the BF-ABI Agreement because of the Company’s approximately 17% equity interest in Alchemy and the resulting significance of the proposed transaction to the Company’s shareholders.
