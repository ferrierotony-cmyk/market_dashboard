(“NEXA” or “Parent Company”) is a public limited liability company (société anonyme) incorporated and domiciled in the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg. Its shares are publicly traded on the New York Stock Exchange (“NYSE”). The Company’s registered office is located at 37A, Avenue J. F. Kennedy in the city of Luxembourg in the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg. NEXA and its subsidiaries (the “Company”) operate large-scale, mechanized underground and open pit mines, as well as smelters. The Company owns and operates three polymetallic mines in Peru and two polymetallic mines in Brazil. Additionally, the Company owns and operates a zinc smelter in Peru and two zinc smelters in Brazil. NEXA’s majority shareholder is Votorantim S.A. (“VSA”), which holds 64.68% of its equity. (e) Iran conflict impacts on the Company´s financial statements and operations Ongoing geopolitical tensions involving the United States, Israel, and Iran have significantly heightened security concerns and increased global economic uncertainty. In June 2026, the United States and Iran announced a Memorandum of Understanding intended to cease hostilities and support negotiations toward a broader resolution of the conflict. However, subsequent developments, including incidents affecting maritime navigation and regional security, resulted in renewed tensions and continued geopolitical uncertainty. As of the date of this report, the situation in the Middle East remains volatile, with ongoing risks to global trade routes, including the Strait of Hormuz and the Bab al-Mandab Strait, as well as potential impacts on energy markets and broader macroeconomic conditions. Consequently, the geopolitical landscape remains uncertain. In this context, Nexa conducted an initial cross-functional assessment, incorporating inputs from Supply Chain, Market Intelligence, Commercial, and Strategic Planning, to monitor potential impacts on energy supply, logistics, and macroeconomic conditions, and to evaluate contingency measures for implementation, if necessary. As of the date of this report, the Company has not identified any material impacts on its operations, financial condition, or cash flows. However, the Company cannot predict the potential future impact of the conflict on its business and operation and continues to closely monitor the situation. (f) “El Niño” Climate Phenomenon impacts Management has been monitoring the evolution of weather conditions and their potential effects on the Company's operations and supply chain, including the supply of raw materials and other inputs, production, logistics, energy costs and availability, and the sale of concentrates and metal products, in connection with the potential effects of the El Niño climate phenomenon. Potential adverse effects associated with this phenomenon may include above-average rainfall, flooding, landslides and logistics disruptions in Peru, as well as below-average rainfall, water scarcity, wildfires and heat exposure in Brazil, which could affect mining, smelting and logistics activities and result in increased operating costs. As of September 30, 2026, Management had not identified any material impacts arising from these conditions on the Company's operations, supply chain, financial position, or results of operations. As a precautionary measure, and considering the possibility of adverse weather conditions affecting operations and logistics during the fourth quarter of 2026 and the first quarter of 2027, the Company has implemented actions to mitigate potential risks, including the acquisition of additional concentrate volumes to increase inventory levels and support operational continuity. Any potential impact resulting from significant changes in the conditions currently observed, including those affecting energy markets, operating costs, the integrity of critical infrastructure, and the continuity of logistics and operational activities, will continue to be monitored and, when applicable, reflected in the financial statements. 11 of 35 Nexa Resources S.A. Under the terms of the agreement, Votorantim will transfer all of its shares in the Company to Boliden through a share-for-share exchange, receiving 0.250 newly issued Boliden common shares for each Nexa common share. As the transaction is being effected between shareholders, the Company will not receive any proceeds from the transaction. The transaction is subject to the satisfaction of customary conditions precedent, including shareholder and regulatory approvals, and is expected to be completed during the first quarter of 2027. In connection with the transaction, Boliden has agreed, subject to closing, to commence a voluntary cash tender offer to purchase all remaining outstanding shares of the Company held by minority shareholders. Following completion of the transaction, Boliden will also commence mandatory tender offers for the remaining outstanding shares of certain of the Company's subsidiaries listed in Peru, as required under applicable Peruvian regulations. The offer price in such mandatory tender offers will be determined in accordance with applicable regulations, and the offers are expected to commence within six months following closing. Following completion, the Company is expected to remain a separate legal entity incorporated in Luxembourg, organized under the laws of Luxembourg, continue to be listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE), and continue to report under the U.S. Securities Exchange Act of 1934.
