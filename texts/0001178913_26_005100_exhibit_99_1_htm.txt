Pursuant to the data detailed in the Electricity Authority’s decision, within a period of about two months new requests for information were filed with the System Operator regarding connection of data centers with a cumulative scope of about 19 gigawatts, which are in addition to the existing list of open (awaiting) requests of about 8 gigawatts, such that the total demand reflected by the requests is about 27 gigawatts. Further to the Temporary Order, in October 2026 the Electricity Authority published a proposed decision for the public’s response for regulation of connection of data centers with a connection size of more than 8 megawatts (“the Hearing”) wherein it is proposed, among other things to: allot an additional designated quota of up to 1,000 megawatts, along with about 2,000 megawatts that has already been approved, and hold an additional quota of up to 300 megawatts for local needs; limit the size of the requested connection to data centers to up to 200 megawatts; and determine a two-stage process that includes examination of applications and a request to receive a connection commitment, subject to threshold planning, technical and commercial conditions. For purposes of competing for the quota, provision of collateral is proposed, in the amount of NIS 0.5 million for every megawatt requested, which will be replaced by a collateral of NIS 2 million for every megawatt in the stage of the connection commitment request. The collaterals will be returned or foreclosed based on compliance with the milestones and conditions detailed in the Hearing. In addition, it is proposed to apply a load shedding (discontinuance), which will permit the System Operator to reduce the electricity supply to the data centers, including those already connected to the grid, for a period of up to 200 hours per year on the conditions detailed in the Hearing.
