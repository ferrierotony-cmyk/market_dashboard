The Consent Solicitations expired at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 12, 2026. As of the expiration, the Company had received valid consents from holders of 73.44% of the aggregate principal amount of the outstanding 2029 Notes and 90.05% of the aggregate principal amount of the outstanding 2032 Notes, in each case exceeding the majority required under the applicable Indenture (the “ Requisite Consents ”). On November 16, 2026, following receipt of the Requisite Consents and satisfaction of the other conditions to the Consent Solicitations, the Company entered into (i) a Third Supplemental Indenture to the 2029 Indenture (the “ 2029 Supplemental Indenture ”) and (ii) a Third Supplemental Indenture to the 2032 Indenture (the “ 2032 Supplemental Indenture ” and, together with the 2029 Supplemental Indenture, the “ Supplemental Indentures ”), in each case between the Company and U.S. Bank, as trustee. The Supplemental Indentures became effective and operative immediately upon execution and are binding on all holders of the applicable series of Notes, including holders that did not deliver consents. Among other things, the Supplemental Indentures amend the Indentures to: (i) modify certain definitions so that, among other things, Lugano Holdings, Inc. is treated as a “Non-Operating Subsidiary” and is no longer a Restricted Subsidiary, and clarify the definition of “Bank Facility” to permit the Company to refinance its existing credit facility, in the same or additional amounts, through credit agreements, indentures or other instruments; (ii) amend Section 4.03 (Reports to Holders) to provide that any delinquency in the delivery of required information will be cured if such information is subsequently filed or furnished; (iii) modify Section 4.07 (Restricted Payments) to remove the restriction on payments under the Company’s Management Services Agreement, permit the use of Asset Sale proceeds to repurchase, redeem, acquire or retire Capital Stock of the Company or any Restricted Subsidiary, and permit payments to the Allocation Member as required by the Company’s LLC Agreement; (iv) amend Section 4.09(b) (Incurrence of Indebtedness) to increase the limit on Attributable Debt to $100 million at any time outstanding and confirm that the PIK Payments constitute Permitted Indebtedness; (v) revise Section 4.11(a)(3) (Asset Sales) to permit the use of Asset Sale proceeds to repurchase, redeem or otherwise acquire or retire Capital Stock of the Company or any Restricted Subsidiary; (vi) amend Section 4.19 to permit the Additional PIK Payment (as described below); and (vii) revise Section 12 of the Global Notes to conform to Section 6.01 of the Indentures. The Supplemental Indentures further provide that, as of their date, the Company is deemed to have complied with all obligations, thresholds, covenants and limitations set forth in the Indentures, and that, following their date, the Company is deemed not to have exhausted any portion of any limitation, cap, basket or similar restriction under the Indentures. In connection with the effectiveness of the Proposed Amendments, each holder of record of the Notes as of November 2, 2026, whether or not such holder delivered a consent, is entitled to a one-time payment-in-kind (the “ Additional PIK Payment ”) in an aggregate amount of (i) $7,755,532 with respect to the 2029 Notes and (ii) $2,326,659 with respect to the 2032 Notes (in each case, equivalent to $7.53 per $1,000 principal amount of Notes outstanding), payable by increasing the principal amount of the applicable outstanding Global Notes. Following the Additional PIK Payment, approximately $1,037,126,334 aggregate principal amount of 2029 Notes and $311,137,891 aggregate principal amount of 2032 Notes are outstanding. In addition, pursuant to Section 7.08(b) of each Indenture, holders of a majority in aggregate principal amount of each series of Notes, through their consents, removed U.S. Bank as trustee. The Supplemental Indentures do not alter the maturity dates of the Notes, the interest rates on the Notes or the Company’s obligation to pay principal of, premium, if any, and interest on the Notes. The foregoing description of the Supplemental Indentures does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the 2029 Supplemental Indenture and the 2032 Supplemental Indenture, copies of which are filed as Exhibits 4.1 and 4.2, respectively, to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated herein by reference.
