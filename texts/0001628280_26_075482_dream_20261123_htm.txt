As previously announced on October 20, 2026, Dream Finders Homes, Inc., a Texas corporation (the “Company”) completed the transactions contemplated by the Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated August 6, 2026 (the “Merger Agreement”), by and among the Company, Bulldogs Merger Sub, Inc., a Delaware corporation and wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company and Beazer Homes USA, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Beazer”). As a result of the transactions contemplated by the Merger Agreement, Beazer became a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company. Upon the closing, Beazer remained the issuer of (i) $250 million in aggregate principal amount of 7.500% Senior Notes due 2031 (the “2031 Beazer Notes”), issued under an indenture, dated as of March 15, 2024 (as supplemented, the “2031 Beazer Indenture”), and (ii) $400 million in aggregate principal amount of 8.000% Senior Notes due 2032 (the “2032 Beazer Notes” and, together with the 2031 Beazer Notes, the “Beazer Notes”), issued under an indenture, dated as of June 23, 2026 (as supplemented, the “2032 Beazer Indenture” and, together with the 2031 Beazer Indenture, the “Beazer Indentures”). On November 23, 2026 (the “Settlement Date”), the Company completed its previously announced (i) offer to exchange (the “Exchange Offer”) any and all (to the extent held by eligible holders) of the outstanding (a) 2031 Beazer Notes for newly-issued 7.500% Senior Notes due 2031 of the Company (the “2031 Dream Finders Notes”) and (b) 2032 Beazer Notes for newly-issued 8.000% Senior Notes due 2032 of the Company (the “2032 Dream Finders Notes” and, together with the 2031 Dream Finders Notes, the “Dream Finders Notes”) and (ii) related consent solicitation (the “Consent Solicitation”) made by the Company on behalf of Beazer to adopt certain proposed amendments (the “Amendments”) to each series of Beazer Notes and the Beazer Indentures that would eliminate substantially all of the restrictive covenants and events of default. The Exchange Offer and Consent Solicitation expired at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 20, 2026 (the “Expiration Date”). As of the Expiration Date, (i) $248,062,000 in aggregate principal amount of outstanding 2031 Beazer Notes, representing 99.22% of the $250 million total outstanding principal amount of the 2031 Beazer Notes, had been validly tendered and not validly withdrawn and (ii) $394,360,000 in aggregate principal amount of outstanding 2032 Beazer Notes, representing 98.59% of the $400 million total outstanding principal amount of the 2032 Beazer Notes, had been validly tendered and not validly withdrawn. All Beazer Notes exchanged were cancelled. Following such cancellation, $1,938,000 and $5,640,000 in aggregate principal amount of the 2031 Beazer Notes and 2032 Beazer Notes, respectively, remain outstanding. Prior to the settlement of the Exchange Offer and Consent Solicitation and upon receipt of the requisite consents to adopt the Amendments with respect to each series of Beazer Notes, Beazer entered into (i) a supplemental indenture to the 2031 Beazer Indenture with Regions Bank, as trustee (the “2031 Beazer Supplemental Indenture”), and (ii) a supplemental indenture to the 2032 Beazer Indenture with Regions Bank, as trustee (the “2032 Beazer Supplemental Indenture” and, together with the 2031 Beazer Supplemental Indenture, the “Beazer Supplemental Indentures”), in each case implementing the Amendments with respect to the applicable series of Beazer Notes. Each of the Beazer Supplemental Indentures became effective upon its execution and delivery, and the Amendments became operative on the Settlement Date. The 2031 Dream Finders Notes were issued under an indenture, dated as of November 23, 2026 (the “2031 Dream Finders Indenture”), and the 2032 Dream Finders Notes were issued under a separate indenture, dated as of November 23, 2026 (the “2032 Dream Finders Indenture” and, together with the 2031 Dream Finders Indenture, the “Dream Finders Indentures”), in each case among the Company, the guarantors party thereto and U.S. Bank Trust Company, National Association, as trustee. The Dream Finders Notes are senior unsecured obligations of the Company and are guaranteed on a senior unsecured basis by each of the Company’s existing and future direct and indirect subsidiaries that guarantee the Company’s amended and restated credit agreement dated June 2, 2022, as amended, including Beazer and certain of its subsidiaries. Each series of Dream Finders Notes has the same interest payment and maturity dates and interest rate and redemption provisions as the corresponding series of Beazer Notes. The 2031 Dream Finders Notes bear interest at the rate of 7.500% per year, payable on March 15 and September 15 of each year, and will mature on March 15, 2031. The 2032 Dream Finders Notes bear interest at the rate of 8.000% per year, payable on January 15 and July 15 of each year, and will mature on January 15, 2032. The foregoing summary of the Dream Finders Notes, the Dream Finders Indentures and the Beazer Supplemental Indentures does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of (i) the 2031 Dream Finders Indenture attached as Exhibit 4.1 hereto, (ii) the 2032 Dream Finders Indenture attached as Exhibit 4.2 hereto, (iii) the 2031 Beazer Supplemental Indenture attached as Exhibit 4.3 hereto and (iv) the 2032 Beazer Supplemental Indenture attached as Exhibit 4.4 hereto.
