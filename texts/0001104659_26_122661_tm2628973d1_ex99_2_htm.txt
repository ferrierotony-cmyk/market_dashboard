Launches Cash Tender Offers and Consent Solicitations for its 4.125% Senior Notes due 2028 and 4.125% Senior Notes due 2029 (LYNCHBURG, Va. – Nov. 4, 2026) BWX Technologies, Inc. (NYSE: BWXT) (“BWXT”) announced today that it has commenced (i) offers to purchase for cash (the “Offers”) from each holder and beneficial owner (each, a “Holder”) any and all of its outstanding 4.125% Senior Notes due 2028 (CUSIP Nos. 05605H AB6 / U12213 AB7) (the “2028 Notes”) and 4.125% Senior Notes due 2029 (CUSIP Nos. 05605H AC4 / U12213 AC5) (the “2029 Notes” and, together with the 2028 Notes, the “Notes” and each, a “Series”) and (ii) solicitations (the “Solicitations”) of consents (“Consents”) to certain proposed amendments to each of the indentures governing the Notes (as amended or supplemented, the “Indentures”), as described below. The Offers and the Solicitations are being made on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase and Consent Solicitation Statement, dated the date hereof (the “Statement”). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this press release have the meanings given to them in the Statement. Such determination will be made at 3:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 10, 2026. The applicable Consideration, plus accrued and unpaid interest, for Notes that are validly tendered and Consents that are validly delivered at or prior to the Expiration Time and accepted for purchase will be paid by BWXT promptly following the Expiration Time (the “Settlement Date”). BWXT expects the Settlement Date will be November 13, 2026, the second business day after the Expiration Time. Tenders of Notes and delivery of Consents pursuant to the Offers and the Solicitations will be accepted only in principal amounts equal to minimum denominations of $2,000 and integral multiples of $1,000 in excess thereof. No alternative, conditional or contingent tenders will be accepted. Holders who tender less than all of their Notes must continue to hold Notes in at least the minimum authorized denominations set forth above. In connection with the Offers, BWXT is soliciting Consents to (i) eliminate substantially all of the restrictive covenants and certain events of default in each of the Indentures and (ii) accelerate BWXT’s ability to satisfy and discharge the Indentures and any Notes that are not purchased in the Offers (collectively, the “Proposed Amendments”). In order for the Proposed Amendments to be adopted with respect to a Series of Notes, Consents must be received in respect of not less than a majority in aggregate principal amount of the outstanding Notes of such Series (the “Requisite Consents”). Assuming receipt of the Requisite Consents for a Series of Notes, BWXT expects to execute and deliver a supplemental indenture to the applicable Indenture (a “New Supplemental Indenture”) promptly following the Expiration Time. The New Supplemental Indentures will become effective upon execution, but the Proposed Amendments will not become operative until the Notes with respect to which Consents have been delivered are paid for on the Settlement Date. If an Offer is terminated or withdrawn for a Series, or a Series of Notes is never accepted for purchase, the Proposed Amendments with respect to such Series will not become operative. The Proposed Amendments for each Series are being presented as one proposal for such Series. Accordingly, the delivery of a Consent by a Holder with respect to a Series of Notes will constitute delivery of a Consent to all of the Proposed Amendments for such Series, and the delivery of a Consent purporting to consent to only some of the Proposed Amendments will not be valid. BWXT’s obligation to accept for purchase, and to pay for, Notes and Consents validly tendered or delivered and not validly withdrawn or revoked pursuant to the Offers and the Solicitations is subject to the satisfaction or waiver of certain conditions described in the Statement, including (1) the successful completion of an offering (the “New Notes Offering”) of one or more series of senior guaranteed unsecured notes (the “New Notes”) generating net proceeds that, together with cash on hand, are sufficient in BWXT’s reasonable judgment to effect (i) the repurchase of Notes validly tendered and Consents validly delivered, which are accepted for purchase pursuant to the Offers and the Solicitations, and (ii) the satisfaction and discharge or covenant defeasance of the Indentures and any Notes that are not purchased in the Offers, and to pay the costs and expenses incurred in connection with the foregoing, and (2) certain general conditions, each as described in the Statement. BWXT may waive any condition, in whole or in part, with respect to either or both Series, and may amend, extend or terminate the Offers and the Solicitations, subject to applicable law. No assurance can be given that the New Notes Offering will be completed. The New Notes Offering is not conditioned upon the consummation of the Offers and the Solicitations. If the Proposed Amendments become operative, any Notes that are not purchased pursuant to the Offers may be satisfied and discharged more than one year in advance of their maturity date. If there are any Notes that remain outstanding following the consummation of the Offers, BWXT intends to satisfy and discharge the applicable Indenture or effect covenant defeasance with respect to such Notes, as described below. If the Requisite Consents are obtained with respect to a Series and the Proposed Amendments become operative, BWXT intends to effect the satisfaction and discharge of the applicable Indenture in accordance with the provisions thereof (as amended by the Proposed Amendments) on the Settlement Date, upon which the guarantees of such Notes by the Guarantors will be released. If the Requisite Consents are not obtained with respect to a Series, BWXT intends to effect covenant defeasance with respect to such Series of Notes in accordance with the terms of the applicable Indenture, in which case Holders of such Notes will continue to receive interest on each scheduled interest payment date and principal on the stated maturity date but will not benefit from any restrictive covenants and related events of default removed pursuant to such covenant defeasance or from the guarantees of such Notes by the Guarantors, which will be released upon the satisfaction of the conditions to such covenant defeasance. The Proposed Amendments do not need to be adopted in order to effect covenant defeasance with respect to either or both Series of Notes in accordance with the terms of the applicable Indenture. There can be no assurance that the Indentures will be satisfied and discharged or that covenant defeasance will be effected with respect to either Series of Notes. By validly tendering Notes of a Series, a Holder will automatically deliver its Consent to the Proposed Amendments to the Indenture governing that Series. Holders may not deliver Consents without tendering the related Notes, and a valid withdrawal of tendered Notes will constitute a revocation of the related Consents. If the Requisite Consents are received with respect to a Series and the Proposed Amendments become operative, the Proposed Amendments will be binding on all the Holders of Notes of such Series, including those that do not tender their Notes in the Offers. Holders are advised to check with any broker, dealer, commercial bank, trust company or other nominee through which they hold Notes as to when it would need to receive instructions to participate in, or (where permitted) withdraw from, the Offers and the Solicitations. The deadlines set by any such nominee and The Depository Trust Company (“DTC”) will be earlier than those specified in this press release and the Statement.
