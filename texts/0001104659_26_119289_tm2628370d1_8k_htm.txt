Exchange Offers On October 27, 2026, Kimberly-Clark Corporation, a Delaware corporation (“Kimberly-Clark”), announced that it has extended the expiration date of its previously announced offers to exchange (each, an “Exchange Offer” and, collectively, the “Exchange Offers”) any and all outstanding notes (the “Kenvue Notes”) issued by Kenvue Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Kenvue”), for up to $7.0 billion aggregate principal amount of new notes issued by Kimberly-Clark. Kimberly-Clark extended such expiration date from 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on October 27, 2026, to 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on November 2, 2026 (as the same may be further extended, the “Expiration Date”). The Exchange Offers are being made solely pursuant to the conditions set forth in the confidential offering memorandum and consent solicitation statement dated September 28, 2026 (as amended from time to time) in a private offering exempt from, or not subject to, registration under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended. The Exchange Offers are conditioned, among other things, upon the consummation of Kimberly-Clark’s previously announced pending acquisition of Kenvue (the “Acquisition”). On the early participation date of October 9, 2026, requisite consents were received to adopt certain amendments (the “Amendments”) to the indenture governing the Kenvue Notes (the “Kenvue Indenture”) and a supplemental indenture to the Kenvue Indenture containing the Amendments was executed. The Amendments will become operative only upon settlement of the Exchange Offers. The settlement date for the Exchange Offers is expected to occur promptly after the Expiration Date. A copy of the press release issued by Kimberly-Clark is attached as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated herein in its entirety. Item 9.01.
