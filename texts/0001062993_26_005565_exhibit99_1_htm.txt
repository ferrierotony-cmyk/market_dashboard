The repurchases were funded with cash on hand, including proceeds of the $180 million strategic investment from Ganfeng Lithium Group Co., Ltd. (" Ganfeng ") that closed on September 15, 2026. Following the repurchases, approximately $59 million aggregate principal amount of the 2027 Notes remains outstanding. Lithium Argentina intends to repay the remaining balance at or before its January 15, 2027 maturity from cash on hand, which exceeded $100 million following the repurchases, further reducing its overall debt. "Repurchasing the majority of the 2027 Notes with cash is the next step in deleveraging and strengthening our balance sheet," said Sam Pigott, Chief Executive Officer. "With no other corporate-level debt maturing until 2032 and Cauchari-Olaroz generating strong cash flow, we are well positioned to allocate capital in the way that best serves our shareholders." Pursuant to the terms of the indenture governing the 2027 Notes, the Company may from time to time repurchase 2027 Notes through privately negotiated transactions or otherwise, depending on market conditions, applicable securities law and other factors. There can be no assurance as to the timing or amount of any further repurchases. This news release is for informational purposes only and does not constitute an offer to purchase, or the solicitation of an offer to sell, any 2027 Notes or other securities. The transactions described in this news release were effected through privately negotiated agreements with a limited number of holders located outside Canada. No offer to purchase 2027 Notes has been made, nor is any such offer being made, directly or indirectly, in Canada, and no resident of Canada is being solicited to participate in any such transaction. ABOUT LITHIUM ARGENTINA Lithium Argentina jointly owns and manages the Cauchari-Olaroz lithium brine operation in the Jujuy province of Argentina and is advancing the PPG lithium brine development in the Salta province of Argentina. Lithium is a key material used in the production of lithium-ion batteries, electric vehicles, and battery energy storage systems (BESS). The Company currently trades on the TSX and on the NYSE under the ticker "LAR".
