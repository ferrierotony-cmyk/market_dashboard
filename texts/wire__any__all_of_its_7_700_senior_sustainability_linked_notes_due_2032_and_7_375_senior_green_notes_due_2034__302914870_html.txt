OF ITS 7.700% SENIOR SUSTAINABILITY-LINKED NOTES DUE 2032 AND 7.375% SENIOR GREEN NOTES DUE 2034 News provided by NEXT Properties (Trust 2401) Oct 20, 2026, 23:10 ET Share this article Share this article MEXICO CITY , Oct. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- NEXT Properties (Trust 2401), a trust formed under the laws of the United Mexican States (the "Trust" or "NEXT Properties"), today announced the expiration date results of its offers to exchange (collectively, the "Exchange Offers") any and all of its outstanding 7.700% Senior Sustainability-Linked Notes due 2032 (the "NEXT Properties 2032 Notes") and 7.375% Senior Green Notes due 2034 (the "NEXT Properties 2034 Notes") for newly issued 6.793% Senior Sustainability-Linked Notes due 2036 (the "New 2036 Notes" or the "New Notes"), in each case upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Exchange Offer Memorandum dated October 8, 2026, as supplemented by the press releases issued by the Trust on October 12, 2026 and October 16, 2026 (the "Exchange Offer Memorandum"), and the related eligibility certification. The NEXT Properties 2032 Notes and the NEXT Properties 2034 Notes are herein referred to collectively as the "Old Notes." Capitalized terms used but not defined herein have the meanings assigned to them in the Exchange Offer Memorandum. As of October 20, 2026 at 5:00 p.m., New York City time (the "Expiration Date") the following amounts had been validly tendered and not withdrawn and, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions set forth in the Exchange Offer Memorandum, NEXT Properties has accepted the following for exchange under the Exchange Offers: Title of Series of Old Notes Principal Amount Outstanding Principal Amount Tendered and Accepted for Exchange % Tendered New Notes Consideration 7.700% Senior Sustainability-Linked Notes due 2032 US$ 409,781,000 US$289,926,000 70.75 % US$312,461,855 7.375% Senior Green Notes due 2034 US$ 299,900,000 US$245,647,000 81.91 % US$260,213,799 Total: US$ 709,681,000 US$535,573,000 75.47 % US$572,675,654 The Withdrawal Deadline expired at 5:00 p.m., New York City time on October 20, 2026. The Old Notes validly tendered pursuant to the Exchange Offers may no longer be withdrawn, unless otherwise required by applicable law. The Trust has accepted for exchange US$535,573,000 aggregate principal amount of the tendered Old Notes, including US$289,926,000 aggregate principal amount of the NEXT Properties 2032 Notes and US$245,647,000 aggregate principal amount of the NEXT Properties 2034 Notes and will issue US$572,675,654 aggregate principal amount of New 2036 Notes in exchange therefor, in accordance with the procedures described in the Exchange Offer Memorandum. In addition to the applicable Revised Total Consideration, Eligible Holders whose Old Notes were validly tendered (and not validly withdrawn) and accepted by the Trust for exchange pursuant to the Exchange Offers will also receive the applicable Accrued Interest Payment, which will be US$19.04 per US$1,000 principal amount of NEXT Properties 2032 Notes and US$14.13 per US$1,000 principal amount of NEXT Properties 2034 Notes. The New 2036 Notes will be issued, and the Accrued Interest Payment will be paid in cash, on the Settlement Date, which is expected to be October 22, 2026. Any tendered Old Notes that are not accepted for exchange will be returned or credited without expense to the holder's account. All conditions to the Exchange Offers, including the applicable Minimum Issuance Condition (as defined in the press release issued by the Trust on October 12, 2026), have been satisfied or waived. The Exchange Offers were made only (a) to holders of Old Notes who are "qualified institutional buyers" (as defined in Rule 144A under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the "Securities Act")) and (b) outside the United States to holders of Old Notes who are persons other than U.S. persons, who are not acquiring New Notes for the account or benefit of a U.S. person, in offshore transactions in compliance with Regulation S under the Securities Act, and who are a "non-U.S. qualified offeree" (as defined in the Exchange Offer Memorandum). The holders of Old Notes who have certified to the Trust that they are eligible to participate in the Exchange Offers pursuant to at least one of the foregoing conditions are referred to as Eligible Holders. Only Eligible Holders were authorized to receive or review the Exchange Offer Memorandum or to participate in the Exchange Offers. Old Notes could be tendered only in principal amounts equal to minimum denominations of US$200,000 and integral multiples of US$1,000 in excess thereof. The Trust has retained Citigroup Global Markets Inc. ("Citigroup") to act as Dealer Manager for the Exchange Offers. Holders with questions about the Exchange Offers can contact Citigroup at +1 (800) 558-3745 (toll free) or +1 (212) 723-6106 (collect). Global Bondholder Services Corporation is acting as the Exchange and Information Agent for the Exchange Offers.
