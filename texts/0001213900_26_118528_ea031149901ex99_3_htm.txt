Chairman of the Board of Directors 49.1. Election of Chairman. Subject to Article 49.2 and applicable law, the Board of Directors may, by a decision taken by a majority of the Directors, elect one of its members to serve as Chairman of the Board of Directors. 49.2. Removal or replacement of a serving Chairman. Subject to any mandatory provision of applicable law, any resolution of the Board of Directors to remove a serving Chairman from that office, to terminate his or her service in that office, or to elect another Director in his or her place while he or she continues to hold that office shall require the affirmative votes of at least two-thirds of the Directors then in office who are legally entitled to vote on that resolution. This requirement applies whether the removal or replacement is proposed as a standalone resolution, as part of a resolution electing a successor, or as the first step in a series of resolutions or actions to effect the replacement. The Board of Directors shall not effect a removal or replacement by designating another Director as acting or interim Chairman in circumstances that would otherwise fall within this Article 49.2. The selection of a Director solely to preside at an individual meeting under Article 49.5 is governed by that Article. 49.3. Calculation of required votes. For purposes of Article 49.2, the required number of affirmative votes shall be calculated by multiplying the number of Directors then in office who are legally entitled to vote on the resolution by two-thirds. If the result is a fraction of a Director, it shall be rounded up to the next whole number. A Director who is legally entitled to vote shall be included in that calculation even if absent, abstaining or otherwise not voting. A vacant seat and a Director who is legally disqualified from voting on the resolution shall be excluded from the calculation. The meeting and vote shall otherwise be subject to the applicable notice, quorum and procedural requirements of these Articles and applicable law. 49.4. Vacancies. Article 49.2 does not apply to the election of a Chairman to fill a vacancy arising from the former Chairman’s voluntary resignation, death, cessation of office as a Director by operation of law, or other cessation of service independent of a decision or action by the Board of Directors to remove or replace that Chairman. An election to fill such a vacancy shall be governed by Article 49.1. If the Board of Directors has validly removed a Chairman under Article 49.2 and has not elected a successor as part of the same resolution, the election to fill the resulting vacancy shall also be governed by Article 49.1. 49.5. Chairing meetings and voting. The Chairman of the Board of Directors shall preside at every meeting of the Board of Directors.
