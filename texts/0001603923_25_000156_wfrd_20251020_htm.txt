On October 20, 2025, Weatherford issued a notice of conditional partial redemption (the “Partial Redemption”) to the holders of 8.625% Senior Notes due 2030 (the “2030 Notes”), providing for the redemption of an aggregate principal amount of 2030 Notes equal to $407,172,000 less the total amount of 2030 Notes tendered pursuant to Weatherford’s previously-announced tender offer (the “Tender Offer”) during the period following the early tender deadline and the expiration date of the Tender Offer. The Partial Redemption is conditioned on the consummation of the Tender Offer. The redemption price for the 2030 Notes to be redeemed will be equal to 102.156% of the principal amount of the 2030 Notes to be redeemed as of, and accrued and unpaid interest to, but excluding, the redemption date. The redemption date for the Partial Redemption will be October 30, 2025. This disclosure does not constitute a notice of redemption with respect to the 2030 Notes. Item 8.01 Other Information.
