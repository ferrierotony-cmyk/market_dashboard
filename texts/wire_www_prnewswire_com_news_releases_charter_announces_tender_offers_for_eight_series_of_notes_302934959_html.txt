Nov 10, 2026, 08:31 ET Share this article Share this article STAMFORD, Conn. , Nov. 10, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Charter Communications, Inc. (NASDAQ: CHTR , CHTRP) (along with its subsidiaries, "Charter") announced today the commencement by its wholly-owned subsidiaries, Charter Communications Operating, LLC ("CCO") and Charter Communications Operating Capital Corp. ("CCO Capital" and, together with CCO, collectively, the "Issuers" or the "Company"), of eight separate offers to purchase for cash the outstanding series of their notes listed in the table below (collectively, the "Notes" and such tender offers, the "Offers") for aggregate Consideration (as defined below) of up to $1,500,000,000, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase dated November 10, 2026 (the "Offer to Purchase"). The Company is offering to purchase validly tendered Notes using a "waterfall" methodology under which the Notes will be accepted in the order of their Acceptance Priority Levels, subject to the Offer Cap (each as defined below). The Offers are subject to the terms and conditions described in the Offer to Purchase, including (i) the Acceptance Priority Procedures (as described below) and (ii) a $1.5 billion cap (the "Offer Cap") on the aggregate Consideration that the Company will be obligated to pay for all Notes purchased in the Offers (excluding the applicable Accrued Interest (as defined below) and fees and expenses related to the Offers). The Company may increase the amount of Notes accepted for payment in the Offers by no more than 2% of the outstanding Notes of the applicable series subject to proration without amending or extending the Offers. On the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase, the Company is offering to purchase the following outstanding notes issued by the Issuers for the consideration described below. Terms not otherwise defined are defined in the Offer to Purchase. (4) The consideration for each $1,000 principal amount of each series of Notes validly tendered, not validly withdrawn and accepted for purchase (the "Consideration") will be determined in accordance with standard market practice, as described in the Offer to Purchase, to result in a price as of the Settlement Date (as defined below) based on a yield to the applicable par call date or maturity date (in accordance with market practice) in accordance with the formula set forth in Schedule A to the Offer to Purchase, for the applicable series of Notes, equal to the sum of (i) the yield corresponding to the bid side price of the applicable Reference U.S. Treasury Security specified in the table above for such series of Notes, as calculated by the Lead Dealer Managers (as defined below) at 3:00 p.m. (New York City time) on the Price Determination Date (as defined below), quoted on the Bloomberg reference page "FIT1," plus (ii) the applicable "Fixed Spread" specified in the table above for such series of Notes. If the applicable Tender Offer Yield (as defined in the Offer to Purchase) is less than the contractual annual rate of interest on such series of Notes, the Consideration will be calculated based on the par call date; otherwise, the Consideration will be calculated based on the maturity date. The Consideration does not include the applicable Accrued Interest, which will be payable in cash in addition to the applicable Consideration. The Offers will each expire at 5:00 p.m. (New York City time) on November 17, 2026, unless extended or earlier terminated by the Company (such date and time, as the same may be extended, the "Expiration Date"). All Holders (as defined in the Offer to Purchase) whose Notes are accepted in an Offer will receive a cash payment equal to accrued and unpaid interest on such Notes to, but excluding, the Settlement Date (as defined below) (the "Accrued Interest") in addition to their Consideration. Notes may be validly withdrawn at any time at or prior to the earlier of (i) the Expiration Date and (ii) if the applicable Offer is extended, the 10th business day after commencement of such Offer (such time and date, as the same may be extended, the "Withdrawal Deadline"), but not thereafter, unless otherwise required by applicable law. Upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase, Holders who validly tender, and do not validly withdraw, Notes at or prior to the Expiration Date, and whose Notes are accepted for purchase by the Company, will receive the applicable Consideration per $1,000 principal amount of such Notes. The Offers are subject to the satisfaction or waiver of certain conditions, including the General Conditions (as defined in the Offer to Purchase). The Offers are not conditioned on any minimum amount of Notes being tendered, and no Offer is conditioned on the consummation of any other Offer. Subject to applicable law and limitations described in the Offer to Purchase, the Company expressly reserves the right to amend, extend or terminate any of the Offers at any time prior to the Expiration Date. Subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions of the Offers, the "Acceptance Priority Procedures" with respect to the Offers will operate as follows: if the aggregate Consideration for all Notes validly tendered, and not validly withdrawn, at or prior to the Expiration Date does not exceed the Offer Cap, then the Company will accept all such Notes; and if the aggregate Consideration for all Notes validly tendered, and not validly withdrawn, at or prior to the Expiration Date exceeds the Offer Cap (subject to any increase in the Offer Cap at the Company's discretion, subject to applicable law), then the Company will (i) accept for purchase all validly tendered Notes of each series starting at the highest Acceptance Priority Level (level 1) and moving sequentially to Notes of each series having a lower Acceptance Priority Level (the lowest of which is level 8) until the aggregate Consideration for all validly tendered Notes of the applicable series, together with the aggregate Consideration for all Notes accepted for purchase from series with higher Acceptance Priority Levels, is as close as possible to, but does not exceed, the Offer Cap, (ii) accept on a prorated basis validly tendered Notes of the series with the next lower Acceptance Priority Level, subject to the Company's ability to increase the amount of Notes of such prorated series by no more than 2% of the outstanding Notes of such series without amending or extending the applicable Offer and (iii) not accept for purchase any Notes of a series with an Acceptance Priority Level below the prorated series. The "Settlement Date," if any, is the date on which the Company will settle all Notes validly tendered and accepted for purchase, subject to all conditions having been satisfied or waived by the Company. The Settlement Date is expected to be the second business day following the Expiration Date (November 19, 2026), unless the Expiration Date is extended with respect to any Offer. The applicable Consideration payable by the Company for each $1,000 principal amount of each series of Notes validly tendered at or prior to the Expiration Date, and not validly withdrawn, and accepted by the Company will be paid in cash on the Settlement Date. Promptly after 3:00 p.m. (New York City time) on November 17, 2026, the scheduled Expiration Date, unless extended (such date and time, as the same may be extended, the "Price Determination Date"), the Company will publicly announce the Consideration for each series of Notes. If proration of tendered Notes is required, the Company will use commercially reasonable efforts to announce the results of proration by press release prior to 10:00 a.m. (New York City time) on the business day following the Expiration Date.
