Announces Confidential Submission of Draft Registration Statement for Proposed Exchange Offers for Its 6.00% Senior Notes due January 2028 and 5.25% Senior Notes due August 2028 News provided by BRC Group Holdings, Inc. Nov 17, 2026, 07:31 ET Share this article Share this article LOS ANGELES , Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- BRC Group Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: RILY ) ("BRC Group" or the "Company") today announced that it has confidentially submitted a draft registration statement on Form S-4 to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the "SEC") relating to proposed offers to exchange (the "Exchange Offers") its outstanding 6.00% Senior Notes due January 2028 (Nasdaq: RILYT ) and 5.25% Senior Notes due August 2028 (Nasdaq: RILYZ ) (together, the "Existing Notes") for up to $200 million aggregate principal amount of newly issued 8.00% Secured Notes due June 2031 (the "New Notes"). Under the proposed terms, holders would receive $25 principal amount of New Notes plus accrued and unpaid interest on the Existing Notes in exchange for each $25 principal amount of Existing Notes accepted for exchange. The 6.00% Senior Notes due January 2028 would be accepted before the 5.25% Senior Notes due August 2028, and tenders may be prorated. The New Notes would be guaranteed by certain subsidiaries of the Company, secured on a third-priority basis and subordinated in right of payment to the Company's first-lien and second-lien indebtedness. The Exchange Offers are expected to commence after the SEC declares the registration statement on Form S-4 effective, subject to market and other conditions. There can be no assurance that the Exchange Offers will be commenced or completed, or as to their final terms or timing. This press release is being issued in accordance with Rule 135 under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, and does not constitute an offer to sell or the solicitation of an offer to buy any securities, or an offer to exchange or the solicitation of an offer to exchange any Existing Notes, nor shall there be any sale or exchange of securities in any jurisdiction in which such offer, solicitation, sale or exchange would be unlawful prior to registration or qualification under the securities laws of that jurisdiction. The draft registration statement has not been publicly filed and has not become effective. The Exchange Offers will be made only by means of a prospectus forming part of an effective registration statement, which holders of the Existing Notes should read when available, free of charge, at www.sec.gov .
