Publicly Held Company CNPJ/MF No. 02.421.421/0001-11 NIRE No. 33.300.324.631 NOTICE TO THE MARKET Final Result of the Public Offer Addressed to Telecom Italia S.P.A. Shareholders TIM S.A. (“TIM” or “Company”) (B3: TIMS3; NYSE: TIMB), following up on the material fact published on September 18, 2026 regarding the voluntary public tender and exchange offer launched by Poste Italiane S.p.A. (“Poste Italiane”) for the ordinary shares of Telecom Italia S.p.A. ("Telecom Italia") (“Offer”), hereby informs its shareholders and the market in general about the final results of such Offer. According to the information publicly disclosed, the Offer resulted in Poste Italiane attaining an aggregate interest corresponding to approximately 85.823% of the share capital of Telecom Italia, represented by 1,832,941,911 ordinary shares. The settlement of the consideration relating to shares tendered during the reopening of the acceptance period occurred on October 02, 2026. On the 1 st of October, Poste announced it has carried out purchase transactions concerning the ordinary shares of Telecom Italia on the market. Following these transactions, Poste stated it will hold a total of 1,857,060,167 Telecom Italia’s shares, equal to approximately 86.952% of the share capital. The Company will keep its shareholders and the market informed of any material developments related to this matter. Rio de Janeiro, October 2, 2026 TIM S.A.
