CoinDesk reports: CAE (NASDAQ: CAE; TSX: CAE) announced on Thursday that it has initiated a solicitation of consents and proxies to amend the indenture governing its 5.541% Series 1 senior unsecured notes, which mature on June 12, 2028, and represent an aggregate principal amount of $400 million. CAE states that this solicitation of written consents and proxies aims to allow holders to consider amending the definitions related to accounting standards in the master trust indenture by special resolution. The full text of the proposed amendments is set forth in the joint consent and proxy solicitation statement. The company stated that, as part of its ongoing transformation and to align CAE’s financial reporting with that of its aerospace and defense peers, it is exploring the possibility of transitioning from IFRS to U.S. Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (U.S. GAAP). If adopted, this change would apply to the fourth quarter and full fiscal year ending March 31, 2027, and would be applied using the full retrospective method. CAE indicated that a project team has been established and a project plan developed, and it is currently assessing the potential impact of transitioning to U.S. GAAP on its consolidated financial statements. This proposed amendment is directly related to this potential transition; if this accounting policy change is adopted, the indenture governing the master trust will also need to be updated accordingly. CAE is seeking written consents and proxies from note holders for the proposed amendment. If the required threshold of written consents specified in the solicitation statement is not obtained, the company intends to hold a meeting of note holders on December 16, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. Eastern Time, at the offices of Norton Rose Fulbright Canada LLP, 27th Floor, 1 Place Ville Marie, Montreal, Quebec, Canada. The record date for determining shareholders entitled to receive notice, submit written consents and proxies, and vote at the meeting is November 9, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time. As of the record date, each $1,000 principal amount of notes shall be entitled to one vote. The proposed amendments are based on the agreement, with relevant details provided in the solicitation statement; the revised supplementary agreement text is included in Appendix B of the statement. If the CAE obtains the required approvals through written consent or by resolution passed at a meeting, and the proposed amendments become effective upon execution of a second supplemental indenture, then holders of notes who were authorized and instructed by the deadline for consent to elect consent through their CDS participants, and whose participants made such election via CDSX, or who voted in favor of the proposed amendments at the meeting, or caused their notes to be voted in favor thereof at the meeting, shall be eligible to receive a consent fee in the amount of one dollar per $1,000 principal amount. Holders who fail to submit a valid consent prior to the consent deadline, fail to submit a valid proxy prior to the proxy deadline, or do not vote in favor of the amendment resolution at the meeting shall not be entitled to receive any consent fee, regardless of whether the proposed amendments are approved. Any consent fee payable to note holders shall be paid by the CAE as soon as practicable after the effectiveness of the second supplemental indenture to Computershare Advantage Trust of Canada (as paying agent), which shall then pay such fee to CDS & Co., and such fee shall subsequently be distributed in accordance with the applicable procedures of CDS Clearing and Depository Services Inc. The consent fee shall bear no interest and no interest shall be paid thereon. The proposed amendments shall take effect upon satisfaction of either of the following conditions: (i) written consent from holders representing at least two-thirds and two-thirds of the total outstanding principal amount of the notes; or (ii) approval by holders of at least two-thirds and two-thirds of the outstanding principal amount of the notes present and entitled to vote on the resolution at the meeting. The CAE board unanimously recommends that note holders approve and vote in favor of the proposed amendment. The deadline for holders to submit consents is 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time on November 26, 2026; if a meeting is to be held, the deadline for submitting proxies is 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time on December 14, 2026. If the meeting is postponed or rescheduled, the deadline shall be 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time on the third business day prior to the new date, although CAE may, at its sole discretion, modify, waive, or extend these deadlines. CAE reserves the right to terminate, cancel, extend, or amend the terms of this consent and proxy solicitation at any time prior to the consent deadline, the proxy deadline, or the meeting. Holders of notes held through intermediaries should note that intermediaries may establish earlier deadlines for returning instructions than the consent and proxy deadlines. For questions regarding how to submit instructions, contact your broker or other intermediary for assistance.
