(NYSE: QGEN; Frankfurt Prime Standard: QIA) today announced full results for the third quarter of 2026, with net sales and adjusted diluted earnings per share (EPS) ahead of the outlook. In line with preliminary results, Q3 2026 sales rose 3% to $548 million compared with Q3 2025, also increasing 3% at constant exchange rates (CER) and exceeding the outlook for about 1-2% CER growth. Consumables and related revenues rose 3% CER and were 91% of sales, while instrument sales rose 1% CER. Sales from the pillars rose 7% CER to $415 million, partly offset by a 7% CER decline in the rest of the portfolio. Operating income increased to $132 million from $129 million in Q3 2025. Adjusted operating income, which excludes various charges and other items in both periods, rose 6% to $167 million over Q3 2025. The adjusted operating income margin was 30.4% of sales, an increase of 0.8 percentage points from 29.6% in Q3 2025, as sales growth, a growing contribution from higher-margin consumables and efficiency gains supported investments in Parse Biosciences after the late 2025 acquisition. Diluted EPS was $0.51 in Q3 2026 compared with $0.60 in Q3 2025. Adjusted diluted EPS increased to $0.65 from $0.61, while results at CER of $0.65 were above the outlook for at least $0.62 CER. Portfolio update Among recent developments in QIAGEN's Sample to Insight portfolio: ◦ AI-enabled instruments: QIAGEN joined Anthropic’s Model Hardware Standard (MHS) research preview, developing a working proof of concept on QIAsymphony Connect. The pilot explores how AI agents can help troubleshoot instrument issues faster and guide operators through recovery, with the potential to improve uptime and reduce risk to biological samples. ◦ AI-powered insights: QIAGEN introduced IPA Investigator and the QIAGEN Discovery Platform, expanding its AI bioinformatics portfolio for research and drug discovery. These tools combine AI with curated biomedical knowledge to support biological analysis, target identification and biomarker discovery. ◦ Laboratory automation: The launch of QIAmini completed the rollout of three new sample preparation systems in 2026. Designed for research laboratories processing up to eight samples per run, QIAmini reduced hands-on time by more than 80% in evaluated workflows. Together with QIAsprint Connect and QIAsymphony Connect , it expands automation across laboratories of different sizes and throughput requirements. ◦ Rapid infectious disease testing: QIAGEN completed its U.S. QIAstat-Dx bloodstream infection portfolio with FDA clearances for two complementary panels. Together, the panels cover 33 pathogen targets and 28 antimicrobial resistance markers, and each provides results from positive blood cultures in about one hour to support treatment decisions. ◦ Tuberculosis (TB) prevention: QIAGEN's Global TB Summit brought together over 4,000 participants online and in London to discuss prevention, screening and care. TB caused about 1.2 million deaths in 2024. QuantiFERON-TB Gold Plus is the leading blood-based test for latent TB infection, with more than 160 million QuantiFERON-TB tests sold since launch. Use of adjusted results QIAGEN reports adjusted results and constant exchange rate (CER) measures, along with other non-GAAP financial metrics, to show underlying business performance. These include adjusted gross profit and margin, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted operating income and expenses, adjusted operating income margin, adjusted pre-tax and net income, adjusted tax rate and adjusted diluted EPS, net leverage and free cash flow. Adjusted results are non-GAAP measures that exclude items outside of ongoing core operations or that vary significantly between periods. CER results apply prior-year exchange rates. Free cash flow is defined as operating cash flow less purchases of property, plant and equipment. QIAGEN also uses these measures for planning, forecasting and compensation. They complement, but do not replace, GAAP results, are reconciled in the accompanying tables and may not be comparable to other companies' measures. About QIAGEN QIAGEN N.V., a Netherlands-based holding company, is a global leader in Sample to Insight solutions that enable customers to extract and analyze molecular information from biological samples containing the building blocks of life. Our Sample technologies isolate and process DNA, RNA and proteins from blood, tissue and other materials. Assay technologies prepare these biomolecules for analysis, while bioinformatics support the interpretation of complex data to deliver actionable insights. Automation solutions integrate these steps into streamlined, cost-effective workflows. QIAGEN serves more than 500,000 customers worldwide in the Life Sciences (academia, pharmaceutical R&D and industrial applications such as forensics) and Molecular Diagnostics (clinical healthcare). As of September 30, 2026, QIAGEN employed about 5,400 people across more than 35 locations. For more information, visit www.qiagen.com.
