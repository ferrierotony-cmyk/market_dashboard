Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement. Supplemental Indenture In connection with the consummation of the Merger (as defined below), the Company and Computershare Trust Company, National Association (the “Trustee”), entered into a First Supplemental Indenture (the “Supplemental Indenture”), dated as of November 17, 2026, which amends and supplements the Indenture (the “Original Indenture” and, together with the Supplemental Indenture, the “Indenture”), dated as of May 14, 2024, by and between the Company and the Trustee, governing the Company’s 2.125% Convertible Senior Notes due 2029 (the “Notes”), of which $287.5 million in aggregate principal amount is outstanding as of the date hereof. The Supplemental Indenture provides that, among other things, from and after the Effective Time (as defined below), the right to convert each $1,000 principal amount of Notes into Shares (as defined below) has been changed into a right to convert such principal amount of Notes solely into Reference Property (as defined in the Indenture) consisting of cash equal to $36.50 multiplied by the Conversion Rate in effect on the relevant Conversion Date (including, for conversions in connection with the Make-Whole Fundamental Change, any Additional Shares (each as defined in the Indenture)), payable on the tenth Business Day following such Conversion Date. The Merger, pursuant to which the Shares were converted solely into the right to receive cash, constitutes a Common Stock Change Event, a Make-Whole Fundamental Change, a Fundamental Change and an Exempted Fundamental Change (each as defined in the Indenture) with respect to the Notes. In connection with the Merger, the Company elected to rely on Section 14.05 of the Indenture and will not offer to repurchase any Notes in connection with the Merger. Unwind Agreements in respect of the Capped Call Transactions As previously disclosed, in connection with the issuance of the Notes, the Company entered into capped call transactions (the “Capped Call Transactions”) with certain financial institution counterparties (the “Capped Call Counterparties”), as evidenced by the confirmations, additional confirmations and related side letters and additional side letters (together, the “Capped Call Confirmations”). On November 17, 2026, the Company entered into an unwind agreement for each Capped Call Confirmation with each Capped Call Counterparty in exchange for an agreed upon cash payment by such Capped Call Counterparty to the Company, payable on or about November 17, 2026. The foregoing description of the Indenture and the Capped Call Transactions does not purport to be complete and is subject to, and qualified in its entirety by, the full text of the Original Indenture, which was filed as Exhibit 4.1 of the Company’s Current Report on Form 8-K filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) on May 14, 2024 (the “May 14, 2024 Form 8-K”), and is incorporated into this Item 1.01 by reference, the full text of a form of the Capped Call Confirmation, which was filed as Exhibit 10.1 of the May 14, 2024 Form 8-K, and is incorporated into this Item 1.01 by reference, and the full text of the Supplemental Indenture, which is filed as Exhibit 4.1 hereto and incorporated into this Item 1.01 by reference. Item 2.01 Completion of Acquisition or Disposition of Assets. As previously disclosed, pursuant to the Merger Agreement, on October 30, 2026, Purchaser commenced a tender offer to acquire all of the outstanding shares of common stock of the Company, $0.001 par value per share (the “Shares”), at an offer price of $36.50 per Share in cash, net of applicable withholding taxes and without interest (the “Offer Price”), upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase, dated October 30, 2026 (as amended or supplemented from time to time, the “Offer to Purchase”), and in the related Letter of Transmittal (which, together with the Offer to Purchase and other related materials, as amended or supplemented from time to time, constituted the “Offer”). On November 17, 2026, Viatris announced that the Offer and related withdrawal rights had expired as scheduled at one minute following 11:59 p.m., Eastern Time, on November 16, 2026 (the “Expiration Date”) and the Offer was not extended, and that as of such time, based on the information provided by the depositary and paying agent for the Offer, a total of 32,929,780 Shares, together with the Shares then owned by Purchaser and its “affiliates” (as such term is defined in Section 251(h)(6)(a) of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware (the “DGCL”)), were validly tendered (and not properly withdrawn) pursuant to the Offer prior to the Expiration Date (excluding Shares tendered pursuant to guaranteed delivery procedures that have not yet been “received,” as such term is defined in Section 251(h)(6)(f) of the DGCL, by the “depository,” as such term is defined in Section 251(h)(6)(c) of the DGCL), representing approximately 66.46% of the issued and outstanding Shares as of the Expiration Date. Accordingly, the number of Shares validly tendered (and not properly withdrawn) and received by the depositary pursuant to the Offer, together with the Shares then owned by Purchaser and its affiliates, satisfied the Minimum Condition (as defined in the Merger Agreement), and all other conditions to the consummation of the Offer had been satisfied or waived. As a result of the satisfaction of the Minimum Condition and each of the other conditions to the Offer, on November 17, 2026, Purchaser irrevocably accepted for payment all Shares that were validly tendered (and not properly withdrawn) pursuant to the Offer. Following the consummation of the Offer, the remaining conditions to the Merger set forth in the Merger Agreement were satisfied or waived, and the parties to the Merger Agreement consummated the Merger on November 17, 2026. Pursuant to the Merger Agreement, at the date and time of the filing of the certificate of merger with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware (the “Effective Time”), Purchaser was merged with and into the Company (the “Merger”), without a vote on the adoption of the Merger Agreement by the Company’s stockholders, in accordance with Section 251(h) of the DGCL, with the Company continuing as the surviving corporation of the Merger (the “Surviving Corporation”) and as a wholly owned subsidiary of Viatris. At the Effective Time and as a result of the Merger, each Share issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time (other than Shares held (i) by the Company or its subsidiaries (including Shares held in the Company’s treasury), (ii) by Viatris, Purchaser, or any other direct or indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Viatris, or (iii) by stockholders of the Company who have properly exercised and perfected their statutory rights of appraisal under the DGCL) was automatically converted into the right to receive the Offer Price. Pursuant to the terms of the Merger Agreement, each option to purchase Shares (each, a “Company Option”) that was outstanding as of immediately prior to the Effective Time, whether vested or unvested, was canceled at the Effective Time and converted into the right to receive an amount in cash equal to the product of (i) the total number of Shares subject to such Company Option immediately prior to the Effective Time, multiplied by (ii) the excess, if any, of (A) the Offer Price minus (B) the exercise price payable per Share under such Company Option, subject to applicable tax withholdings; provided, that any Company Option that had an exercise price per Share that was greater than or equal to the Offer Price was canceled at the Effective Time without any consideration payable (whether in the form of cash or otherwise) therefor, whether before or after the Effective Time. Pursuant to the terms of the Merger Agreement, each performance share unit award (each, a “Company PSU”) that was outstanding as of immediately prior to the Effective Time, whether vested or unvested, was canceled at the Effective Time and converted into the right to receive an amount in cash equal to the product of (i) the total number of Shares issuable in settlement of such Company PSU, which was based on deemed achievement of the applicable performance metrics at 100% of target, as determined in accordance with the applicable Company PSU award agreement, multiplied by (ii) the Offer Price, subject to applicable tax withholdings. Pursuant to the terms of the Merger Agreement, each restricted stock unit award (each, a “Company RSU”) that was outstanding as of immediately prior to the Effective Time, whether vested or unvested, was canceled at the Effective Time and converted into the right to receive an amount in cash equal to the product of (i) the total number of Shares issuable in settlement of such Company RSU, multiplied by (ii) the Offer Price, subject to applicable tax withholdings. Pursuant to the terms of the Merger Agreement, each cash-based award granted pursuant to the Company’s cash-based long-term incentive plan (each, a “Company LTIP Award”) that was outstanding as of immediately prior to the Effective Time, whether vested or unvested, was canceled at the Effective Time and converted into the right to receive cash in an amount determined by the Company’s board of directors (the “Board”), pursuant to the terms of the plan, based on actual achievement of the applicable performance metrics (as previously disclosed for awards granted in 2024 and 2025 and at 107.8% of target as of the closing of the Merger for awards granted in 2026), subject to applicable tax withholdings. Pursuant to the terms of the Merger Agreement, each restricted cash award (a “Company Restricted Cash Award”) that was outstanding as of immediately prior to the Effective Time, whether vested or unvested, was canceled at the Effective Time and converted into the right to receive an amount of cash equal to the sum of (i) any portion of the Company Restricted Cash Award that was vested, but remained unpaid, as of the Effective Time and (ii) the amount of Unvested Cash (as defined under the applicable Company Restricted Cash Award agreement) with respect to the Company Restricted Cash Award. The total amount of funds required to pay for the Shares in the Offer and the Merger and to repay certain indebtedness of the Company, was approximately $1.69 billion. These amounts exclude (i) fees and expenses related to the Offer and the Merger and (ii) any Company cash and cash equivalents assumed by Viatris. Viatris provided Purchaser with sufficient funds to purchase all Shares accepted for payment pursuant to the Offer and all Shares purchased in the Merger using Viatris’ available cash on hand and short-term borrowings.
