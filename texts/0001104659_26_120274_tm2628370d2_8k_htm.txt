Senior Notes Offering On October 29, 2026, Kimberly-Clark Corporation (the “Corporation”) completed the previously announced sale of $4.9 billion aggregate principal amount of its senior unsecured notes, consisting of: (i)$750,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.100% Notes due 2028 (the “2028 Notes”), (ii) $1,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.300% Notes due 2029 (the “2029 Notes”), (iii) $1,250,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.450% Notes due 2031 (the “2031 Notes”), (iv) $750,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.650% Notes due 2034 (the “2034 Notes”) and (v) $1,150,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.800% Notes due 2036 (the “2036 Notes” and, collectively with the 2028 Notes, the 2029 Notes, the 2031 Notes and the 2034 Notes, the “Notes”) in a private offering (the “Offering”) that is exempt from registration under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities Act”). The Notes were issued under the First Amended and Restated Indenture (as amended by the first, second and eighth supplemental indentures dated as of November 6, 1992, May 25, 1994 and October 27, 2021, respectively, the “Base Indenture”), dated as of March 1, 1988, by and among the Corporation and The Bank of New York Mellon Trust Company, N.A. (as successor in interest to The First National Bank of Chicago) as trustee (originally executed with Bank of America National Trust and Savings Association) and the Eleventh Supplemental Indenture, dated as of October 29, 2026 (the “Eleventh Supplemental Indenture” and the Base Indenture as supplemented by the Eleventh Supplemental Indenture, the “Indenture”)), by and among the Corporation and U.S. Bank Trust Company, National Association, as successor trustee under the Indenture in respect to the Notes. The Notes are the Corporation’s general, unsecured senior obligations, and rank equally in right of payment with all of the Corporation’s existing and future unsecured senior indebtedness, liabilities and other obligations, including the Corporation’s existing senior notes. The Offering was conducted in connection with the Corporation’s previously announced proposed acquisition (“Acquisition”) of Kenvue Inc. (“Kenvue”). The Corporation intends to use the net proceeds from the Offering, together with cash on hand and/or borrowings under its commercial paper program, to fund the cash consideration payable in connection with the Acquisition, and to pay related fees and expenses. The Corporation expects to use any remaining net proceeds from the Offering for general corporate purposes. Interest and Maturity The 2028 Notes bear interest at a rate of 5.100% per annum and will mature on October 29, 2028. The 2029 Notes bear interest at a rate of 5.300% per annum and will mature on October 29, 2029. The 2031 Notes bear interest at a rate of 5.450% per annum and will mature on October 29, 2031. The 2034 Notes bear interest at a rate of 5.650% per annum and will mature on January 29, 2034. The 2036 Notes bear interest at a rate of 5.800% per annum and will mature on October 29, 2036. Interest on the 2028 Notes, the 2029 Notes, the 2031 Notes and the 2036 Notes will be payable on April 29 and October 29 of each year, commencing April 29, 2027. Interest on the 2034 Notes will be payable on January 29 and July 29 of each year, commencing January 29, 2027. Optional and Special Mandatory Redemption The Corporation may redeem the Notes at its option, in whole or in part, at any time and from time to time, at the redemption prices and on the terms and conditions set forth in the Indenture. In addition, if (i) the Acquisition has not been consummated prior to the date that is five business days after the later of (x) May 31, 2027 or (y) any later date as the parties to the Acquisition agreement may agree as the “Outside Date” thereunder or (ii) the Corporation notifies the trustee for the Notes that the Corporation will not pursue the consummation of the Acquisition, then the Corporation will be required to redeem all outstanding Notes at a special mandatory redemption price equal to 101% of the aggregate principal amount of the Notes, plus accrued and unpaid interest, if any, to, but not including, the applicable special mandatory redemption date. Kenvue Guarantees As previously reported, in connection with the Acquisition, on September 28, 2026, the Corporation commenced an offer to exchange all of Kenvue’s existing senior notes in exchange for new senior notes to be issued by the Corporation (the “Exchange Offers”) pursuant to a confidential offering memorandum and consent solicitation statement. If (i) the Exchange Offers are withdrawn (but the Acquisition has been consummated and the Notes are issued and outstanding) or (ii) if the Acquisition is consummated, the Notes are issued and outstanding and the Exchange Offers are not completed within four (4) business days after the expiration date of the Exchange Offers (as may be extended pursuant to the terms described in the offering memorandum and consent solicitation statement), the Notes will be fully and unconditionally guaranteed (the “Kenvue Guarantees”), on an unsubordinated unsecured basis, by Kenvue (or its successor, as applicable). This Current Report on Form 8-K does not constitute an offer to exchange with respect to the Exchange Offers and shall not constitute an offer, solicitation or sale in any jurisdiction in which such offer, solicitation or sale would be unlawful. Covenants and Events of Default The Indenture contains covenants that, among other things, limit the ability of the Corporation and its restricted subsidiaries to issue, assume or guarantee specified secured debt, enter into sale and leaseback transactions with respect to any principal properties and consolidate with, merge into, or sell or transfer all or substantially all of its property and assets to, any other corporation. The Indenture also provides for customary events of default. Registration Rights Agreement On October 29, 2026, in connection with the completion of the Offering, the Corporation and J.P. Morgan Securities LLC, Citigroup Global Markets Inc. and Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC, as representatives of the initial purchasers of the Notes, entered into a registration rights agreement with respect to the Notes (the “Registration Rights Agreement”). The Corporation agreed under the Registration Rights Agreement to, among other things, use its commercially reasonable efforts to (i) file a registration statement on an appropriate registration form with respect to a registered offer to exchange the Notes for new notes, with terms substantially identical in all material respects to the Notes (except that the new Notes will not contain terms with respect to transfer restrictions and additional interest (as described below)), (ii) cause such registration statement to be declared effective under the Securities Act and (iii) complete the registered exchange offer within 365 days after the closing of the Acquisition. In addition, the Corporation has agreed to use commercially reasonable efforts to file, under certain circumstances, a shelf registration statement to cover resales of the Notes under the Securities Act. The Corporation shall be obligated to pay additional interest on the Notes if (A) the Corporation has not consummated the exchange offer within 365 days after the closing of the Acquisition, or, if a shelf registration statement is required and has not been declared effective under the Securities Act, on or prior to the later of (i) 365 days after the closing of the Acquisition and (ii) 365 days after the date, if any, on which the Corporation became obligated to have the shelf registration statement declared effective under the Securities Act pursuant to the Registration Rights Agreement; or (B) if applicable, a shelf registration statement covering resales of the Notes has been declared effective and such shelf registration statement ceases to be effective at any time during the effectiveness period (subject to certain exceptions) and such failure to remain effective exists for more than 90 days (whether or not consecutive) in any 12-month period during the effectiveness period. The foregoing description of the Registration Rights Agreement is a summary and is qualified in its entirety by reference to such document, which is attached as Exhibit 10.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated herein by reference.
