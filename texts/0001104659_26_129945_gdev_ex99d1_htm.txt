The decrease was primarily driven by lower bookings, partially offset by a larger net release of revenue deferred in prior periods. Bookings for all periods presented include Island Hoppers up to August 13, 2026, the date on which the game and the related intellectual property were sold. The game contributed approximately 1% of our bookings in the first nine months of 2026 and approximately 3% in the same period in 2025. Platform commissions decreased by $4 million (or 20%) in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the same period in 2025 reflecting the decrease in bookings and a lower effective commission rate as a growing share of our bookings is generated through our own distribution channels. Game operation costs remained stable at $14 million in the third quarter of both 2026 and 2025. Selling and marketing expenses in the third quarter of 2026 increased by $10 million vs. the same period in 2025, amounting to $39 million. The increase primarily reflects higher user acquisition investment in Hero Wars compared with the reduced level of spending in the third quarter of 2025, as well as the scaling of user acquisition for Aliens vs Zombies. User acquisition spending across our titles continues to be managed against our payback targets. General and administrative expenses remained relatively stable at $9 million in the third quarter of 2026 vs. $8 million in the third quarter of 2025. Other operating income in the third quarter of 2026 amounted to $6 million vs. $1 million in the same period in 2025, primarily reflecting income from the sale of the Island Hoppers intellectual property rights in August 2026. As a result of the factors above, together with (i) the share of profits of equity-accounted associates of $1 million in the third quarter of 2026 vs. the share of losses of $3 million in the same period of the prior year, (ii) the non-recurrence of the $4 million gain from the change in fair value of the convertible loan to an associate recognized in the third quarter of 2025 prior to its conversion into shares, and (iii) net finance expenses of $2 million in the third quarter of 2026 vs. net finance income of $0.2 million in the same period of 2025, we recorded a profit for the period, net of tax, of $17 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared with $24 million in the same period of 2025. Adjusted EBITDA in the third quarter of 2026 amounted to $13 million, a decrease of $13 million compared with the same period in 2025 driven primarily by the same factors as those affecting the profit, except for the share of profits or losses of equity- accounted associates, the change in fair value of financial instruments and other investments, and the income from the sale of intellectual property rights, which do not impact the Adjusted EBITDA. Recent developments On August 31, 2026, the Company launched a tender offer to purchase for cash up to $20 million in value of its shares at a purchase price of $11.03 per share (the “ Tender Offer ” ). The Tender Offer expired on September 28, 2026, resulting in the Company ’ s accepting for purchase 57,110 shares for an aggregate cost of approximately $629.9 thousand, accounted for as a cash outflow used in financing activities in Q3 2026. The shares acquired pursuant to the Tender Offer are held by the Company as treasury shares. About GDEV GDEV is a gaming and entertainment holding company, focused on development and growth of its franchise portfolio across various genres and platforms. With a diverse range of subsidiaries including Nexters and Cubic Games, among others, GDEV strives to create games that will inspire and engage millions of players for years to come. Its franchises, such as Hero Wars, Pixel Gun 3D and others have accumulated over 550 million installs and $2.5 billion of bookings worldwide. The Company defines Adjusted EBITDA as the profit/loss for the period, net of tax as presented in the Company’s financial statements in accordance with IFRS, adjusted to exclude (i) goodwill and investments in equity-accounted associates’ impairment, (ii) loss on disposal of subsidiaries, (iii) income tax expense, (iv) other financial income, finance income and expenses other than foreign exchange gains and losses and bank charges, (v) change in fair value of share warrant obligations and other financial instruments, (vi) share of profit/(loss) of equity-accounted associates, (vii) depreciation and amortization, (viii) share-based payments expense and (ix) certain non-cash or other special items that we do not consider indicative of our ongoing operating performance. The Company uses this Non-IFRS Financial Measure for business planning purposes and in measuring its performance relative to that of its competitors. The Company believes that this Non-IFRS Financial Measure is a useful financial metric to assess its operating performance from period-to-period by excluding certain items that the Company believes are not representative of its core business. This Non-IFRS Financial Measure is not intended to replace, and should not be considered superior to, the presentation of the Company’s financial results in accordance with IFRS. The use of the Non-IFRS Financial Measure terms may differ from similar measures reported by other companies and may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures.
