Business Combination Agreement The Domestication The Business Combination Agreement provides, among other things, that at least one day prior to the closing of the transactions contemplated by the Business Combination Agreement (the “Closing” and the date upon which the Closing actually occurs, the “Closing Date”), ADAC will transfer by way of continuation out of the Cayman Islands and into the State of Texas so as to domesticate as a Texas corporation in accordance with ADAC’s governing documents, Section 10.102 of the Texas Business Organizations Code (the “TBOC”) and Part XII of the Cayman Islands Companies Act (2025 Revision) (the “Domestication”). In connection with the Domestication, ADAC will change its name to “EasyPost, Inc.” (ADAC following the Domestication, “New EasyPost”). In connection with and as part of the Domestication, each Class A ordinary share of ADAC, par value $0.0001 per share (the “ADAC Class A Shares”), and each Class B ordinary share of ADAC, par value $0.0001 per share (the “ADAC Class B Shares” or the “Founder Shares,” and together with the ADAC Class A Shares, the “ADAC Ordinary Shares”), in each case, that is issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Domestication (after giving effect to the redemption of the ADAC Class A Shares initially issued in ADAC’s initial public offering (“IPO”) (the “Public Shares” and the holders of Public Shares, the “Public Shareholders”) that are validly submitted for redemption and not withdrawn), will be converted into one share of Class A common stock, par value $0.0001 per share, of New EasyPost (the “New EasyPost Class A Common Stock”). ADAC Shareholder Redemptions ADAC will provide the holders of ADAC Class A Shares the right to have all or a portion of their ADAC Class A Shares redeemed for cash in connection with the Business Combination, in accordance with ADAC’s governing documents, for a per-share price equal to the pro rata portion of the funds then in ADAC’s trust account (including interest not previously released to pay taxes). Subject to receipt of the requisite ADAC shareholder approvals, any such redemptions will be completed at least one business day prior to the Domestication. The Mergers Subject to the terms and conditions of the Business Combination Agreement, on the Closing Date, and following the Domestication (which will occur at least one day prior to the Closing Date), (a) Merger Sub will merge with and into EasyPost (the “First Merger”), with EasyPost surviving the First Merger as a wholly owned subsidiary of New EasyPost (EasyPost, in its capacity as the surviving company in the First Merger, the “Surviving Company”), and (b) immediately following the First Merger, and as part of the same overall transaction as the First Merger, the Surviving Company will merge with and into Merger Sub II (the “Second Merger” and, together with the First Merger, the “Mergers”), with Merger Sub II surviving the Second Merger (Merger Sub II, in its capacity as the surviving entity of the Second Merger, the “Surviving Entity”) as a direct wholly owned subsidiary of New EasyPost. The First Merger will be consummated by filing certificates of merger with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware and the Secretary of State of the State of Texas (the “First Certificates of Merger”) in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Delaware General Corporation Law (the “DGCL”) and the TBOC, and will become effective at the time the First Certificates of Merger are accepted for filing or such later time as is agreed by ADAC and EasyPost and specified therein (the “First Effective Time”). As soon as practicable following the First Effective Time, and in any case on the same day, the Second Merger will be consummated by filing certificates of merger with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware and the Secretary of State of the State of Texas (the “Second Certificates of Merger”) in accordance with the relevant provisions of the DGCL and the TBOC, and will become effective at the time of such filing or such later time as is agreed by ADAC and EasyPost and specified therein (the “Second Effective Time”). At the First Effective Time, (i) each share of EasyPost common stock (collectively, “EasyPost Common Shares”) issued and outstanding as of immediately prior to the First Effective Time (including such shares issued upon (x) the conversion or exercise of the Company Warrants, (y) the conversion of the Company Convertible Instruments, and (z) the conversion of all shares of EasyPost preferred stock into EasyPost Common Shares prior to the First Effective Time in accordance with the terms of the Business Combination Agreement, but excluding EasyPost Common Shares held in treasury, by the Founder or by EasyPost stockholders who have properly demanded appraisal of such EasyPost Common Shares in accordance with Section 262 of the DGCL) will be automatically canceled and extinguished and converted into the right to receive a number of shares of New EasyPost Class A Common Stock equal to an exchange ratio, which is based on an EasyPost equity value of approximately $479 million and a value of $10.00 per share of New EasyPost Common Stock (the “Exchange Ratio”); (ii) each share of EasyPost capital stock held by the Founder issued and outstanding as of immediately prior to the First Effective Time will be automatically canceled and extinguished and converted into the right to receive a number of shares of Class B common stock, par value $0.0001 per share, of New EasyPost (the “New EasyPost Class B Common Stock,” and together with the New EasyPost Class A Common Stock, the “New EasyPost Common Stock”) equal to the Exchange Ratio; (iii) each option to purchase EasyPost Common Shares (each, an “EasyPost Option”), whether vested or unvested, will be assumed by New EasyPost, will cease to represent the right to purchase EasyPost Common Shares and will be converted into an option to purchase shares of New EasyPost Class A Common Stock under the applicable EasyPost equity plan, in an amount equal to the product (rounded down to the nearest whole number) of (x) the number of EasyPost Common Shares subject to such EasyPost Option immediately prior to the First Effective Time, multiplied by (y) the Exchange Ratio, at an exercise price per share (rounded up to the nearest whole cent) equal to the quotient of (i) the exercise price per share of such EasyPost Option immediately prior to the First Effective Time, divided by (ii) the Exchange Ratio, and generally subject to the same terms and conditions (including applicable vesting, expiration and forfeiture provisions) that applied to the corresponding EasyPost Option immediately prior to the First Effective Time; and (iv) each restricted stock unit award that is outstanding with respect to EasyPost Common Shares (each, an “EasyPost RSU Award”) will be assumed by New EasyPost, will cease to have any rights in respect of the EasyPost Common Shares and will be converted into a restricted stock unit award under the applicable EasyPost equity plan that settles in a number of shares of New EasyPost Class A Common Stock (rounded down to the nearest whole share) equal to the product of the number of EasyPost Common Shares subject to such EasyPost RSU Award immediately prior to the First Effective Time, multiplied by the Exchange Ratio, and that will generally be subject to the same terms and conditions (including applicable vesting, expiration and forfeiture provisions) that applied to the corresponding EasyPost RSU Award immediately prior to the First Effective Time. The Exchange Ratio will be equal to the quotient obtained by dividing (a) the Transaction Share Consideration (as defined in the Business Combination Agreement) by (b) the number of Fully-Diluted Shares. “Fully-Diluted Shares” means, without duplication, (a) the aggregate number of EasyPost Common Shares and any other shares of capital stock of EasyPost issued and outstanding as of immediately prior to the First Effective Time, calculated on a fully-diluted, as-converted-to-EasyPost Common Shares basis in accordance with EasyPost’s certificate of incorporation, including EasyPost Common Shares issued (i) upon the exercise of the Company Warrants immediately prior to the Closing (assuming exercise on a net exercise basis), (ii) upon the conversion of the Company Convertible Instruments, to the extent converted prior to the Closing, and (iii) in connection with the conversion of all shares of EasyPost preferred stock into EasyPost Common Shares (the “Company Preferred Share Conversion”), plus (b) the aggregate number of EasyPost Common Shares issuable upon the full exercise, settlement, exchange or conversion of EasyPost equity awards outstanding as of immediately prior to the First Effective Time (assuming such exercise, settlement, exchange or conversion on a net exercise basis). Pursuant to the New EasyPost certificate of formation, each share of New EasyPost Class A Common Stock will be entitled to one (1) vote per share, and each share of New EasyPost Class B Common Stock will be entitled to five (5) votes per share. The shares of New EasyPost Class B Common Stock will not be publicly listed or traded and will be freely convertible into shares of New EasyPost Class A Common Stock on a one-for-one basis, upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the New EasyPost certificate of formation. From and after the First Effective Time, EasyPost Common Shares held by EasyPost stockholders who have properly demanded appraisal will be canceled and such holders will be entitled only to the rights granted under Section 262 of the DGCL. At the Second Effective Time, each share of capital stock of the Surviving Company will be canceled and converted into one share of the Surviving Entity.
