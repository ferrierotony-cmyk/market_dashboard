Item 8.01. Other Events. On October 13, 2026, the Company issued a press release announcing that it intends to offer, subject to market and other conditions, $850.0 million aggregate principal amount of convertible senior notes due 2032 (the “notes”) in a private offering to persons reasonably believed to be qualified institutional buyers in reliance on Rule 144A under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, and grant to the initial purchasers of the notes an option to purchase, within a 13-day period beginning on, and including, the date on which the notes are first issued, up to an additional $150.0 million aggregate principal amount of the notes. Concurrently with the offering, the Company expects to enter into separate, privately negotiated transactions with a limited number of holders of its 1.75% Convertible Senior Notes due 2030 (the “2030 notes”) to exchange a portion of the 2030 notes for shares of the Company’s common stock (the “Concurrent 2030 Notes Exchange”). A copy of the press release announcing the offering and the Concurrent 2030 Notes Exchange is filed as Exhibit 99.2 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference. The information included in this Current Report on Form 8-K is neither an offer to sell nor a solicitation of an offer to buy any securities and does not constitute an offer to exchange or repurchase, or a notice of redemption with respect to, the 2030 notes.
