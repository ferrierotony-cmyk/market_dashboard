Wix began as an easy-to-use yet powerful visual development and editing environment that eliminated the need for complex coding and expensive design services, primarily attracting users creating websites for their business or personal use. Beginning in 2019, we evolved our business strategy to expand our total addressable market (“ TAM ”) beyond the Self Creator market into the professional world of design agencies, developers, and freelancers creating content and applications for their clients. Over the past two decades, Wix has evolved into an AI leader in website creation and management, offering an industry-leading suite of generative AI tools that transform natural language prompts into fully designed, production-ready websites, branded content, and tailored business solutions delivered through our freemium SaaS model (both free and premium services). Wix continues to push the boundaries of what’s possible with AI, providing a powerful platform for anyone, from solo creators to global enterprises, to succeed online. In June 2025, we expanded our TAM with the acquisition of Base44, a fast-growing vibe coding platform. After democratizing website creation for two decades, AI now extends that mission from building a website to the broader online creation space, most importantly building software. Today, we are pursuing multiple platform expansions at once: AI-powered website creation, software development and small and medium-sized business (“ SMB ”) tools, all in a completely open ecosystem. The Company’s management and Board remain committed to responsible management of the Company’s dilution posture and continue to take significant measures to reduce dilution, while balancing the Company’s need to hire and retain talented employees and executives, who are the drivers of the Company’s success. Such measures include the application of a net exercise mechanism for outstanding options, regular cancellations of unallocated equity, our prior decision to transition from grants of options to mostly restricted share units (“ RSUs ”) for our wider employee and management base, and our discontinuation of granting stock options to our Co-CEOs, as discussed in the proposed Compensation Package in Proposal No. 2 below. Beginning in the second quarter of 2026, we introduced organizational realignment measures which included reducing our workforce by approximately 20%. This reduction in headcount has naturally led to fewer ordinary shares underlying equity awards being allocated to new and existing employees, thereby easing pressure on our dilution.
