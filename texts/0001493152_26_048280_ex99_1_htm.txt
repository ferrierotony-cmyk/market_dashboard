Under the Cayman Islands Companies Act, a person who has agreed to become a member of a Cayman Islands company and whose name is entered on the register of members of such company is considered a member of the company. Under Cayman Islands law, the register of members is prima facie evidence of any matters by the Cayman Islands Companies Act directed or authorised to be inserted therein. Accordingly, a CDP Depositor holding Shares through CDP would not be recognised as our Shareholder under the laws of the Cayman Islands and does not have the right to attend and vote at the general meetings of our Company. OVERVIEW Our Company was incorporated in the Cayman Islands on 16 February 2024 under the Cayman Islands Companies Act as an exempted company with limited liability, under the name of “HomesToLife Ltd”, and has been listed on Nasdaq since 1 October 2024 under the stock code “HTLM”. Our Company’s registration number is 407249. We are a global home furniture company headquartered in Singapore. Leveraging 50 years of heritage established by our founders, being our Executive Directors, our Group combines wholesale distribution, consumer retail and sourcing capabilities, supported by a diversified sourcing and supplier network across China, Vietnam and India. Our Group operates through three core business divisions: (a) export division for supplying furniture to wholesale customers such as retailers and distributors worldwide; (b) leather trading division; and (c) retail division, with direct retail operations in Singapore and South Korea. Across these business divisions, our Group operates an integrated supply chain model that encompasses product design and development, sourcing from our manufacturing partners, and the coordination of logistics and distribution channels. This operating model enables our Group to translate designs into production-ready products efficiently, supporting both branded and white-label offerings, and allows us to deliver scale and consistency across multiple markets. OUR COMPETITIVE STRENGTHS We believe that we have the following competitive strengths: (1) We operate an integrated product design and development, sourcing and distribution platform Our Group operates an integrated platform spanning product design and development, procurement of leather hides, sourcing of upholstered furniture from manufacturing partners, and coordination of logistics and distribution. Through this platform, our Group has access to the scale and depth of the design and production resources available through the GHC Group, while maintaining a relatively lean in-house team in Singapore. This facilitates the development of a broad and regularly refreshed portfolio comprising hundreds of models, from which our retail, export sales and wholesale customers may select products suited to their respective markets and customer segments. By coordinating product specifications, procurement, production schedules and delivery requirements across these functions, our Group is also able to respond flexibly to local market requirements and customer preferences. This integrated platform enables us to offer scalable and production-ready product offerings across different markets. 12 (2) We have an established export platform with global market reach and quick fulfilment capability Our Group has built an established wholesale export platform serving furniture retailers and distributors in more than 30 countries across Asia-Pacific, Europe and North America, with our products sold in more than 5,000 retail outlets globally. We offer both ready-stock and made-to-order fulfilment options, providing our customers with flexibility to plan regular inventory replenishment and respond to urgent or ad hoc orders. Our close working relationships and multi-year contract manufacturing arrangements with our related party manufacturers within the GHC Group support continuity of supply, production coordination and quick turnaround for orders. (3) We have established multi-brand, white label, and customisation capabilities serving different customer segments Our Group maintains a diversified portfolio of six furniture brands, each positioned to address different consumer preferences and price segments across markets. In addition to distributing products under our own brands, we supply white-label products and offer a range of customisation options, including choice of material, colour, configuration and sizing. These capabilities enable us to serve different customer segments and distribution channels while maintaining consistent quality standards across our product lines. (4) We have an experienced management team with deep industry relationships and market knowledge Our Group is led by an experienced management team with extensive knowledge and experience in the global upholstered furniture industry. Each of Mr. Phua Yong Pin, our Executive Chairman, and Mr. Phua Yong Tat, our Executive Vice Chairman, has over 40 years of experience in the furniture industry. Their industry knowledge, long-standing customer relationships and familiarity with different geographic markets and customer segments, have supported the development and expansion of our retail and export business across Asia-Pacific, Europe and North America. (5) We are uniquely positioned to offer a one-stop shop for retail customers Our Group offers retail customers a broad range of upholstered furniture, case goods, accessories and other home furnishing products. In addition to our own portfolio of upholstered furniture brands, our case goods, accessories and complementary furniture products are sourced internationally and carefully curated by us to complement our upholstered furniture offerings. This allows customers to purchase multiple categories of furniture and accessories through our retail platform. Our trained sales personnel also provide product selection and space-planning guidance based on customers’ furnishing preferences, intended use, available space and budget. Together, these capabilities strengthen our positioning in the premium furniture retail segment. Our initiatives will include adopting consistent brand messaging, developing curated product collections, enhancing retail and trade marketing activities, participating selectively in exhibitions and industry platforms, and promoting our brands through social media, key opinion leaders, in-house content creation and consumer events. These initiatives are intended to strengthen brand differentiation, enhance engagement with consumers and business partners, and support the long-term development of our brands. (2) Deepen and grow our export and wholesale channels in existing and new markets We intend to deepen our relationships with existing wholesale customers in our core European, Asia-Pacific and North American markets through (a) product range expansion by developing curated product collections tailored to the specific preferences and market positioning of individual wholesale customers, including customisation options and white-label capabilities; (b) consistent brand messaging by adopting a unified brand positioning across channels to reinforce our Group’s value proposition to wholesale partners; (c) enhanced retail and trade marketing support by providing wholesale customers with retail merchandising support, point-of-sale materials, and digital marketing assets; and (d) selective trade fair participation by maintaining an active presence at key international furniture exhibitions (including the Canton Fair, Shanghai Fair, and Brussels Fair) to showcase new products and strengthen trade relationships, while expanding into new wholesale markets through sales partners, distribution networks, business development activities and other market-entry arrangements. We also intend to grow our leather trading business by maintaining stable supply relationships and exploring opportunities to expand our supplier network for raw leather hides. These initiatives are intended to strengthen our position in our core markets while capturing scalable growth opportunities in Asia and other high-potential regions. (3) Strategic expansion of our retail presence in Singapore, South Korea and overseas We intend to selectively expand our retail presence in Singapore by opening further stores in locations with good customer demand and footfall, while continuing to refine our showroom formats and product displays. In South Korea, we plan to expand our retail touchpoints through direct retail stores, department store concessions and pop-up locations. We also intend to extend the HomesToLife retail store concept into selected cities in the Asia-Pacific region and explore mergers and acquisitions, strategic partnerships and distributorship arrangements with local companies to deepen our connection with local markets. (4) Invest in design innovation and product development capabilities and collaborate with designers and technology partners We intend to maintain a regular pipeline of new product launches, continue introducing new offerings at international and regional exhibitions, and offer customisation options to meet evolving market needs. We also plan to collaborate with designers and technology partners, including Zeica Labs, in which we made a strategic investment in January 2026. Zeica Labs is a Singapore technology company that owns the spatial-audio intellectual property and engineering capabilities behind Super X-Fi, a patented audio technology designed to deliver immersive, personalised sound experiences. We plan to explore the integration of spatial-audio and other smart home technologies into selected furniture collections. These initiatives are intended to enhance product differentiation, strengthen our premium positioning and maintain our competitive position in the global upholstered furniture market.
