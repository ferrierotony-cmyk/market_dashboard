Aircraft Financing Transactions Since March 31, 2026, the Company completed several aircraft financing transactions. In April 2026, the Company completed the final drawdown of $25.1 million under our predelivery deposit financing facility, resulting in the facility being fully drawn. In April 2026, the Company borrowed $115.0 million under an aircraft secured credit facility. The note bears interest at a variable rate based on three-month SOFR and is payable in quarterly installments over a term of 3 years. In April 2026, the Company also entered into an aircraft financing facility providing for borrowings of up to $176.0 million, secured by new aircraft upon delivery. In May 2026, the Company drew $44.0 million available under the agreement. Borrowings under this facility bear interest at a variable rate based on three-month SOFR and is payable in quarterly installments over a term of 10 years. In May 2026, the Company entered into an aircraft secured financing facility providing for borrowings of up to $85.6 million, of which $40.6 million was drawn in the same month. The note bears interest at a variable rate based on a three-month SOFR and is payable in quarterly installments over a term of 12 years. Item 8.01 Other Events.
