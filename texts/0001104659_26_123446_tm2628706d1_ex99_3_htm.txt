ANI has been leveraging its established commercial and operational infrastructure to build a robust portfolio of durable and differentiated assets and drive sustainable growth. ANI has followed Egetis’ progress for a significant amount of time and has been highly impressed by Egetis’ achievements in building a differentiated rare disease platform and advancing EMCITATE ® toward commercialization. EMCITATE ® is approved in the European Union and was, on September 28, 2026, approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (the “ FDA ”) as the first and only approved treatment of peripheral thyrotoxicosis in adults and pediatric patients with MCT8 deficiency (Allan–Herndon–Dudley syndrome) in the United States. In connection with the approval, the FDA granted Egetis a PRV. The consideration in the Offer fully reflects the value of the PRV attributable to Egetis. ANI believes that Egetis represents a highly compelling strategic fit. Egetis’ focus on rare diseases, its expertise in helping underserved patient populations and the significant commercial opportunity associated with EMCITATE ® align closely with ANI’s strategic priorities and long-term vision to transform into a leading rare disease company. ANI believes that it is well positioned to support Egetis’ next phase of growth, including the commercial launch of EMCITATE ® in the United States. ANI has an established U.S. rare disease commercial organization, and believes that its track record of launching and growing specialized pharmaceutical products, can support the launch of EMCITATE ® in the United States, thereby accelerating patient access to the only approved treatment for MCT8 deficiency. Successfully bringing a rare disease product to market in the United States requires substantial upfront investment in a dedicated commercial organization, including sales and medical affairs personnel, market access and reimbursement capabilities, patient identification and support programs and distribution infrastructure, and such investment is typically required well in advance of the product generating meaningful revenues. As a standalone company, Egetis would need to continue to build and fund such an organization in the United States, with the associated costs, time requirements and execution risk. ANI believes that the Offer allows Egetis’ shareholders to realize today, in cash, the value of the EMCITATE ® opportunity without bearing the additional costs and risks associated with the U.S. commercial build-out and launch. The Offer ANI offers SEK 11.25 in cash for each Egetis share, SEK 65,750,817 in cash for the EUR 3 million principal amount of the Convertible and SEK 6.99 in cash for each Warrant. The Convertible consists of a convertible loan of EUR 3 million forming part of Egetis’ 2023 debt financing, which may be converted into shares in Egetis at a conversion price of approximately EUR 0.5133 per share, repayable no later than April 1, 2027 absent prior conversion. The consideration for the Convertible in the Offer has been determined based on the price per share in the Offer and the conversion price. Upon conversion, the Convertible entitles the holder to 5,844,517 shares in Egetis. Based on the price per share in the Offer, SEK 11.25, ANI accordingly offers SEK 65,750,817 in cash for the EUR 3 million principal amount of the Convertible. The Warrants consist of 1,090,977 warrants issued in connection with the above-mentioned 2023 debt financing, each conferring the right on the holder to subscribe, in cash, for one share in Egetis, at an exercise price of SEK 4.26 per share, no later than the tenth anniversary of the date of grant. The consideration for the Warrants in the Offer has been determined based on the see-through value of the Warrants. Based on the price per share in the Offer, SEK 11.25, and the exercise price per share under the Warrants, SEK 4.26, ANI accordingly offers SEK 6.99 in cash for each Warrant.
