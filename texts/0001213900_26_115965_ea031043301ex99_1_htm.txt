Announces Initial US$3.0 Million Convertible Note Registered Direct Offering and Potential Additional Investments of Up to US$9.0 Million SHANGHAI, November 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ — Youlife Group Inc. (“Youlife” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: YOUL), a leading blue-collar lifetime service provider in China, today announced that it has entered into a securities purchase agreement (the “Purchase Agreement”) with an institutional investor for the issuance and sale of a 9% senior original issue discount convertible promissory note with an original principal amount of US$3.0 million (the “Note”) in a registered direct offering (the “Offering”). The Purchase Agreement also provides for potential subsequent closings involving up to an additional US$9.0 million in aggregate principal amount of convertible notes, subject to mutual consent of the parties and the satisfaction of the applicable conditions set forth therein. The Note will be issued for a subscription amount of US$2.73 million, reflecting a 9% original issue discount. In connection with the Offering, the Company will also issue 262,009 Class A ordinary shares represented by American depositary shares (“ADSs”) as an additional 2% original issue discount, equal to US$60,000, on the principal amount of the Note. The Company expects to receive gross cash proceeds of approximately US$2.7 million, before deducting offering expenses. The Note will mature 12 months after issuance and will not bear interest before an event of default. It will be convertible at the investor’s option into the Company’s Class A ordinary shares represented by ADSs, with up to 65,502,184 Class A ordinary shares represented by ADSs issuable upon conversion of the Note, at the lower of a fixed conversion price and a market-based alternative conversion price, subject to a floor price, applicable adjustments and a 9.99% beneficial ownership limitation, as more fully described in the prospectus supplement relating to the Offering. In connection with the Offering, 3,300,000 treasury shares represented by ADSs will be delivered to the investor and held in abeyance as pre-delivery ADSs to facilitate the settlement of future conversions of the Note. These ADSs are subject to restrictions on sale, voting and the exercise of shareholder rights before their application in accordance with the transaction documents, as well as additional registration, purchase and cancellation provisions described in the prospectus supplement. The Offering is expected to close on or about November 3, 2026, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the applicable closing conditions. The Company intends to use the net proceeds for working capital and general corporate purposes. The securities are being offered pursuant to the Company’s shelf registration statement on Form F-3 (File No. 333-299194), which was declared effective by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) on October 19, 2026. A prospectus supplement relating to the Offering has been filed with the SEC. Investors should read the prospectus supplement and accompanying prospectus, together with the documents incorporated by reference therein, for complete information about the Company and the Offering. These documents will be available through the SEC’s website.
